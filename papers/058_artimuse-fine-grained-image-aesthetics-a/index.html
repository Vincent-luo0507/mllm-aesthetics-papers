中文摘要

教育应用、艺术创作与人工智能生成内容（AIGC）技术的快速推进，大幅提高了对综合性图像美学评估（IAA）的实际需求，尤其需要既能给出量化评分、又具备专业理解力的方法。基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的 IAA 方法比传统方法具有更强的感知与泛化能力，但存在模态偏置（只出分数或只出文本），且缺乏细粒度属性分解，因而无法支撑更深入的美学评估。为此，本文提出：（1）ArtiMuse，一种具备联合评分与专家级理解能力的创新型 MLLM 图像美学评估模型；（2）ArtiMuse-10K，首个由专家整理的图像美学数据集，包含 10,000 张图像，覆盖 5 个大类与 15 个子类，每张均由专业专家标注 8 个维度的属性分析与整体分数。模型与数据集都将公开，以推动该领域发展。

Abstract (原文)

The rapid advancement of educational applications, artistic creation, and AI-generated content (AIGC) technologies has substantially increased practical requirements for comprehensive Image Aesthetics Assessment (IAA), particularly demanding methods capable of delivering both quantitative scoring and professional understanding. Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM)-based IAA methods demonstrate stronger perceptual and generalization capabilities compared to traditional approaches, yet they suffer from modality bias (score-only or text-only) and lack fine-grained attribute decomposition, thereby failing to support further aesthetic assessment. In this paper, we present:(1) ArtiMuse, an innovative MLLM-based IAA model with Joint Scoring and Expert-Level Understanding capabilities; (2) ArtiMuse-10K, the first expert-curated image aesthetic dataset comprising 10,000 images spanning 5 main categories and 15 subcategories, each annotated by professional experts with 8-dimensional attributes analysis and a holistic score. Both the model and dataset will be made public to advance the field.

1 Introduction

1 引言

In the era of digitalization and visual information explosion, images have become an essential medium for human beings to perceive the world, document daily life, and express emotions. From professional photography and painting to casual snapshots and sharing, images play a crucial role in conveying aesthetic values, emotional narratives, and storytelling. The advent of artificial intelligence generated content (AIGC) technologies [1, 2, 3] has further democratized visual content creation. However, this abundance of visual content also poses new challenges for quality assessment, filtering, and recommendation. While existing image quality assessment (IQA) techniques [4, 5, 6] have matured in detecting low-level degradations such as blurriness, noise, and compression artifacts, they largely focus on the technical fidelity of images and fail to capture their higher-level aesthetic attributes. Image aesthetics assessment (IAA) [7, 8, 9], which evaluates aspects such as artistic appeal, color harmony, and emotional expression, is increasingly recognized as a fundamental capability in applications including AIGC content evaluation, creative assistance, and photography education.

在数字化与视觉信息爆炸的时代，图像已成为人类感知世界、记录日常生活与表达情感的重要媒介。从专业摄影、绘画到随手拍摄与分享，图像在传递美学价值、情感叙事与故事性方面起着关键作用。人工智能生成内容（AIGC）技术 [1, 2, 3] 的出现进一步降低了视觉内容创作的门槛。然而，视觉内容的极大丰富也给质量评估、筛选与推荐带来了新挑战。现有图像质量评价（IQA）技术 [4, 5, 6] 在检测模糊、噪声、压缩伪影等底层退化方面已相当成熟，但它们主要关注图像的技术保真度，无法捕捉更高层的美学属性。图像美学评估（IAA）[7, 8, 9] 审视艺术感染力、色彩和谐、情感表达等方面，正日益被视为 AIGC 内容评测、创作辅助与摄影教育等应用中的一项基础能力。

Despite the growing demand, current IAA methods face notable limitations. Most existing approaches rely on simplistic score predictions without capturing the inherent subjectivity, multidimensionality, and nuanced interpretations of aesthetics. Moreover, available datasets are often small in scale, coarse in granularity, and lack professionally curated annotations based on established aesthetic theories. This gap severely limits the ability of state-of-the-art multimodal large models (MLLMs) [10, 11] to understand and reason about aesthetics.

尽管需求持续增长，现有 IAA 方法仍存在明显局限。多数方法只做简单的分数预测，未能刻画美学固有的主观性、多维性与细微阐释。此外，现有数据集往往规模小、粒度粗，且缺乏基于成熟美学理论的专业标注。这一缺口严重制约了当前最优多模态大模型（MLLM）[10, 11] 理解与推理美学的能力。

图 2：与现有模型相比，ArtiMuse 在多维评估中同时实现准确评价与精确美学评分，表现更优。

To address these challenges, we introduce ArtiMuse, a multimodal large language model (MLLM) for professional aesthetic understanding, together with ArtiMuse-10K, a meticulously curated, expert-annotated dataset. Collaborating with domain experts in aesthetics, each with 3 to over 30 years of experience, we systematically define eight explainable and fine-grained aesthetic attributes, covering aspects such as Composition & Design, Visual Elements & Structure, and Originality & Creativity, among others. Based on these attributes, we construct ArtiMuse-10K, the largest and most comprehensive fine-grained image aesthetics dataset to date, featuring both quantitative aesthetic scores and expert-written textual analyses across diverse visual domains, including graphic design, 3D design, AIGC-generated images, photography, and painting & calligraphy.

为应对这些挑战，我们推出 ArtiMuse——一个面向专业美学理解的多模态大语言模型（MLLM），以及精心整理、由专家标注的数据集 ArtiMuse-10K。我们与美学领域专家（从业经验从 3 年到 30 余年不等）合作，系统定义了八个可解释的细粒度美学属性，涵盖构图与设计、视觉元素与结构、原创性与创造力等方面。基于这些属性，我们构建了 ArtiMuse-10K——迄今规模最大、最完备的细粒度图像美学数据集，既包含量化的美学分数，也包含专家撰写的文本分析，覆盖平面设计、三维设计、AIGC 生成图像、摄影以及绘画与书法等多个视觉领域。

Leveraging this dataset, ArtiMuse is trained to jointly predict aesthetic scores and generate expert-level, fine-grained textual feedback, advancing aesthetic AI from mere score prediction toward holistic aesthetic reasoning and user-interpretable analysis. Notably, ArtiMuse achieves state-of-the-art performance across multiple widely used public aesthetics benchmarks, demonstrating its robust generalization ability and superior performance in both quantitative assessment and qualitative explanation, as shown in Fig. 1.

依托该数据集，ArtiMuse 被训练为同时预测美学分数并生成专家级、细粒度的文本反馈，把美学 AI 从单纯的分数预测推向整体性的美学推理与用户可理解的分析。如图 1 所示，ArtiMuse 在多个广泛使用的公开美学基准上取得当前最优性能，展现出稳健的泛化能力，在定量评估与定性解释两方面均表现优异。

In addition, a core technical challenge in aesthetics modeling lies in continuous score prediction using MLLMs, which are inherently designed for discrete token generation. Existing methods such as Q-Align [12] attempt to transform continuous scores into discrete ratings, and then reconstruct continuous values by weighted averaging over rating logits. However, this discretization inevitably incurs significant information loss and often leads to inaccurate predictions. To overcome this limitation, we propose a novel Token As Score strategy that densely maps predefined discrete tokens to continuous values. Specifically, we utilize existing tokens within the native LLM tokenizer to represent numeric values, thus eliminating the need to expand the vocabulary or retrain the tokenizer. This lightweight yet effective technique enables precise and robust modeling of continuous values within the MLLM framework, substantially improving the fidelity of aesthetics scoring.

此外，美学建模的一个核心技术难点在于：MLLM 天生是为离散词元生成而设计的，却要用于连续分数预测。Q-Align [12] 等已有方法尝试把连续分数转换为离散等级，再通过对等级 logits 加权平均重建连续值。然而这种离散化不可避免地造成显著信息损失，并常导致预测不准。为克服这一局限，我们提出一种新颖的 Token As Score 策略，把预定义的离散词元稠密地映射到连续值。具体地，我们利用原生 LLM 分词器中已有的词元来表示数值，因而无需扩展词表或重新训练分词器。这一轻量而有效的技术使 MLLM 框架内连续值的建模更精确、更稳健，大幅提升了美学评分的保真度。

(3) Token As Score, which enables precise continuous aesthetics scoring in MLLMs by mapping existing tokens to numeric values, avoiding quantization loss and tokenizer changes. It offers a lightweight, effective solution for accurate and stable score prediction.

2 Related Work

2 相关工作

2.1 Multi-modality Large Language Models

2.1 多模态大语言模型

With the advancement of MLLMs [13, 14, 11, 10], their ability has expanded from basic image-text matching to understanding high-level semantic content, offering new possibilities for image aesthetics assessment. However, current MLLMs still struggle with objective evaluation, often producing overly positive and superficial judgments. Moreover, the text they generate differs significantly from the professional descriptions used by human experts, making them less suitable for high-quality automated aesthetic evaluation. Therefore, it is necessary to systematically optimize and guide these models through fine-tuning strategies.

随着 MLLM [13, 14, 11, 10] 的发展，其能力已从基础的图文匹配扩展到理解高层语义内容，为图像美学评估提供了新的可能。然而，当前 MLLM 在客观评价上仍有困难，常给出过于正面、流于表面的判断；而且它们生成的文本与人类专家所用的专业描述差异显著，难以胜任高质量的自动化美学评估。因此有必要通过微调策略对这类模型进行系统优化与引导。

2.2 Image Aesthetics Assessment

2.2 图像美学评估

Datasets. As summarized in Table 1, existing IAA datasets suffer from three key limitations: (1) Many [15, 16, 17, 18] offer overall aesthetics scores but lack detailed evaluative descriptions, while others [19, 20] provide only vague comments without numerical ratings; (2) Most [9, 21] focus solely on overall impressions, lacking fine-grained aesthetic attribute annotations; (3) In terms of content, datasets [16, 17, 18] are mainly photographic, with limited inclusion of artworks [9, 19, 22] and little to no AIGC or everyday scene coverage. These gaps hinder comprehensive aesthetic modeling, underscoring the need for a more diverse, well-annotated benchmark.

数据集。如表 1 所总结，现有 IAA 数据集存在三方面关键局限：（1）许多数据集 [15, 16, 17, 18] 提供整体美学分数却缺少详细的评价性描述，另一些 [19, 20] 只给出模糊评论而没有数值评分；（2）多数数据集 [9, 21] 只关注整体印象，缺少细粒度美学属性标注；（3）在内容上，数据集 [16, 17, 18] 以摄影为主，艺术作品 [9, 19, 22] 收录有限，AIGC 与日常场景几乎没有覆盖。这些缺口阻碍了全面的美学建模，凸显出构建更多样、标注更完善基准的必要性。

Models. IAA models have evolved from simple regression to multimodal generative evaluation with integrated language understanding. Existing approaches fall into two categories: (1) Regression-based models (e.g., TANet [15], AesMamba [8]) directly predict aesthetics scores from image features but lack interpretability and generalization; (2) MLLM-based generative models leverage vision-language understanding to align better with human perception. Instruction-tuned models [23, 24] improve text generation but with limited granularity. AesExpert [7] produces expert-style descriptions but lacks score prediction. Q-Align [12] and UNIAA [25] combine text and discrete scores, yet lack fine-grained dimension-level evaluation. To overcome these gaps, we introduce ArtiMuse, a unified model that generates expert-level analysis and accurate aesthetics scores.

模型。IAA 模型已从简单的回归演进为融合语言理解的多模态生成式评估。现有方法分两类：（1）基于回归的模型（如 TANet [15]、AesMamba [8]）直接从图像特征预测美学分数，但缺乏可解释性与泛化能力；（2）基于 MLLM 的生成式模型借助视觉语言理解，与人类感知契合更好。经指令微调的模型 [23, 24] 改善了文本生成，但粒度有限。AesExpert [7] 能生成专家式描述，却不具备分数预测能力。Q-Align [12] 与 UNIAA [25] 结合了文本与离散分数，仍缺少细粒度的维度级评估。为弥补这些不足，我们提出 ArtiMuse——一个统一的模型，既能生成专家级分析，也能给出准确的美学分数。

3.3 Aesthetic Attributes

3.3 美学属性

To establish a fine-grained annotated dataset for image aesthetics assessment, the primary task involves developing a comprehensive assessment system. Through systematic consultations with artistic experts, we have formulated a novel aesthetic assessment system. This system comprises 8 specific aesthetic attributes and an overall aesthetics score, systematically defining key dimensions of image aesthetics including Composition & Design, Visual Elements & Structure, Technical Execution, Originality & Creativity, Theme & Communication, Emotion & Viewer Response, Overall Gestalt and Comprehensive Evaluation. Notably, our system is content-agnostic and universally applicable to image types from natural to AIGC.

要建立细粒度标注的图像美学评估数据集，首要任务是构建一套完备的评估体系。通过与艺术专家的系统性咨询，我们制定了一套新的美学评估体系。该体系由 8 个具体美学属性与一个整体美学分数构成，系统定义了图像美学的关键维度，包括构图与设计、视觉元素与结构、技术执行、原创性与创造力、主题与传达、情感与观者反应、整体完形以及综合评价。值得注意的是，我们的体系与内容无关，普遍适用于从自然图像到 AIGC 图像的各种类型。

4.1 Dataset Collection & Processing

4.1 数据采集与处理

Richer data sources and more meticulous manual annotations are crucial for enhancing dataset quality. In addition to the ArtiMuse-10K dataset, we carefully curate over 350,000 high-quality annotated images from existing datasets, including APDDv2 [9], PARA [17], Impressions [20] and so on.

更丰富的数据来源与更细致的人工标注对提升数据集质量至关重要。除 ArtiMuse-10K 数据集外，我们还从现有数据集（APDDv2 [9]、PARA [17]、Impressions [20] 等）中精心挑选了超过 350,000 张高质量标注图像。

Aesthetic Caption Quality. We place particular emphasis on the aesthetic caption quality. Our selection criteria prioritize datasets that include valuable aesthetic-related captions such as aesthetics scores, comprehensive textual analyses, and aesthetic attribute tags. These captions are subsequently utilized in the Annotation Generation phase to systematically enhance dataset quality.

美学描述质量。我们特别重视美学描述的质量。筛选标准优先考虑那些包含有价值美学相关描述的数据集，例如美学分数、综合性文本分析与美学属性标签。这些描述随后在标注生成阶段被系统性地用于提升数据集质量。

Aesthetic Quality Diversity. Our collection specifically incorporates images with varying aesthetic qualities, including intentionally retained lower-quality samples, to address both dataset diversity requirements and mitigate the prevalent preference bias observed in contemporary LLMs. This carefully balanced composition strategy enhances model training through controlled inclusion of suboptimal visual materials, thereby improving discriminative capabilities in aesthetic assessment.

美学质量多样性。我们的采集特意纳入美学质量各异的图像，包括有意保留的低质量样本，以兼顾数据集的多样性要求，并缓解当前 LLM 中普遍存在的偏好偏置。这种经过仔细权衡的构成策略，通过可控地引入次优视觉素材来强化模型训练，从而提升美学评估中的判别能力。

4.2 标注生成

The Annotation Generation stage aims to enrich the dataset with detailed descriptive and evaluative annotations, illustrated in Fig. 5. This process involves creating distinct annotation types based on the available information for each image. Type 2: For images with partial text captions containing specific aesthetic descriptions, we employ a prompt to instruct the MLLM to generate fine-grained evaluations. For each image, the model produces a structural analysis across 8 aesthetic attributes, utilizing both the textual and visual inputs. Type 3: For professionally selected images, we engage experts to conduct structural analysis based on pre-defined aesthetic attributes, along with providing an overall aesthetics score. 4.3 Training Strategy

4.3 训练策略

ArtiMuse is built on InternVL-3-8B [10]. We modify the dynamic resolution strategy to a fixed-resolution approach while retaining the remaining components. The training process consists of two distinct phases: text pretraining and score fine-tuning, as illustrated in Fig. 5. In both stages, we jointly train the vision encoder, MLP, and LLM components, with the LLM undergoing LoRA-based fine-tuning. The ArtiMuse uses common GPT loss [32], i.e. minimizing the cross-entropy loss between the predicted logits and target tokens.

ArtiMuse 以 InternVL-3-8B [10] 为基础构建。我们把动态分辨率策略改为固定分辨率策略，其余组件保持不变。训练过程包含两个不同阶段：文本预训练与分数微调，如图 5 所示。两个阶段中，我们联合训练视觉编码器、MLP 与 LLM 组件，其中 LLM 采用基于 LoRA 的微调。ArtiMuse 使用常规的 GPT loss [32]，即最小化预测 logits 与目标词元之间的交叉熵损失。

Text Pretrain. The text pretraining phase utilizes our complete collected image dataset, where each image is paired with its corresponding aesthetic analysis caption generated during the annotation generation stage. This phase aims to equip the model with accurate structural aesthetic analysis capabilities while largely preserving the MLLM’s pretrained knowledge. To achieve this balance, we apply LoRA fine-tuning specifically to the LLM component.

文本预训练。文本预训练阶段使用我们采集到的完整图像数据集，每张图像配以其在标注生成阶段产生的美学分析描述。该阶段旨在赋予模型准确的结构化美学分析能力，同时大体保留 MLLM 的预训练知识。为实现这一平衡，我们对 LLM 组件专门施加 LoRA 微调。

Score Finetune. After establishing foundational aesthetic understanding through pretraining, we proceed to score fine-tuning. In this phase, we convert each image’s overall aesthetics score into a specialized scoring token designed exclusively for aesthetics scoring, which then serves as the training caption. Inspired by previous works [12, 25, 33], we propose a novel score prediction strategy called Token As Score, which eliminates the need for vocabulary expansion or tokenizer retraining. Specifically, we designate 101 existing tokens as [Aes_Score_Token]s, each corresponding to integer scores ranging from 0 to 100. We select tokens that are concise and inherently carry ordinal semantic information from the vocabulary. In our implementation, we employ twin-letter combinations as tokens (e.g., Score 1 is represented as [Aes_Score_Token_1], where the actual token is ab. See supp. for more details). During data preprocessing, we first normalize aesthetics scores to the [0,100] range and then map them to their corresponding tokens. This methodology enables the construction of training data where continuous scores are discretized into token representations. The model is subsequently fine-tuned to predict these discrete tokens. During inference, we convert the predicted tokens back to their numerical values, and the final aesthetics score is derived by computing the expectation over the probability distribution of all possible score tokens. 分数微调。在通过预训练建立基础美学理解之后，我们进入分数微调阶段。在该阶段，我们把每张图像的整体美学分数转换为一个专用于美学评分的专用评分词元，并将其作为训练描述。受已有工作 [12, 25, 33] 启发，我们提出一种名为 Token As Score 的新型分数预测策略，无需扩展词表或重新训练分词器。具体地，我们指定 101 个已有词元作为 [Aes_Score_Token]，每个对应 0 到 100 的整数分数。我们从词表中挑选简洁且天然携带序数语义信息的词元。在我们的实现中，采用双字母组合作为词元（例如分数 1 表示为 [Aes_Score_Token_1]，其实际词元为 ab，详见补充材料）。在数据预处理时，我们先把美学分数归一化到 [0,100] 区间，再映射到对应词元。通过这一方法，可构造出连续分数被离散化为词元表示的训练数据，模型随后被微调以预测这些离散词元。推理时，我们把预测的词元还原为数值，最终美学分数由所有可能分数词元的概率分布求期望得到。具体地，我们记 lil_{i} 与 pip_{i} 分别为 [Aes_Score_Token_i] 的 logits 与概率，最终美学分数 SAesS_{\text{Aes}} 计算为：SAes=∑i=0100i×pi=∑i=0100i×eli∑j=0100eljS_{\text{Aes}}=\sum_{i=0}^{100}i\times p_{i}=\sum_{i=0}^{100}i\times\frac{e^{l_{i}}}{\sum_{j=0}^{100}e^{l_{j}}}。

Why Token As Score? Current approaches for scoring with MLLMs primarily fall into two categories: (1) directly prompting the LLM to output scores as text (Text As Score), or (2) predefining discrete levels corresponding to specific score intervals and computing the final score based on the model’s predicted token distribution (Level As Score). Previous works [12, 33, 25] demonstrate that directly generating scores as text leads to severe hallucination issues. Thus, we adopt the Token As Score approach and investigate the impact of token granularity on model performance. A comparison of these score prediction methods is shown in Fig. 6. Further experiments in Tab. 4 show that 100 aesthetics score tokens achieve optimal results.

为何采用 Token As Score？当前用 MLLM 打分的方法主要分两类：（1）直接提示 LLM 以文本形式输出分数（Text As Score）；（2）预定义对应特定分数区间的离散等级，再依据模型预测的词元分布计算最终分数（Level As Score）。已有工作 [12, 33, 25] 表明，直接以文本生成分数会引发严重的幻觉问题。因此我们采用 Token As Score 方案，并考察词元粒度对模型性能的影响。这些分数预测方法的对比见图 6。表 4 中的进一步实验表明，100 个美学分数词元可取得最优结果。

Maintaining Text Ability. A widely recognized challenge in IAA and IQA tasks is that MLLMs often struggle to simultaneously preserve their textual understanding and scoring capabilities [4, 6]. Since the training data in the score fine-tuning phase is significantly more monotonous than in text pretraining, full fine-tuning of the LLM can easily degrade its structural aesthetic analysis ability. To mitigate this issue while maintaining textual proficiency, we employ LoRA-based fine-tuning for the LLM, enabling the model to retain both its linguistic and scoring capabilities.

保持文本能力。IAA 与 IQA 任务中一个广为人知的挑战是：MLLM 往往难以同时保持其文本理解能力与评分能力 [4, 6]。由于分数微调阶段的训练数据比文本预训练阶段单调得多，对 LLM 做全量微调很容易削弱其结构化美学分析能力。为在保持文本能力的同时缓解这一问题，我们对 LLM 采用基于 LoRA 的微调，使模型同时保留语言能力与评分能力。

5.1 实现细节

In our experiments, we adopt InternVL-3-8B [10] as the base model initialized with its pretrained weights. During text pretraining, we implement a batch size of 128 and learning rate of 4​e−54e-5 with a cosine annealing schedule [34], training for one epoch to balance convergence with prior knowledge preservation. For the score fine-tuning, we maintain the batch size at 128 while adjusting the learning rate to 2​e−52e-5 across 2 training epochs. We maintain identical configurations across all experiments, with all training conducted on 4 * NVIDIA A100 80GB GPUs. The text pretraining phase typically takes 5 hours, while the score fine-tuning duration varies between 10 minutes to 4 hours depending on dataset size, demonstrating efficient convergence across different scales.

6 Conclusion

6 结论

We introduce ArtiMuse-10K, a large expert-annotated dataset for image aesthetics assessment, and ArtiMuse, the first model to achieve expert-level textual evaluation and precise aesthetics scoring. Additionally, we propose Token As Score, a lightweight yet effective method enabling precise continuous score prediction in MLLMs. Together these contributions will advance the field of image aesthetics assessment by providing more comprehensive dataset, more superior model, and more efficient scoring paradigm.

我们提出 ArtiMuse-10K——一个面向图像美学评估的大型专家标注数据集，以及 ArtiMuse——首个同时实现专家级文本评价与精确美学评分的模型。此外，我们提出 Token As Score，一种轻量而有效的方法，使 MLLM 能够精确预测连续分数。这些贡献将从更完备的数据集、更优越的模型与更高效的评分范式三个方面推动图像美学评估领域的发展。

Limitations. The current model is limited to understanding and analyzing, and is unable to provide professional aesthetic enhancement recommendations, which will be addressed in future work.

局限性。当前模型仅限于理解与分析，尚不能提供专业的美学改进建议，这将在未来工作中解决。

    However, in ArtiMuse, we adopt a fixed-resolution strategy instead of the dynamic approach. Aesthetic evaluation relies heavily on holistic image features, such as composition, color harmony, and spatial relationships, which can be disrupted by splitting an image into localized tiles. The dynamic strategy’s tile-based processing risks fragmenting these global characteristics, thereby degrading performance in tasks requiring an integrated understanding of visual aesthetics. By resizing all images to a uniform resolution without tiling, we preserve the structural and semantic coherence of the entire image. This adjustment ensures that the model captures aesthetic qualities through a consistent, undistorted representation of the input, aligning better with the requirements of fine-grained aesthetic analysis. Our experiments demonstrate that employing the fixed-resolution strategy yields approximately 0.3 improvements in both SRCC and PLCC metrics for aesthetic scoring tasks compared to the dynamic high-resolution strategy, while simultaneously more than doubling training and inference efficiency.

    然而在 ArtiMuse 中，我们采用固定分辨率策略而非动态策略。美学评估高度依赖构图、色彩和谐、空间关系等整体性图像特征，而把图像切分为局部小块会破坏这些特征。动态策略基于分块的处理方式有可能割裂这些全局特性，从而在需要整体理解视觉美学的任务上降低性能。通过把所有图像缩放到统一分辨率而不做切分，我们保留了整幅图像在结构与语义上的连贯性。这一调整确保模型通过一致、无失真的输入表示来捕捉美学特质，更契合细粒度美学分析的要求。我们的实验表明，在美学评分任务上，采用固定分辨率策略相比动态高分辨率策略可使 SRCC 与 PLCC 指标各提升约 0.3，同时把训练与推理效率提高一倍以上。
