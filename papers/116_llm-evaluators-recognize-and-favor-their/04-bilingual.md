**EN** 1 Introduction

**ZH** 1 引言

**EN** Self-evaluation is becoming a prominent part of the large language model (LLM) lifecycle. In methods like reward modeling (Leike et al., 2018; Stiennon et al., 2020), model-based benchmarks (Shashidhar et al., 2023; Zeng et al., 2023; Yuan et al., 2023; Fu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024), self-refinement (Saunders et al., 2022; Madaan et al., 2023; Lee et al., 2023; Shridhar et al., 2023), and constitutional AI (Bai et al., 2022), LLMs are increasingly used to provide assessment, supervision, and oversight for themselves and other LLMs. LLM evaluators are shown to be highly accurate at approximating human annotators on various tasks, and are significantly more scalable (Hackl et al., 2023).

**ZH** 自我评估正成为大语言模型（LLM）生命周期中的一个重要环节。在奖励建模（Leike 等, 2018；Stiennon 等, 2020）、基于模型的基准（Shashidhar 等, 2023；Zeng 等, 2023；Yuan 等, 2023；Fu 等, 2023；Li 等, 2024）、自我精修（Saunders 等, 2022；Madaan 等, 2023；Lee 等, 2023；Shridhar 等, 2023）与宪法 AI（Bai 等, 2022）等方法中，LLM 越来越多地被用于为自身及其他 LLM 提供评估、监督与看护。研究表明，LLM 评估者在各类任务上逼近人类标注者的准确度很高，且可扩展性显著更强（Hackl 等, 2023）。

**EN** In self-evaluation, as the name suggests, the same underlying LLM acts as both the evaluator and the evaluatee. As a result, the neutrality of the evaluator is in question, and the evaluation can suffer from biases where the LLM evaluators diverge from humans in systematic ways (Zheng et al., 2024; Bai et al., 2024). One such bias is self-preference, where an LLM rates its own outputs higher than texts written by other LLMs or humans, while human annotators judge them as equal quality. Self-preference has been observed in GPT-4-based dialogue benchmarks (Bitton et al., 2023b; Koo et al., 2023), as well as for text summarization (Liu et al., 2023).

**ZH** 顾名思义，在自我评估中，同一个底层 LLM 同时充当评估者与被评估方。因此评估者的中立性存疑，评估可能受到偏差影响，使 LLM 评估者以系统性的方式偏离人类（Zheng 等, 2024；Bai 等, 2024）。其中一类偏差是自我偏好：LLM 给自身输出的评分高于其他 LLM 或人类所写文本，而人类标注者认为二者质量相当。自我偏好已在基于 GPT-4 的对话基准（Bitton 等, 2023b；Koo 等, 2023）以及文本摘要（Liu 等, 2023）中被观察到。

**EN** Towards understanding and mitigating self-preference, we study self-recognition—an LLM’s capability of recognizing its own outputs. We ask: Is self-preference truly self-preference, in the sense that the LLM prefers a text because it was generated by itself?

**ZH** 为理解并缓解自我偏好，我们研究自我识别——即 LLM 识别自身输出的能力。我们要问的是：自我偏好名副其实吗？也就是说，LLM 偏好某段文本，确实是因为这段文本由它自己生成？

**EN** We measure their correlation while using prompting and fine-tuning to alter the LLM’s self-recognition capability. In order to provide signals for the causal link between self-recognition and self-preference, we also fine-tune the LLM on a comprehensive set of potential confounding properties.

**ZH** 我们度量二者的相关性，同时用提示与微调来改变 LLM 的自我识别能力。为给自我识别与自我偏好之间的因果联系提供信号，我们还针对一整套潜在的混杂属性对 LLM 做了微调。

**EN** Our main findings are as follows:

**ZH** 我们的主要发现如下：

**EN** 1. Frontier LLMs exhibit self-preference in self-evaluation. On two summarization tasks, LLMs (GPT-3.5 Turbo, GPT-4, and Llama 2) disproportionately favor summaries written by themselves over those by other LLMs and from humans.

**ZH** 1. 前沿 LLM 在自我评估中表现出自我偏好。在两项摘要任务上，LLM（GPT-3.5 Turbo、GPT-4 与 Llama 2）不成比例地偏爱自己撰写的摘要，胜过其他 LLM 与人类所写的摘要。

**EN** 2. LLMs have non-trivial self-recognition capability out of the box. All three LLMs we evaluate achieve over ⟦SLOT_0⟧ accuracy at distinguishing their own outputs from other sources using simple prompts without fine-tuning. GPT-4 is ⟦SLOT_1⟧ accurate distinguishing itself from two other LLMs and humans.

**ZH** 2. LLM 开箱即具备不可忽视的自我识别能力。我们所评估的三个 LLM，在不经微调、仅用简单提示的情况下，区分自身输出与其他来源的准确率都超过 ⟦SLOT_0⟧。GPT-4 区分自身与另外两个 LLM 及人类的准确率为 ⟦SLOT_1⟧。

**EN** 3. Fine-tuning leads to near-perfect self-recognition. GPT-3.5 and Llama 2 both achieve over ⟦SLOT_2⟧ accuracy at self-recognition after fine-tuning on 500 examples.

**ZH** 3. 微调带来近乎完美的自我识别。GPT-3.5 与 Llama 2 在 500 个样例上微调后，自我识别准确率都超过 ⟦SLOT_2⟧。

**EN** 4. Self-preference strength is linearly correlated with self-recognition. We further fine-tune LLMs to increase or decrease self-recognition, and discover a linear correlation between these two properties (Figure 1).

**ZH** 4. 自我偏好强度与自我识别呈线性相关。我们进一步微调 LLM 以增强或削弱自我识别，发现这两项属性之间存在线性相关（图 1）。

**EN** 2 Definition and Measurement of Self-Preference and Self-Recognition

**ZH** 2 自我偏好与自我识别的定义与度量

**EN** Self-preference is the phenomenon in which an LLM favors its own outputs over texts from other LLMs and humans.

**ZH** 自我偏好指的是 LLM 偏爱自身输出、胜过其他 LLM 与人类文本的现象。

**EN** Self-recognition is the capability of an LLM to distinguish its own outputs from texts from other LLMs or by humans.

**ZH** 自我识别指的是 LLM 把自身输出与其他 LLM 或人类文本区分开来的能力。

**EN** For both definitions, we follow the prosaic rather than the intentional interpretation. That is, we use the term “self” in an empirical sense, without claiming that the LLMs have any notion or representation of itself. The prosaic interpretation allows these two concepts to exist independent of one another: An LLM can prefer texts it generated without recognizing that those texts were in fact generated by itself.

**ZH** 对这两个定义，我们采取平实（prosaic）而非意向性（intentional）的解读。也就是说，我们在经验意义上使用「自我」一词，并不声称 LLM 拥有任何关于自身的概念或表征。平实解读使这两个概念可以彼此独立地存在：一个 LLM 可以偏爱自己生成的文本，却并未意识到那些文本事实上由它自己生成。

**EN** In our experiments, one LLM can play up to three different roles: generator, evaluator, and authorship identifier, depending on the instructions that they are given. Statements like “an LLM evaluator is used to evaluate texts generated by itself” emphasize the fact that the generator and the evaluator use the same underlying model, although it receives different prompts and can behave differently.

**ZH** 在我们的实验中，同一个 LLM 可以扮演最多三种不同角色：生成器、评估者与作者身份识别器，具体取决于所收到的指令。诸如「用一个 LLM 评估者来评估它自己生成的文本」这类说法，强调的是生成器与评估者使用同一个底层模型，尽管它接收到不同的提示并可能表现出不同的行为。

**ZH** 2.1 数据集与模型

**EN** We focus on text summarization, a task where self-evaluation has been used extensively in both model-based benchmarks and reward modeling (Stiennon et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2021). For human-written summaries, we sample 1,000 news articles from each of two datasets: XSUM (Narayan et al., 2018) and CNN/DailyMail (Nallapati et al., 2016) Each example includes news article and a human-written summary.

**ZH** 我们聚焦于文本摘要——一项在基于模型的基准与奖励建模中被广泛使用自我评估的任务（Stiennon 等, 2020；Wu 等, 2021）。对人类撰写的摘要，我们从两个数据集各采样 1,000 篇新闻文章：XSUM（Narayan 等, 2018）与 CNN/DailyMail（Nallapati 等, 2016）。每条样例包含新闻文章与一条人类撰写的摘要。

**EN** To produce and evaluate LLM-generated summaries, we use instruction-tuned LLMs: Llama-2-7b-chat (Touvron et al., 2023), GPT-3.5 (Brown et al., 2020), and GPT-4 (OpenAI, 2023). Our fine-tuning experiments include Llama 2 (our own implementation) and GPT-3.5 (via API). We sample summaries from each of the LLMs at temperature zero using simple prompts, and standardize the punctuation and initial capitalization to match the formatting of the human summaries. The following evaluations of out-of-the-box LLMs are all based on these sampled summaries.11 1 Our code for evaluating GPT-4, GPT-3.5, and Llama 2, as well as for fine-tuning Llama 2 is available at https://bit.ly/llm_self_recognition.

**ZH** 为生成并评估 LLM 撰写的摘要，我们使用经过指令微调的 LLM：Llama-2-7b-chat（Touvron 等, 2023）、GPT-3.5（Brown 等, 2020）与 GPT-4（OpenAI, 2023）。我们的微调实验包括 Llama 2（自行实现）与 GPT-3.5（通过 API）。我们在温度为 0 的条件下用简单提示从各 LLM 采样摘要，并统一标点与首字母大写，以匹配人类摘要的格式。以下对未微调 LLM 的评估全部基于这些采样得到的摘要。11

**EN** 2.2 Measurements

**ZH** 2.2 度量方法

**EN** For both properties, we measure LLMs by prompting them using one of the following two formats:

**ZH** 对两项属性，我们都通过以下两种格式之一提示 LLM 来度量：

**EN** Pairwise measurement. The LLM is presented two pieces of text, one generated by the LLM itself, and another by a second LLM or a human. We ask the LLM to choose which text has higher quality for self-preference, and which one was generated by itself for self-recognition. The identity of the alternative source is not revealed to the evaluator. We compute a prediction confidence by normalizing the output probabilities of tokens associated with the two options.

**ZH** 成对测量。向 LLM 呈现两段文本，一段由它自己生成，另一段由第二个 LLM 或人类生成。对自我偏好，我们让 LLM 选择哪段文本质量更高；对自我识别，则让其选择哪段由它自己生成。备选来源的身份不会透露给评估者。我们通过归一化与两个选项相关联的 token 输出概率来计算预测置信度。

**EN** To account for the LLMs’ ordering bias (Pezeshkpour & Hruschka, 2023), we prompt the LLMs twice for each example by swapping the options, and compute the average of the two confidence scores.

**ZH** 为消除 LLM 的顺序偏差（Pezeshkpour & Hruschka, 2023），我们对每条样例在交换选项后再次提示 LLM，并取两次置信度分数的平均。

**EN** Individual measurement. The LLM is presented a single piece of text either generated by itself, by another LLM, or by a human. For self-recognition, we prompt the LLM evaluator to answer a yes/no question of whether a given summary is of its own generation, with the confidence score computed in the same way as pairwise measurement. For self-preference, we prompt the LLM evaluator to rate a given summary with a Likert-scale rating of one to five for a given summary, and compute the final rating as the average of the five possible scores weighted by the output probability of each number token.

**ZH** 单独测量。向 LLM 呈现单段文本，其来源可能是它自己、另一个 LLM 或人类。对自我识别，我们提示 LLM 评估者回答一个是/否问题：给定摘要是否由它自己生成，置信度的计算方式与成对测量相同。对自我偏好，我们提示 LLM 评估者用 1 到 5 的李克特量表为给定摘要打分，并把五个可能分值按各自数字 token 的输出概率加权平均，得到最终评分。

**EN** Figure 2: Self-recognition before fine-tuning, evaluated using pairwise (left) and individual (right) measurements; scores are aggregated over two datasets.

**ZH** 图 2：微调前的自我识别，用成对（左）与单独（右）测量评估；分数在两个数据集上聚合。

**EN** To make scores comparable across individual and pairwise measurements, we further normalize the individually measured confidence and Likert-scale scores. Let’s say in a pairwise measurement, GPT-4 is evaluated for self-recognition on a summary produced by itself and another one by Llama 2. In individual measurement, we gather these two scores separately, and divide the GPT-4’s confidence on its own summary by the sum of two scores; we consider that to be GPT-4’s confidence on this pair of examples. We similarly re-normalize the Likert scale scores for self-preference.

**ZH** 为使分数在单独与成对测量之间可比，我们进一步对单独测量得到的置信度与李克特量表分数做归一化。举例来说，在成对测量中，GPT-4 就一条由它自己生成的摘要与一条由 Llama 2 生成的摘要接受自我识别评估。在单独测量中，我们分别采集这两个分数，并用 GPT-4 对自身摘要的置信度除以两个分数之和；我们把该值视为 GPT-4 在这一对样例上的置信度。对自我偏好，我们以类似方式重新归一化李克特量表分数。

**EN** We refer to the results of these measurements as self-recognition and self-preference scores (noting whether they were computed in the pairwise or individual setting); taking the average of these scores over a whole dataset provides the overall self-recognition and self-preference scores of an evaluator model.

**ZH** 我们把这些度量的结果称为自我识别分数与自我偏好分数（并注明它们是在成对还是单独设定下计算的）；把整个数据集上这些分数取平均，即得到某个评估模型总体的自我识别与自我偏好分数。

**EN** 3 Measuring Correlation between Self-Preference and Self-Recognition

**ZH** 3 度量自我偏好与自我识别的相关性

**EN** Having validated the existence of self-preference and self-recognition, we now turn to study the correlation between the two. The main research question is to understand whether they have a causal relationship; in particular, our hypothesis is that self-recognition causes self-preference—that LLMs prefer their own outputs because they recognize them. Our investigation of the hypothesis is motivated by its safety implications, which we discuss in detail in Section 5.

**ZH** 在确认自我偏好与自我识别确实存在之后，我们转而研究二者之间的相关性。核心研究问题是理解它们是否具有因果关系；具体地，我们的假设是自我识别导致自我偏好——即 LLM 偏爱自身输出是因为它们识别出了这些输出。我们对这一假设的考察源于其安全含义，详见第 5 节。

**EN** We finetune LLMs to alter their self-recognition capability, and measure how the strength of self-preference changes accordingly. The correlation doesn’t by itself prove the causal hypothesis—validating the causal hypothesis would require mechanistic tools that do not exist for LLMs yet (more discussion in Section 5). But we take two approaches to provide more evidence for the hypothesis:

**ZH** 我们微调 LLM 以改变其自我识别能力，并度量自我偏好强度随之发生的变化。相关性本身不足以证明因果假设——验证因果假设需要目前 LLM 尚不具备的机制性工具（更多讨论见第 5 节）。但我们采取两条途径为该假设提供更多证据：

**EN** 1. We invalidate the inverse causal relationship that self-preference causes self-recognition (Section 3.3).

**ZH** 1. 我们否证了「自我偏好导致自我识别」这一反向因果关系（第 3.3 节）。

**EN** 2. We control for confounders by fine-tuning LLMs on tasks unrelated to self-recognition (Section 3.4).

**ZH** 2. 我们通过在与自我识别无关的任务上微调 LLM 来控制混杂因素（第 3.4 节）。

**EN** 3.1 Fine-tuning for Self-Recognition

**ZH** 3.1 面向自我识别的微调

**EN** We use supervised fine-tuning on pairwise examples to alter the LLMs’ self-recognition capability. The training examples are generated in the same way as the evaluation examples we use in the pairwise measurement. Each example consists of two summaries of a given article, one by the LLM evaluator and the other by another LLM or human source. The evaluators are trained to predict the final token, representing the the correct choice among two options, using supervised learning with cross-entropy loss. To train an LLM evaluator, we use 500 training articles and collect examples from all alternative sources (two other LLMs and human). The remaining 500 articles and the associated summaries are used as the evaluation dataset. We train on the two datasets separately but evaluate both in- and out-of-domain. GPT-3.5 was fine-tuned via API, and Llama 2 using our own implementation. The Llama models are quantized to 8 bits and fine-tuned for one epoch using Adam optimization and a learning rate of ⟦SLOT_4⟧.

**ZH** 我们在成对样例上做监督微调，以改变 LLM 的自我识别能力。训练样例的生成方式与成对测量中所用评测样例相同。每条样例包含给定文章的两条摘要，一条来自 LLM 评估者，另一条来自另一个 LLM 或人类来源。评估者被训练去预测最后一个 token——它代表两个选项中的正确选择——使用带交叉熵损失的监督学习。训练一个 LLM 评估者时，我们使用 500 篇训练文章，并从所有备选来源（另两个 LLM 与人类）收集样例。剩余 500 篇文章及其关联摘要用作评测数据集。我们在两个数据集上分别训练，但同时做域内与域外评测。GPT-3.5 通过 API 微调，Llama 2 使用我们自己的实现。Llama 模型被量化为 8 比特，并用 Adam 优化器以 ⟦SLOT_4⟧ 的学习率微调一个轮次。

**EN** 3.3 Invalidating the Inverse Causal Relationship

**ZH** 3.3 否证反向因果关系

**EN** It’s important to ensure that the causal direction is not the reverse—that the LLM does not recognize its own outputs because the quality is objectively higher, in which case the correlation does not reflect favoritism and should not raise any safety concerns. To show that the inverse causal relationship is not the case, we show that LLM evaluators do not systematically favor the summaries generated by fine-tuned models to those generated by the original model.

**ZH** 确保因果方向不是反向的十分重要——即 LLM 并非因为自身输出客观上质量更高才识别出它们；若如此，这种相关性就不反映偏袒，也就不应引发任何安全担忧。为说明反向因果关系不成立，我们证明 LLM 评估者并不会系统性地偏爱微调模型生成的摘要胜过原始模型生成的摘要。

**ZH** 部分微调运行导致生成质量退化（例如只包含字符「1」与「2」的摘要）。其余运行显示，对微调模型生成文本的平均偏好为 0.46，反映出对新生成文本的轻微负面偏好。只有 22% 的评估者—域配对显示出对微调模型生成文本大于 ⟦SLOT_9⟧ 的偏好。

**EN** 4 Related Work

**ZH** 4 相关工作

**EN** 4.1 Self-Preference and Biases of Self-Evaluation

**ZH** 4.1 自我偏好与自我评估的偏差

**EN** The general tendency of LLMs to prefer their own generations was first recognized in the context of LLM-based benchmarks (Bitton et al., 2023a; Zheng et al., 2024; Bai et al., 2024). Liu et al. (2023), similarly to us, study self-preference bias between BERT, T5, and GPT-3.5 on text-summarization. As we discussed The larger capability gap between these models compared to ours make it difficult to control for summarization quality.

**ZH** LLM 偏爱自身生成内容的总体倾向，最初是在基于 LLM 的基准语境中被认识到的（Bitton 等, 2023a；Zheng 等, 2024；Bai 等, 2024）。Liu 等（2023）与我们类似，研究了 BERT、T5 与 GPT-3.5 在文本摘要上的自我偏好偏差。正如我们所讨论的，这些模型之间的能力差距比我们的更大，因而难以控制摘要质量。

**EN** Koo et al. (2023) include self-preference in a suite of tests for LLM cognitive biases in a question-answering setting using pairwise measurements. They find GPT-4 to demonstrate lower self-preference than GPT-3.5 out-of-the-box, contrary to our findings, which suggests that evaluation on more datasets is necessary to draw a generalizable conclusion. Neither of these previous works attempted to provide an explanation for self-preference, nor did they study methods to alter self-preference strength.

**ZH** Koo 等（2023）在问答设定下用成对测量把自我偏好纳入一套 LLM 认知偏差测试中。他们发现 GPT-4 在未微调时表现出的自我偏好低于 GPT-3.5，与我们的发现相反，这说明需要在更多数据集上评测才能得出可推广的结论。此前这些工作都未尝试解释自我偏好，也未研究改变自我偏好强度的方法。

**EN** 4.2 Self-recognition and situational awareness

**ZH** 4.2 自我识别与情境意识

**EN** Hoelscher-Obermaier et al. (2023) evaluate GPT-3.5, GPT-4, and Claude-2 on their out-of-the-box self-recognition capabilities. The authors use pairwise measurement on pairs of two ten-sentence fables based on BIG-bench (Srivastava et al., 2023). On this task, contrary to our findings, GPT-3.5 is more accurate than GPT-4, which is less than 50% accurate, again showing the need to experiment on more datasets for generalizable conclusions.

**ZH** Hoelscher-Obermaier 等（2023）评估了 GPT-3.5、GPT-4 与 Claude-2 未微调时的自我识别能力。作者基于 BIG-bench（Srivastava 等, 2023）对两则十句寓言的配对做成对测量。在该任务上，与我们的发现相反，GPT-3.5 比 GPT-4 更准确，后者的准确率不足 50%，这同样说明需要在更多数据集上实验以得出可推广的结论。

**EN** Self-recognition can be seen as a form of situational awareness or self-awareness (Laine et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024; Berglund et al., 2023; Perez & Long, 2023). Among existing work in this direction, self-recognition is most similar to calibration (Kadavath et al., 2022; Yin et al., 2023; Amayuelas et al., 2023). In this line of work, an LLM is considered to possess self-knowledge if its verbalized uncertainty is well-calibrated. Whereas they measure the correlation between verbalized uncertainty and accuracy, we measure the correlation between the uncertainty on two different tasks.

**ZH** 自我识别可被视为情境意识或自我意识的一种形式（Laine 等, 2023；Wang 等, 2024；Berglund 等, 2023；Perez & Long, 2023）。在这一方向的已有工作中，自我识别与校准（calibration）最为相近（Kadavath 等, 2022；Yin 等, 2023；Amayuelas 等, 2023）。在这条研究线索中，若一个 LLM 言语化的不确定性经过良好校准，就被认为具备自我知识。他们度量的是言语化不确定性与准确性之间的相关性，而我们度量的是两项不同任务上不确定性之间的相关性。

**EN** 4.3 LLM detection

**ZH** 4.3 LLM 检测

**EN** Detection of LLM-generated text is important to both AI safety and combating misinformation (Jawahar et al., 2020; Crothers et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Yang et al., 2023; Kumarage et al., 2024). Despite having similar goals, self-recognition focuses on the introspective capability of language models, rather than how well a third party can discern various sources of text. The self-recognition task can be seen as a highly restricted version of detection where the method is limited to prompting the LLM. In particular, the detector LLM is not given explicit access to information such as perplexity, which in many detection methods is a crucial component (Mitchell et al., 2023; Hans et al., 2024).

**ZH** LLM 生成文本的检测对 AI 安全与打击虚假信息都很重要（Jawahar 等, 2020；Crothers 等, 2023；Wu 等, 2023；Yang 等, 2023；Kumarage 等, 2024）。尽管目标相近，自我识别关注的是语言模型的内省能力，而非第三方辨别各类文本来源的能力。自我识别任务可被视为检测任务的一个高度受限版本——其方法被限定为对 LLM 进行提示。具体地，检测方 LLM 并未被显式赋予困惑度等信息，而困惑度在许多检测方法中是关键组成部分（Mitchell 等, 2023；Hans 等, 2024）。

**EN** 5 Limitations, Discussion, and Conclusion

**ZH** 5 局限性、讨论与结论

**EN** 5.1 Safety Concerns Related to Self-Recognizing LLMs

**ZH** 5.1 与具备自我识别能力的 LLM 相关的安全担忧

**EN** Self-recognition is a general capability that can potentially affect many multi-LLM interactions. In this paper, we focus on self-preference as the downstream property and provide initial evidence towards their causal relationship, but we see evidence that both the capability and causal hypothesis can generalize to more downstream properties. In particular, by evaluating LLMs on datasets with distinct construction processes, we observe that self-recognition fine-tuning generalizes across the two datasets and that our hypothesis holds out-of-distribution. Motivated by these results, we discuss safety risks caused by self-recognition as a general capability as well as its causal effect on various biases.

**ZH** 自我识别是一项通用能力，可能影响许多多 LLM 交互场景。本文把自我偏好作为下游属性加以聚焦，并为二者的因果关系提供了初步证据；我们也看到证据表明，这项能力与因果假设都可以推广到更多下游属性。具体地，通过在构建流程各异的数据集上评测 LLM，我们观察到自我识别微调可跨两个数据集泛化，且我们的假设在分布外依然成立。受这些结果启发，我们讨论自我识别作为一项通用能力所引发的安全风险，以及它对各类偏差的因果作用。

**EN** Biased self-evaluation directly affects model-based benchmarks (Shashidhar et al., 2023; Zeng et al., 2023; Yuan et al., 2023; Fu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024): a model’s rating can be inflated simply because it’s most similar to the model used for evaluation. The bias is also a risk for methods designed for safety and alignment, such as reward modeling (Leike et al., 2018; Stiennon et al., 2020) and constitutional AI (Bai et al., 2022), for similar reasons: the reward model gives higher scores to models similar to itself, leading to weaker oversight and supervision. Such bias can be further amplified if the model is updated with feedback or training signal generated by itself (Pan et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024).

**ZH** 有偏的自我评估直接影响基于模型的基准（Shashidhar 等, 2023；Zeng 等, 2023；Yuan 等, 2023；Fu 等, 2023；Li 等, 2024）：一个模型的评分可能仅仅因为它与用于评估的模型最为相似就被抬高。出于类似的原因，这一偏差对为安全与对齐而设计的方法——如奖励建模（Leike 等, 2018；Stiennon 等, 2020）与宪法 AI（Bai 等, 2022）——同样构成风险：奖励模型给与自己相似的模型打出更高分数，导致监督与看护被削弱。若模型再用自身生成的反馈或训练信号来更新，这类偏差还会被进一步放大（Pan 等, 2024；Xu 等, 2024）。

**EN** Our work provides a basis for countermeasures against self-preference. If future evaluation confirms self-preference to be as pervasive as other biases such as ordering bias, countermeasures such as authorship obfuscation should be incorporated into standard prompting practice.

**ZH** 我们的工作为对抗自我偏好提供了依据。若未来的评估确认自我偏好与顺序偏差等其他偏差同样普遍，那么作者身份混淆等对策就应被纳入标准的提示实践。

**EN** White-box adversarial attacks for free and unbounded reward hacking. In an adversarial setting (see Raina et al. (2024) for example), an LLM defender is no longer protected by black-box access if the adversary LLM recognizes their similarities. In the worst case scenario where the adversary use the same LLM as the defender, the adversary can gain unbounded access to the defender. A similar concern applies to the non-adversarial setting, where similar LLMs are use as both optimizer and reward model, as well: the strength of potential reward hacking is unbounded even if the two LLMs only communicate textually. For example, the optimizer can ignore the feedback provided by the reward model, and instead directly optimize for the shared, unaligned representation of the human-specified objectives.

**ZH** 面向自由且无界奖励劫持的白盒对抗攻击。在对抗设定下（例如参见 Raina 等（2024）），一旦对抗方 LLM 识别出彼此的相似性，LLM 防御方就不再受到黑盒访问的保护。在最坏情形下，若对抗方与防御方使用同一个 LLM，对抗方可获得对防御方无限制的访问权限。类似的担忧也适用于非对抗设定——例如当相似的 LLM 同时被用作优化器与奖励模型时：即便两个 LLM 仅以文本方式通信，潜在奖励劫持的强度也是无界的。例如，优化器可以忽略奖励模型提供的反馈，而直接针对人类指定目标的共享、未对齐表征进行优化。

**EN** 5.2 Limitations and Future Work

**ZH** 5.2 局限性与未来工作

**EN** Validation of the causal hypothesis. Despite the use of a diverse set of control tasks, our experiments can only provide evidence towards the causal hypothesis without fully validating it. The argument can be further strengthened by experimenting (and rejecting) more hypothesis for potential confounders between the two properties, but only up to a point—for hypotheses based on properties that we would consider to be valid explanations for self-recognition, failure to reject those hypotheses not invalidate our claim. For example, if an LLM uses verbosity as a cue to recognize itself, then the fact that fine-tuning on verbosity prediction leads to high self-recognition and self-preference doesn’t mean it’s a confounder between the two.

**ZH** 因果假设的验证。尽管使用了多样的对照任务，我们的实验只能为因果假设提供证据，而无法完全验证它。通过（并否证）更多关于两项属性之间潜在混杂因素的假设，这一论证可以被进一步加强，但也仅能加强到一定程度——对那些基于我们认为是自我识别有效解释的属性的假设，未能否证它们并不否定我们的主张。例如，若一个 LLM 把冗长程度用作识别自身的线索，那么基于冗长度预测做微调会带来高自我识别与高自我偏好，并不意味着冗长度是二者之间的混杂因素。

**EN** Controlling for groundtruth generation quality. Self-preference can be justifiable if the LLM’s generation is indeed higher quality than the alternative. From a safety perspective, what we are interested in is disproportionate self-preference, e.g., an LLM preferring its own generation even when it’s equal or worse quality than the alternative. This would require controlling for generation quality when measuring self-preference using groundtruth annotation. Although our hypothesis does not require self-preference to be disproportionate, the addition of control would improve its relevance to safety. Our existing results provide indirect evidence for disproportionate self-preference: the sum of self-preference scores of a pair of LLMs exceeds one, which means that for at least a portion of the dataset they both prefer themselves.

**ZH** 控制真实生成质量。若 LLM 的生成确实比备选文本质量更高，自我偏好就可能是合理的。从安全视角看，我们关心的是过度的自我偏好，例如 LLM 即便在自身生成质量与备选相当或更差时仍偏爱它。这需要在度量自我偏好时用真值标注来控制生成质量。尽管我们的假设并不要求自我偏好是过度的，加入这一控制会提升其与安全的关联度。我们已有的结果为过度的自我偏好提供了间接证据：一对 LLM 的自我偏好分数之和超过一，意味着至少在数据集的一部分上它们都偏爱自己。

**EN** Example-level causal hypothesis. Our central hypothesis can be interpreted on either the example or capability level. We focus on the capability level: high self-recognition capability causes LLMs to show stronger self-preference. The example level counterpart would be: an LLM shows preference towards a piece of text because it recognizes the text as its own generation, an hypothesis of interest to interpretability. Although we observe on the correlation of the two properties on the confidence of individual predictions, our control experiments cannot further the causal argument on the example level. One approach to gather evidence for the example-level causal hypothesis is to perturb or paraphrase LLM-generated text to inhibit self-recognition and measure self-preference. Note that this task—paraphrasing text to inhibit self-recognition—has the same goal as methods designed to bypass LLM detection, so we can repurpose these methods for our experiment.

**ZH** 样例层面的因果假设。我们的核心假设既可以在样例层面、也可以在能力层面解读。我们聚焦能力层面：高自我识别能力导致 LLM 表现出更强的自我偏好。其样例层面的对应表述是：LLM 偏好某段文本，是因为它把这段文本识别为自己生成的——这是一个对可解释性颇有价值的假设。尽管我们在单个预测的置信度上观察到了两项属性的相关性，我们的对照实验无法进一步推进样例层面的因果论证。为样例层面的因果假设收集证据的一条途径是扰动或改写 LLM 生成的文本以抑制自我识别，并度量自我偏好。值得注意的是，这一任务——改写文本以抑制自我识别——与为绕过 LLM 检测而设计的方法目标相同，因此我们可以把这些方法改造用于我们的实验。

**EN** Limited number of experiment conditions. We focus on text summarization as a realistic problem with existing high quality data that have seen successful application of self-evaluation. Our cross-dataset evaluation provides initial evidence that self-recognition is a general capability that can be amplified easily by fine-tuning on a small number of examples from one dataset. Our future work will validate the hypothesis on more text summarization datasets, more tasks, as well as more frontier LLMs. We will also experiment with fine-tuning for self-recognition on the general domain rather than on a specific task.

**ZH** 实验条件数量有限。我们聚焦文本摘要，因为它是已有高质量数据、且自我评估已获成功应用的现实问题。我们的跨数据集评测提供了初步证据，表明自我识别是一项通用能力，且只需在一个数据集的少量样例上微调即可轻易放大。未来的工作将在更多文本摘要数据集、更多任务以及更多前沿 LLM 上验证该假设，也将尝试在通用领域而非特定任务上做自我识别微调。

**EN** Variance reduction. Our preliminary experiments indicate that the strength of both properties are insensitive to prompts, so all conditions use the same straightforward prompt design. To reduce variance, we will expand our experiments with more prompt designs in future work, including instructions to condition LLMs for better calibration (and reduce rejection responses). Along the lines of fine-tuning on the general domain, we will also mix self-recognition with standard instruction following datasets to improve coverage on the spectrum of self-recognition signal strength.

**ZH** 降低方差。我们的初步实验表明，两项属性的强度对提示不敏感，因此所有条件都使用同样直白的提示设计。为降低方差，我们未来将用更多提示设计扩展实验，包括为改善校准（并减少拒答）而对 LLM 施加条件约束的指令。沿着在通用领域做微调的思路，我们还将把自我识别与标准指令遵循数据集混合，以提升在自我识别信号强度谱系上的覆盖。

**EN** 5.3 Conclusions

**ZH** 5.3 结论

**EN** We provide initial evidence towards the hypothesis that LLMs prefer their own generations because they recognize themselves. In addition to evaluating LLMs out-of-the-box, we show that fine-tuning on a small number of examples elicit strong, generalizable self-recognitiono capability on summarization datasets. By varying fine-tuning task, we observe a linear correlation between self-recognition and self-preference, and validate that the correlation cannot be explained away by potential confounders. Our results establish self-recognition as a crucial factor in unbiased self-evaluation as well as an important safety-related property. The experiment design also provides a blueprint to explore the effects of self-recognition on other downstream properties.

**ZH** 我们为「LLM 偏爱自身生成是因为它们识别出了自己」这一假设提供了初步证据。除评估未微调的 LLM 之外，我们还表明，在少量样例上做微调即可在摘要数据集上引发强大且可泛化的自我识别能力。通过改变微调任务，我们观察到自我识别与自我偏好之间存在线性相关，并验证了这一相关性无法被潜在混杂因素解释掉。我们的结果确立了自我识别是无偏自我评估中的关键因素，也是一项重要的安全相关属性。该实验设计也为探索自我识别对其他下游属性的影响提供了一份蓝图。
