中文摘要

现代视觉语言模型（VLM）展现出卓越的视觉与语言能力，在图像识别、目标定位等各类任务上取得了令人印象深刻的表现。然而，它们在细粒度任务上的有效性仍是一个悬而未决的问题。在日常场景中，人们在看到杂志、字体排印教程、研究论文或品牌内容等设计材料时，可能希望识别其中文字所使用的美观字体。鉴于 VLM 具备多模态能力且可自由获取，许多人把它们视为字体识别的潜在工具。这就引出一个根本问题：VLM 是否真的具备识别字体的能力？为此，我们提出字体识别基准（FRB）——一个结构紧凑、组织良好的数据集，包含 15 种常用字体。FRB 含两个版本：（i）简单版本，以不同字体渲染 10 个句子；（ii）困难版本，每个文本样本由这 15 种字体的名称本身构成，从而引入 Stroop 效应对模型感知构成挑战。通过在字体识别任务上对多种 VLM 开展广泛评估，我们得到以下关键发现：（i）当前 VLM 的字体识别能力有限，许多最先进模型未能取得令人满意的表现，且容易受到文本信息所引入的 Stroop 效应影响；（ii）少样本学习与思维链（CoT）提示在提升各 VLM 字体识别准确率上收效甚微；（iii）注意力分析揭示了 VLM 在捕捉语义特征上的固有局限。

Abstract (原文)

Modern Vision-Language Models (VLMs) exhibit remarkable visual and linguistic capabilities, achieving impressive performance in various tasks such as image recognition and object localization. However, their effectiveness in fine-grained tasks remains an open question. In everyday scenarios, individuals encountering design materials, such as magazines, typography tutorials, research papers, or branding content, may wish to identify aesthetically pleasing fonts used in the text. Given their multimodal capabilities and free accessibility, many VLMs are often considered potential tools for font recognition. This raises a fundamental question: Do VLMs truly possess the capability to recognize fonts? To investigate this, we introduce the Font Recognition Benchmark (FRB), a compact and well-structured dataset comprising 15 commonly used fonts. FRB includes two versions: (i) an easy version, where 10 sentences are rendered in different fonts, and (ii) a hard version, where each text sample consists of the names of the 15 fonts themselves, introducing a stroop effect that challenges model perception. Through extensive evaluation of various VLMs on font recognition tasks, we arrive at the following key findings: (i) Current VLMs exhibit limited font recognition capabilities, with many state-of-the-art models failing to achieve satisfactory performance and being easily affected by the stroop effect introduced by textual information. (ii) Few-shot learning and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting provide minimal benefits in improving font recognition accuracy across different VLMs. (iii) Attention analysis sheds light on the inherent limitations of VLMs in capturing semantic features.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Font recognition is a fundamental visual task with applications across various domains. It is particularly relevant to graphic design, publishing, advertising, and digital content creation, where typography plays a crucial role in visual communication (Zramdini & Ingold, 1998; Chen et al., 2014b; Zhu et al., 2001). For example, researchers may seek to identify aesthetically appealing fonts in academic papers or images, while designers sometimes encounter unique typefaces they wish to integrate into their projects. Statistics indicate that over 60% of websites utilize custom or non-system fonts, highlighting the significance of font identification in web design. Additionally, Google Fonts are used on more than 50 million websites, demonstrating the extensive adoption of diverse typefaces (Solomons, 2023). This widespread demand underscores the need for efficient and accurate font recognition tools.

2 Related Works

2 相关工作

2.1 Vision-Language Models

2.1 视觉语言模型

With the foundation laid by Transformer and CLIP (Vaswani et al., 2023; Radford et al., 2021), Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have emerged as powerful multimodal systems that seamlessly integrate both visual and linguistic modalities. These models bridges the gap between image and text understanding, enabling them to process and interpret complex multimodal data efficiently (OpenAI et al., 2024a; Bai et al., 2025; Team et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2023; Caffagni et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024a; Yin et al., 2024; Team et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024).

在 Transformer 与 CLIP（Vaswani 等，2023；Radford 等，2021）奠定的基础之上，视觉语言模型（VLM）已成为强大的多模态系统，能够无缝融合视觉与语言两种模态。这些模型弥合了图像理解与文本理解之间的鸿沟，使其能高效处理与解释复杂的多模态数据（OpenAI 等，2024a；Bai 等，2025；Team 等，2024；Li 等，2024；Liu 等，2023；Caffagni 等，2024；Zhang 等，2024a；Yin 等，2024；Team 等，2025；Chen 等，2024）。

Recent advancements in large-scale image-text datasets and contrastive learning have significantly improved VLM performance. These models now exhibit strong general representation capabilities, effectively aligning visual and textual information and achieving state-of-the-art results across multiple benchmarks, establishing themselves as key technologies in multimodal AI research (Zhang et al., 2024c; Bordes et al., 2024; Schuhmann et al., 2022; Li et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2020).

大规模图文数据集与对比学习的最新进展显著提升了 VLM 的性能。这些模型现已展现出较强的通用表征能力，能有效对齐视觉与文本信息，并在多个基准上取得最先进的结果，确立了其在多模态 AI 研究中的关键技术地位（Zhang 等，2024c；Bordes 等，2024；Schuhmann 等，2022；Li 等，2023；Chen 等，2025；Chen 等，2020）。

The strong multimodal capabilities of VLMs make them valuable tools in real-world applications. Models such as BLIP (Li et al., 2022a), Flamingo (Alayrac et al., 2022) and later GPT-4o (OpenAI et al., 2024b) exhibit impressive performance in visual question answering, image captioning, and multimodal dialogue, enabling applications in interactive AI agents and assistive technologies for visually impaired individuals (Agrawal et al., 2016; Zhang et al., 2024b; Guo et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2024; Zhai et al., 2024). These advancements underscore the versatility of VLMs in handling complex multimodal tasks.

VLM 强大的多模态能力使其成为真实应用中的得力工具。BLIP（Li 等，2022a）、Flamingo（Alayrac 等，2022）以及后来的 GPT-4o（OpenAI 等，2024b）等模型在视觉问答、图像描述与多模态对话上表现出色，支撑了交互式 AI 智能体与面向视障人士的辅助技术中的应用（Agrawal 等，2016；Zhang 等，2024b；Guo 等，2024；Gao 等，2024；Zhai 等，2024）。这些进展凸显了 VLM 处理复杂多模态任务的通用性。

2.2 Font Recognition

2.2 字体识别

Font recognition, a critical task for people such as researchers and designers, inherently combines visual and textual components. However, it has received limited attention within the Vision-Language Models (VLMs) research community. Traditionally, it has been approached as a text-image classification problem, predominantly relying on CNN-based methods (Wang et al., 2015b; Tensmeyer et al., 2017; Qi et al., 2025; Wang & Zong, 2023; Mohammadian et al., 2022; Cui & Inoue, 2021). While these approaches achieve relatively high accuracy with their proposed CNN-based architectures on specific datasets, they typically require extensive fine-tuning on large-scale datasets. This requirement makes them time-consuming and also limits their generalization capabilities.

字体识别对研究者与设计师等人群而言是一项关键任务，天然融合了视觉成分与文本成分。然而，它在视觉语言模型（VLM）研究社群中获得的关注有限。传统上，它被当作文本图像分类问题处理，主要依赖基于 CNN 的方法（Wang 等，2015b；Tensmeyer 等，2017；Qi 等，2025；Wang 和 Zong，2023；Mohammadian 等，2022；Cui 和 Inoue，2021）。这些方法虽凭其提出的 CNN 架构在特定数据集上取得较高准确率，但通常需要在大规模数据集上做大量微调，既耗时也限制了泛化能力。

VLMs have recently emerged as versatile tools in visual tasks, offering strong adaptability through large-scale pretraining. They present a promising, convenient solution for font recognition. However, their capabilities in this task remain underexplored, motivating our study to provide a systematic evaluation and identify potential limitations.

VLM 近来已成为视觉任务中的通用工具，凭借大规模预训练具备很强的适应性，为字体识别提供了一种前景良好且便捷的方案。然而，其在该任务上的能力仍未得到充分探索，这也促使我们开展系统评估并识别潜在局限。

3 Font Recognition Benchmark

3 字体识别基准

We propose the Font Recognition Benchmark (FRB) to assess VLMs’ ability to recognize typographic styles from images. Unlike different versions or subsets of the VFR dataset, which contains more than 2,400 font classes and further categorizes the same font style into regular, bold, and other sub-types, the VFR dataset is more suitable for CNN-based model training rather than for basic capability exploration of VLMs in line with our objectives (Wang et al., 2015a; Chen et al., 2014a). In contrast, FRB follows three principles: practicality, using commonly encountered fonts; diversity, covering multiple font categories; and graduated challenge, testing different recognition scenarios. It includes two versions: an easy version, where sentences appear in various fonts, and a hard version, where font names are displayed in mismatched typefaces, creating a stroop effect.

我们提出字体识别基准（FRB），用以评估 VLM 从图像中识别字体排印风格的能力。VFR 数据集包含超过 2,400 种字体类别，并把同一字体样式进一步细分为常规体、粗体等子类型；与 VFR 数据集的不同版本或子集不同，VFR 更适合训练基于 CNN 的模型，而不适合按我们的目标去探索 VLM 的基础能力（Wang 等，2015a；Chen 等，2014a）。相比之下，FRB 遵循三条原则：实用性——采用日常常见的字体；多样性——覆盖多个字体类别；梯度式挑战——测试不同的识别场景。它包含两个版本：简单版本中句子以各种字体呈现；困难版本中字体名称以不匹配的字型显示，从而产生 Stroop 效应。

3.2 Benchmark Image Construction

3.2 基准图像构建

We generate 10 images per font, each containing a distinct sentence designed to incorporate as many of the 26 English letters as possible. This results in a total of 15×10=15015\times 10=150 images, forming the easy version of the benchmark. These images are created using a consistent Python script that ensures uniform line breaks, font size, and background, maintaining a balanced aspect ratio aligned with the model’s input requirements. This easy version allows VLMs to focus exclusively on font recognition without interference from textual content.

我们为每种字体生成 10 张图像，每张包含一个不同的句子，句子在设计上尽可能多地涵盖 26 个英文字母。这共得到 15×10=15015\times 10=150 张图像，构成基准的简单版本。这些图像由统一的 Python 脚本生成，确保换行、字号与背景一致，并保持与模型输入要求相匹配的均衡宽高比。该简单版本使 VLM 能专注于字体识别而不受文本内容干扰。

For the hard version of the benchmark, we generate 15 additional images per font, each displaying its respective name. The image generation process employs a unified Python script utilizing the ImageDraw and ImageFont packages, ensuring consistency in font size and background dimensions across all images. This approach eliminates discrepancies associated with alternative methods such as screenshots. The inclusion of font names introduces the stroop effect, increasing the benchmark’s difficulty. In total, this results in 15×15=22515\times 15=225 images, constituting the complete hard version of the benchmark.

对于基准的困难版本，我们为每种字体额外生成 15 张图像，每张显示其自身的名称。图像生成过程使用统一的 Python 脚本，调用 ImageDraw 与 ImageFont 包，确保所有图像在字号与背景尺寸上一致；这种做法消除了截图等其他方法带来的差异。字体名称的引入带来了 Stroop 效应，提高了基准的难度。这共得到 15×15=22515\times 15=225 张图像，构成基准完整的困难版本。

Finally, to support few-shot learning experiments, we generate 52 additional images, each containing one uppercase or lowercase letter from the 26 English alphabets. These images serve as reference samples, allowing VLMs to leverage few-shot learning for feature comparison and adaptation in subsequent experiments.

最后，为支持少样本学习实验，我们额外生成 52 张图像，每张包含 26 个英文字母中的一个大写或小写形式。这些图像用作参考样本，使 VLM 能在后续实验中借助少样本学习进行特征比较与适配。

3.3 Challenges

3.3 挑战

3.3.1 Font Recognition

3.3.1 字体识别

When the text content in an image consists of sentences, this task simulates real-world scenarios where individuals capture screenshots of textual content to identify the font used. The primary objective is to assess the VLMs’ ability to recognize fonts. The presence of a greater number of letters enhances the models’ capacity to capture distinctive font characteristics, while the relatively small text size further challenges its ability to perform fine-grained recognition.

当图像中的文本内容由句子构成时，该任务模拟了这样一类真实场景：人们截取文本内容的截图以识别所用字体。其主要目标是评估 VLM 识别字体的能力。字母数量更多有助于模型捕捉字体的独特特征，而相对较小的文本尺寸则进一步考验其细粒度识别能力。

3.3.2 Stroop Effect

3.3.2 Stroop 效应

When image text contains font names, a conflict between the textual meaning and the visual font triggers the stroop effect (MacLeod, 1991; Verhaeghen & De Meersman, 1998; Besner et al., 1997). For instance, the word “Times New Roman” rendered in Lobster font may mislead a model to predict the text’s meaning rather than the actual font. Even with clear visual differences, models often misclassify due to cognitive interference. This setup challenges VLMs to disentangle textual semantics from typographic appearance.

当图像文本包含字体名称时，文本含义与视觉字体之间的冲突会触发 Stroop 效应（MacLeod，1991；Verhaeghen 和 De Meersman，1998；Besner 等，1997）。例如，以 Lobster 字体渲染的「Times New Roman」一词，可能误导模型去预测文本的含义而非真实字体。即便视觉差异明显，模型也常因认知干扰而误判。该设置考验 VLM 能否把文本语义与排印外观分离开来。

5.3 Few-shot Learning Provides Limited Improvements

5.3 少样本学习带来的改善有限

In real-world scenarios, when given reference images of individual letters written in a specific font style, humans can carefully compare these samples with a test image to determine the font type. This raises the question of whether few-shot learning can enhance VLMs’ font recognition capabilities — specifically, whether VLMs can focus on the features provided in the demonstrations and leverage comparison-based reasoning to identify the correct font in the final test image.

在真实场景中，若给定以特定字体样式书写的单个字母的参考图像，人类可以仔细比对这些样本与测试图像来判断字体类型。这引出一个问题：少样本学习能否增强 VLM 的字体识别能力——具体而言，VLM 能否聚焦于示例所提供的特征，并借助基于比较的推理在最终测试图像中识别出正确字体。

To investigate this, we conduct few-shot experiments on six open-weight and closed-source VLMs under the setting described in Section 4.2. As shown in Figure 4, increasing the number of shots from 1 to 6 results in only a marginal improvement in model accuracy, with gains of less than 4 percentage points across all models. Given the dataset size for the hard version of the benchmark evaluation, this level of improvement is not ideal. Furthermore, we observe that, with the exception of the Qwen2-VL-72B-Instruct, whose accuracy either increases or remains stable as the number of shots increases, other models exhibit fluctuations in accuracy despite receiving more examples, which is counterintuitive. This instability further underscores the limited capabilities of VLMs in font recognition under few-shot settings. Notably, this contrasts sharply with human performance in fine-grained recognition tasks, where individuals effectively leverage reference samples to improve accuracy.

为此，我们在 4.2 节所述设置下对六个开放权重与闭源 VLM 开展少样本实验。如图 4 所示，把样本数从 1 增至 6 只带来微弱的准确率提升，所有模型的增益都不足 4 个百分点。考虑到困难版本评估所用的数据集规模，这样的提升幅度并不理想。此外我们观察到，除 Qwen2-VL-72B-Instruct 外（其准确率随样本数增加或升或持平），其他模型即便获得更多示例，准确率仍出现波动，这与直觉相悖。这种不稳定性进一步凸显了 VLM 在少样本设置下字体识别能力的局限。值得注意的是，这与人类在细粒度识别任务中的表现形成鲜明反差——人类能有效利用参考样本提升准确率。

Instead, only a few letters receive more attention than other parts, and in many cases, the model assigns higher attention to the white background rather than the textual content. The red-highlighted areas in the attention matrix cover most of the text, even though the highest attention weights are not fully concentrated on the letters. Nevertheless, this represents a notable improvement over Llama-3.2-11B-Vision-Instruct ’s performance on the hard version of the benchmark, where attention is more poorly distributed.

Based on our analysis of the above two VLMs, we infer that current VLMs remain inadequate for font recognition tasks, even for widely used font styles. This limitation likely arises from their inability to focus on the critical regions of the textual input that are essential for accurate font identification. We posit that this shortcoming stems from an insufficient alignment between visual attention mechanisms and the subtle typographic features that define font identity. Consequently, advancing font recognition within VLMs demands a more targeted modeling of local visual regions and a deeper integration of typographic priors.

基于对上述两个 VLM 的分析，我们推断：即便面对广泛使用的字体样式，当前 VLM 仍不足以胜任字体识别任务。这一局限很可能源于它们无法聚焦于文本输入中那些对准确识别字体至关重要的区域。我们认为，该短板出自视觉注意力机制与决定字体身份的细微排印特征之间对齐不足。因此，要在 VLM 中推进字体识别，需要对局部视觉区域作更有针对性的建模，并把排印先验更深入地融合进来。

8 Conclusion

8 结论

In this paper, we introduce a specially designed Font Recognition Benchmark (FRB) comprising 15 popular font styles, designed with both easy and hard versions to comprehensively assess the capabilities of contemporary VLMs in this fine-grained recognition task. We conduct extensive experiments with a range of VLMs and observe that these models consistently struggle with font recognition, particularly on the hard version of the benchmark, where the stroop effect substantially reduces accuracy. This finding highlights that VLMs remain susceptible to misleading visual cues even when performing semantically grounded tasks. To further examine the capacity of VLMs in this setting, we evaluate various inference strategies, including CoT prompting, Multiple-Choice Question answering, and Few-Shot Learning. However, our results demonstrate that these methods offer only marginal improvements in overall accuracy. Additionally, we categorize the error types exhibited by VLMs into four distinct categories and analyze the attention patterns of two publicly available VLMs. Our analysis reveals that these models fail to adequately focus on the edge information of characters in the input images, which impairs their ability to correctly identify font styles and renders them vulnerable to variations in texture content.

本文提出一个专门设计的字体识别基准（FRB），包含 15 种流行字体样式，设有简单与困难两个版本，用以全面评估当代 VLM 在这一细粒度识别任务上的能力。我们在一系列 VLM 上开展了大量实验，观察到这些模型在字体识别上一贯表现吃力，尤其在困难版本上——Stroop 效应大幅拉低了准确率。这一发现凸显出，即便在执行有语义依据的任务时，VLM 仍易受误导性视觉线索影响。为进一步检验 VLM 在该设置下的能力，我们评估了多种推理策略，包括 CoT 提示、选择题作答与少样本学习；然而结果表明，这些方法对整体准确率只有微弱改善。此外，我们把 VLM 表现出的错误类型归为四个不同类别，并分析了两个公开可得 VLM 的注意力模式。分析揭示，这些模型未能充分关注输入图像中字符的边缘信息，这削弱了它们正确识别字体样式的能力，并使它们易受纹理内容变化的影响。

9 Limitation

9 局限性

In this paper, we propose a specially designed benchmark comprising two task versions to enable a comprehensive evaluation of VLMs’ performance on font recognition and analyze the factors contributing to their suboptimal performance. However, our study still has the following limitations: (i) Due to budget constraints, we are unable to conduct few-shot inference on more powerful models such as Claude-3.5-Sonnet and GPT-4o. (ii) As certain high-performing models are closed-source, we are unable to analyze their attention matrices, limiting our understanding of their decision-making processes. (iii) Our experiments rely on straightforward prompts for inference. Designing more sophisticated prompts, such as guiding models to focus on key regions in the input image, may enhance their performance on this task. We plan to explore these directions in future work.

本文提出一个专门设计的、包含两个任务版本的基准，以便全面评估 VLM 在字体识别上的表现，并分析导致其表现欠佳的因素。然而本研究仍存在以下局限：（i）受预算限制，我们无法在 Claude-3.5-Sonnet、GPT-4o 等更强大的模型上开展少样本推理；（ii）由于某些高性能模型闭源，我们无法分析其注意力矩阵，限制了对它们决策过程的理解；（iii）我们的实验依赖简单直接的提示进行推理，而设计更精巧的提示（例如引导模型关注输入图像中的关键区域）或可提升其在该任务上的表现。我们计划在未来工作中探索这些方向。

    Fonts are generally categorized into three major types: Serif Fonts, Sans-Serif Fonts, and Script & Decorative Fonts.

    我们使用最直接的指标：准确率。模型性能通过从文本输出中提取预测标签，并与真值标签作字符串匹配比较来评估。模型准确率计算如下：

    Accuracy=1N​∑i=1NI​(yi=y^i)\text{Accuracy}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\text{I}(y_{i}=\hat{y}_{i}) (1)

    where I​(⋅)\text{I}(\cdot) is an indicator function that is 1 if the condition is true and 0 otherwise, yiy_{i} represents the true label, y^i\hat{y}_{i} is the predicted label, and NN is the total number of samples.

    其中 I​(⋅)\text{I}(\cdot) 为指示函数，条件成立时取 1、否则取 0；yiy_{i} 表示真实标签；y^i\hat{y}_{i} 为预测标签；NN 为样本总数。

    Appendix D Capability Gaps Between Open-weight and Closed-source Models

    附录 D 开放权重模型与闭源模型的能力差距

    The experimental results indicate that contemporary VLMs struggle with font recognition tasks, with a significant accuracy gap persisting between open-weight and closed-source models.

    实验结果表明，当代 VLM 在字体识别任务上表现吃力，且开放权重模型与闭源模型之间存在显著的准确率差距。

    In the sentence-based font recognition task, which is more commonly encountered in real-world scenarios, three out of four closed-source VLMs achieve at least 18% accuracy across various inference settings, with the best-performing model reaching nearly 67% under the multiple-choice question (MCQ) setting. In contrast, among all open-weight VLMs evaluated in this study, only Llama-3.2-11B-Vision-Instruct and Qwen2-VL-72B-Instruct attain approximately 18% accuracy, while the remaining models fall below 10%, with some approaching 0% accuracy. These findings highlight the substantial performance disparity between open-weight and closed-source VLMs in font recognition.

    在真实场景中更常见的基于句子的字体识别任务中，四个闭源 VLM 中有三个在各种推理设置下都至少取得 18% 的准确率，表现最好的模型在选择题（MCQ）设置下接近 67%。相比之下，本研究评估的所有开放权重 VLM 中，只有 Llama-3.2-11B-Vision-Instruct 与 Qwen2-VL-72B-Instruct 达到约 18% 的准确率，其余模型均低于 10%，有些甚至接近 0%。这些发现凸显了开放权重与闭源 VLM 在字体识别上的巨大性能落差。

    For the more challenging version of the task, overall performance remains low across both open-weight and closed-source models. The hard version introduces the stroop effect, adding an additional cognitive challenge that negatively impacts nearly all VLMs. Under the MCQ setting, all closed-source models are able to achieve accuracy above 6.67%, indicating that, in some cases, they successfully identify font styles rather than being misled by the textual content in the image.
