**EN** 1 Introduction

**ZH** 1 引言

**EN** Text-to-image (T2I) generation models [43, 12, 57, 60, 42, 59, 17] are rapidly becoming a key to content creation in various domains, including entertainment, art, design, and advertising, and are also being generalized to image editing [45, 4, 28, 51], video generation [23, 36, 54], among many other applications. Despite significant recent advances, the outputs still usually suffer from issues such as artifacts/implausibility, misalignment with text descriptions, and low aesthetic quality [55, 31, 53]. For example, in the Pick-a-Pic dataset [31], which mainly consists of images generated by Stable Diffusion model variants , many images (e.g. Fig. 1) contain distorted human/animal bodies (e.g. human hands with more than five fingers), distorted objects and implausibility issues such as a floating lamp. Our human evaluation experiments find that only ⟦SLOT_0⟧10% of the generated images in the dataset are free of artifacts and implausibility. Similarly, text-image misalignment issues are common too, e.g., the prompt is “a man jumping into a river” but the generated image shows the man standing.

**ZH** 文本到图像（T2I）生成模型 [43, 12, 57, 60, 42, 59, 17] 正迅速成为娱乐、艺术、设计与广告等诸多领域内容创作的关键工具，并被推广到图像编辑 [45, 4, 28, 51]、视频生成 [23, 36, 54] 等大量应用中。尽管近期进展显著，生成结果仍普遍存在伪影/不合理、与文本描述不一致、美学质量偏低等问题 [55, 31, 53]。例如，在主要由 Stable Diffusion 系列模型生成的 Pick-a-Pic 数据集 [31] 中，许多图像（如图 1）包含扭曲的人体/动物躯体（如超过五根手指的人手）、扭曲的物体，以及悬浮台灯之类的不合理现象。我们的人类评估实验发现，该数据集中仅有 ⟦SLOT_0⟧10% 的生成图像不存在伪影与不合理问题。图文不一致同样常见，例如提示词为“一名男子跳入河中”，而生成的图像中该男子却是站立的。

**EN** Existing automatic evaluation metrics for generated images, however, including the well-known IS [44] and FID [20], are computed over distributions of images and may not reflect nuances in individual images. Recent research has collected human preferences/ratings to evaluate the quality of generated images and trained evaluation models to predict those ratings [55, 31, 53], notably ImageReward [55] or Pick-a-Pic [31]. While more focused, these metrics still summarize the quality of one image into a single numeric score. In terms of prompt-image alignment, there are also seminal single-score metrics such as CLIPScore [19] and more recent question generation and answering pipelines [25, 58, 10, 8]. While more calibrated and explainable, these are expensive and complex models that still do not localize the regions of misalignment in the image.

**ZH** 然而，现有的生成图像自动评估指标，包括广为人知的 IS [44] 与 FID [20]，都是在图像分布上计算的，未必能反映单张图像的细微差异。近期研究转而收集人类偏好/评分来评价生成图像质量，并训练评估模型预测这些评分 [55, 31, 53]，代表性工作有 ImageReward [55] 与 Pick-a-Pic [31]。这些指标虽更具针对性，却仍把一张图像的质量压缩为单一数值分数。在提示词—图像对齐方面，也有 CLIPScore [19] 等经典的单分数指标，以及更新的问题生成与问答流程 [25, 58, 10, 8]。后者校准性更好、可解释性更强，但模型昂贵且复杂，依然无法定位图像中发生不一致的区域。

**EN** In this paper, we propose a dataset and a model of fine-grained multi-faceted evaluations that are interpretable and attributable (e.g., to regions with artifacts/implausibility or image-text misalignments), which provide a much richer understanding of the image quality than single scalar scores. As a first contribution, we collect a dataset of Rich Human Feedback on 18K images (RichHF-18K), which contains (i) point annotations on the image that highlight regions of implausibility/artifacts, and text-image misalignment; (ii) labeled words on the prompts specifying the missing or misrepresented concepts in the generated image; and (iii) four types of fine-grained scores for image plausibility, text-image alignment, aesthetics, and overall rating. Equipped with RichHF-18K, we design a multimodal transformer model, which we coin as Rich Automatic Human Feedback (RAHF) to learn to predict these rich human annotations on generated images and their associated text prompt. Our model can therefore predict implausibility and misalignment regions, misaligned keywords, as well as fine-grained scores. This not only provides reliable ratings, but also more detailed and explainable insights about the quality of the generated images. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first rich feedback dataset and model for state-of-the-art text-to-image generation models, providing an automatic and explainable pipeline to evaluate T2I generation.

**ZH** 本文提出一个细粒度、多面的评估数据集与模型，其评估结果可解释、可溯源（例如可归因到出现伪影/不合理或图文不一致的区域），比单一标量分数能提供更丰富的图像质量理解。第一项贡献是收集了覆盖 18K 图像的 Rich Human Feedback 数据集（RichHF-18K），其中包含：（i）图像上标出不合理/伪影区域与图文不一致区域的点标注；（ii）在提示词上标出生成图像中缺失或表征错误的词；（iii）针对图像合理性、图文对齐度、美学与总体评价的四类细粒度评分。基于 RichHF-18K，我们设计了一个多模态 Transformer 模型，命名为 Rich Automatic Human Feedback（RAHF），用于学习预测生成图像及其对应文本提示上的这些丰富人类标注。该模型因而能够预测不合理区域与不一致区域、不一致关键词以及细粒度评分。这不仅提供了可靠的评分，也给出了关于生成图像质量更细致、可解释的洞察。据我们所知，这是面向当前最先进文本到图像生成模型的首个丰富反馈数据集与模型，为评估 T2I 生成提供了一条自动化且可解释的流程。

**EN** The main contributions of this paper are summarized below:

**ZH** 本文的主要贡献概括如下：

**EN** 1. The first Rich Human Feedback dataset (RichHF-18K) on generated images (consisting of fine-grained scores, implausibility(artifact)/misalignment image regions, and misalignment keywords), on 18K Pick-a-Pic images.

**ZH** 1. 首个面向生成图像的 Rich Human Feedback 数据集（RichHF-18K），涵盖 18K 张 Pick-a-Pic 图像，包含细粒度评分、不合理（伪影）/不一致图像区域以及不一致关键词。

**EN** 2. A multimodal Transformer model (RAHF) to predict rich feedback on generated images, which we show to be highly correlated with the human annotations on a test set.

**ZH** 2. 一个用于预测生成图像丰富反馈的多模态 Transformer 模型（RAHF），实验表明其预测结果与测试集上的人类标注高度相关。

**EN** 3. We further demonstrate the usefulness of the predicted rich human feedback by RAHF to improve image generation: (i) by using the predicted heatmaps as masks to inpaint problematic image regions and (ii) by using the predicted scores to help finetune image generation models (like Muse [6]), e.g., via selecting/filtering finetuning data, or as reward guidance. We show that in both cases we obtain better images than with the original model.

**ZH** 3. 进一步验证了 RAHF 预测的丰富人类反馈对改进图像生成的作用：（i）以预测热力图作为掩膜，对存在问题的图像区域进行重绘；（ii）利用预测评分辅助微调图像生成模型（如 Muse [6]），例如筛选微调数据，或作为奖励引导。两种情形下，所得图像均优于原始模型。

**EN** 4. The improvement on the Muse model, which differs from the models that generated the images in our training set, shows the good generalization capacity of our RAHF model.

**ZH** 4. 在 Muse 模型上的改进表明 RAHF 具有良好的泛化能力：Muse 与生成训练集图像的模型并不相同。

**EN** 2 Related works

**ZH** 2 相关工作

**EN** Text-to-image generation

**ZH** 文本到图像生成

**EN** Text-to-image (T2I) generation models have evolved and iterated through several popular model architectures in the deep learning era. An early work is the Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) [16, 3, 27], which trains a generator for image generation and a discriminator to distinguish between real and generated images, in parallel (also see [61, 56, 63, 39, 33, 48] among others). Another category of generation models develops from variational auto-encoders (VAEs) [30, 21, 49], which optimize evidence lower bound (ELBO) for the likelihood of the image data.

**ZH** 深度学习时代，文本到图像（T2I）生成模型历经多种主流架构的演进迭代。早期工作是生成对抗网络（GAN）[16, 3, 27]，它并行训练一个用于生成图像的生成器与一个区分真实图像和生成图像的判别器（另见 [61, 56, 63, 39, 33, 48] 等）。另一类生成模型由变分自编码器（VAE）[30, 21, 49] 发展而来，通过优化图像数据似然的证据下界（ELBO）进行训练。

**EN** Most recently, Diffusion Models (DMs) [47, 22, 37, 42] have emerged as the state-of-the-art (SOTA) for Image Generation [13]. DMs are trained to generate images progressively from random noise, with the ability to capture more diversity than GANs and achieve good sample quality [13]. Latent Diffusion Models [42] are a further refinement that performs the diffusion process in a compact latent space for more efficiency.

**ZH** 近年来，扩散模型（DM）[47, 22, 37, 42] 成为图像生成的最新最优方法（SOTA）[13]。扩散模型被训练为从随机噪声逐步生成图像，能够捕捉比 GAN 更丰富的多样性，并取得良好的样本质量 [13]。潜在扩散模型 [42] 在此基础上进一步改进，在紧凑的潜空间中执行扩散过程，效率更高。

**EN** Text-to-image evaluation and reward models

**ZH** 文本到图像评估与奖励模型

**EN** There has been much recent work on evaluation of text-to-image models along many dimensions [55, 31, 26, 53, 52, 38, 32, 9]. Xu et al. [55] collect a human preference dataset by requesting users to rank multiple images and rate them according to their quality. They trained a reward model ImageReward for human preference learning, and proposed Reward Feedback Learning (ReFL) for tuning diffusion models with the ImageReward model. Kirstain et al. [31] built a web application to collect human preferences by asking users to choose the better image from a pair of generated images, resulting in a dataset called Pick-a-Pic with more than 500K examples generated by T2I models such as Stable Diffusion 2.1, Dreamlike Photoreal 2.05, and Stable Diffusion XL variants. They leveraged the human preference dataset to train a CLIP-based [40] scoring function, called PickScore, to predict human preferences. Huang et al. [26] proposed a benchmark called T2I-CompBench for evaluating text-to-image models, which consists of 6,000 text prompts describing attribute binding, object relationships, and complex compositions. They utilized multiple pretrained vision-language models such as CLIP [40] and BLIP [35] to calculate multiple evaluation metrics. Wu et al. [53], Wu et al. [52] collected a large scale dataset of human choices on generated images and utilized the dataset to train a classifier that outputs a Human Preference Score (HPS). They showed improvement in image generation by tuning Stable Diffusion with the HPS. Recently, Lee [32] proposed a holistic evaluation for T2I models with multiple fine-grained metrics.

**ZH** 近期有大量工作从多个维度评估文本到图像模型 [55, 31, 26, 53, 52, 38, 32, 9]。Xu 等人 [55] 要求用户对多张图像排序并按质量打分，收集了人类偏好数据集，训练出用于人类偏好学习的奖励模型 ImageReward，并提出 Reward Feedback Learning（ReFL）以利用 ImageReward 调优扩散模型。Kirstain 等人 [31] 构建一个网页应用，让用户在成对生成图像中挑选更优者，由此得到包含 50 万余条样本的 Pick-a-Pic 数据集，图像由 Stable Diffusion 2.1、Dreamlike Photoreal 2.05 与 Stable Diffusion XL 等 T2I 模型生成；他们利用该偏好数据集训练了一个基于 CLIP [40] 的打分函数 PickScore，用于预测人类偏好。Huang 等人 [26] 提出评测基准 T2I-CompBench，包含 6,000 条描述属性绑定、物体关系与复杂构图的文本提示，并借助 CLIP [40]、BLIP [35] 等多个预训练视觉—语言模型计算多项评估指标。Wu 等人 [53]、Wu 等人 [52] 收集了大规模生成图像人类选择数据集，用以训练输出人类偏好分数（HPS）的分类器，并证明用 HPS 调优 Stable Diffusion 可改进图像生成。Lee [32] 最近提出一种面向 T2I 模型的整体性评估方法，包含多个细粒度指标。

**EN** Despite these valuable contributions, most existing works only use binary human ratings or preference ranking for construction of feedback/rewards, and lack the ability to provide detailed actionable feedback such as implausible regions of the image, misaligned regions, or misaligned keywords on the generated images. One recent paper related to our work is Zhang et al. [62], which collected a dataset of artifact regions for image synthesis tasks, trained a segmentation-based model to predict artifact regions, and proposed a region inpainting method for those regions. However, the focus of their work is artifact region only, while in this paper, we collected rich feedback for T2I generation containing not only artifact regions, but also misalignment regions, misaligned keywords, and four fine-grained scores from multiple aspects. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work on heterogeneous rich human feedback for text-to-image models.

**ZH** 这些工作虽有价值，但多数仅用二值人类评分或偏好排序来构造反馈/奖励，无法给出图像中不合理区域、不一致区域或不一致关键词等可操作的具体反馈。与本文相近的一项近期工作是 Zhang 等人 [62]，他们为图像合成任务收集了伪影区域数据集，训练基于分割的模型预测伪影区域，并提出针对这些区域的重绘方法。然而该工作只关注伪影区域；本文为 T2I 生成收集的丰富反馈不仅包含伪影区域，还包含不一致区域、不一致关键词以及来自多个方面的四类细粒度评分。据我们所知，这是首个面向文本到图像模型的异构丰富人类反馈工作。

**EN** 3.1 Data collection process

**ZH** 3.1 数据采集流程

**EN** In this section, we discuss our procedure to collect the RichHF-18K dataset, which includes two heatmaps (artifact/implausibility and misalignment), four fine-grained scores (plausibility, alignment, aesthetics, and overall score), and one text sequence (misaligned keywords).

**ZH** 本节介绍 RichHF-18K 数据集的采集流程。该数据集包含两类热力图（伪影/不合理与不一致）、四类细粒度评分（合理性、对齐度、美学与总体评分），以及一段文本序列（不一致关键词）。

**EN** For each generated image, the annotators are first asked to examine the image and read the text prompt used to generate it. Then, they mark points on the image to indicate the location of any implausibility/artifact or misalignment w.r.t the text prompt. The annotators are told that each marked point has an “effective radius” (1/20 of the image height), which forms an imaginary disk centering at the marked point. In this way, we can use a relatively small amount of points to cover the image regions with flaws. Lastly, annotators label the misaligned keywords and the four types of scores for plausibility, image-text alignment, aesthetic, and overall quality, respectively, on a 5-point Likert scale. Detailed definitions of image implausibility/artifact and misalignment can be found in the supplementary materials. We designed a web UI, as shown in Fig. 1, to facilitate data collection. More details about data collection process can be found in the supplementary materials.

**ZH** 对于每张生成图像，标注者首先查看图像并阅读生成它所用的文本提示，然后在图像上标点，指出任何不合理/伪影之处，或相对文本提示的不一致之处。我们告知标注者，每个标记点具有一个“有效半径”（图像高度的 1/20），即在标记点周围形成一个假想圆盘。这样便可用较少的点覆盖有缺陷的图像区域。最后，标注者标注不一致关键词，并在 5 点李克特量表上分别给出合理性、图文对齐度、美学与总体质量四类评分。图像不合理/伪影与不一致的详细定义见补充材料。我们设计了如图 1 所示的网页界面以方便数据采集，采集流程的更多细节见补充材料。

**EN** 3.2 Human feedback consolidation

**ZH** 3.2 人类反馈整合

**EN** To improve the reliability of the collected human feedback on generated images, each image-text pair is annotated by three annotators. We therefore need to consolidate the multiple annotations for each sample. For the scores, we simply average the scores from the multiple annotators for an image to obtain the final score. For the misaligned keyword annotations, we perform majority voting to get the final sequence of indicators of aligned/misaligned, using the most frequent label for the keywords. For the point annotations, we first convert them to heatmaps for each annotation, where each point is converted to a disk region (as discussed in the last subsection) on the heatmap, and then we compute the average heatmap across annotators. The regions with clear implausibility are likely to be annotated by all annotators and have a high value on the final average heatmap.

**ZH** 为提升生成图像上人类反馈的可靠性，每个图文对由三名标注者标注，因此需要整合同一样本的多个标注。评分方面，直接对同一图像的多个标注取平均得到最终分数。不一致关键词方面，采用多数投票确定每个词一致/不一致的最终标签序列，即取出现频次最高的标签。点标注方面，先将每个标注转换为热力图（每个点在热力图上转换为前述的圆盘区域），再对各标注者的热力图取平均。明显不合理的区域很可能被所有标注者标记，因而在最终平均热力图上取值较高。

**EN** 3.3 RichHF-18K: a dataset of rich human feedback

**ZH** 3.3 RichHF-18K：丰富人类反馈数据集

**EN** We select a subset of image-text pairs from the Pick-a-Pic dataset for data annotation. Although our method is general and applicable to any generated images, we choose the majority of our dataset to be photo-realistic images, due to its importance and wider applications. Moreover, we also want to have balanced categories across the images. To ensure balance, we utilized the PaLI visual question answering (VQA) model [7] to extract some basic features from the Pick-a-Pic data samples. Specifically, we asked the following questions for each image-text pair in Pick-a-Pic. 1) Is the image photorealistic? 2) Which category best describes the image? Choose one in ‘human’, ‘animal’, ‘object’, ‘indoor scene’, ‘outdoor scene’. PaLI’s answers to these two questions are generally reliable under our manual inspection. We used the answers to sample a diverse subset from Pick-a-Pic, resulting in 17K image-text pairs. We randomly split the 17K samples into two subsets, a training set with 16K samples and a validation set with 1K samples. The distribution of the attributes of the 16K training samples is shown in the supplementary materials. Additionally, we collect rich human feedback on the unique prompts and their corresponding images from the Pick-a-Pic test set as our test set. In total, we collected rich human feedback on the 18K image-text pairs from Pick-a-Pic. Our RichHF-18K dataset consists of 16K training, 1K validation, and 1K test samples.

**ZH** 我们从 Pick-a-Pic 数据集中选取一部分图文对进行标注。尽管本方法通用、适用于任意生成图像，考虑到写实图像的重要性与广泛应用，数据集主体仍以写实图像为主。此外，我们也希望各类别在图像间保持均衡。为此，我们使用 PaLI 视觉问答（VQA）模型 [7] 从 Pick-a-Pic 样本中提取若干基本属性，具体对每对图文提出两个问题：1）该图像是否写实？2）哪一类别最能描述该图像？请在“人物”“动物”“物体”“室内场景”“室外场景”中选择其一。经人工检查，PaLI 对这两个问题的回答总体可靠。我们据此从 Pick-a-Pic 中采样出多样化的子集，得到 17K 图文对，并将其随机划分为 16K 训练样本与 1K 验证样本。16K 训练样本的属性分布见补充材料。此外，我们针对 Pick-a-Pic 测试集中的唯一提示词及其对应图像收集丰富人类反馈，作为测试集。综上，我们共为 Pick-a-Pic 中的 18K 图文对收集了丰富人类反馈。RichHF-18K 数据集由 16K 训练样本、1K 验证样本与 1K 测试样本构成。

**ZH** 3.4 RichHF-18K 数据统计

**EN** In this section, we summarize the statistics of the scores and perform the annotator agreement analysis for the scores. We standardize the scores ⟦SLOT_1⟧ with the formula ⟦SLOT_2⟧ (⟦SLOT_3⟧ and ⟦SLOT_4⟧) so that the scores lie in the range [0, 1].

**ZH** 本节汇总评分统计，并对评分进行标注者一致性分析。我们用公式 ⟦SLOT_2⟧（⟦SLOT_3⟧ 与 ⟦SLOT_4⟧）对评分 ⟦SLOT_1⟧ 做标准化，使其落在 [0, 1] 区间内。

**EN** The histogram plots of the scores are shown in Fig. 2. The distribution of the scores is similar to a Gaussian distribution, while the plausibility and text-image alignment scores have a slightly higher percentage of score 1.0. The distribution of the collected scores ensures that we have a reasonable number of negative and positive samples for training a good reward model.

**ZH** 评分直方图如图 2 所示。评分分布接近高斯分布，其中合理性评分与图文对齐度评分取 1.0 的比例略高。所收集评分的分布保证了训练良好奖励模型所需的正负样本数量合理。

**EN** To analyze the rating agreement among annotators for an image-text pair, we calculate the maximum difference among the scores: ⟦SLOT_5⟧ where scores are the three score labels for an image-text pair. We plot the histogram of ⟦SLOT_6⟧ in Fig. 4. We can see that around 25% of the samples have perfect annotator agreement and around 85% of the samples have good annotator agreement (⟦SLOT_7⟧ is less than or equal to 0.25 after the standardization or 1 in the 5-point Likert scale).

**EN** 4 Predicting rich human feedback

**ZH** 4 预测丰富人类反馈

**EN** 4.1 Models

**ZH** 4.1 模型

**EN** 4.1.1 Architecture

**ZH** 4.1.1 架构

**EN** The architecture of our model is shown in Fig. 3. We adopt a vision-language model based on ViT [14] and T5X [41] models, inspired by the Spotlight model architecture [34], but modifying both the model and pretraining datasets to better suit our tasks. We use a self-attention module [50] among the concatenated image tokens and text tokens, similar to PaLI [7], as our tasks require bidirectional information propagation. The text information is propagated to image tokens for text misalignment score and heatmap prediction, while the vision information is propagated to text tokens for better vision-aware text encoding to decode the text misalignment sequence. To pretrain the model on more diverse images, we add the natural image captioning task on the WebLI dataset [7] to the pretraining task mixture.

**ZH** 模型架构如图 3 所示。我们采用基于 ViT [14] 与 T5X [41] 的视觉—语言模型，借鉴 Spotlight 的架构 [34]，但对模型与预训练数据集做了修改以更适配本文任务。由于任务需要双向信息传播，我们像 PaLI [7] 一样，在拼接后的图像词元与文本词元之间使用自注意力模块 [50]：文本信息传播到图像词元，用于预测不一致评分与热力图；视觉信息传播到文本词元，用于获得更具视觉感知的文本编码，以解码不一致文本序列。为使模型在更多样的图像上预训练，我们在预训练任务混合中加入了 WebLI 数据集 [7] 上的自然图像描述任务。

**EN** Specifically, the ViT takes the generated image as input and outputs image tokens as high-level representations. The text prompt tokens are embedded into dense vectors. The image tokens and embedded text tokens are concatenated and encoded by the Transformer self-attention encoder in T5X. On top of the encoded fused text and image tokens, we use three kinds of predictors to predict different outputs. For heatmap prediction, the image tokens are reshaped into a feature map and sent through convolution layers, deconvolution layers, and sigmoid activation, and outputs implausibility and misalignment heatmaps. For score prediction, the feature map is sent through convolution layers, linear layers, and sigmoid activation, resulting in scalars as fine-grained scores.

**ZH** 具体而言，ViT 以生成图像为输入，输出图像词元作为高层表示；文本提示词元被嵌入为稠密向量；图像词元与嵌入后的文本词元拼接，由 T5X 中的 Transformer 自注意力编码器编码。在融合编码后的文本与图像词元之上，我们使用三类预测器预测不同输出。热力图预测方面，图像词元被重塑为特征图，依次经过卷积层、反卷积层与 sigmoid 激活，输出不合理性热力图与不一致热力图。评分预测方面，特征图经过卷积层、线性层与 sigmoid 激活，输出标量形式的细粒度评分。

**EN** To predict the keyword misalignment sequence, the original prompt used to generate the image is used as text input to the model. A modified prompt is used as the prediction target for the T5X decoder. The modified prompt has a special suffix (‘⟦SLOT_8⟧’) for each misaligned token, e.g., a yellow_0 cat if the generated image contains a black cat and the word yellow is misaligned with the image. During evaluation, we can extract the misaligned keywords using the special suffix.

**ZH** 预测关键词不一致序列时，生成图像所用的原始提示词作为模型的文本输入，修改后的提示词作为 T5X 解码器的预测目标。该修改版提示词为每个不一致词元添加特殊后缀（“⟦SLOT_8⟧”），例如当生成图像中是一只黑猫、而“yellow”一词与图像不一致时，目标为 a yellow_0 cat。评估时，可依据该特殊后缀提取不一致关键词。

**EN** 4.1.2 Model variants

**ZH** 4.1.2 模型变体

**EN** We explore two model variants for the prediction heads of the heatmaps and scores.

**ZH** 针对热力图与评分的预测头，我们探索了两种模型变体。

**EN** Multi-head

**ZH** 多头（Multi-head）

**EN** A straightforward way to predict multiple heatmaps and scores is to use multiple prediction heads, with one head for each score and heatmap type. This will require seven prediction heads in total.

**ZH** 预测多个热力图与评分的直接做法是使用多个预测头，每个评分与热力图类型各对应一个头，共需七个预测头。

**EN** Augmented prompt

**ZH** 增强提示（Augmented prompt）

**EN** Another approach is to use a single head for each prediction type, i.e., three heads in total, for heatmap, score, and misalignment sequence, respectively. To inform the model of the fine-grained heatmap or score type, we augment the prompt with the output type. More specifically, we prepend a task string (e.g., ‘implausibility heatmap’) to the prompt for each particular task of one example and use the corresponding label as the training target. During inference, by augmenting the prompt with the corresponding task string, the single heatmap (score) head can predict different heatmaps (scores). As we show in the experiments, this augmented prompt approach can create task-specific vision feature maps and text encodings, which performs significantly better in some of the tasks.

**ZH** 另一种做法是每类预测只用一个头，即热力图、评分与不一致序列共三个头。为告知模型具体的热力图或评分类型，我们在提示词中加入输出类型进行增强：具体而言，为样本的每项具体任务在提示词前拼接任务字符串（如“implausibility heatmap”，即不合理性热力图），并以相应标签作为训练目标。推理时，通过在提示词中拼入对应任务字符串，单个热力图（评分）头即可预测不同的热力图（评分）。实验表明，这种增强提示方式能为不同任务生成特定的视觉特征图与文本编码，在部分任务上表现明显更好。

**EN** 4.1.3 Model optimization

**ZH** 4.1.3 模型优化

**EN** We train the model with a pixel-wise mean squared error (MSE) loss for the heatmap prediction, and MSE loss for the score prediction. For misalignment sequence prediction, the model is trained with teacher-forcing cross-entropy loss. The final loss function is the weighted combination of the heatmap MSE loss, score MSE loss, and the sequence teacher-forcing cross-entropy loss.

**ZH** 热力图预测采用逐像素均方误差（MSE）损失训练，评分预测同样采用 MSE 损失；不一致序列预测采用教师强制（teacher-forcing）交叉熵损失。最终损失函数为热力图 MSE 损失、评分 MSE 损失与序列教师强制交叉熵损失的加权组合。

**EN** Evaluation metrics

**ZH** 评估指标

**EN** For score prediction tasks, we report the Pearson linear correlation coefficient (PLCC) and Spearman rank correlation coefficient (SRCC), which are typical evaluation metrics for score predictions [29]. For heatmap prediction tasks, a straightforward way to evaluate the results would be to borrow standard saliency heatmap evaluation metrics such as NSS/KLD [5]. However, these metrics cannot be applied directly in our case as all these metrics assume the ground truth heatmap is not empty; yet in our case, empty ground truth is possible (e.g., for artifact/implausibility heatmap, it means the image does not have any artifact/implausibility). As such, we report MSE on all samples and on those with empty ground truth, respectively, and report saliency heatmap evaluation metrics like NSS/KLD/AUC-Judd/SIM/CC [5] for the samples with non-empty ground truth. For the misaligned keyword sequence prediction, we adopt the token-level precision, recall, and F1-score. Specifically, the precision/recall/F1 scores are computed for the misaligned keywords over all the samples.

**ZH** 评分预测任务报告皮尔逊线性相关系数（PLCC）与斯皮尔曼秩相关系数（SRCC），这是评分预测的常用评估指标 [29]。热力图预测任务直观上可借用 NSS/KLD [5] 等标准显著性热力图评估指标，但这些指标不能直接用于本文：它们都假定真值热力图非空，而本文允许真值热力图为空（例如伪影/不合理热力图为空表示图像不含任何伪影/不合理）。因此，我们分别报告全部样本上与真值为空样本上的 MSE，并对真值非空的样本报告 NSS/KLD/AUC-Judd/SIM/CC [5] 等显著性热力图评估指标。不一致关键词序列预测采用词元级精确率、召回率与 F1 分数，具体在所有样本上针对不一致关键词计算 precision/recall/F1。

**EN** Baselines

**ZH** 基准方法

**EN** For comparison, we finetune two ResNet-50 models [18], with multiple fully connected layers and deconvolution heads to predict the scores and heatmaps, respectively. We also use the off-the-shelf PickScore model [31] to compute the PickScores and calculate the metrics w.r.t each of our four ground truth scores. We use the off-the-shelf CLIP model [40] as a baseline to compute the cosine similarity of the image and text embeddings and use it to calculate the text-image alignment metric, as the CLIP cosine similarity is designed to reflect the alignment between images and prompts. Besides, we also fine-tune a CLIP model to predict the four types of scores using our training dataset. For misalignment heatmap prediction, we use CLIP gradient [46] map as a baseline.

**ZH** 作为对比，我们微调两个 ResNet-50 模型 [18]，分别用多个全连接层与反卷积头预测评分和热力图。我们也使用现成的 PickScore 模型 [31] 计算 PickScore，并针对四类真值评分分别计算各项指标；使用现成的 CLIP 模型 [40] 作为基准，计算图像与文本嵌入的余弦相似度，并据此计算图文对齐度指标，因为 CLIP 余弦相似度本就用于反映图像与提示词之间的对齐程度。此外，我们还在训练集上微调了一个 CLIP 模型来预测四类评分。不一致热力图预测以 CLIP 梯度 [46] 图作为基准。

**EN** Quantitative analysis

**ZH** 定量分析

**EN** The experimental results of our model prediction on the four fine-grained scores, the implausibility heatmap, misalignment heatmap, and misalignment keyword sequence on our RichHF-18K test set are presented in Tab. 1, Tab. 2, Tab. 3, and Tab. 4 respectively.

**ZH** 模型在 RichHF-18K 测试集上对四类细粒度评分、不合理性热力图、不一致热力图与不一致关键词序列的预测结果分别见表 1、表 2、表 3 与表 4。

**EN** In both Tab. 1 and Tab. 3, the two variants of our proposed model both significantly outperform ResNet-50 (or CLIP for text-image alignment score). Yet, in Tab. 2, the multi-head version of our model performs worse than ResNet-50, but our augmented prompt version outperforms ResNet-50. The main reason might be that in the multi-head version, without augmenting the prediction task in the prompt, the same prompt is used for all the seven prediction tasks, and hence the feature maps and text tokens will be the same for all tasks. It might not be easy to find a good tradeoff among these tasks, and hence the performance of some tasks such as artifact/implausibility heatmap became worse. However, after augmenting the prediction task into a prompt, the feature map and text token can be adapted to each particular task with better results. Additionally, we note that misalignment heatmap prediction generally has worse results than artifact/implausibility heatmap prediction, possibly because misalignment regions are less well-defined, and the annotations may therefore be noisier.

**ZH** 在表 1 与表 3 中，本文模型的两个变体均显著优于 ResNet-50（图文对齐度评分上则优于 CLIP）。而在表 2 中，多头版本的模型表现不及 ResNet-50，增强提示版本则优于 ResNet-50。主要原因可能在于：多头版本未在提示词中指明预测任务，七个预测任务共用同一提示词，因而所有任务的特征图与文本词元相同，难以在这些任务间取得良好权衡，导致伪影/不合理热力图等部分任务性能下降。相反，把预测任务拼入提示词后，特征图与文本词元可针对具体任务自适应调整，结果更好。此外我们注意到，不一致热力图预测的结果普遍劣于伪影/不合理热力图预测，原因可能是不一致区域的界定更模糊，标注噪声也更大。

**EN** 5 Learning from rich human feedback

**ZH** 5 从丰富人类反馈中学习

**EN** In this section, we investigate whether the predicted rich human feedback (e.g., scores and heatmaps) can be used to improve image generation. To ensure that the gains from our RAHF model generalize across generative model families, we mainly use Muse [6] as our target model to improve, which is based on a masked transformer architecture and thus different from the Stable Diffusion model variants in our RichHF-18K dataset.

**ZH** 本节考察预测得到的丰富人类反馈（如评分与热力图）能否用于改进图像生成。为确保 RAHF 带来的增益可跨生成模型体系泛化，我们主要以 Muse [6] 为改进目标。Muse 基于掩码式 Transformer 架构，与 RichHF-18K 数据集中的 Stable Diffusion 系列模型不同。

**EN** Finetuning generative models with predicted scores

**ZH** 用预测评分微调生成模型

**EN** We first illustrate that finetuning with RAHF scores can improve Muse. First, we generate eight images for each of the ⟦SLOT_18⟧ prompts (the prompt set is created via PaLM 2 [11, 1] with some seed prompts) using the pre-trained Muse model. We predict RAHF scores for each image, and if the highest score for the images from each prompt is above a fixed threshold, it will be selected as part of our finetuning dataset. The Muse model is then finetuned with this dataset. This approach could be viewed as a simplified version of Direct Preference Optimization [15].

**ZH** 我们首先说明用 RAHF 评分微调可以改进 Muse。先用预训练 Muse 模型为 ⟦SLOT_18⟧ 条提示词（该提示词集由 PaLM 2 [11, 1] 基于若干种子提示词生成）各生成八张图像，再预测每张图像的 RAHF 评分；若某条提示词对应图像的最高分超过固定阈值，该图像即被选入微调数据集，随后用于微调 Muse。该方法可视为直接偏好优化（Direct Preference Optimization）[15] 的简化版本。

**EN** In Fig. 8 (a)-(b), we show one example of finetuning Muse with our predicted plausibility score (threshold=0.8). To quantify the gain from Muse finetuning, we used 100 new prompts to generate images, and asked 6 annotators to perform side-by-side comparisons (for plausibility) between two images from the original Muse and the fine-tuned Muse respectively. The annotators choose from five possible responses (image A is significantly/slightly better than image B, about the same, image B is slightly/significantly better than image A), without knowledge of which model is used to generate the image A/B. The results in Tab. 5 demonstrate that the finetuned Muse with RAHF plausibility scores possesses significantly fewer artifacts/implausibility than the original Muse.

**ZH** 图 8（a）-（b）展示了以预测合理性评分（阈值 0.8）微调 Muse 的一个示例。为量化微调带来的增益，我们用 100 条新提示词生成图像，并请 6 名标注者对原始 Muse 与微调 Muse 的两张图像做并排比较（针对合理性）。标注者从五种回答中选择其一（图像 A 显著/略优于图像 B、两者大致相当、图像 B 略优/显著优于图像 A），且不知道 A/B 各由哪个模型生成。表 5 的结果表明，用 RAHF 合理性评分微调后的 Muse，其伪影/不合理现象明显少于原始 Muse。

**EN** Moreover, in Fig. 8 (c)-(d), we show an example of using the RAHF aesthetic score as Classifier Guidance to the Latent Diffusion model [42], similar to the approach in Bansal et al. [2], demonstrating that each of the fine-grained scores can improve different aspects of the generative model/results.

**ZH** 此外，图 8（c）-（d）展示了把 RAHF 美学评分作为分类器引导用于潜在扩散模型 [42] 的示例，做法与 Bansal 等人 [2] 类似，说明各类细粒度评分可分别改进生成模型/结果的不同方面。

**EN** Region inpainting with predicted heatmaps and scores

**ZH** 用预测热力图与评分做区域重绘

**EN** We demonstrate that our model’s predicted heatmaps and scores can be used to perform region inpainting to improve the quality of generated images. For each image, we first predict implausibility heatmaps, then create a mask by processing the heatmap (using thresholding and dilating). Muse inpainting [6] is applied within the masked region to generate new images that match the text prompt. Multiple images are generated, and the final image is chosen by the highest predicted plausibility score by our RAHF.

**ZH** 我们证明模型预测的热力图和评分可用于区域重绘，从而提升生成图像质量。对每张图像，先预测不合理性热力图，再对热力图做阈值化与膨胀处理得到掩膜，然后在掩膜区域内应用 Muse 重绘 [6] 生成符合文本提示的新图像。生成多张候选图像后，取 RAHF 预测合理性评分最高者作为最终图像。

**EN** In Fig. 9, we show several inpainting results with our predicted implausibility heatmaps and plausibility scores. As shown, more plausible images with fewer artifacts are generated after inpainting. Again, this shows that our RAHF generalizes well to images from a generative model very different from the ones whose images are used to train RAHF. More details and examples can be found in the supplementary material.

**ZH** 图 9 展示了基于预测不合理性热力图与合理性评分的若干重绘结果。可以看到，重绘后生成图像的合理性更高、伪影更少。这再次说明 RAHF 能很好地泛化到与训练集图像生成模型差异很大的其他生成模型。更多细节与示例见补充材料。

**EN** 6 Conclusions and limitations

**ZH** 6 结论与局限性

**EN** In this work, we contributed RichHF-18K, the first rich human feedback dataset for image generation. We designed and trained a multimodal Transformer to predict the rich human feedback, and demonstrated some instances to improve image generation with our rich human feedback.

**ZH** 本文贡献了 RichHF-18K——首个面向图像生成的丰富人类反馈数据集；设计并训练了一个多模态 Transformer 来预测丰富人类反馈，并给出若干利用该反馈改进图像生成的实例。

**EN** While some of our results are quite exciting and promising, there are several limitations to our work. First, the model performance on the misalignment heatmap is worse than that on the implausibility heatmaps, possibly due to the noise in the misalignment heatmap. It is somewhat ambiguous how to label some misalignment cases such as absent objects on the image. Improving the misalignment label quality is one of the future directions. Second, it would be helpful to collect more data on generative models beyond Pick-a-Pic (Stable Diffusion) and investigate their effect on the RAHF models. Moreover, while we present three promising ways to leverage our model to improve T2I generation, there is a myriad of other ways to utilize rich human feedback that can be explored, e.g., how to use the predicted heatmaps or scores as a reward signal to finetune generative models with reinforcement learning, and how to use the predicted heatmaps as a weighting map, or how to use the predicted misaligned sequences in learning from human feedback to help improve image generation, etc. We hope RichHF-18K and our initial models inspire quests to investigate these research directions in future work.

**ZH** 部分结果令人鼓舞，但本工作仍存在若干局限。其一，模型在不一致热力图上的表现不及不合理性热力图，可能源于不一致热力图中的噪声；某些不一致情形（如图像中缺失物体）本身标注标准较为模糊，提升不一致标注质量是未来方向之一。其二，有必要在 Pick-a-Pic（Stable Diffusion）之外的生成模型上采集更多数据，并考察其对 RAHF 模型的影响。此外，本文给出三种利用本模型改进 T2I 生成的可行途径，而丰富人类反馈的用法远不止这些，例如如何把预测热力图或评分作为奖励信号、以强化学习微调生成模型，如何把预测热力图用作加权图，以及如何在人类反馈学习中利用预测的不一致序列来改进图像生成等。希望 RichHF-18K 与我们的初始模型能激发后续研究朝这些方向探索。

**EN** 8.4 Discussions and limitations

**ZH** 8.4 讨论与局限

**EN** We choose points over bounding boxes in our region annotation because we find that points are much faster to mark and can have a reasonable coverage over image regions with various shapes when we specify an effective radius for each point as discussed in the main paper.

**ZH** 在区域标注中我们选择点而非边界框，因为我们发现打点速度快得多；正如正文所述，只要为每个点指定有效半径，点标注就能对各种形状的图像区域给出合理覆盖。

**EN** As a limitation in our region/heatmap annotations, we notice there is an over-annotation issue in the artifacts/implausibility region annotation. Specifically, our annotators tend to annotate more human faces and hands on the images than necessary. One reason is that human faces and hands in the Pick-a-Pic dataset indeed have more artifacts/implausibility than other parts. Moreover, the annotators, as humans, may naturally pay more attention to human faces and hands, resulting in over-annotation of these parts. Nevertheless, the over-annotation issue is minor in our final dataset, as we strive to provide feedback to the annotators frequently to make them less nitpicking about human faces and hands.

**ZH** 区域/热力图标注的一个局限是伪影/不合理区域存在过度标注现象：标注者倾向于标注超出必要数量的人脸和手。原因之一是 Pick-a-Pic 数据集中人脸和手的伪影/不合理现象确实多于其他部位；此外，标注者作为人，天然会更关注人脸和手，导致这些部位被过度标注。不过，由于我们频繁向标注者反馈、提醒其不必对人脸和手过分挑剔，过度标注在最终数据集中并不严重。

**EN** Another limitation is the diversity of the subjects in the prompts/images. The Pick-a-Pic dataset (and many others) is predominantly full of human, dog, and cat subjects. For this reason, it is challenging to find a very diverse dataset for annotation. We strive to make the subjects more diverse by using balanced categories as indicated by the PaLI attributes (as in Fig. 10). We didn’t choose more fine-grained categories for PaLI to test as there would be an endless list of subjects we could consider. Therefore, we leave the goal of annotating more diverse images/prompts in future works.

**ZH** 另一个局限是提示词/图像中主体的多样性不足。Pick-a-Pic 数据集（以及其他许多数据集）的主体以人物、狗和猫为主，因此很难找到非常多样的数据集用于标注。我们借助 PaLI 属性（如图 10）做类别均衡，尽量丰富主体类型，但并未让 PaLI 测试更细粒度的类别——那样可考虑的主体将无穷无尽。因此，我们把标注更多样的图像/提示词留待未来工作。

**EN** 9 Experimental details

**ZH** 9 实验细节

**EN** Hyperparameters

**ZH** 超参数

**EN** The main model components consist of a ViT B16 encoder for image encoding, a T5 base encoder for mixing image and text tokens, and three predictors for score, heatmap, and text misalignment, respectively. The ViT B16 encoder uses a 16x16 patch size, 12 layers with 12 heads with a hidden dimension of 768, wherein the MLP has a hidden dimension of 3072. The T5 base encoder uses 12 layers with 12 heads with a hidden dimension of 768, wherein the MLP has a hidden dimension of 2048. The score predictor consists of four convolutional layers with layer norm and ReLU activation, and the filter size, kernel size, and strides are ⟦SLOT_19⟧, respectively. Three dense layers of output sizes 2048, 1024, and 1, respectively, are used to generate a scalar with ReLU activation for the first two layers and sigmoid for the last. The heatmap predictor consists of two convolution layers with filter size, kernel size, and stride as ⟦SLOT_20⟧, respectively. It then uses four de-convolution layers to up-sample to the required output size, with the filter size, kernel size, and stride as ⟦SLOT_21⟧, respectively. Each de-convolution layer is with two read-out convolution layers of kernel size 3 and stride 1. Layer norm and ReLU are used for each layer. In the end, two read-out convolution layers and a final sigmoid activation are used to generate the heatmap prediction. The text predictor is implemented using a T5 base decoder with 12 layers of 12 heads, MLP dimension 2048, and hidden dimension 768. The output token length is 64.

**ZH** 模型主要组件包括：用于图像编码的 ViT B16 编码器、用于混合图像与文本词元的 T5 base 编码器，以及分别用于评分、热力图与文本不一致的三个预测器。ViT B16 编码器采用 16×16 的块大小，12 层、12 个注意力头、隐藏维度 768，其中 MLP 隐藏维度为 3072。T5 base 编码器同样为 12 层、12 头、隐藏维度 768，MLP 隐藏维度 2048。评分预测器由四个卷积层组成，含层归一化与 ReLU 激活，卷积核数量、卷积核大小与步幅分别为 ⟦SLOT_19⟧；其后接输出大小为 2048、1024 与 1 的三个稠密层生成标量，前两层用 ReLU 激活，最后一层用 sigmoid。热力图预测器包含两个卷积层，卷积核数量、卷积核大小与步幅分别为 ⟦SLOT_20⟧；随后用四个反卷积层上采样到所需输出尺寸，卷积核数量、卷积核大小与步幅分别为 ⟦SLOT_21⟧；每个反卷积层配两个核大小 3、步幅 1 的读出卷积层，各层均使用层归一化与 ReLU。最后用两个读出卷积层与 sigmoid 激活生成热力图预测。文本预测器采用 T5 base 解码器，12 层、12 头，MLP 维度 2048，隐藏维度 768，输出词元长度为 64。

**EN** We train the model on the datasets with a batch size of 256 for 20K iterations. We utilize the AdamW optimizer with a base learning rate of 0.015. We linearly increase the learning rate from 0 to the base learning rate in the first 2000 iterations, and then decrease the learning rate with a reciprocal square root scheduler w.r.t the number of iterations. We trained the model using 64 Google Cloud TPU v3 chips.

**ZH** 模型在数据集上以批量大小 256 训练 20K 次迭代，使用 AdamW 优化器，基础学习率 0.015。前 2000 次迭代将学习率从 0 线性提升至基础学习率，之后按迭代次数的倒数平方根调度衰减。模型使用 64 块 Google Cloud TPU v3 芯片训练。

**EN** Image augmentations

**ZH** 图像增强

**EN** For each image, we randomly crop it by sampling a bounding box with 80%-100% width and 80%-100% height. The cropping is applied by 50% chance and otherwise the original image is used. Note that we also crop the corresponding part of the implausibility heatmap and misalignment heatmap to match the cropped image. We then create an augmented version of the image by applying several random augmentations including random brightness (max delta 0.05), random contrast (random contrast factor between 0.8 and 1), random hue (max delta 0.025), random saturation (random saturation factor between 0.8 and 1) and random jpeg noise (jpeg quality between 70 and 100). By 10% chance the augmented version is used instead of the original image. We convert the image to grayscale by 10% probability as the final image.

**ZH** 对每张图像，我们随机采样一个宽度和高度各为原图 80%–100% 的边界框进行裁剪；裁剪以 50% 的概率执行，否则使用原图。相应地，不合理性热力图与不一致热力图也被裁剪到与图像对应的区域。随后我们通过若干随机变换生成增强版本，包括随机亮度（最大增量 0.05）、随机对比度（对比度因子在 0.8 与 1 之间）、随机色相（最大增量 0.025）、随机饱和度（饱和度因子在 0.8 与 1 之间）与随机 JPEG 噪声（JPEG 质量在 70 与 100 之间）。增强版本以 10% 的概率替代原图。最后以 10% 的概率将图像转为灰度图。

**EN** Finetuning generative models with predicted scores

**ZH** 用预测评分微调生成模型

**EN** To generate the training prompt set, we provide five hand-crafted seed prompts as examples and then ask PaLM 2 [1] to generate similar textual prompts. We include additional instructions that specify the prompt length and the object category. We then explain why we do not use existing benchmark datasets for training. Theoretically, we can get an infinite number of prompts using the prompt synthesis technique we proposed above. Existing datasets are 1) relatively small (e.g., TIFA [24] has 4k prompts, Davidsonian Scene Graph (DSG) [10] has only 1k prompts), or 2) containing prompts that are simple and not diverse enough, for example, only measuring single objects in Parti benchmark [59]. This motivates us to synthesize a larger set of diverse prompts for training purposes. For the 100 prompts for our human evaluation, they are sampled from the existing benchmark: TIFA [24]. We only did our evaluation on 100 prompts due to the high cost of the human annotation.

**ZH** 为生成训练用提示词集，我们先提供五条手工编写的种子提示词作为示例，再让 PaLM 2 [1] 生成风格相近的文本提示，并在指令中额外指定提示词长度与物体类别。下面说明为何不使用现有基准数据集进行训练：理论上，用上述提示词合成技术可以得到无限多的提示词；而现有数据集要么 1）规模偏小（如 TIFA [24] 有 4k 条提示词，Davidsonian Scene Graph（DSG）[10] 仅 1k 条），要么 2）提示词简单、多样性不足，例如 Parti 基准 [59] 只考察单个物体。这促使我们合成规模更大、更多样的提示词用于训练。人类评估所用的 100 条提示词则从现有基准 TIFA [24] 中采样；受限于人工标注的高成本，我们只在这 100 条提示词上做评估。
