中文摘要

设计演示幻灯片这类结构化视觉内容，是沟通需求中的一项基础工作，它同时要求内容创作与视觉规划两种能力。本文着力攻克自动幻灯片生成这一挑战：模型依据自然语言（NL）指令生成幻灯片演示。我们首先提出 SlidesBench 基准——首个面向幻灯片生成的基准，含 7k 条训练示例与 585 条测试示例，派生自 10 个领域的 310 个幻灯片组。SlidesBench 支持两类评测：(i) 基于参考，用于衡量与目标幻灯片的相似度；(ii) 无参考，用于单独衡量所生成幻灯片的设计质量。我们对端到端图像生成与程序生成方法在多种模型上作了基准评测，发现程序化方法能产出质量更高、且用户可交互编辑的格式。在程序生成成功的基础上，我们创建了 AutoPresent——一个基于 Llama 的 8B 模型，在 7k 条「指令—代码」配对上训练而成，取得了可与闭源模型 GPT-4o 相媲美的结果。我们进一步探索了迭代式设计精修，即让模型对自身输出作自我精修，发现该过程能提升幻灯片质量。我们希望本工作能为未来在结构化视觉内容生成上的研究提供基础。

Abstract (原文)

Designing structured visuals such as presentation slides is essential for communicative needs, necessitating both content creation and visual planning skills. In this work, we tackle the challenge of automated slide generation, where models produce slide presentations from natural language (NL) instructions. We first introduce the SlidesBench benchmark, the first benchmark for slide generation with 7k training and 585 testing examples derived from 310 slide decks across 10 domains. SlidesBench supports evaluations that are (i)reference-based to measure similarity to a target slide, and (ii)reference-free to measure the design quality of generated slides alone. We benchmark end-to-end image generation and program generation methods with a variety of models, and find that programmatic methods produce higher-quality slides in user-interactable formats. Built on the success of program generation, we create AutoPresent, an 8B Llama-based model trained on 7k pairs of instructions paired with code for slide generation, and achieve results comparable to the closed-source model GPT-4o. We further explore iterative design refinement where the model is tasked to self-refine its own output, and we found that this process improves the slide's quality. We hope that our work will provide a basis for future work on generating structured visuals.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Designing structured visuals such as presentation slides from scratch is an essential skill for effective communication and conveying complex ideas [30]. Among various forms of visual communication, creating a compelling set of slides is a challenging problem, requiring content creation (text, pictures, diagrams, and more) and visual planning skills, to ensure the slides are well designed [25] and convey insights with clarity [3, 36]. Even human experts may need to spend hours iterating and polishing their slide decks [10] to produce high-quality designs with clear insights. While digital agents have demonstrated impressive capabilities in tasks such as software engineering [49], web navigation [54, 46], and free-form image design generation [8, 32], their creative capabilities in generating semi-structured communicative media like slide decks has not been extensively tested. Therefore, we ask: Can we employ powerful AI agents to create high-quality presentation slides that are well-structured and insight-revealing?

从零开始设计演示幻灯片这类结构化视觉内容，是有效沟通与传达复杂思想的一项必备技能 [30]。在各种视觉沟通形式中，制作一套有说服力的幻灯片是个颇具挑战的问题：它既需要内容创作（文字、图片、图表等），也需要视觉规划能力，以确保幻灯片设计得当 [25] 并清晰地传达洞见 [3, 36]。即便是人类专家，为了产出设计精良、洞见清晰的高质量作品，也可能需要花上数小时反复迭代与打磨自己的幻灯片组 [10]。尽管数字智能体已在软件工程 [49]、网页导航 [54, 46]、自由形式的图像设计生成 [8, 32] 等任务上展现出令人印象深刻的能力，它们在生成幻灯片组这类半结构化传播媒介上的创造能力尚未被充分检验。因此我们提出这样一个问题：能否借助强大的 AI 智能体，制作出结构良好、洞见彰显的高质量演示幻灯片？

In this work, we formulate the natural language (NL) to slide generation task. At a high level, the user provides the system with a natural language instruction about the desired slide, and the system then generates an editable presentation, as shown in . We consider three types of user instructions: (1) detailed instruction with images. (2) detailed instructions only. (3) high-level instructions, reflecting varying levels of design freedom.

本文形式化了自然语言（NL）到幻灯片的生成任务。在高层次上，用户向系统提供一段关于所需幻灯片的自然语言指令，系统随后生成一份可编辑的演示文稿，如图所示。我们考虑三类用户指令：(1) 带图像的详细指令；(2) 仅详细指令；(3) 高层指令，反映不同程度的设计自由度。

Since there are no existing tools for quantifying agent performance in slide generation tasks, we propose the SlidesBench benchmark (§2) as a training source and test bed for method comparisons. SlidesBench contains 7kk training examples and 585 testing examples of varied instruction difficulties, constructed from 310 publicly available slide decks from 10 different domains, including art, business, and technology. To evaluate generated slides, we introduce two sets of evaluation metrics: reference-based metrics to examine position, content, and color match against the reference slide; and reference-free metrics inspired by slide design principles [9, 34, 5, 44, 39] to measure the design quality of agent-created slides alone, given that many good designs for the same instructions may vary from the reference slide.

由于现有工具无法量化智能体在幻灯片生成任务上的性能，我们提出 SlidesBench 基准（§2），作为方法比较的训练来源与测试平台。SlidesBench 包含 7kk 条训练示例与 585 条测试示例，指令难度各异，由来自艺术、商业、科技等 10 个不同领域的 310 个公开可得的幻灯片组构建而成。为评估生成的幻灯片，我们引入两套评测指标：基于参考的指标，用于考察与参考幻灯片在位置、内容与颜色上的匹配；以及受幻灯片设计原则 [9, 34, 5, 44, 39] 启发的无参考指标，用于单独衡量智能体所生成幻灯片的设计质量——因为对同一指令而言，许多优秀设计可能与参考幻灯片并不相同。

To enable controlled and structured slide generation, we propose to create slides using program generation, where a model first generates a program from the natural language instruction, and then the program is executed to get the slide. We apply this approach to large language models (LLMs; LlaMa [11], DeepseekCoder [16], CodeLlama [33], GPT-4o [2]) and vision-language models (VLMs; LlaVa [51]). As illustrated in , given a natural language instruction, the model first generates a Python program and then executes it to obtain a PPTX slide. We find that small models such as LlaMa (8B) and LlaVa (7B) are often unable to produce executable code. While GPT-4o can produce reasonable slides, it still exhibits a substantial gap in design quality compared to human-generated slides (§5). By further conducting iterative refinement, we find that models can self-refine and further improve slide quality. We also find that code generation approaches substantially outperform end-to-end image generation methods (Stable Diffusion [32], Dall-E [8]).

为实现可控、结构化的幻灯片生成，我们提出用程序生成的方式来制作幻灯片：模型先从自然语言指令生成一个程序，再执行该程序得到幻灯片。我们把这一方法应用于大语言模型（LLM；LlaMa [11]、DeepseekCoder [16]、CodeLlama [33]、GPT-4o [2]）与视觉语言模型（VLM；LlaVa [51]）。如图所示，给定自然语言指令，模型先生成一个 Python 程序，再执行它以获得一张 PPTX 幻灯片。我们发现，LlaMa（8B）与 LlaVa（7B）等小模型常常无法产出可执行代码；GPT-4o 虽能生成尚可的幻灯片，但其设计质量与人类制作的幻灯片相比仍有明显差距（§5）。进一步开展迭代精修后，我们发现模型能够自我改进并进一步提升幻灯片质量。我们还发现，代码生成方法显著优于端到端图像生成方法（Stable Diffusion [32]、Dall-E [8]）。

To further enhance the current model’s ability to generate high-quality slides, we present our open-sourced AutoPresent (8B) model (§4.2) which is fine-tuned from LlaMa 8B on the SlidesBench training set. AutoPresent achieves state-of-the-art performance among small open-sourced models and approaches the performance of the closed-sourced model GPT-4o. Since directly generating a long program is difficult for current models [14], we further create the SlidesLib library to simplify the program generation process. SlidesLib contains high-level functions that are basic such as add_title, and image-related such as search_image and generate_image. We show that LLMs and VLMs generally perform better when given access to SlidesLib.

为进一步提升现有模型生成高质量幻灯片的能力，我们开源了 AutoPresent（8B）模型（§4.2），它在 SlidesBench 训练集上由 LlaMa 8B 微调而来。AutoPresent 在小型开源模型中取得最先进性能，并接近闭源模型 GPT-4o 的表现。由于直接生成长程序对当前模型而言较为困难 [14]，我们进一步创建了 SlidesLib 库以简化程序生成过程。SlidesLib 既包含 add_title 等基础高层函数，也包含 search_image 与 generate_image 等图像相关函数。我们表明，在获得 SlidesLib 之后，LLM 与 VLM 的表现普遍更好。

    2 SlidesBench

    2 SlidesBench

    In this section, we describe the creation of the SlidesBench benchmark. Each instance consists of a natural language instruction to create a slide, and the slide itself (in PPTX format) as a reference. SlidesBench includes three scenarios of varying difficulty levels designed to evaluate models with different user input. We describe the slide data collection (§2.1), three task setups (§2.2), and the annotation process (§2.3).

    本节描述 SlidesBench 基准的创建过程。每个实例由一条用于创建幻灯片的自然语言指令，以及该幻灯片本身（PPTX 格式）作为参考所构成。SlidesBench 包含三种难度各异的场景，旨在以不同的用户输入来评测模型。我们分别介绍幻灯片数据采集（§2.1）、三种任务设定（§2.2）与标注流程（§2.3）。

    2.1 Slides Data Collection

    2.1 幻灯片数据采集

    We search the web and collect presentation slide decks from 10 domains, including art, marketing, environment, technology, etc. To select the highest-quality slide decks from each domain, we manually go through the relevant slide decks and conduct initial processing, by checking if all its slides (i) have visually structured layouts, and (ii) extractable media such as images (if any). For the slide decks with all slides satisfying (i) and (ii) in each domain, we incorporate one slide deck into the test set, and others into the training set. This results in a total of 10 and 300 slide decks (in PPTX format) for testing and training, each containing 20 slides on average. To respect the rights of the slide creators, we do not redistribute the slides. Instead, we provide a list of URLs for the slides that we used so that others can download the slides directly from the original website. We also provide an opt-out mechanism for any creator who does not want their slides in the dataset. We provide implementation details in §A.

    我们检索网络，收集了艺术、营销、环境、科技等 10 个领域的演示幻灯片组。为从每个领域挑选出质量最高的幻灯片组，我们人工逐一检视相关幻灯片组并作初步处理，检查其全部幻灯片是否 (i) 具有视觉上结构化的布局，(ii) 含有可提取的图像等媒体（若有）。对每个领域中所有幻灯片都满足 (i) 与 (ii) 的幻灯片组，我们把其中一个纳入测试集，其余纳入训练集。由此共得到 10 个测试用与 300 个训练用幻灯片组（PPTX 格式），每组平均含 20 张幻灯片。为尊重幻灯片创作者的权益，我们不重新分发这些幻灯片，而是提供所用幻灯片的 URL 列表，使他人可直接从原始网站下载。我们也为任何不希望其幻灯片出现在数据集中的创作者提供了退出机制。实现细节见 §A。

    2.2 Three Task Setups

    2.2 三种任务设定

    We formulate the task as an NL-to-slide generation process. Given the reference slide, we curate three versions of natural language instructions, as shown in Figure 2, to represent slide generation tasks under varied difficulty levels. We introduce each setup below.

    我们把该任务形式化为一个自然语言到幻灯片的生成过程。针对参考幻灯片，我们策管出三个版本的自然语言指令，如图 2 所示，用以代表不同难度级别下的幻灯片生成任务。下面逐一介绍每种设定。

    Detailed Instructions with Images  The first and easiest setting is to provide the models with all the necessary information and assets to produce the reference slide, including text and image content, formatting and layout specifications. This setting evaluates models’ visual planning abilities, such as arranging spatial layouts, maintaining formatting consistency, balancing content proportions, and emphasizing key elements.

    带图像的详细指令 第一种、也是最简单的设定，是向模型提供复现参考幻灯片所需的全部信息与素材，包括文字与图像内容、格式与布局规格。该设定考察模型的视觉规划能力，例如安排空间布局、维持格式一致、平衡内容比例以及突出重点元素。

    Detailed Instructions Only  Since a user may not specify, or know exactly what images to put on a slide, we propose a detailed instruction only setting, where we provide the same natural language instruction provided in the detailed instruction with images setting, but replace the provided images with their natural language descriptions (e.g., “two people shaking hands”) generated by gpt-4o-mini. We then instruct the models to obtain the images using image searching or image generation tools. This setting further challenges models to interpret complex or compositional descriptions of images and obtain visuals that align with the slide context.

    仅详细指令 由于用户可能不会指定、也不一定确切知道该在幻灯片上放哪些图像，我们提出了「仅详细指令」设定：给出与带图像详细指令设定中相同的自然语言指令，但把所提供的图像替换为由 gpt-4o-mini 生成的自然语言描述（例如“两个人握手”）。随后我们指示模型通过图像检索或图像生成工具来获取图像。该设定进一步考验模型对复杂或组合式图像描述的理解，以及获取与幻灯片语境相符的视觉素材的能力。

    High-Level Instructions  In contrast to users who have a concrete target slide in mind and can spell out all detailed instructions, some users may only be able to express their needs on a high level. We thus devise a high-level instruction setting, where the natural language instructions are rather high-level and only provide a general topical idea of the slide, such as “create a title slide for Airbnb,” instead of detailing what logos and text to add and where, as exemplified in Figure 2. Models in this case need to both acquire or create content, and arrange the elements properly.

    高层指令 与那些心中已有明确目标幻灯片、并能把所有细节指令一一列出的用户不同，部分用户可能只能从较高层次表达自己的需求。因此我们设计了高层指令设定：自然语言指令相当概括，只给出幻灯片的大致主题，例如“为 Airbnb 制作一张标题页”，而不具体说明要添加哪些标志与文字、放在何处，如图 2 所示。在此情形下，模型既要获取或创作内容，也要妥善排布元素。

    2.3 Example Annotation

    2.3 示例标注

    To annotate the dataset, we collect natural language instructions paired with each slide. For each slide, we create three versions corresponding to the three setups in §2.2.

    为标注数据集，我们采集与每张幻灯片配对的自然语言指令。对每张幻灯片，我们创建对应 §2.2 中三种设定的三个版本。

    Detailed Instructions with Images  To produce detailed instruction with images, we use a scalable approach combining human-written examples and model-generated annotations. For each slide deck, we first write instructions for three example slides manually — including all necessary information (content, layout, formatting) to reproduce the slide, and providing paths to the images used in the slide (e.g., media/image_0.png), as shown in Figure 2 (top). We then use these (human-written instruction, reference slide) pairs as few-shot examples to prompt LLM (specifically, gpt-4o-mini) to generate natural language instructions for each slide in the current slide deck.** * Including the three slides with human-written instructions, to ensure instructions for all slides are consistent in style and specificity. Further, for the test set, we manually examined and refined the instructions by correcting incorrect specifications, adding missing content, and removing unnecessary or untrue content.

    带图像的详细指令 为产出带图像的详细指令，我们采用了一种结合人工撰写示例与模型生成标注的可扩展方法。对每个幻灯片组，我们先为三张示例幻灯片人工撰写指令——包含复现该幻灯片所需的全部信息（内容、布局、格式），并给出幻灯片中所用图像的路径（如 media/image_0.png），如图 2（上）所示。随后，我们把这些（人工撰写指令，参考幻灯片）对作为少样本示例，提示 LLM（具体为 gpt-4o-mini）为当前幻灯片组中的每张幻灯片生成自然语言指令。** * 包括人工撰写指令的那三张幻灯片，以确保所有幻灯片的指令在风格与具体程度上保持一致。此外，对测试集，我们人工审阅并精修了指令，纠正错误的规格说明、补充缺失内容、删除不必要或不实的内容。

    Detailed Instructions Only  To produce detailed instruction only, we replace the image paths (e.g., media/image_0.png) with the natural language descriptions of the images(‘‘an artistic, colorful background’’). These descriptions are generated by gpt-4o-mini. For the test set, we manually refine the instructions to ensure that they do not refer to unavailable image paths (e.g., removing phrases like “use the provided images”), as shown in Figure 2 (middle).

    仅详细指令 为产出仅详细指令，我们把图像路径（如 media/image_0.png）替换为图像的自然语言描述（“一幅富有艺术感、色彩丰富的背景”）。这些描述由 gpt-4o-mini 生成。对测试集，我们人工精修指令，确保它们不再指向不可用的图像路径（例如删除“使用所提供的图像”之类的措辞），如图 2（中）所示。

    High-Level Instructions  To create high-level instructions, we start with a similar approach by manually annotating three examples and then prompting the model to generate for all slides. Human-written instructions only provide a topical description of the slide and intentionally leave out specific content or layout details. This process ensures that the generated instructions remain concise and general, as shown in Figure 2 (bottom).

    高层指令 为创建高层指令，我们采用相近的做法：先人工标注三个示例，再提示模型为所有幻灯片生成指令。人工撰写的指令只给出幻灯片的主题性描述，有意略去具体内容或布局细节。该过程确保生成的指令保持简洁、概括，如图 2（下）所示。

    Overall, the instructions have an average of 115.6, 118.3, and 26.6 words under detailed instruction with images, detailed instruction only, and high-level instructions settings respectively, accompanied by an average of 1.1, 0.0, and 0.0 provided images.

    总体而言，在带图像的详细指令、仅详细指令与高层指令三种设定下，指令的平均词数分别为 115.6、118.3 与 26.6，伴随提供的图像平均数分别为 1.1、0.0 与 0.0。

    3 Evaluation Metrics

    3 评测指标

    In this section, we describe the evaluation metrics that we designed for SlidesBench. We propose two sets of evaluation metrics: reference-based metrics for measuring models’ instruction-following abilities (§3.1), and reference-free metrics to examine the design quality of model-generated content (§3.2). We also use executability to examine the success rate of each model (§3.3).

    本节描述我们为 SlidesBench 设计的评测指标。我们提出两套评测指标：用于衡量模型指令遵循能力的基于参考的指标（§3.1），以及用于检验模型生成内容设计质量的无参考指标（§3.2）。我们还用可执行性来考察每个模型的成功率（§3.3）。

    3.1 Reference-Based Metrics

    3.1 基于参考的指标

    Inspired by Design2Code [38] metrics, we implement four dimensions to examine the similarity between the model-produced slides and the reference slide.

    受 Design2Code [38] 指标的启发，我们实现了四个维度来检验模型产出的幻灯片与参考幻灯片之间的相似度。

    Element matching  For the slide layout, we measure the total sizes of matched elements (in generated and reference slides) divided by the total sizes of all element, where each textbox, image, or shape constitutes an element. More concretely, we accurately parse out each element in the generated and reference slides, and compute their maximum matching using the match library.

    元素匹配 对于幻灯片布局，我们衡量（生成幻灯片与参考幻灯片中）匹配元素的总尺寸除以所有元素的总尺寸，其中每个文本框、图像或形状都构成一个元素。更具体地，我们准确解析出生成幻灯片与参考幻灯片中的每个元素，并用 match 库计算它们的最大匹配。

    Content similarity  For each pair of matched elements, we compute their content similarity. If the reference element is text, we calculate the textual similarity using the cosine similarity of the embeddings produced with sentence-transformer with the default all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model [29]. If the reference element is an image, we calculate the CLIP score [19] of the image in two elements. We report the average content similarity across all matched element pairs, if either element contains a non-empty text string or an image component.

    内容相似度 对每一对匹配的元素，我们计算其内容相似度。若参考元素是文字，我们用 sentence-transformer 默认的 all-MiniLM-L6-v2 模型 [29] 所产生嵌入的余弦相似度来计算文本相似度；若参考元素是图像，我们计算两个元素中图像的 CLIP 分数 [19]。只要任一元素含有非空文本串或图像成分，我们就报告所有匹配元素对上的平均内容相似度。

    Color similarity  We also measure the coloring similarity using the CIEDE2000 color difference formula [26], to quantify the perceptual difference between the colors. For every matched element pair, we measure the text font color similarity and element background color (if any). We additionally measure the color similarity between the background color of generated and reference slides.

    颜色相似度 我们还用 CIEDE2000 色差公式 [26] 衡量着色相似度，以量化颜色之间的感知差异。对每一对匹配元素，我们衡量文本字体颜色的相似度与元素背景颜色（若有）的相似度；此外还衡量生成幻灯片与参考幻灯片背景颜色之间的颜色相似度。

    Position similarity  In addition to content and formatting, we also calculate the positional similarity between each pair of matched elements. More concretely, we follow Si et al. [38] to normalize the element coordinates to [0,1][0,1] by the slide page length and width. We compute the Manhattan distance between the elements and formulate positional similarity as s​i​m​(r,g)=1−m​a​x​(a​b​s​(xr−xg,yr−yg))sim(r,g)=1-max(abs(x_{r}-x_{g},y_{r}-y_{g})).

    位置相似度 除内容与格式外，我们还计算每对匹配元素之间的位置相似度。更具体地，我们遵循 Si et al. [38] 的做法，用幻灯片的页面长与宽把元素坐标归一化到 [0,1][0,1]。我们计算元素之间的曼哈顿距离，并把位置相似度表述为 s​i​m​(r,g)=1−m​a​x​(a​b​s​(xr−xg,yr−yg))sim(r,g)=1-max(abs(x_{r}-x_{g},y_{r}-y_{g}))。

    Note that a low text, color, or position similarity score could come from differences in text, color, and positions, or derivative errors caused by the inaccurate element-matching process (e.g., it may match the title box in the generated slide to a content textbox in the reference slide, which has different content or coloring requirements).

    需要注意的是，文本、颜色或位置相似度分数偏低，可能源自文本、颜色与位置本身的差异，也可能源自元素匹配过程不准确所引起的衍生误差（例如，它可能把生成幻灯片中的标题框匹配到参考幻灯片中的内容文本框，而后者在内容或配色上要求不同）。

    3.2 Reference-Free Metrics

    3.2 无参考指标

    A well-designed slide generated by models may look very different from the reference slide. Therefore, we also propose four reference-free evaluation metrics, to independently assess the design quality of model-generated slides. To establish the metrics, we surveyed a wide range of literature on slide design principles [9, 34, 5, 44, 39], and summarized four major points as below and detailed in Table 1:

    模型生成的设计精良的幻灯片，可能与参考幻灯片看上去截然不同。因此我们还提出四项无参考评测指标，用以独立评估模型生成幻灯片的设计质量。为确立这些指标，我们调研了大量关于幻灯片设计原则的文献 [9, 34, 5, 44, 39]，并总结出如下四点（详见表 1）：

    Image Use high-quality images with a reasonable proportion.

    Text  Using concise texts is important for slides to engage with the audience. An ideal slide should have a clear title, concise main content, and readable formatting.

    Image  Using appropriate visuals can engage audiences. We hence measure if models can find high-quality images and properly use them to enhance the slide quality.

    图像 使用恰当的视觉素材能够吸引受众。因此我们衡量模型能否找到高质量的图像，并妥善地用它们来提升幻灯片质量。

    Color  Vivid and consistent color use in slides can help deliver insights. We check if the slide uses high-contrast colors to facilitate visibility, and avoid high-glaring colors to discourage user engagement.

    Validation of Reference-Free Evaluation

    无参考评测的验证

    For all the metrics, we provide the image version of the slide and ask the gpt-4o model to produce a score between 0–5.     对所有指标，我们提供幻灯片的图像版本，并请 gpt-4o 模型给出 0–5 之间的分数。为检验这种基于模型的评测的可靠性，我们开展了一项人工研究，在所有真值幻灯片上比较两名人工标注者与模型评测之间的组内相关系数（ICC）。我们的检视显示四项指标上 ICC 都很高：73.8%73.8\%–85.3%85.3\%，完全落在通常被认为“高度一致”的区间内。在后续章节的实验中，我们把这些 0–5 量表的分数换算到 0–100 区间，以便在更标准的尺度上作比较。†† † 我们仍以 0–5 量表进行评测，以维持稳健、与人类对齐的评测过程。

    3.3 Executability

    3.3 可执行性

    Particularly for methods based on code generation (§4.1), we additionally measure the execution success rate to account for invalid programs. Concretely, we count the percentage of successfully executing programs generated by models among all examples. We report reference-based and free scores for executable slides only, to fairly compare their design quality. But we report ‘Overall’ scores for all slides by assigning zeros to non-executing slides, to account for execution failures. We report all metrics for successfully executing and all slides in §E.

    特别是对于基于代码生成的方法（§4.1），我们额外衡量执行成功率，以计入无效程序的情形。具体地，我们统计模型生成的程序中成功执行的程序在所有示例中所占的百分比。我们只对可执行的幻灯片报告基于参考的分数与无参考分数，以公平比较它们的设计质量；但在报告“总体”分数时，我们覆盖所有幻灯片，并对未成功执行的幻灯片赋零，以计入执行失败的情况。我们在 §E 中报告成功执行的幻灯片与所有幻灯片的全部指标。

    4.3 Iterative Refinement

    4.3 迭代精修

    Slide generation is by nature an iterative process and often requires visual-based refinements after the first draft. To enable models to refine slides as humans do, we explore an iterative refinement procedure, where the model is tasked to self-refine the slide it generated. Specifically, in the setting using SlidesLib, we provide GPT-4o (capable of consuming slide images) with the original language instruction, the program it generated in the first pass, and a snapshot of the rendered slide; the model is then asked to generate a new program based on these information to refine the slide quality by tweaking colors, spacing, and other aspects of the slide. See the prompts of this process in §D.

    幻灯片生成本质上是一个迭代过程，初稿之后往往还需要基于视觉的精修。为使模型能像人一样精修幻灯片，我们探索了一种迭代精修流程：让模型对自身生成的幻灯片作自我精修。具体在 SlidesLib 设定下，我们向 GPT-4o（能够接收幻灯片图像）提供原始语言指令、它在第一轮生成的程序，以及渲染出的幻灯片快照；然后请模型基于这些信息生成一个新程序，通过调整颜色、间距与幻灯片的其他方面来提升幻灯片质量。该过程的提示词见 §D。

    6 Related Work

    6 相关工作

    Language and Vision Model-Based Agents  Agents based on large language models (LLMs) [2, 11] and vision-language models (VLMs) [4, 51] have been widely adopted in various tasks such as web navigation [52, 54, 24], software engineering [49, 50], and web development [27, 38]. Creation of presentation materials is another common task [10] that has both similarities and differences from these more widely examined tasks.

    基于语言与视觉模型的智能体 基于大语言模型（LLM）[2, 11] 与视觉语言模型（VLM）[4, 51] 的智能体已被广泛应用于网页导航 [52, 54, 24]、软件工程 [49, 50]、网页开发 [27, 38] 等各类任务。演示材料的制作是另一项常见任务 [10]，它与这些被更广泛研究的任务既有相似之处，也有不同之处。

    Generating Programs for Vision Tasks  End-to-end image generation models such as diffusion [20, 32, 48] and GAN [15, 35] are widely used at producing scenic images, yet falling short on more structured visuals such as websites and slides [38]. Generating programs (i.e., image-editing actions) is a useful means to get structured visuals [18, 43, 41, 45, 14], including Tikz figures [6, 7], SVG [31, 37], posters [51], and user interfaces [38, 28]. However, they often require detailed inputs and are limited to specific, simple figure types, so they are still far from creating complex, editable presentation slides from scratch. Our work extends this line of research by formulating and benchmarking the natural-language-to-slide generation task.

    为视觉任务生成程序 扩散 [20, 32, 48] 与 GAN [15, 35] 等端到端图像生成模型被广泛用于生成风景图像，但在网站与幻灯片等更结构化的视觉内容上表现不足 [38]。生成程序（即图像编辑动作）是获得结构化视觉内容的有效手段 [18, 43, 41, 45, 14]，涵盖 Tikz 图形 [6, 7]、SVG [31, 37]、海报 [51] 与用户界面 [38, 28]。然而，它们往往需要详细的输入，且局限于特定、简单的图形类型，因而离从零开始创建复杂、可编辑的演示幻灯片仍很遥远。我们的工作通过形式化并对自然语言到幻灯片的生成任务作基准评测，拓展了这一研究脉络。

    Automatic Slide Generation  Previous works on slide creation mostly focus on basic extraction from provided documents [23, 22, 36, 42, 12] or having models generate content given a topic [3, 47, 1] without addressing how to organize content visually. More recently, some benchmarks [17, 53] and methods [13] have emerged that follow detailed instructions for slide editing (e.g., adjust the font size of the title from 20 to 24) of an existing slide. In contrast, we synthesize more complex and structured programs that can generate slides from scratch, including content creation, visual arrangement, and fine-grained editing, instead of refining an existing slide.

    自动幻灯片生成 以往关于幻灯片制作的工作，大多聚焦于从给定文档中作基础抽取 [23, 22, 36, 42, 12]，或让模型按给定主题生成内容 [3, 47, 1]，而未解决如何在视觉上组织内容的问题。更近期出现了一些基准 [17, 53] 与方法 [13]，它们遵循详细指令对已有幻灯片作编辑（例如把标题字号从 20 调整到 24）。相比之下，我们合成的是更复杂、更结构化的程序，能从零开始生成幻灯片，涵盖内容创作、视觉排布与细粒度编辑，而非对已有幻灯片作精修。

    7 Conclusion and Limitations

    7 结论与局限性

    In this work, we address the challenge of creating structured visuals from scratch. Specifically, we introduced SlidesBench, the first benchmark for automatic slide generation with evaluation metrics based on and free of reference slides. We benchmark multiple end-to-end image and program generation approaches, and demonstrate that AutoPresent with SlidesLib achieves comparable performance with the top GPT-4o model. Our further exploration in iterative refinement also reveals certain effectiveness in self-refinement. This work is an initial step towards automated generation of structured visuals. Specifically, it focuses on single-slide generation and produces full slide code in a single pass, without leveraging iterative design workflows. Future research could address these limitations by expanding to full slide decks, adopting gradual and interactive slide generation, and incorporating slide-specific features like animations. Further, integrating more design principles, such as optimizing for attention capture and information clarity, would be crucial for making generated slides more impactful and effective.

    本文着力解决从零开始创建结构化视觉内容这一挑战。具体地，我们提出了 SlidesBench——首个面向自动幻灯片生成的基准，配有基于参考与不依赖参考的两类评测指标。我们对多种端到端图像生成与程序生成方法作了基准评测，并证明配有 SlidesLib 的 AutoPresent 取得了可与顶级 GPT-4o 模型相媲美的性能。我们进一步对迭代精修的探索也揭示了自我精修的一定有效性。本工作是迈向结构化视觉内容自动生成的第一步：具体地说，它聚焦于单页幻灯片的生成，并在单次过程内产出完整的幻灯片代码，没有利用迭代式的设计工作流。未来研究可通过扩展到完整的幻灯片组、采用渐进且交互式的幻灯片生成、并纳入动画等幻灯片特有功能来克服这些局限。此外，融入更多设计原则（例如针对注意力捕获与信息清晰度作优化），对于让生成的幻灯片更具影响力、更有效也至关重要。
