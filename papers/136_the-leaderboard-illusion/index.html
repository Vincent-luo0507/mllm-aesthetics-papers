中文摘要

度量进步是任何科学领域发展的基础。随着基准的作用日益中心化，它们也变得更容易被扭曲。Chatbot Arena 已成为对最强 AI 系统排名的首选排行榜。然而，本工作识别出导致竞争环境被扭曲的系统性问题。我们发现，未披露的私有测试实践让少数提供方受益：他们能在公开发布前测试多个变体，并在需要时撤回分数。我们证实，这些提供方选择最佳分数的能力，会由于性能结果的选择性披露而导致 Arena 分数有偏。极端情况下，我们识别出 Meta 在 Llama-4 发布前测试了 27 个私有 LLM 变体。我们还证实，专有闭源模型的采样率（对战次数）更高、被从 arena 中移除的模型更少，而开放权重与开源模型则相反。这两项政策随时间推移造成了巨大的数据访问不对称。据估计，Google 与 OpenAI 分别获得了 arena 上全部数据的 19.2% 与 20.4%；相比之下，合计 83 个开放权重模型只获得了估计 29.7% 的数据。我们表明，访问 Chatbot Arena 数据能带来可观收益；按我们的保守估计，即便只是少量额外数据，也能在 arena 分布上带来高达 112% 的相对性能增益。这些动态共同导致了对 Arena 特有动态、而非通用模型质量的过拟合。Arena 建立在组织者与维护这一宝贵评测平台的开放社区的巨大努力之上。我们提出可操作的建议，以改革 Chatbot Arena 的评测框架，并推动本领域更公平、更透明的基准评测。

Abstract (原文)

Measuring progress is fundamental to the advancement of any scientific field. As benchmarks play an increasingly central role, they also grow more susceptible to distortion. Chatbot Arena has emerged as the go-to leaderboard for ranking the most capable AI systems. Yet, in this work we identify systematic issues that have resulted in a distorted playing field. We find that undisclosed private testing practices benefit a handful of providers who are able to test multiple variants before public release and retract scores if desired. We establish that the ability of these providers to choose the best score leads to biased Arena scores due to selective disclosure of performance results. At an extreme, we identify 27 private LLM variants tested by Meta in the lead-up to the Llama-4 release. We also establish that proprietary closed models are sampled at higher rates (number of battles) and have fewer models removed from the arena than open-weight and open-source alternatives. Both these policies lead to large data access asymmetries over time. Providers like Google and OpenAI have received an estimated 19.2% and 20.4% of all data on the arena, respectively. In contrast, a combined 83 open-weight models have only received an estimated 29.7% of the total data. We show that access to Chatbot Arena data yields substantial benefits; even limited additional data can result in relative performance gains of up to 112% on the arena distribution, based on our conservative estimates. Together, these dynamics result in overfitting to Arena-specific dynamics rather than general model quality. The Arena builds on the substantial efforts of both the organizers and an open community that maintains this valuable evaluation platform. We offer actionable recommendations to reform the Chatbot Arena's evaluation framework and promote fairer, more transparent benchmarking for the field

1 Introduction

1 引言

Any observed statistical regularity will tend to collapse once pressure is placed upon it for control purposes. — Charles A. E. Goodhart

任何被观察到的统计规律性，一旦为控制目的而对其施加压力，就往往会崩塌。——Charles A. E. Goodhart

Benchmarks have long played an integral role in the development of machine learning systems (Church 2018; Koch & Peterson 2024), from the early days of NLP (Harman 1993; Koehn & Monz 2006, e.g., TREC competitions and WMT shared tasks,) to the recent flurry of deep learning models (Deng et al. 2009, e.g., ImageNet,), to the rise of large language models (Wang et al. 2018, e.g., GLUE,). A meaningful benchmark demonstrates the relative merits of new research ideas over existing ones, and thereby heavily influences research directions, funding decisions, and, ultimately, the shape of progress in our field. The recent meteoric rise of generative AI models—in terms of public attention, commercial adoption, and the scale of compute and funding involved (Kaplan et al. 2020; Hoffmann et al. 2022; Samsi et al. 2023; Hooker 2024)—has substantially increased the stakes and pressure placed on leaderboards (Orr & Kang 2024).

基准长期以来在机器学习系统的发展中扮演着不可或缺的角色（Church 2018; Koch & Peterson 2024），从 NLP 早期（Harman 1993; Koehn & Monz 2006，例如 TREC 竞赛与 WMT 共享任务），到近年深度学习模型的涌现（Deng et al. 2009，例如 ImageNet），再到大语言模型的兴起（Wang et al. 2018，例如 GLUE）。一个有意义的基准能展示新研究思路相对于已有思路的相对优势，因而深刻影响研究方向、资助决策，并最终塑造本领域的进步形态。生成式 AI 模型近年的急剧崛起——体现在公众关注、商业采用，以及所涉算力与资金的规模上（Kaplan et al. 2020; Hoffmann et al. 2022; Samsi et al. 2023; Hooker 2024）——大幅提高了押在排行榜上的赌注与压力（Orr & Kang 2024）。

Recently, Chatbot Arena has become the de facto standard for comparing generative AI models, holding enormous sway over media11 1 https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/the-uc-berkeley-project-that-is-thewai-industrys-obsession-bc68b3e322 2 https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-18/before-deepseek-blew-up-one-website-announced-its-arrival, the AI industry (Maslej et al. 2025), and academia (Grattafiori et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2024). Created in 2023, Chatbot Arena rates LLMs by allowing anyone to submit a prompt and subsequently rank two anonymous responses from different models. Chatbot Arena quickly rose to prominence because it helped fill a critical gap in evaluation at a time of rapid technological change. As generative AI technology has grown more capable, academic multiple-choice evaluations (Hendrycks et al. 2021; Liang et al. 2022; Romanou et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024b; Wang et al. 2024; Adelani et al. 2025; Salazar et al. 2025) have failed to reflect the real-world open-ended use cases these models are now being used for (Üstün et al. 2024; Chiang et al. 2024; Mizrahi et al. 2024). As a dynamic, user-driven evaluation framework where new questions can be asked daily, Chatbot Arena can, at least in principle, evolve alongside model capabilities. Furthermore, the open-ended nature of the interaction can capture emerging real-world use cases that differ from those initially envisioned by model creators.

近来，Chatbot Arena 已成为比较生成式 AI 模型的事实标准，对媒体11 1 https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/the-uc-berkeley-project-that-is-thewai-industrys-obsession-bc68b3e322 2 https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-18/before-deepseek-blew-up-one-website-announced-its-arrival、AI 产业（Maslej et al. 2025）与学术界（Grattafiori et al. 2024; Yang et al. 2024）都握有巨大影响力。Chatbot Arena 创建于 2023 年，它通过让任何人提交提示、随后对来自不同模型的两个匿名回复排序，来对 LLM 评分。Chatbot Arena 之所以迅速崛起，是因为它在技术快速变革的时期填补了评测中的一项关键空白。随着生成式 AI 技术能力增强，学术型的多项选择评测（Hendrycks et al. 2021; Liang et al. 2022; Romanou et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024b; Wang et al. 2024; Adelani et al. 2025; Salazar et al. 2025）已无法反映这些模型如今被投入使用的真实开放式场景（Üstün et al. 2024; Chiang et al. 2024; Mizrahi et al. 2024）。作为一个动态的、用户驱动的评测框架，新问题可以每天被提出，Chatbot Arena 至少在原则上能够与模型能力同步演进。此外，交互的开放式性质能捕捉到与模型创造者最初设想不同的新兴真实用例。

However, the over-reliance on a single leaderboard creates a risk that providers may overfit to the aspects of leaderboard performance, without genuinely advancing the technology in meaningful ways (Ensmenger 2011; Thomas & Uminsky 2020; Raji et al. 2021; Bowman & Dahl 2021). As Goodhart’s Law states, when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. In this work, we show that willful engagement from a handful of providers along with preferential policies from Chatbot Arena towards the same small group have amplified the potential for gamification, in place of innovative progress.

然而，对单一排行榜的过度依赖带来一种风险：提供方可能对排行榜表现的某些方面过拟合，而没有以有意义的方式真正推进技术（Ensmenger 2011; Thomas & Uminsky 2020; Raji et al. 2021; Bowman & Dahl 2021）。正如古德哈特定律所言：当一项度量成为目标，它就不再是好的度量。在本文中，我们表明，少数提供方有意为之的参与，加上 Chatbot Arena 对同一小团体的倾斜性政策，放大了策略性博弈而非创新进步的空间。

Our systematic review of Chatbot Arena involves combining data sources encompassing 2M battles, auditing 42 providers and 243 models across a fixed time period (January 2024 - April 2025). This comprehensive analysis reveals that over an extended period, a handful of preferred providers have been granted disproportionate access to data and testing. In particular, we identify an undisclosed Chatbot Arena policy that allows a small group of preferred model providers to test many model variants in private before releasing only the best-performing checkpoint. Using Arena data, we simulate and demonstrate how this strategy distorts overall arena ratings by beneficially skewing results. In addition, we find substantial data asymmetries are apparent regarding the amount of feedback data given to proprietary vs. open-weight vs. open-source model providers. This is due to a combination of unequal sampling rates (how often a model appears in a Chatbot Arena battle) and deprecation policies (which models are retired from the arena). We show that these differences lead to distorted and unreliable Arena rankings and create conditions in which providers may be overfitting to Arena-specific dynamics rather than general model quality.     2.1 Introduction to the Arena Score

    2.1 Arena 分数简介

    The Arena score is the key metric used in Chatbot Arena to rank models based on how humans judge their performance. In Arena, users engage in pairwise comparisons, voting on which of two models performs better (or declaring a tie) in a given interaction. A naive way to compute rankings would be to average each model’s win-rates, simply counting how often they win. However, this approach does not consider the strength of the opponents and requires all models to play against each other. Furthermore, averaging win-rates treats all wins equally, which can lead to misleading or unfair rankings, especially when models face opponents of varying strength levels.

    Arena 分数是 Chatbot Arena 用来依据人类对模型表现的评价对模型排序的核心指标。在 Arena 中，用户进行成对比较，在一次交互中投票选出两个模型中表现更好的一个（或宣布平局）。一种朴素的计算排名方式是取每个模型胜率的平均，只统计它们获胜的次数。然而，这种做法没有考虑对手的强弱，并且要求所有模型两两对战过。此外，平均胜率对所有胜利一视同仁，这会导致排名具有误导性或不公平，尤其当模型面对强弱不一的对手时。

    Instead, Chatbot Arena ranks models based on their Arena Score, which is a normalized version of the Bradley-Terry (BT) model (Bradley & Terry 1952), a probabilistic framework for estimating skill levels based on pairwise comparisons. BT is a widely used model to estimate skill levels in diverse fields such as online gaming (e.g., StarCraft II) (Leitner et al. 2010; Liquipedia 2019; Wise 2021, i.a.) and sports rankings (Glickman 1999; Hunter 2004b). Compared to alternative systems like Elo (Elo 1978; Boubdir et al. 2024), which was initially used in the first version of Chatbot Arena, BT provides a more robust and statistically grounded basis for ranking. BT produces well-calibrated estimates while naturally accommodating ties and missing comparisons, and providing confidence intervals for the rankings as long as the underlying model conditions are met.

    相反，Chatbot Arena 依据 Arena 分数对模型排名，这是 Bradley-Terry（BT）模型（Bradley & Terry 1952）的一个归一化版本——BT 是一种基于成对比较估计技能水平的概率框架。BT 被广泛用于估计不同领域的技能水平，如在线游戏（例如星际争霸 II）（Leitner et al. 2010; Liquipedia 2019; Wise 2021，等）与体育排名（Glickman 1999; Hunter 2004b）。相比 Elo（Elo 1978; Boubdir et al. 2024）等替代体系——Chatbot Arena 最初版本使用的就是 Elo——BT 为排名提供了更稳健、统计上更有依据的基础。只要底层模型条件得到满足，BT 就能给出良好校准的估计，同时自然地容纳平局与缺失的比较，并为排名提供置信区间。

    While BT is considered to be a principled ranking system, its effectiveness depends on several key assumptions. Background on these assumptions will provide important context for our discussions in Section 5.1 and Section 5.2, where we examine how violations of BT’s assumptions affect the reliability of Arena rankings. The BT model assumes that pairwise comparisons are drawn from an unbiased sampling process, so that each model’s skill parameter is estimated independently and based on representative outcomes. It also does not require all players to play each other because of transitivity of rankings—if Model A is judged to beat Model B and Model B in turn beats Model C, then Model A is estimated to outperform Model C (even if they never actually play). Finally, it assumes that the comparison network is fully interconnected—that is, every model must be linked directly or indirectly through pairwise matchups. Deviations from these assumptions, such as biased comparisons or a fragmented graph, can compromise the reliability and consistency of the strength estimates. For further details, we include the mathematical formulation of the BT model in Appendix B.

    虽然 BT 被视为一个有原则的排名体系，其有效性取决于若干关键假设。这些假设的背景将为我们第 5.1 节与第 5.2 节的讨论提供重要语境——在那里我们考察违反 BT 假设会如何影响 Arena 排名的可靠性。BT 模型假定成对比较来自无偏的采样过程，从而每个模型的技能参数被独立估计、且基于有代表性的结果。它也不要求所有选手彼此对战，因为排名具有传递性——若判定模型 A 胜过模型 B，而模型 B 又胜过模型 C，则估计模型 A 优于模型 C（即便它们从未真正对战）。最后，它假定比较网络是完全连通的——即每个模型都必须通过成对对局直接或间接地相连。偏离这些假设（例如有偏的比较或碎片化的图）会损害强度估计的可靠性与一致性。更多细节见附录 B 中 BT 模型的数学表述。

    3.2 Simulated Experiments on Private Testing and Retraction

    3.2 关于私有测试与撤回的模拟实验

    Private testing coupled with the option to retract enables a best-of-N strategy, where an organization submits multiple model variants to Chatbot Arena, privately evaluates them, and retains only the top-performing variant to be publicly published on the leaderboard. In this section, we show that best-of-N submissions violate the BT unbiased sampling assumption. This systematically inflates model rankings and distorts the leaderboard ranking.

    私有测试加上可撤回的选项，使一种 best-of-N 策略成为可能：机构向 Chatbot Arena 提交多个模型变体，私下评测它们，只保留表现最好的那个变体公开发布到排行榜上。在本节中，我们表明 best-of-N 提交违反了 BT 的无偏采样假设。这会系统性地抬高模型排名，并扭曲排行榜排序。

    图 7：被测试的私有变体数量对最佳期望 Arena 分数的影响。我们模拟了一个潜在平均 Arena 分数为 1200 的模型变体族。随着我们逐步增加被测试的私有变体数量——并随后发现它们相应的 Arena 分数——选中处于性能分布高端的模型的概率也随之上升。这使提供方能有效识别出分数最高的模型。

    Unbiased Sampling Assumption. To study the selection bias scenario, assume a provider submits NN variants of a model, each variant kk having a true underlying skill parameter βk\beta_{k}, sampled from a distribution centered at some base skill level β\beta. The probability of observing an exceptionally high-performing variant increases with the number of submissions NN. Thus, the observed skill of the submitted model is: β^Best=max⁡{β^1,β^2,…,β^N}\hat{\beta}_{\text{Best}}=\max\{\hat{\beta}_{1},\hat{\beta}_{2},\dots,\hat{\beta}_{N}\}.

    无偏采样假设。为研究这一选择偏差场景，假设某提供方提交 NN 个模型变体，每个变体 kk 都有一个真实的潜在技能参数 βk\beta_{k}，采样自以某个基础技能水平 β\beta 为中心的分布。观察到表现异常出色的变体的概率随提交数量 NN 的增加而上升。因此，所提交模型被观测到的技能为：β^Best=max⁡{β^1,β^2,…,β^N}\hat{\beta}_{\text{Best}}=\max\{\hat{\beta}_{1},\hat{\beta}_{2},\dots,\hat{\beta}_{N}\}。

    Since each β^k\hat{\beta}_{k} is subject to statistical fluctuation due to finite match sampling, selecting the best variant based on observed performance introduces an upward bias. Specifically, the expected value of the best-performing variant is strictly greater than that of a regular submission:

    由于每个 β^k\hat{\beta}_{k} 都会因有限对局采样而受到统计波动的影响，依据观测表现挑选最佳变体会引入向上的偏差。具体地，表现最佳变体的期望值严格大于一次常规提交的期望值：

    𝔼⁡[β^Best]>𝔼⁡[β^k]​,∀k∈{1,2,…,N}.\mathbb{E}[\hat{\beta}_{\text{Best}}]>\mathbb{E}[\hat{\beta}_{k}]\text{,}\qquad\forall k\in\{1,2,\dots,N\}. (1)

    where the draws are non-degenerate (Var⁡(β^k)>0\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\beta}_{k})>0) and N≥2N\geq 2 (See Appendix C for further details). This violates the BT model’s assumption of unbiased sampling and alters the likelihood landscape. The reported rating no longer reflects a single, unbiased estimate of skill, but an extreme value from multiple independent estimations. As a result, the BT estimator systematically inflates the ratings of models submitted under the best-of-N strategy, distorting leaderboard rankings.

    其中抽样是非退化的（Var⁡(β^k)>0\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\beta}_{k})>0）且 N≥2N\geq 2（详见附录 C）。这违反了 BT 模型的无偏采样假设，并改变了似然曲面。所报告的评分不再是对技能的单一无偏估计，而是多次独立估计中的极值。结果，BT 估计器会系统性抬高采用 best-of-N 策略提交的模型的评分，扭曲排行榜排序。

    图 8：best-of-N 提交策略对 Arena 排行榜排名的模拟影响。模型族 A 的平均 Arena 分数低于模型族 B，但通过提交多个私有变体并挑选表现最好的那个，它可以超过模型族 B 的单一公开提交。该模拟展示了提供方如何通过私下评测若干变体、只发布得分最高的那个来获取排行榜优势。

    Role of the number of private variants. In the Chatbot Arena, we observe that while only a handful of providers know about private testing, there is also an asymmetry in the number of private models tested. At an extreme, Meta tested 27 private variants in the lead up to Llama 4 launch. To investigate how the number of private tests affects model selection outcomes, we simulated the expected lift in Arena Score as the number of private variants increased from 0 to 50. This involved generating sample battles and estimating Arena Scores for the private variants using the Bradley-Terry model. We also employed bootstrapping to estimate the confidence intervals for the corresponding Arena Scores. Figure 7 presents the results, plotting the number of private variants tested (x-axis) against the average of the maximum discovered Arena Scores across simulations (y-axis). As the number of privately tested models increases, the expected maximum Arena Score discovered rises accordingly. In our simulation, testing just 20 variants yields a notable increase of approximately 50 points in the maximum score identified, compared to the baseline scenario with no private testing. We provide more details about this simulation in Appendix I.

    私有变体数量的作用。在 Chatbot Arena 上，我们观察到，虽然只有少数提供方知道私有测试，但被测试的私有模型数量也存在不对称。极端情况下，Meta 在 Llama 4 发布前测试了 27 个私有变体。为研究私有测试次数如何影响模型选择结果，我们模拟了当私有变体数量从 0 增加到 50 时期望的 Arena 分数增益。这包括生成采样对战，并用 Bradley-Terry 模型估计各私有变体的 Arena 分数。我们还采用自助法来估计相应 Arena 分数的置信区间。图 7 给出了结果，横轴为被测试的私有变体数量，纵轴为各次模拟中发现的 Arena 分数最大值的平均。随着被私有测试的模型数量增加，被发现的最大 Arena 分数的期望也相应上升。在我们的模拟中，与不做任何私有测试的基线场景相比，仅测试 20 个变体就能使发现的最大分数显著增加约 50 分。关于该模拟的更多细节见附录 I。

    Asymmetries in which providers have access to private testing. We observe in practice that only a few preferred providers were allowed to test many variants and handpick the best result. As we show in Figure 8, restricting private testing to a subset of providers can lead to scenarios where a weaker family of models (Family A) enabled with private testing can outperform a stronger family of models (Family B), which is restricted to a single submission. Although the performance of both model families is in a similar range, Family A’s models have a lower average Arena score across all models compared to Family B’s. In contrast to model provider B, who is unaware of the best-of-N strategy, model provider A evaluates multiple models on the Chatbot Arena distribution and selects the best-performing model. This allows provider A to evaluate multiple models on the Chatbot Arena distribution and select the best-performing model, leveraging the tail of the distribution, to achieve a higher leaderboard ranking. As a result, despite having a generally stronger model pool, Family B ranks lower than Family A on the leaderboard.

    哪些提供方能使用私有测试的不对称。我们实践中观察到，只有少数受偏爱的提供方被允许测试许多变体并挑出最佳结果。如图 8 所示，把私有测试限制在部分提供方，可能造成这样的情形：启用了私有测试、但整体较弱的模型族（族 A），能够胜过被限制为单一提交的更强模型族（族 B）。虽然两个模型族的性能处于相近区间，但族 A 的模型在所有模型上的平均 Arena 分数低于族 B。与不知道 best-of-N 策略的模型提供方 B 不同，模型提供方 A 在 Chatbot Arena 分布上评测多个模型并选出表现最好的那个，从而利用分布的尾部取得更高的排行榜排名。结果，尽管族 B 的模型池总体上更强，它在排行榜上的排名却低于族 A。

    Having to pick from multiple final “candidate” models is a very common scenario during the development of LLMs. At the end of the development process of a new model, a model provider typically ends up with multiple variants, each excelling in different tasks due to variations in post-training strategies or hyperparameter settings. They often perform well on different tasks, while still being within similar overall performance bands. Selecting a final “official” model often involves a compromise across a broad range of evaluation sets. A strong signal—such as a clear lead on an arena-style leaderboard—can significantly influence this decision, tipping the preference toward specific variants that perform best under the given evaluation framework. Hence, with an “informed selection” strategy for model variants, a model provider can improve its ranking and reach the top of the leaderboard compared to when it makes an “unguided selection” to choose the variant that can be released on the public leaderboard.

    在 LLM 开发过程中，必须从多个最终「候选」模型中做挑选，这是非常常见的情形。在一个新模型开发流程的末尾，模型提供方通常会得到多个变体，由于后训练策略或超参数设置的不同，它们各在不同任务上表现突出。它们往往在不同任务上各有擅长，而整体性能仍处于相近的区间。挑选一个最终的「正式」模型，通常需要在广泛的评测集之间做出折中。一个强烈的信号——例如在 arena 式排行榜上的明显领先——会显著影响这一决策，使偏好倾向那些在给定评测框架下表现最好的特定变体。因此，与在公开排行榜上发布哪个变体时做「无引导的选择」相比，采用「有信息的选择」策略的模型提供方可以提升自己的排名并登上榜首。

    3.3 Real-world Chatbot Arena Experiment

    3.3 真实世界的 Chatbot Arena 实验

    Our simulation previously demonstrated that when model submissions are not standardized across model providers, those who submit a larger number of private model variants gain a systematic advantage, creating an uneven playing field for ranking. Since we do not have access to the final scores of private model variants observed during our Arena scrape, we design a real-world experiment to complement and validate our simulation findings. Specifically, we model two scenarios of best-of-N testing to assess the impact of submitting multiple model variants.

    我们此前的模拟表明，当模型提交在各提供方之间没有被标准化时，提交更多私有模型变体的一方会获得系统性优势，从而为排名制造了不公平的竞争环境。由于我们无法获得抓取 Arena 期间观察到的私有模型变体的最终分数，我们设计了一项真实实验来补充并验证模拟发现。具体地，我们建模了 best-of-N 测试的两种场景，以评估提交多个模型变体的影响。

    Lower bound estimate of benefits of multiple submissions. We model the most conservative case – measuring the gain from submitting and then only releasing the best Arena score obtained by two identical checkpoints. For this purpose, we submitted two identical variants of Aya-Vision-8B to the Arena in March 2025. This scenario is a very conservative lower bound of gains, as any difference in Arena score can be attributed to the benefits of multiple submissions rather than differences in model quality. We used only two checkpoints because we wanted to limit the amount of human annotator time we diverted to this exercise. We did not disclose that these were identical checkpoints to Chatbot Arena. In Figure 9, we observe notable differences in final Arena scores between identical checkpoints: 1052 (±21/22) and 1069 (±19/23). As a result, 4 models are positioned between our two identical checkpoints. This suggests that even with identical variants, it is very feasible to obtain a biased positive advantage.

    多次提交收益的下界估计。我们建模最保守的情形——衡量提交两个相同检查点、然后只发布其中获得的更高 Arena 分数所带来的增益。为此，我们于 2025 年 3 月向 Arena 提交了 Aya-Vision-8B 的两个相同变体。这一场景是增益的一个非常保守的下界，因为 Arena 分数的任何差异都可归因于多次提交的收益，而非模型质量的差异。我们只用了两个检查点，是因为希望限制这件事所占用的人类标注时间。我们没有向 Chatbot Arena 披露这是两个相同的检查点。在图 9 中，我们观察到相同检查点之间的最终 Arena 分数存在明显差异：1052（±21/22）与 1069（±19/23）。结果，有 4 个模型位于我们这两个相同检查点之间。这表明，即便使用相同的变体，获得正向的偏差优势也非常可行。

    Realistic estimate of benefits of multiple submissions. In practice, a provider would likely optimize the differences in the variants they are testing to maximize the signal from the Arena users. To study this scenario, we compare two different variants of Aya-Vision-32B. The two checkpoints are variants of the same model, with each showing slightly better performance on different subsets of benchmarks. In Figure 9, we illustrate the extreme ends of the scores obtained by the two models (1097 (±29/25) vs. 1059 (±18/23)), with 9 models falling in between both variants on the leaderboard.

    多次提交收益的现实估计。在实践中，提供方很可能会优化被测变体之间的差异，以最大化来自 Arena 用户的信号。为研究这一场景，我们比较了 Aya-Vision-32B 的两个不同变体。这两个检查点是同一模型的变体，各自在不同基准子集上表现略优。在图 9 中，我们展示了两个模型所得分数的极端两端（1097（±29/25）vs. 1059（±18/23）），排行榜上有 9 个模型落在这两个变体之间。

    4.2 Risk of Potential Overfitting

    4.2 潜在过拟合风险

    One of the questions we want to answer is “What are the implications of data asymmetries? Does having access to more data enable overfitting to the Arena?”. Overfitting refers to a phenomenon in which a model learns not only the generalizable patterns in its training data but also the specific noise or characteristics, resulting in strong performance on familiar or seen examples but degraded performance on unseen and out-of-distribution inputs.

    我们想要回答的问题之一是「数据不对称意味着什么？获得更多数据是否会导致对 Arena 过拟合？」。过拟合指的是这样一种现象：模型不仅学到了训练数据中的可泛化模式，还学到了特定的噪声或特性，从而在熟悉或见过的样本上表现很好，但在未见与分布外输入上性能下降。

    Overfitting is a particularly salient challenge in static evaluation settings, where fixed test sets are vulnerable to overfitting through repeated exposure, data contamination, or intentional tuning (Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Roberts et al. 2023; Dong et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024a). In contrast, Chatbot Arena has been widely adopted in part because it allows human users to ask any questions they want, which creates a non-static test set (Don-Yehiya et al. 2024). This means that, at least in principle, the test set is potentially harder to overfit to. However, this assumes that the distribution is constantly changing.

    过拟合在静态评测设定中是一个尤为突出的问题，因为固定的测试集容易因反复暴露、数据污染或刻意调参而被过拟合（Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Roberts et al. 2023; Dong et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024a）。相比之下，Chatbot Arena 之所以被广泛采用，部分原因是它允许人类用户提出任何问题，从而形成一个非静态的测试集（Don-Yehiya et al. 2024）。这意味着，至少在原则上，该测试集可能更难被过拟合。然而，这假定分布是持续变化的。

    To understand whether this is the case with data from Chatbot Arena, we do an exhaustive analysis and observe that the true picture on Chatbot Arena is more complex. We observe two phenomena: (1) The characteristics of prompts do shift notably over time, and (2) a non-trivial portion of prompts in one month are either exact duplicates or near-duplicates of prompts from previous months. This means that some test prompts are very similar to (or even the same as) prompts seen previously. Together, these suggest that having access to a large sample of last month’s data will enable a developer to perform significantly better on next month’s test set.

    为弄清 Chatbot Arena 的数据是否如此，我们做了详尽的分析，观察到 Chatbot Arena 的真实图景更为复杂。我们观察到两种现象：（1）提示的特征确实随时间发生显著变化；（2）某一个月中的提示有相当一部分是此前月份提示的完全重复或近似重复。这意味着某些测试提示与此前见过的提示非常相似（甚至完全相同）。两者共同表明，获得上个月数据的较大样本，将使开发者在下个月的测试集上表现得显著更好。

    Uniqueness of Arena Data. One reason providers may be motivated to explicitly optimize for Chatbot Arena distribution is if it differs substantially from other evaluation settings that providers may care about. There is sufficient signal to suggest this is the case. For a global technology provider, real-world commercial applications may differ significantly from this distribution.

    Experimental Setup: To estimate the potential for overfitting to Chatbot Arena using data from a similar distribution, we fine-tuned a language model with identical training setups that only differ in the composition of arena data used in the mixture. We construct our training dataset by sampling at different rations from two different data pools: 1) arena-mix, which consists of samples from Arena battles, and 2) other-sft-mix, a proprietary dataset which includes a variety of supervised fine-tuning datasets focusing on instruction following, multilingual tasks, math, and code. We fine-tuned a 7B base model that is used for the Cohere Command family (Cohere et al. 2025) in these experiments. We refer to the three different training mixes as 0_arena, 30_arena, 70_arena which have 0, 30%, 70% of the training dataset sampled from the arena-mix respectively. For all variants, the remainder percentage is sampled from other-sft-mix. All three models are fine-tuned for 1.3K steps using a batch size of 128. We note that our goal here is not to produce a state-of-the-art model but rather to estimate a lower bound for the performance gains that could be expected from asymmetries in access to Arena data. Hence, we do not optimize with ablations the correct weighting or data or conduct any hyperparameter sweeps.

    实验设置：为估计使用相近分布的数据对 Chatbot Arena 过拟合的可能性，我们用完全相同的训练设置微调了一个语言模型，只是混合数据中 arena 数据的构成不同。我们通过从两个不同的数据池以不同比例采样来构建训练数据集：1）arena-mix，由 Arena 对战样本组成；2）other-sft-mix，一个专有数据集，包含聚焦指令跟随、多语言任务、数学与代码的多种监督微调数据集。我们在这些实验中微调了一个用于 Cohere Command 系列（Cohere et al. 2025）的 7B 基座模型。我们把三种不同的训练混合称为 0_arena、30_arena、70_arena，其训练数据集分别有 0、30%、70% 采样自 arena-mix。对所有变体，剩余百分比从 other-sft-mix 中采样。三个模型都以批大小 128 微调 1.3K 步。我们指出，此处我们的目标不是产出一个最先进模型，而是为「Arena 数据访问不对称可带来的性能增益」估计一个下界。因此，我们没有通过消融实验去优化正确的权重或数据配比，也没有做任何超参数扫描。

    Evaluation method: Our goal is to measure the lift provided from the training on Arena data on the Arena held-out set. To do so, we measure relative improvements on the 500 English LMArena ArenaHard prompts, an in-distribution test set published by Chatbot Arena that demonstrates exceptionally high correlation (98.6%) with human preference rankings from Chatbot Arena battles (Li et al. 2024c). This dataset consists of challenging user-submitted prompts from Arena that have been carefully curated and evaluated against several criteria, including, but not limited to, domain knowledge, complexity, and problem-solving. This dataset is widely used to gauge expected performance on the Arena. To measure improvements, we simulate human preferences using LM-as-a-judge, which allows us to measure a reasonable estimate of the gains in a controlled setting. Various works have shown that this is correlated with human preferences (Dubois et al. 2023; Rafailov et al. 2023; Kim et al. 2023). We compare against Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct (Grattafiori et al. 2024) and measure win-rates using gpt-4o-2024-11-20 as our judge model2424 24 https://platform.openai.com/docs/models/gpt-4o.

    评测方法：我们的目标是衡量在 Arena 数据上训练相对于 Arena 留出集所带来的增益。为此，我们测量在 500 条英文 LMArena ArenaHard 提示上的相对改进——这是 Chatbot Arena 发布的一个同分布测试集，与 Chatbot Arena 对战中的人类偏好排序表现出极高（98.6%）的相关性（Li et al. 2024c）。该数据集由 Arena 上具有挑战性的用户提交提示构成，经过精心筛选并依据多项判据评估，包括但不限于领域知识、复杂度与问题解决能力。该数据集被广泛用来估计模型在 Arena 上的预期表现。为衡量改进，我们用「以语言模型充当裁判」来模拟人类偏好，从而在受控设定下合理估计增益。多项工作表明这与人类偏好相关（Dubois et al. 2023; Rafailov et al. 2023; Kim et al. 2023）。我们与 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct（Grattafiori et al. 2024）比较，并用 gpt-4o-2024-11-20 作为裁判模型测量胜率2424 24 https://platform.openai.com/docs/models/gpt-4o。

    Results: From Figure 10, we observe that as the amount of arena-mix data increases, the models improve in their evaluation on the ArenaHard prompts. Variant 0_arena scores a win-rate of 23.5%, 30_arena scores a win-rate of 42.7%, and variant 70_arena, which is trained on the most arena-mix, data scores a win-rate of 49.9% against Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.     We also note that often it is not necessary to train explicitly on the data to gain from data access. For example, providers may use the composition of the data to make decisions about weighting different data sources (Üstün et al. 2024), or may use a small subset of the data to create high-quality synthetic data that is close to the original distribution (Odumakinde et al. 2024; Shimabucoro et al. 2024; Dang et al. 2024a; Aakanksha et al. 2024). Given the stakes behind ranking highly on Chatbot Arena, it is likely that the data at a provider’s disposal from Chatbot Arena is being actively leveraged by different providers to gain an advantage.

    我们还注意到，往往并不需要显式地在数据上训练才能从数据访问中获益。例如，提供方可以利用数据的构成来决定不同数据源的权重（Üstün et al. 2024），或取一小部分数据来生成接近原始分布的高质量合成数据（Odumakinde et al. 2024; Shimabucoro et al. 2024; Dang et al. 2024a; Aakanksha et al. 2024）。鉴于在 Chatbot Arena 上取得高排名的利害关系，提供方手头来自 Chatbot Arena 的数据很可能正被不同提供方积极利用以获取优势。

    While deprecating older models is necessary to maintain a dynamic leaderboard, especially as new models are introduced regularly, we question how excessive deprecation may compromise the reliability and stability of model rankings. When models are deprecated, future models that enter the Arena will not have direct comparisons with them. Intuitively, transitivity means that if model A is better than model B, and model B is better than model C, then model A should also be better than model C. Transitivity allows the BT model to infer missing outcomes—if two models share common opponents, their relative ranking can be deduced even without a direct comparison (Bradley & Terry 1952).     Transitivity is a critical property that allows for inferring rankings using fewer data points. However, transitivity requires several underlying properties to hold. In particular, two of these properties are salient for our study of the impact of deprecation on reliability:

    传递性是一项关键性质，它使得用更少的数据点推断排名成为可能。然而，传递性要求若干底层性质成立。其中有两项性质对我们研究下架对可靠性的影响尤为突出：

    Assumption 1: Evaluation conditions remain constant. Transitivity relies on the assumption that evaluation conditions remain constant—that is, paired comparisons must come from a consistent set of tasks or contexts. For example, consider a setting where Tom wins against Susan in chess, and Susan wins against Ronald in both chess and tennis. We can infer Tom’s ranking relative to Ronald in chess, but not in tennis. In practice, Chatbot Arena’s prompt categories and task types evolve over time, and some models become deprecated, meaning they are no longer re-evaluated under the current conditions. We show in Section 5.1 that when a model’s battle history is limited to outdated conditions, its comparisons no longer accurately reflect performance in the new context.

    • • 假设 1：评测条件保持不变。传递性依赖于「评测条件保持不变」这一假设——即成对比较必须来自一致的任务或语境集合。例如，考虑这样一个设定：Tom 在国际象棋中战胜 Susan，而 Susan 在国际象棋和网球中都战胜 Ronald。我们可以推断 Tom 相对于 Ronald 在国际象棋上的排名，但在网球上不行。在实践中，Chatbot Arena 的提示类别与任务类型随时间演化，且一些模型被下架，意味着它们不再在当前条件下被重新评测。我们在第 5.1 节表明，当一个模型的对战历史局限于过时的条件时，它的比较结果就不再准确反映其在新语境下的表现。

    Assumption 2: Network of comparisons must be fully interconnected. Every model must be linked directly or indirectly through pairwise matchups. So in a chess example, we can estimate Tom’s performance against Ronald because they have each played at least one competitor in common (in this case, Susan). If Ronald has had no matches with anyone who has played Tom, we can no longer infer reliably what their rankings are relative to each other.     5.1 Transitivity Under Changing Evaluation Conditions

    5.1 评测条件变化下的传递性

If all models were continuously sampled across all points in time, the BT model would likely remain robust because every model would be evaluated on the evolving distribution of tasks. However, as shown in Figure 13, many models are deprecated over time, and their scores stop getting updated.

    5.2 Sparse Battle History Risks

    5.2 稀疏对战历史的风险

    In this section, we show that the deprecation policy can lead to a sparse matrix and disconnected comparison graphs, which in turn distort the resulting rankings. As demonstrated by Ford Jr 1957, the maximum likelihood estimate does not exist if models can be partitioned into two non-empty subsets without comparisons between them or if all comparisons between the two groups are one-sided (i.e., one group always wins). Therefore, to ensure a unique and finite estimation, the comparison graph must be connected. For any possible partition of models, there must be at least one win going in each direction across the partition. This ensures that no subset of models is entirely isolated in the win/loss structure. The Chatbot Arena comparison matrix can potentially become disconnected because of the extremely high levels of model removals over time (as discussed in Figure 13).

    在本节中，我们表明下架政策会导致稀疏矩阵与不连通的比较图，进而扭曲所得排名。正如 Ford Jr 1957 所证明的，如果模型可以被划分为两个非空子集且二者之间没有比较，或者两组之间的所有比较都是单向的（即一组总是获胜），那么极大似然估计不存在。因此，为保证估计唯一且有限，比较图必须是连通的。对模型的任意一种划分，都必须至少各有一个方向的胜利跨越该划分。这确保没有哪个模型子集在胜负结构中是完全孤立的。Chatbot Arena 的比较矩阵可能因极高的模型移除率而变得不连通（如图 13 所述）。

    6 Recommendations and Guidelines for Improving Leaderboards

    6 改进排行榜的建议与准则

    When my information changes, I alter my conclusions. What do you do, sir? — John Maynard Keynes

    当我的信息改变时，我会改变我的结论。先生，您会怎么做？——约翰·梅纳德·凯恩斯

    We include below recommendations that we believe are urgent and critical. However, we also believe these recommendations are very achievable and will restore scientific credibility and trust within the wider research ecosystem:

    我们在下面列出我们认为紧迫且关键的建议。同时我们也相信，这些建议是切实可行的，并将在更广泛的研究生态中恢复科学可信度与信任：

    Prohibit score retraction after submission. Currently, providers who have engaged in private testing are allowed to retract submissions and only submit the best variant to the public leaderboard. As we have shown in Section 4.1, this can lead to overfitting and obscures meaningful progress as it makes it difficult to distinguish between models that have legitimately improved versus those that have exploited statistical shortcuts (Ying 2019). We urge Chatbot Arena to prohibit retraction after submission, ensuring all tested variants’ scores are permanently visible on the leaderboard. Providers should also disclose the total number of private variants tested prior to public launch, including historical submissions, to contextualize their results.

    禁止提交后撤回分数。目前，参与了私有测试的提供方被允许撤回提交，只把最好的变体提交到公开排行榜。如第 4.1 节所示，这会导致过拟合，并掩盖有意义的进步，因为它使「真正改进的模型」与「利用了统计捷径的模型」难以区分（Ying 2019）。我们敦促 Chatbot Arena 禁止提交后撤回，确保所有被测试变体的分数在排行榜上永久可见。提供方还应披露公开发布前测试的私有变体总数，包括历史提交，以便为他们的结果提供语境。

    Establish transparent limits on the number of private variants per provider. As illustrated in Figure 6, private testing volume varies widely across providers, creating unfair advantages. To curb overfitting and level the playing field, Chatbot Arena should enforce a strict cap of private variants per provider for any given model launch. This should be enforced at a provider level, and not per model type and size as that is impossible to audit with API hosting. This strict limit should be disclosed to all providers (proprietary, open-weights, open-source) and to the wider Chatbot Arena community. This restriction would discourage excessive undisclosed testing while still allowing limited iteration.

    为每个提供方的私有变体数量设定透明上限。如图 6 所示，各提供方的私有测试量差异极大，造成了不公平的优势。为抑制过拟合、拉平竞争环境，Chatbot Arena 应对任何一次模型发布中每个提供方的私有变体数量施加上限。这一上限应在提供方层面执行，而不是按模型类型与规模执行，因为在 API 托管的情形下后者无法审计。这一上限应向所有提供方（专有、开放权重、开源）以及更广泛的 Chatbot Arena 社区公开。该限制既能抑制过度的未披露测试，又仍允许有限的迭代。

    Establish clear and auditable model deprecation criteria. The current criteria, which states “Models may be retired after 3000 votes if there are two more recent models in the same series and/or if there are more than 3 providers that offer models cheaper or same price and strictly better (according to overall Arena score) than this model” is ambiguous and make it impossible to audit the logic in practice. Key terms like “same series” and “more recent” lack formal definitions, making it unclear how to determine whether a model is eligible for retirement based on lineage. Additionally, the requirement that “more than 3 providers” must offer strictly better and cheaper models introduces confusion around what threshold of improvement in Arena Score is considered meaningful. The use of “and/or” further complicates interpretation, as it’s unclear whether meeting one condition is sufficient or if both must be satisfied. Lastly, using price as a filtering criterion is problematic since it’s subject to change, varies across hosting platforms, and is not inherently tied to a model’s performance or utility. We note that many of these models are hosted for free on the Arena, and so there should be clarity about what source of pricing is being used. This lack of precision makes it challenging to apply the rule consistently or verify retirement decisions.

    建立清晰且可审计的模型下架标准。当前标准表述为「模型在获得 3000 票后可能被退役，如果同系列中有两个更新的模型，和/或如果有超过 3 家提供方提供更便宜或同价且（按整体 Arena 分数）明显更好的模型」，这一表述含糊不清，使该逻辑在实践中无法审计。「同系列」「更新」等关键术语缺乏正式定义，导致不清楚如何依据谱系判断一个模型是否符合退役条件。此外，「超过 3 家提供方」必须提供更优且更便宜的模型这一要求，也带来了困惑：究竟 Arena 分数提升多少才算有意义。「和/或」的使用进一步加剧了解释上的困难——不清楚满足其中一个条件是否足够，还是两个条件都必须满足。最后，用价格作为筛选标准是有问题的，因为价格会变动、在不同托管平台间不同，并且与模型的性能或效用没有内在关联。我们注意到这些模型中有许多在 Arena 上是免费托管的，因此应当明确所使用的是哪种价格来源。这种精确性的缺失使该规则难以被一致地适用，也难以验证退役决策。

    We recommend a stratified approach that retires models proportionally across proprietary, open-weight, and open-source categories to preserve balance and fairness in the evaluation based on two criteria: availability and performance. We propose retiring the bottom 30th percentile within each category of open-source, open-weight, and proprietary after rankings converge. This stratified pruning prevents provider-type bias, keeps strong models from underrepresented groups visible, and maintains comparison graph connectivity. It also reduces ranking inconsistencies seen with uneven retirement, as discussed in Section 5.

    我们建议采取一种分层方法，在专有、开放权重与开源三个类别中按比例退役模型，以在基于可用性与性能两项判据的评测中保持平衡与公平。我们提议在排名收敛后，在开源、开放权重与专有各类别中退役末位 30% 的模型。这种分层剪枝可避免提供方类型偏见，让来自代表性不足群体的强模型保持可见，并维持比较图的连通性。它还能减少第 5 节所讨论的、因退役不均造成的排名不一致。

    This formulation effectively prioritizes under-evaluated and high-variance pairs, aligning sampling with the goal of rapidly reducing uncertainty in rankings. While this sampling rule is clearly articulated in the paper, we have not seen evidence of its deployment in the current leaderboard. We recommend adopting this sampling strategy in practice and providing periodic reporting on its usage. Doing so would align the platform’s operations with its methodological innovations, support more balanced and transparent evaluations, and improve confidence in leaderboard dynamics over time.

    这一公式有效地优先选择评测不足与高方差的模型对，使采样与「快速降低排名不确定性」的目标相一致。虽然该采样规则在论文中被清晰阐述，但我们没有在当前排行榜上看到它被部署的证据。我们建议在实践中采用这一采样策略，并对其使用情况进行定期报告。这样做将使平台的运营与其方法创新保持一致，支持更均衡、更透明的评测，并随时间提升对排行榜动态的信心。

    Provide public transparency into all tested models, deprecations, and sampling rates. Most of these findings were only possible through access to private model testing, or crawling Chatbot Arena over a period of time. Providing transparency into the full suite of models that were tested, deprecated, and how often they were sampled against which other models, would enable the oversight and trust in the benchmark that Chatbot Arena affirms in their policies. This transparency could be provided on a rolling basis (e.g., every quarter, for the prior quarter of model battles). It would enable the community to help in the process of continuing to improve this community benchmark. For instance, the backend codebase of Chatbot Arena– FastChat – publicly lists deprecated models on GitHub25, where 47 models are explicitly marked as deprecated. However, four times that number have been silently deprecated without warning. We recommend Chatbot Arena expand the definition of “deprecated” to include models that are no longer being regularly sampled from, and list these deprecated models on their website to make it transparent for everyone which models are no longer active on Chatbot Arena.

    为所有被测试的模型、下架情况与采样率提供公开透明度。这些发现大多只能通过访问私有模型测试，或持续爬取 Chatbot Arena 一段时间才得以获得。若能对被测试、被下架的全部模型，以及它们与哪些其他模型对战、被采样的频率提供透明度，将有助于实现 Chatbot Arena 在其政策中所宣称的对该基准的监督与信任。这种透明度可以按滚动方式提供（例如每季度公布上一季度的模型对战情况）。它将使社区得以参与持续改进这一社区基准的过程。例如，Chatbot Arena 的后端代码库 FastChat 在 GitHub 上公开列出了已下架的模型25，其中 47 个模型被明确标记为已下架。然而，有四倍于此数量的模型在毫无预警的情况下被静默下架。我们建议 Chatbot Arena 扩大「已下架」的定义，把那些不再被定期采样的模型也纳入其中，并在其网站上列出这些已下架模型，让所有人都能清楚看到哪些模型在 Chatbot Arena 上已不再活跃。

    7 Limitations

    7 局限性

    We do not have insight into Chatbot Arena’s raw data: A subset of the data sources utilized for this study have undergone pre-processing by Chatbot Arena, which as stated by Chatbot Arena often involves de-duplication, removal of battles corresponding to suspicious voting patterns, etc (Chiang et al. 2024). Through this work, we also establish that private battles are removed from the datasets released by Chatbot Arena. Although we also have proprietary API data for the models we test on the Arena, it only reveals a subset of battles. Without access to original and comprehensive raw data, it is hard to investigate patterns related to adversarial voting where users intentionally submit votes to manipulate rankings or undermine the system. Various previous works have shown that adversarial voting is a critical concern for the reliability of any crowd-sourced evaluation platform like Chatbot Arena (Huang et al. 2025) (Min et al. 2025). We do not explore this in this work, but see more investigation here as an important topic for future work.

    我们无法看到 Chatbot Arena 的原始数据：本研究使用的部分数据源经过了 Chatbot Arena 的预处理，据 Chatbot Arena 说明，这通常包括去重、移除对应可疑投票模式的对战等（Chiang et al. 2024）。通过本工作我们还证实，私有对战会从 Chatbot Arena 发布的数据集中被移除。尽管我们也拥有我们在 Arena 上测试的模型的专有 API 数据，但它只揭示了一部分对战。在无法获得原始且完整的数据的情况下，很难研究与对抗性投票相关的模式——即用户故意提交投票以操纵排名或破坏系统。多项已有研究表明，对抗性投票是 Chatbot Arena 这类众包评测平台可靠性的关键隐患（Huang et al. 2025）（Min et al. 2025）。本文不对此展开探讨，但认为这方面的进一步研究是未来工作的重要课题。

    Our scraped data snapshot only covers a limited period: Our scraped-random-sample was the only way to identify private variants being tested by various providers. However, it covers a limited time period from January–March, 2025. This time frame coincided with Meta’s launch of Llama 4, and so we find them to be the provider with the highest number of private variants in our analysis. We believe we might be underestimating the counts for providers having fewer model launches during this period.

    我们抓取的数据快照只覆盖有限的时段：我们的 scraped-random-sample 是识别各提供方正在测试的私有变体的唯一途径。但它只覆盖 2025 年 1—3 月这一有限时段。这一时间窗口恰好与 Meta 发布 Llama 4 重合，因此我们发现它是我们分析中私有变体数量最多的提供方。我们认为，对于在该时段内模型发布较少的提供方，我们可能低估了其数量。

    Our training experiments likely underestimate the potential to overfit: Our estimate of overfitting is likely conservative, as it is based on training with only a fraction of the data believed to be available to some proprietary model providers. This disparity suggests that proprietary models may be trained on 5 to 10 times more data than we use, potentially increasing the risk of overfitting to patterns not present in our smaller subset. These observations underscore the importance of further examining data scale and its implications for model overfitting to certain leaderboards.

    我们的训练实验可能低估了过拟合的可能性：我们对过拟合的估计很可能是保守的，因为它是基于只用「据信某些专有模型提供方所能获得数据」的一小部分来训练得出的。这一差距意味着专有模型训练所用的数据可能是我们的 5 到 10 倍，从而可能提升对我们更小子集中所不存在的模式过拟合的风险。这些观察凸显了进一步考察数据规模及其对模型针对特定排行榜过拟合之影响的重要性。

    We rely on the model’s self-identification to attribute private models to their respective providers: Since the identity of anonymous models is not publicly disclosed, we use model self-identification as a proxy—prompting each model directly and observing how it responds. When a model consistently names a particular provider across multiple prompts, we attribute it to that provider. While this method provides a reasonable signal, it is inherently approximate. Due to limited data and the potential for models to respond inconsistently or ambiguously, some misattributions may occur. To encourage validation of our estimates, we include the codename and our estimate of identity in Section E.4. We welcome correspondence with the authors from providers if any of the estimates of ownership are incorrect.

    我们依赖模型的自我识别来把私有模型归属到各自提供方：由于匿名模型的身份未被公开披露，我们使用模型的自我识别作为代理——直接提示每个模型并观察它如何回答。当某个模型在多次提示中一致地自称属于某提供方时，我们就把它归属给该提供方。虽然这一方法提供了合理的信号，但它本质上是近似的。由于数据有限，且模型的回答可能不一致或含糊，可能会出现一些误判。为鼓励对我们的估计进行验证，我们在第 E.4 节中列出了代号与我们对身份的估计。若任何所有权估计有误，我们欢迎各提供方的作者来信指正。

    8.1 Meta-studies on the Rigor of Benchmarking in AI

    8.1 关于 AI 基准严谨性的元研究

    Our work contributes to a wider body of work examining the role of benchmarks in determining progress in machine learning. Benchmarking has played a central role in shaping research priorities and incentives within the deep learning community (Koch & Peterson 2024). Research has found that benchmarks are rarely impartial and instead shaped by the environments in which the benchmarks are made, finding that assumptions, commitments, and dependencies can often have large implications in final outcomes (Aniba et al. 2010; Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020). Creating a meaningful and reliable benchmark is challenging, and there has been critical work identifying key benchmark desiderata and open challenges.

    我们的工作为「考察基准在决定机器学习进步中作用」的更广泛研究做出贡献。基准化在塑造深度学习社区的研究优先级与激励方面一直处于中心位置（Koch & Peterson 2024）。研究发现，基准很少是中立的，而是被其创建环境所塑造——假设、承诺与依赖关系往往对最终结果有重大影响（Aniba et al. 2010; Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020）。创建一个有意义且可靠的基准极具挑战，已有批判性工作指出了关键的基准期望属性与开放挑战。

    Propensity for overfitting. Static task-based leaderboards, such as Hugging Face’s Open LLM Leaderboard (Fourrier et al. 2024; Gao et al. 2024) and OpenCompass (Contributors 2023), aim to evaluate models across a broad range of skills but are often susceptible to data contamination and implicit overfitting (Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Roberts et al. 2023; Dong et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024a; Longpre et al. 2024a). Prior works (Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Yang et al. 2023) have proposed various methods for detecting contamination, while Dong et al. 2024 discusses how such contamination impedes the ability to distinguish true generalization, ultimately hindering progress. Although dynamic, live benchmarks like Chatbot Arena significantly reduce the risk of overfitting, we report in this paper that certain practices—such as multiple submissions during the anonymous testing period and best-of-N submissions—tend to favor large, proprietary players with disproportionate access to data. As a result, model development may be deliberately optimized for performance on Chatbot Arena.

    过拟合倾向。Hugging Face 的 Open LLM Leaderboard（Fourrier et al. 2024; Gao et al. 2024）与 OpenCompass（Contributors 2023）等静态任务型排行榜旨在跨广泛技能评测模型，但常常容易受到数据污染与隐性过拟合的影响（Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Roberts et al. 2023; Dong et al. 2024; Singh et al. 2024a; Longpre et al. 2024a）。已有工作（Deng et al. 2024; Golchin & Surdeanu 2024; Yang et al. 2023）提出了多种污染检测方法，而 Dong et al. 2024 讨论了此类污染如何阻碍区分真正的泛化，最终阻碍进步。虽然 Chatbot Arena 这类动态的、实时的基准显著降低了过拟合风险，我们在本文中报告指出，某些做法——例如在匿名测试期内多次提交与 best-of-N 提交——往往有利于拥有不成比例数据访问权的大型专有参与者。结果，模型开发可能被刻意针对 Chatbot Arena 的表现做优化。

    Lack of standardization across benchmarks. The lack of standardization in benchmarks complicates meaningful comparisons due to inconsistent metrics and task definitions. Ethayarajh & Jurafsky 2020 critique NLP leaderboards for prioritizing accuracy over dimensions like model compactness and fairness. Similarly, Ruder 2021 highlights that benchmarks such as SuperGLUE (Sarlin et al. 2020) are quickly saturated, with models reaching superhuman performance while still failing in real-world scenarios, underscoring the need for dynamic and standardized evaluation. This inconsistency risks misleading practitioners, as echoed in recent critiques (Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020; Reuel et al. 2024).

    跨基准缺乏标准化。基准缺乏标准化使有意义的比较变得复杂，原因是度量与任务定义不一致。Ethayarajh & Jurafsky 2020 批评 NLP 排行榜把准确率置于模型紧凑性与公平性等维度之上。类似地，Ruder 2021 指出 SuperGLUE（Sarlin et al. 2020）等基准很快饱和，模型达到超人性能却在真实场景中仍然失败，凸显了对动态与标准化评测的需求。这种不一致有可能误导实践者，近期批评（Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020; Reuel et al. 2024）也呼应了这一点。

    Quality of data and limited reproducibility. A recent study by Vendrow et al. 2025 revealed widespread label errors that compromise evaluation reliability, showing that even frontier LLMs can struggle with seemingly simple tasks. Similarly, Digan et al. 2021 identified reproducibility challenges arising from complex data streams, which affect result consistency. Related work (Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020; Longpre et al. 2023; Reuel et al. 2024; Albalak et al. 2024) further emphasizes that poor data quality and limited reproducibility can lead to unreliable evaluations and undermine scientific credibility.

    数据质量与可复现性有限。Vendrow et al. 2025 的一项近期研究揭示了普遍存在的标签错误损害评测可靠性，表明即便是最前沿的 LLM 也可能在看似简单的任务上表现不佳。类似地，Digan et al. 2021 指出了由复杂数据流带来的可复现性挑战，影响结果的一致性。相关工作（Bartz-Beielstein et al. 2020; Longpre et al. 2023; Reuel et al. 2024; Albalak et al. 2024）进一步强调，糟糕的数据质量与有限的可复现性会导致评测不可靠，并损害科学可信度。

    Favored benchmarks may not capture performance in the real world. Commonly used benchmarks often fail to capture real-world performance, creating a gap between test scores and practical utility due to their tendency to overlook the dynamic and complex nature of real-world tasks. Recent studies (Ott et al. 2022; Parli 2022) highlight this disconnect, observing that models frequently excel on benchmarks while underperforming in practical applications, especially as benchmarks quickly reach saturation.

    受偏爱的基准可能无法捕捉真实世界的性能。常用基准往往无法捕捉真实世界的表现，由于其倾向于忽视真实世界任务的动态性与复杂性，造成测试分数与实际效用之间的鸿沟。近期研究（Ott et al. 2022; Parli 2022）强调了这一脱节，观察到模型常常在基准上表现出色，却在真实应用中表现不佳，尤其当基准很快达到饱和时。

    8.2 Human Voting-based Benchmarks

    8.2 基于人类投票的基准

    Wider studies on the role and benefits of human voting-based benchmarks. Chatbot Arena is an example of a human voting-based benchmark. Human judgment has long been regarded as the gold standard for evaluating the quality of model-generated outputs. These models should ultimately align with human values, and certain nuanced qualities, such as coherence, harmlessness, and readability, are best assessed by humans (Van Der Lee et al. 2019; Boubdir et al. 2023). Platforms like Chatbot Arena (Chiang et al. 2024), Talk Arena (Li et al. 2024a), and Game Arena (Hu et al. 2024), Aya UI Interface (Singh et al. 2024c) effectively use crowdsourcing to gather large volumes of real-world user prompts and feedback. Many opt for Elo-like or BT-style rankings to rank models. Moreover, collecting human preference data has also proven invaluable for alignment techniques like Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) (Christiano et al. 2017; Ouyang et al. 2022; Ahmadian et al. 2024; Dang et al. 2024a), which helps fine-tune models to generate more natural and human-preferred responses. Human voting has been shown to mitigate some of the biases associated with using LLM-as-a-judge approaches, which, while improving evaluation efficiency, may raise concerns about robustness (Raina et al. 2024) and introduce various forms of bias (Koo et al. 2023; Shimabucoro et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2024; Zheng et al. 2024). Furthermore, live leaderboards offer several advantages over static task benchmarks, including a lower risk of data contamination and greater adaptability to evolving evaluation needs.

    关于基于人类投票的基准之作用与价值的更广泛研究。Chatbot Arena 是基于人类投票的基准的一个实例。人类判断长期以来被视为评价模型生成输出质量的金标准。这些模型最终应当与人类价值观一致，而某些细微品质——如连贯性、无害性与可读性——最好由人类评估（Van Der Lee et al. 2019; Boubdir et al. 2023）。Chatbot Arena（Chiang et al. 2024）、Talk Arena（Li et al. 2024a）、Game Arena（Hu et al. 2024）与 Aya UI Interface（Singh et al. 2024c）等平台有效地利用众包收集大量真实世界的用户提示与反馈。许多平台选择类 Elo 或 BT 式的排名来对模型排序。此外，收集人类偏好数据也被证明对 RLHF（Christiano et al. 2017; Ouyang et al. 2022; Ahmadian et al. 2024; Dang et al. 2024a）等对齐技术极有价值，有助于微调模型以生成更自然、更符合人类偏好的回复。人类投票已被证明能缓解使用「LLM 即裁判」方法所带来的某些偏见——后者虽提升了评测效率，却可能引发对稳健性的担忧（Raina et al. 2024）并引入各种形式的偏见（Koo et al. 2023; Shimabucoro et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2024; Zheng et al. 2024）。此外，实时排行榜相较静态任务基准有若干优势，包括更低的数据污染风险与对演化评测需求更强的适应性。

    Critiques of Human-Voting Based Benchmarks. Voting-based live benchmarks like Chatbot Arena also face evaluation challenges not addressed in this paper. Chatbot Arena (Chiang et al. 2024) has made substantial efforts to ensure reliability and security, including malicious user detection, bot protection via Google reCAPTCHA v3, vote limits per IP address, prompt de-duplication, and other safeguards6. Nonetheless, recent work has focused on auditing the reliability of human-voting-based live leaderboards. For instance, studies have demonstrated that such leaderboards are vulnerable to low-cost manipulation, with adversarial users able to de-anonymize model responses and carry out targeted voting attacks (Huang et al. 2025). Additionally, Zhao et al. 2024; Min et al. 2025 suggest that Chatbot Arena rankings can be artificially inflated through various adversarial voting strategies. These vulnerabilities raise concerns about the overall trustworthiness of Chatbot Arena. While our study does not explicitly investigate adversarial voting, we note that Chatbot Arena ’s policy of informing model providers when testing begins and disclosing model aliases may create conditions conducive to leaderboard manipulation.

    对基于人类投票的基准的批评。Chatbot Arena 这类基于投票的实时基准还面临本文未涉及的评测挑战。Chatbot Arena（Chiang et al. 2024）在保障可靠性与安全性方面做了大量工作，包括恶意用户检测、通过 Google reCAPTCHA v3 的机器人防护、按 IP 地址的投票限制、提示去重以及其他防护措施6。尽管如此，近期工作聚焦于审计基于人类投票的实时排行榜的可靠性。例如，有研究表明这类排行榜易受低成本操纵，对抗性用户能够去匿名化模型回复并实施定向投票攻击（Huang et al. 2025）。此外，Zhao et al. 2024; Min et al. 2025 指出，Chatbot Arena 的排名可以通过各种对抗性投票策略被人为抬高。这些漏洞引发了对 Chatbot Arena 整体可信度的担忧。虽然本研究没有专门考察对抗性投票，但我们指出，Chatbot Arena 在测试开始时告知模型提供方、并披露模型别名的政策，可能创造出有利于排行榜操纵的条件。

    9 Conclusion

    9 结论

    It is far easier to point out issues with the Arena than the huge amount of work that went into building it. While our work is motivated by the need to maintain scientific integrity in AI progress, we believe it is important to note the huge amount of work involved for a small group of organizers to build a hugely popular community benchmark. Their efforts have democratized access to many models and enabled a large and varied user base to weigh in on what matters in the real world for model selection. Hence, while we point out systematic issues with Chatbot Arena as it is now, we also acknowledge that many of these issues may have gradually emerged as the leaderboard took on outsized importance in visibility to providers.

    指出 Arena 存在的问题，远比建设它所投入的巨大工作量要容易得多。虽然我们的工作出于维护 AI 进步中科学诚信的需要，我们认为有必要指出：由一小群组织者建立起一个极受欢迎的社区基准，其中蕴含着巨大的工作量。他们的努力使许多模型的获取变得民主化，并让庞大而多元的用户群得以对「在现实世界中模型选择究竟什么重要」发表意见。因此，尽管我们指出了 Chatbot Arena 现状中的系统性问题，我们也承认，其中许多问题可能是逐步产生的——随着排行榜在对提供方的可见度上获得了过大的重要性。

    This work demonstrates the difficulty in maintaining fair evaluations, despite best intentions. We show that coordination among a handful of providers and preferential policies from Chatbot Arena towards the same small group have jeopardized scientific integrity and reliable Arena rankings. The widespread and apparent willful participation in the gamification of arena scores from a handful of top-tier industry labs is undoubtedly a new low for the AI research field. As scientists, we must do better. As a community, we must demand better.

    本工作表明，即便出于最好的意图，维持公平的评测也是困难的。我们表明，少数提供方之间的协同，加上 Chatbot Arena 对同一小团体的倾斜性政策，已经损害了科学诚信与 Arena 排名的可靠性。来自少数顶级产业实验室对 arena 分数的广泛且明显的刻意参与式博弈，无疑是 AI 研究领域的一个新低。作为科学家，我们必须做得更好。作为一个共同体，我们必须要求更好。

    We believe it is very feasible for the organizers of Chatbot Arena to continue to innovate and restore trust by revising their policies. We propose a series of very straightforward recommendations to help reinforce the reliability and fairness of the leaderboard. Most urgently, providers should not be allowed to choose which scores are made public. There should be strict and transparent limits to the number of private variants per provider. Providers, whether from academic or industry labs, should be aware of private testing and the limits should be the same across providers. There should be transparent criteria for model removal from the arena and fairer sampling that is motivated by reducing uncertainty in rankings instead of being skewed towards giving proprietary models more battles. We believe the implementation of these recommendations is critical for addressing ranking distortions, but also necessary in the long term to ensure the benefits from participating on the leaderboard are not concentrated in a handful of providers.

    我们相信，Chatbot Arena 的组织者完全有可能继续创新并通过修订政策来重建信任。我们提出一系列非常直截了当的建议，以帮助强化排行榜的可靠性与公平性。最紧迫的是，不应允许提供方选择哪些分数被公开。每个提供方的私有变体数量应当有严格且透明的上限。提供方——无论来自学术实验室还是产业实验室——都应当知晓私有测试，且各提供方的上限应当一致。模型从 arena 中移除应当有透明的标准，采样也应当更公平，其动机应是降低排名中的不确定性，而不是偏向给专有模型更多对战。我们认为，落实这些建议对于纠正排名失真至关重要，并且从长远看也是必要的，以确保参与排行榜的收益不会集中在少数提供方手中。

    LMSYS originated from a multi-university collaboration involving UC Berkeley, Stanford, UCSD, CMU, and MBZUAI in 2023.     LMArena operates based on human preferences. Chatbot Arena asks users to input prompts in battles.     LMArena 基于人类偏好运行。Chatbot Arena 要求用户在对战中输入提示。用户随后依据模型针对其提示生成的输出，投票选出自己更偏好的模型。Chatbot Arena 再用这些偏好，通过 Online Elo 与 Bradley-Terry 等算法计算模型评分。

    Appendix B Bradley-Terry Rating Model

    附录 B Bradley-Terry 评分模型

    Consider a set of mm players (models) and nn pairwise comparisons between them. Let X∈ℝm×nX\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n} be the design matrix, where each column represents one pairwise comparison. In the Bradley-Terry model, the probability that player ii is preferred over player jj in a comparison is modeled as:

    考虑一组 mm 个参与者（模型）与它们之间的 nn 次成对比较。设 X∈ℝm×nX\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n} 为设计矩阵，其中每一列代表一次成对比较。在 Bradley-Terry 模型中，参与者 ii 在一次比较中被认为优于参与者 jj 的概率建模为：

    P⁡(i preferred over j)=11+e(βj−βi)P(\text{i preferred over j})=\frac{1}{1+e^{(\beta_{j}-\beta_{i})}}

    Then, in the matrix XX, column vector kk has a value of 1 at position ii , -1 at position jj, and 0 elsewhere. Let Y∈{0,1}nY\in\{0,1\}^{n} be the vector of observed outcomes, where Yk=1Y_{k}=1 if player ii wins the kk-th comparison and Yk=0Y_{k}=0 if player jj wins. Our goal is to estimate the Bradley-Terry coefficients β∈ℝm\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{m}, which determine the relative strengths of the players. The coefficients β\beta are estimated via maximum likelihood estimation by minimizing the expected cross-entropy loss,

    于是，在矩阵 XX 中，列向量 kk 在位置 ii 处取值为 1，在位置 jj 处取值为 -1，其余为 0。设 Y∈{0,1}nY\in\{0,1\}^{n} 为观测结果向量，取值 Yk=1Y_{k}=1 表示参与者 ii 赢得第 kk 次比较，取值 Yk=0Y_{k}=0 表示参与者 jj 获胜。我们的目标是估计 Bradley-Terry 系数 β∈ℝm\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{m}，它们决定了参与者的相对强度。系数 β\beta 通过极大似然估计、最小化期望交叉熵损失来估计，

    β^=arg⁡minβ∈ℝm​1n​∑k=1nℓ⁡(σ​(XT​β)k,Yk)\hat{\beta}=\arg\min_{\beta\in\mathbb{R}^{m}}\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}\ell\big(\sigma(X^{T}\beta)_{k},Y_{k}\big)

    where σ⁡(⋅)\sigma(\cdot) is the logistic function that models the relative player strengths, and ℓ⁡(⋅)\ell(\cdot) represents the binary cross-entropy loss between the predicted probabilities and the observed outcomes YY. The estimated coefficient β\beta captures the latent strength of each player.

    其中 σ⁡(⋅)\sigma(\cdot) 是建模参与者相对强度的 logistic 函数，ℓ⁡(⋅)\ell(\cdot) 表示预测概率与观测结果 YY 之间的二元交叉熵损失。估计出的系数 β\beta 捕捉了每个参与者的潜在强度。

    Once the Bradley-Terry model estimates the coefficients, we can scale them to obtain Elo-like ratings using the transformation:

    一旦 Bradley-Terry 模型估计出这些系数，我们就可以通过如下变换把它们缩放为类 Elo 的评分：

    Rm=scale∗β+initial ratingR_{m}=\text{scale}*\beta+\text{initial rating}

    In practice, Chatbot Arena does not rely solely on a model’s Arena Score for ranking. Instead, it also considers the confidence intervals associated with these scores. When the confidence intervals of two models overlap, it becomes difficult to determine which one is truly better. This uncertainty is reflected in the final ranking table, adding nuance and statistical rigor to the leaderboard (Chiang et al. 2024).

    在实践中，Chatbot Arena 并不只依赖模型的 Arena 分数来排名。它还考虑与这些分数相关的置信区间。当两个模型的置信区间重叠时，很难判断哪一个真正更好。这种不确定性会反映在最终排名表中，为排行榜增添了细微差别与统计严谨性（Chiang et al. 2024）。

    rank(m)=1+∑m′∈[M]1{m′>m}\text{rank}(m)=1+\sum_{m^{\prime}\in[M]}1\left\{m^{\prime}>m\right\}

    Appendix C Unbiased Sampling: Why Selecting the Maximum Introduces Bias?

    附录 C 无偏采样：为何选择最大值会引入偏差？

    Let (β^k)k=1N(\hat{\beta}_{k})_{k=1}^{N} be i.i.d. real-valued random variables with common cumulative distribution function FF and finite expectation μ:=𝔼⁡[β^k]\mu:=\mathbb{E}[\hat{\beta}_{k}]. Assume the distribution is non-degenerate, i.e., Var⁡(β^k)>0\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\beta}_{k})>0. The maximum is defined as:

    设 (β^k)k=1N(\hat{\beta}_{k})_{k=1}^{N} 为独立同分布的实值随机变量，具有共同的累积分布函数 FF 与有限期望 μ:=𝔼⁡[β^k]\mu:=\mathbb{E}[\hat{\beta}_{k}]。假设分布是非退化的，即 Var⁡(β^k)>0\operatorname{Var}(\hat{\beta}_{k})>0。最大值定义为：

    Remark 1
    注记 1

    This result formalizes the selection bias arising when one reports the best out of NN noisy skill estimates: statistical fluctuations ensure that the maximum overestimates the expected performance of a typical sample. This is especially relevant in leaderboard scenarios where multiple submissions are made and only the top-performing one is reported. This phenomenon is well-studied in the theory of order statistics (see Arnold et al. 1992; David & Nagaraja 2003).

    这一结果形式化了「从 NN 个含噪技能估计中报告最优者」时产生的选择偏差：统计波动保证了最大值会高估一个典型样本的期望表现。这在排行榜场景中尤为相关——在那里会进行多次提交，且只报告表现最好的那一次。这一现象在次序统计理论中已有充分研究（参见 Arnold et al. 1992; David & Nagaraja 2003）。

    Appendix G Data Access Estimation for Different Providers

    附录 G 各提供方的数据访问估计

    In Figure 4, we show the estimates for the data available to different providers. LMArena has collected around 3M user votes via Chatbot Arena in total. Each of these 3M votes resulted in twice the number of model API calls i.e. 6M since each battle features two models. Each square in Figure 4 represents roughly 5K API calls, illustrating how proprietary providers collectively access a considerably greater volume of data compared to the broader research community, which receives only a fraction.
