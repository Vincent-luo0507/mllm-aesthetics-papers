中文摘要

指令引导的图像编辑已取得长足进展，但现有模型面对复杂指令仍显吃力，往往需要多次采样才能得到满意结果。强化学习（RL）为此提供了颇具前景的解决思路，其在图像编辑中的落地却因缺少高保真、高效率的奖励信号而严重受阻。本文给出一套系统性方法以突破这一壁垒，核心是构建一个面向该任务的先进专用奖励模型。我们首先提出 EditReward-Bench——一个用于系统评测编辑质量奖励模型的综合基准；在此基础上开发 EditScore，一系列用于评估指令引导图像编辑质量的奖励模型（7B–72B）。通过精细的数据策管与筛选，EditScore 的性能可与闭源 VLM 相媲美；配合面向其生成式本质定制的自集成策略，最大规模变体在基准上的表现甚至超过 GPT-5。随后我们证明，高保真的奖励模型正是解锁图像编辑在线强化学习的关键：实验显示，即便规模最大的开源 VLM 也无法提供有效的学习信号，而 EditScore 能支撑高效、稳健的策略优化。把该框架应用于强基线模型 OmniGen2 后，最终模型呈现出显著且一致的性能提升。总体而言，本文给出了从基准评测到奖励建模再到强化学习训练的第一条系统化路径，表明高保真、面向领域的专用奖励模型是释放强化学习在该领域全部潜力的关键。

Abstract (原文)

Instruction-guided image editing has achieved remarkable progress, yet current models still face challenges with complex instructions and often require multiple samples to produce a desired result. Reinforcement Learning (RL) offers a promising solution, but its adoption in image editing has been severely hindered by the lack of a high-fidelity, efficient reward signal. In this work, we present a comprehensive methodology to overcome this barrier, centered on the development of a state-of-the-art, specialized reward model. We first introduce EditReward-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark to systematically evaluate reward models on editing quality. Building on this benchmark, we develop EditScore, a series of reward models (7B-72B) for evaluating the quality of instruction-guided image editing. Through meticulous data curation and filtering, EditScore effectively matches the performance of learning proprietary VLMs. Furthermore, coupled with an effective self-ensemble strategy tailored for the generative nature of EditScore, our largest variant even surpasses GPT-5 in the benchmark. We then demonstrate that a high-fidelity reward model is the key to unlocking online RL for image editing. Our experiments show that, while even the largest open-source VLMs fail to provide an effective learning signal, EditScore enables efficient and robust policy optimization. Applying our framework to a strong base model, OmniGen2, results in a final model that shows a substantial and consistent performance uplift. Overall, this work provides the first systematic path from benchmarking to reward modeling to RL training in image editing, showing that a high-fidelity, domain-specialized reward model is the key to unlocking the full potential of RL in this domain.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Recently, Reinforcement Learning (Li, 2017) has demonstrated immense power in advancing large language models and has also shown remarkable success in the text-to-image (T2I) domain. Works like FlowGRPO (Liu et al., 2025a) and DanceGRPO (Xue et al., 2025) have leveraged RL on flow-matching models to significantly enhance T2I generation across multiple dimensions. Despite these successes, the application of RL to the image editing domain remains largely underexplored. We posit that RL holds significant, untapped potential for image editing. By framing editing as a dynamic trial-and-error process, a policy model can discover novel and more effective editing strategies beyond what is captured in static datasets. Through a well-designed reward mechanism, the model can be progressively steered to better align with user intent and achieve a deeper, more robust editing capability across diverse scenarios.

近来，强化学习（Reinforcement Learning, RL）（Li, 2017）在推进大语言模型方面展现出巨大威力，在文生图（text-to-image, T2I）领域同样取得了引人注目的成功。FlowGRPO（Liu et al., 2025a）与 DanceGRPO（Xue et al., 2025）等工作把强化学习引入流匹配（flow-matching）模型，在多个维度上大幅提升了 T2I 生成效果。尽管进展斐然，强化学习在图像编辑领域的应用仍鲜有探索。我们的判断是，强化学习在图像编辑上蕴含着尚未释放的巨大潜力：把编辑建模为一个动态的试错过程，策略模型便有可能发现静态数据集未曾覆盖的、更新也更有效的编辑策略；借助精心设计的奖励机制，模型还能被逐步引导至更贴合用户意图的方向，从而在多样场景中获得更深入、更稳健的编辑能力。

Despite its theoretical promise, applying online RL to high-resolution image editing remains a formidable and largely unsolved challenge (Wei et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Ahmadi et al., 2025). The primary obstacle lies in the absence of a suitable reward function: a reliable, efficient, and scalable oracle that can accurately score the quality of an edit given an instruction. A natural consideration would be to employ state-of-the-art, general-purpose Visual Language Models (VLMs) like GPT-5 (OpenAI, 2025) or other proprietary APIs as reward providers. However, this approach is impractical for online RL, with these large VLMs are expensive to query at scale. The second alternative, using powerful open-source VLMs, resolves cost issues. However, our investigations reveal a critical performance gap. As we will demonstrate, even Qwen2.5-VL-72B (Bai et al., 2025b) fails to provide a sufficiently accurate and consistent reward signal to effectively guide the policy. Directly using such models as reward functions often causes unstable training or policy collapse, showing that scale alone cannot replace specialized accuracy. Progress thus hinges on an accurate reward model tailored to image editing, yet the community still lacks a powerful open-source option—posing a major barrier to advancing RL-based editing.

尽管理论前景可观，把在线强化学习应用于高分辨率图像编辑仍是一项艰巨且基本未被攻克的挑战（Wei et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Ahmadi et al., 2025）。主要障碍在于缺少合适的奖励函数——一个可靠、高效且可扩展，能根据给定指令准确为编辑质量打分的判别器。最自然的想法是采用 GPT-5（OpenAI, 2025）等最先进的通用视觉语言模型（VLM）或其他专有 API 充当奖励提供方；然而这类大模型的规模化调用成本高昂，对在线强化学习并不现实。第二种方案是改用强大的开源 VLM，成本问题由此消解，但我们的调研揭示出一道关键的性能鸿沟：正如后文所示，即便 Qwen2.5-VL-72B（Bai et al., 2025b）也无法提供足够准确且一致的奖励信号来有效引导策略，直接把这类模型当作奖励函数往往导致训练不稳甚至策略崩溃，说明模型规模本身无法替代专门化的准确性。因此，该领域的进展取决于一个面向图像编辑的精准奖励模型，而社区目前仍缺少强大的开源选项——这构成了推进基于强化学习的编辑研究的主要壁垒。

In this work, we argue that the key to unlocking online RL for image editing is the development of a specialized, high-fidelity, and efficient reward model. We present a systematic approach to build, validate, and deploy such a model. Our contributions are organized as follows:

本文主张，解锁图像编辑在线强化学习的关键，在于构建一个专门化、高保真且高效的奖励模型。我们给出一套系统方法来构建、验证并部署这样的模型，贡献组织如下。

First, to enable rigorous and reproducible research, we introduce EditReward-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating reward models in image editing. It is organized into four main categories, comprising 13 diverse subtasks ranging from simple attribute edits to complex compositional changes. Each entry is evaluated by expert human raters across three key dimensions: prompt following, consistency, and overall quality. All annotations have passed rigorous inter-annotator agreement checks, establishing a reliable standard for measuring reward model quality.

其一，为支撑严谨且可复现的研究，我们提出 EditReward-Bench——一个面向图像编辑奖励模型评测的综合基准。它按四大类组织，涵盖 13 个多样化子任务，从简单的属性编辑到复杂的组合式改动不等。每条数据均由人类专家从指令遵循、一致性与总体质量三个关键维度评判，全部标注通过了严格的标注员间一致性检验，为衡量奖励模型质量确立了可靠标准。

Second, guided by our benchmark, we develop EditScore, a series of powerful reward models (7B–72B) for evaluating the quality of instruction-guided editing. Through careful data curation and filtering and inference-time scaling, EditScore sets a new state of the art for open-source reward models in this domain, even surpassing the accuracy of leading proprietary VLMs.

其二，在该基准的指导下，我们开发了 EditScore——一系列用于评估指令引导编辑质量的强大奖励模型（7B–72B）。通过细致的数据策管、筛选与推理时扩展，EditScore 在该领域创下开源奖励模型的新水平，准确率甚至超越了头部专有 VLM。

To validate its practical utility, we first apply EditScore in a Best-of-NN selection experiment across several state-of-the-art editing models, which confirms that our reward model can effectively improve the performance of diverse editors. We further used EditScore for RL training, resulting in a model that shows a substantial and consistent performance uplift over its base model.

为验证其实用价值，我们首先在若干最先进编辑模型上开展 Best-of-NN 选择实验，证实该奖励模型能够有效提升不同编辑器的表现；随后把 EditScore 用于强化学习训练，所得模型相较其基座模型呈现出显著且稳定的性能提升。

In summary, our key contributions are as follows.

综上，本文的核心贡献如下。

  • •

    We propose EditReward-Bench, a new public benchmark for the direct and reliable evaluation of reward models for instruction-guided image editing.

    • • 提出 EditReward-Bench，一个新的公开基准，可直接、可靠地评测面向指令引导图像编辑的奖励模型。

  • •

    We develop and release EditScore, a series of state-of-the-art open-source reward models for instruction-based image editing that, through our self-ensembling strategy, surpasses the accuracy of leading proprietary VLMs on this task.

    • • 开发并开源 EditScore，一系列面向指令式图像编辑的最先进开源奖励模型；借助自集成策略，其准确率在该任务上超越了头部专有 VLM。

  • •

    We demonstrate the broad, practical utility of EditScore through a Best-of-NN selection experiment, successfully enhancing the performance of multiple diverse base models.

    • • 通过 Best-of-NN 选择实验展示 EditScore 广泛的实用价值，成功提升了多个不同基座模型的性能。

  • •

    We provide a comprehensive analysis of an end-to-end online RL application, identifying key factors for success, including the accuracy and variance of the reward signal.

    • • 对端到端在线强化学习应用给出全面分析，识别出包括奖励信号准确性与方差在内的关键成功因素。

    2 Related Work

    2 相关工作

    Reward Models for Image Generation. Reward models provide signals based on specific preferences and can be applied in various scenarios such as automated evaluation and reinforcement learning. Most research has focused on text-to-image tasks, with works like Wu et al. (2023b); Kirstain et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2024b) fine-tune CLIP-based model with human preference data and  Ma et al. (2025); Wang et al. (2025b); Gao et al. (2025) employ VLMs as a core component for reward generation. Research on reward models for image editing is relatively underexplored: some works focused on controlled edits based on predefined masks (Ren et al., 2024; Gong et al., 2025). For instruction-guided editing,  Zhang et al. (2024a) fine-tunes the BLIP model (Li et al., 2022) with human annotated reward signals for the image generated only by P2P Hertz et al. (2022) and IP2P Brooks et al. (2023), while Chen et al. (2025) leverages CLIP scores to automatically construct training data for training LLaVA-Next-8B Liu et al. (2024) as reward model. However, they used outdated editing models to generate images that needed to be evaluated, or simply used CLIP to annotate and score the images. Meanwhile, they use limited task categories as the prompt pool. The limitations in the narrow scope of task and generation model coverage make them struggle to support online RL for current models. Furthermore, they are not open-source and cannot be used and evaluated by the public. In contrast, our model enables broader evaluation of editing tasks (13 tasks) while supporting SOTA editing models (e.g., Gemini-2.5-image-preview). Moreover, its generative nature enables it to perform inference-time scaling to enhance scoring accuracy (Liu et al., 2025d).

    面向图像生成的奖励模型。 奖励模型依据特定偏好提供信号，可应用于自动评测、强化学习等多种场景。多数研究聚焦于文生图任务：Wu et al. (2023b)、Kirstain et al. (2023)、Zhang et al. (2024b) 等用人类偏好数据微调基于 CLIP 的模型，Ma et al. (2025)、Wang et al. (2025b)、Gao et al. (2025) 则把 VLM 作为奖励生成的核心组件。面向图像编辑的奖励模型研究相对薄弱，部分工作关注基于预定义掩码的受控编辑（Ren et al., 2024; Gong et al., 2025）。在指令引导编辑方面，Zhang et al. (2024a) 用人工标注的奖励信号微调 BLIP 模型（Li et al., 2022），但被评测的图像仅由 P2P Hertz et al. (2022) 与 IP2P Brooks et al. (2023) 生成；Chen et al. (2025) 则利用 CLIP 分数自动构建训练数据，把 LLaVA-Next-8B Liu et al. (2024) 训练成奖励模型。然而，它们要么使用过时的编辑模型生成待评测图像，要么仅靠 CLIP 完成标注与打分，同时只用有限的任务类别构成提示池。任务与生成模型覆盖面的双重局限，使其难以支撑当前模型的在线强化学习；而且它们均未开源，公众无从使用与评测。相比之下，我们的模型支持更广泛的编辑任务评测（13 类），并覆盖最先进的编辑模型（如 Gemini-2.5-image-preview）；其生成式本质还使其能够通过推理时扩展来提升打分准确率（Liu et al., 2025d）。

    Image Reward Model Evaluation. Establishing a reliable benchmark is critical as it ensures the objective measurement of reward modeling performance and offers clear guidance for optimization. In previous work, image reward models are evaluated on their accuracy in predicting human preferences for key generative tasks, such as text-to-image generation (Xu et al., 2023; Kirstain et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023b; Wu et al., 2023a; Ma et al., 2025) and image editing (Ku et al., 2023b; Jiang et al., 2024). Over the past year, generative models such as GPT-Image-1 (Hurst et al., 2024), FLUX-Kontext (Batifol et al., 2025), Qwen-Image-Edit (Wu et al., 2025a) and Nano Banana (Google, 2025) have achieved significant breakthroughs in image editing, particularly in stylization, hybrid edit and text modification. With the growing power of generative models and their expanding editing capabilities, there is an urgent need for reward models that can accurately assess them. This, in turn, necessitates the development of a novel and more comprehensive benchmark for evaluating edit rewards.

    图像奖励模型的评测。 建立可靠的基准至关重要，它既保证对奖励建模性能的客观度量，也为优化给出明确指引。既有工作以图像奖励模型在关键生成任务上预测人类偏好的准确率来评价它们，如文生图（Xu et al., 2023; Kirstain et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023b; Wu et al., 2023a; Ma et al., 2025）与图像编辑（Ku et al., 2023b; Jiang et al., 2024）。过去一年间，GPT-Image-1（Hurst et al., 2024）、FLUX-Kontext（Batifol et al., 2025）、Qwen-Image-Edit（Wu et al., 2025a）与 Nano Banana（Google, 2025）等生成模型在图像编辑上取得重大突破，尤其在风格化、组合编辑与文字修改等方面。随着生成模型能力日益增强、编辑功能不断拓展，亟需能够准确评估它们的奖励模型；这又反过来要求构建更新、更全面的编辑奖励评测基准。

It covers 13 diverse editing tasks and spans 11 heterogeneous editing models for data construction, ranging from open-source baselines to state-of-the-art proprietary models (see Table 1 for comparison). The benchmark is distinguished by the following features.

    EditReward-Bench 是专为系统评测图像编辑奖励建模而设计的基准。它覆盖 13 类多样化编辑任务，在数据构建上横跨 11 个异构编辑模型，从开源基线到最先进的专有模型不等（对比见表 1）。该基准具备如下特点。

    Multi-dimensional Image Evaluation Framework. EditReward-Bench offers three key dimensions for evaluating editing outcomes. These includes Prompt Following (adherence to prompts), consistency (preservation of key visual elements) and overall quality (comparison across all aspects).

    多维度图像评测框架。 EditReward-Bench 给出评估编辑结果的三个关键维度，即指令遵循（Prompt Following，对提示的遵循程度）、一致性（关键视觉元素的保留）与总体质量（跨所有方面的综合比较）。

    Comprehensive Model Performance Spectrum. EditReward-Bench incorporates both state-of-the-art editing models and lower-peforming baselines. It effectively challenges the reward model’s distinguish ability and validates the reward model’s scoring performance on current SOTA models.

    完整的模型性能谱系。 EditReward-Bench 同时纳入最先进的编辑模型与性能偏弱的基线，既有效考验奖励模型的辨别能力，也验证其对当前 SOTA 模型的打分表现。

    Extensive Task Coverage with Real-world Applicability. EditReward-Bench also covers a diverse set of editing tasks that closely align with real-world application scenarios, ensuring authentic and comprehensive evaluation of reward models for image editing.

    广泛的任务覆盖与真实适用性。 EditReward-Bench 覆盖的编辑任务紧贴真实应用场景，确保对图像编辑奖励模型的评测真实而全面。

    3.2 Construction Protocol

    3.2 构建流程

    To create a robust and comprehensive benchmark, we focused on three key pillars: the diversity of editing tasks, the variety of editing models, and the granularity of our evaluation criteria.

    为构建稳健而全面的基准，我们聚焦三根支柱：编辑任务的多样性、编辑模型的多样性，以及评测标准的粒度。

    First, to ensure comprehensive task coverage, we structure our benchmark into four main categories, comprising 13 distinct subtasks curated from established datasets like GEdit-Bench-EN (Liu et al., 2025b) and ImgEdit-Bench (Ye et al., 2025). These categories are designed to span a wide spectrum of complexity. They are: 1) Subject, which includes fundamental tasks like subject addition, subject removal and subject replace; 2) Appearance, covering shape-preserving edits such as color alteration, material modification, style transfer and tone transformation; 3) Scene, which tests understanding of image layout through tasks like background change and extract; and 4) Advanced, the most challenging category, which requires advanced reasoning for tasks like portrait beautification, text modification, motion change and hybrid edit.

    首先，为保证任务覆盖的全面性，我们把基准组织为四大类，包含从 GEdit-Bench-EN（Liu et al., 2025b）与 ImgEdit-Bench（Ye et al., 2025）等既有数据集中精选出的 13 个不同子任务。各类别的复杂度跨度很大，分别是：1）主体（Subject），含主体添加、主体移除与主体替换等基础任务；2）外观（Appearance），含颜色更改、材质修改、风格迁移与色调变换等保持形状不变的编辑；3）场景（Scene），通过背景替换与主体提取等任务检验对图像布局的理解；4）高级（Advanced），难度最高的一类，需对人物美化、文字修改、动作变化与组合编辑等任务作高级推理。

    Second, to populate our benchmark with a diverse distribution of edited images, the candidate pool of outputs were generated by a diverse array of 11 generative models for data construction: Step1X-Edit (Liu et al., 2025b), Step1X-Edit v1.1 (Liu et al., 2025b), Qwen-Image-Edit (Wu et al., 2025a), OmniGen2 (Wu et al., 2025b), FLUX-Kontext-dev (Batifol et al., 2025), FLUX-Kontext-pro (Batifol et al., 2025), Bagel (Deng et al., 2025), MagicBrush (Zhang et al., 2023), Omnigen (Xiao et al., 2025), gpt-image-1 (Hurst et al., 2024), and Gemini-2.5-image-preview (Google, 2025), including both open-source and state-of-the-art proprietary editors.

    其次，为使基准中的编辑图像呈现多样化分布，我们用一个由 11 个生成模型构成的模型池产出候选输出，用于数据构建：Step1X-Edit（Liu et al., 2025b）、Step1X-Edit v1.1（Liu et al., 2025b）、Qwen-Image-Edit（Wu et al., 2025a）、OmniGen2（Wu et al., 2025b）、FLUX-Kontext-dev（Batifol et al., 2025）、FLUX-Kontext-pro（Batifol et al., 2025）、Bagel（Deng et al., 2025）、MagicBrush（Zhang et al., 2023）、Omnigen（Xiao et al., 2025）、gpt-image-1（Hurst et al., 2024）与 Gemini-2.5-image-preview（Google, 2025），开源编辑器与最先进专有编辑器兼而有之。

    Finally, recognizing that the quality of editing results is not monolithic, we designed a multi-dimensional evaluation scheme for better interpretability as inspired by (Ku et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2025b). Each editing result is assessed along three distinct axes: Prompt Following (PF): measuring how faithfully the edit executes the given instruction. Consistency (C): assessing the preservation of unedited image regions. Overall Quality (O): providing a holistic comparison of edits by accounting for all relevant aspects.

    最后，鉴于编辑结果的质量并非单一维度，我们受（Ku et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2025b）启发，设计了一套多维度评测方案以增强可解释性。每个编辑结果沿三条不同的轴线评估：指令遵循（Prompt Following, PF），衡量编辑执行给定指令的忠实程度；一致性（Consistency, C），评估未编辑图像区域的保留情况；总体质量（Overall Quality, O），在综合考量所有相关方面的基础上对编辑结果作整体比较。

    4 Method

    4 方法

    4.1 Edit Reward

    4.1 编辑奖励

    4.1.1 The Approach to Reward Modeling

    4.1.1 奖励建模的思路

    Inspired by (Wang et al., 2025b; Wang et al., 2025a; Wu et al., 2025c), we formulate reward modeling as a conditional textual generation task. We fine-tune models from the powerful Qwen2.5-VL series (Bai et al., 2025b) on a standard autoregressive objective to act as specialized evaluators. Our model EditScore takes (Instruction, Input Image, Output Image) as input, (Reasoning, Scalar Score) as output. The chain-of-thought (Wei et al., 2022) process of output not only enhances the model’s interpretability but also demonstrably improves the accuracy of the final scores.

    受（Wang et al., 2025b; Wang et al., 2025a; Wu et al., 2025c）启发，我们把奖励建模形式化为一个条件文本生成任务：在标准的自回归目标上微调 Qwen2.5-VL 系列（Bai et al., 2025b）中性能强大的模型，使其成为专门的评判器。我们的模型 EditScore 以（指令、输入图像、输出图像）为输入，以（推理过程、标量分数）为输出。输出中的思维链（chain-of-thought）（Wei et al., 2022）过程既增强了模型的可解释性，也切实提高了最终分数的准确性。

    To ensure EditScore can perform a comprehensive evaluation of editing tasks, we adopt the VIEScore framework (Ku et al., 2023a), prompting the model in parallel to assess two orthogonal aspects. The first is Semantic Consistency (SC), which evaluates the degree of instruction following and consistency, ensuring specified objects are correctly modified while unmentioned regions remain preserved. The second is Perceptual Quality (PQ), which assesses overall image quality, focusing on photorealism and the absence of artifacts. The final score is the geometric mean of the SC and PQ scores (Sf​i​n​a​l=SS​C⋅SP​QS_{final}=\sqrt{S_{SC}\cdot S_{PQ}}), yielding a balanced and granular reward signal.

    为使 EditScore 能对编辑任务作全面评估，我们采用 VIEScore 框架（Ku et al., 2023a），并行提示模型评估两个正交的方面。其一是语义一致性（Semantic Consistency, SC），评估指令遵循与一致性的程度，确保指定对象被正确修改、未提及区域得以保留。其二是感知质量（Perceptual Quality, PQ），评估图像的整体质量，重点关注照片真实感与瑕疵的有无。最终分数取 SC 与 PQ 分数的几何平均（Sf​i​n​a​l=SS​C⋅SP​QS_{final}=\sqrt{S_{SC}\cdot S_{PQ}}），得到均衡且细粒度的奖励信号。

    4.1.2 Inference-time ensembling Strategy

    4.1.2 推理时集成策略

    Recent work (Snell et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025c) has shown that the performance of LLMs can be substantially improved by increasing the computational budget at test time. Building on this insight, we introduce an inference-time ensembling strategy for our generative reward model to systematically boost its evaluation accuracy and enhance reward stability. Our approach is straightforward: for a given input triplet 𝐳=(Instruction, Input Image, Output Image)\mathbf{z}=(\text{Instruction, Input Image, Output Image}), we perform KK independent, stochastic forward passes through EditScore, and each pass ii generates a pair (reasoningi,si)(\text{reasoning}_{i},s_{i}). Then we aggregate only the scalar scores {s1,s2,…,sK}\{s_{1},s_{2},\dots,s_{K}\} to compute the final ensembled score, Sf​i​n​a​lS_{final}, using the arithmetic mean:

    近期工作（Snell et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2025c）表明，增加测试时的计算预算可大幅提升大语言模型的性能。基于这一认识，我们为生成式奖励模型引入推理时集成策略，以系统性地提高评测准确率并增强奖励的稳定性。做法很直接：对给定输入三元组 𝐳=(Instruction, Input Image, Output Image)\mathbf{z}=(\text{Instruction, Input Image, Output Image})，我们通过 EditScore 执行 KK 次独立且随机的前向传播，每次传播 ii 生成一对 (reasoningi,si)(\text{reasoning}_{i},s_{i})；随后只汇总其中的标量分数 {s1,s2,…,sK}\{s_{1},s_{2},\dots,s_{K}\}，用算术平均计算最终集成分数 Sf​i​n​a​lS_{final}：

    Sf​i​n​a​l​(𝐳)=1K​∑i=1Ksi,where ​(reasoningi,si)=EditScore​(𝐳).S_{final}(\mathbf{z})=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{i=1}^{K}s_{i},\quad\text{where }(\text{reasoning}_{i},s_{i})=\text{EditScore}(\mathbf{z}). (1)

    An intuitive explanation (Liu et al., 2025c) for this effectiveness is that each of the KK generated reasonings can be viewed as distinct judgment perspectives. Aggregating the scores derived from these diverse perspectives allows the final reward to more accurately reflect the true quality of an edit, leading to significant scaling effectiveness.

    对其有效性有一种直观解释（Liu et al., 2025c）：KK 次生成的推理过程可被视为各自不同的判断视角。汇总这些不同视角导出的分数，能使最终奖励更准确地反映一次编辑的真实质量，从而带来显著的扩展收益。

    4.2 Data Construction Pipeline

    4.2 数据构建流程

    The training data for reward model and subsequent reinforcement learning are constructed following the similar procedure. For reinforcement learning, the training data only requires the input images and instructions, whereas training the reward model additionally requires the generated outputs paired with their corresponding rewards. The construction process involves three steps as follows.

    奖励模型与后续强化学习的训练数据按相近流程构建。强化学习只需输入图像与指令；训练奖励模型则额外需要生成输出及其对应的奖励。构建过程包含以下三个步骤。

    Step I: Images selection and instructions creation. We start by selecting and filtering a diverse set of high-quality images as editing inputs. Next, we construct a series of reference instructions for various editing tasks. Using the input images and random selected reference instructions as guidance, we prompt Qwen-2.5-VL-72B to generate editing instructions that are task-consistent with the references. After generating a large set of image-instruction pairs, we applying a K-center greedy algorithm (Sener and Savarese, 2018) to select 1000 semantically diverse samples per task for reward generation.

    步骤一：图像筛选与指令生成。 我们先筛选一组多样化的高质量图像作为编辑输入，再为各类编辑任务构造一系列参考指令。以输入图像与随机选中的参考指令为引导，提示 Qwen-2.5-VL-72B 生成与参考指令任务一致的编辑指令。得到大量图像—指令对后，用 K-center 贪心算法（Sener and Savarese, 2018）为每个任务挑选 1,000 个语义多样的样本，用于奖励生成。

    Finally, we constructed 70,000 data samples for training the reward model and 60,000 samples for reinforcement learning training. The data for the reward model requires further annotation as follows.

    最终我们构建了 70,000 条用于训练奖励模型的数据样本，以及 60,000 条用于强化学习训练的数据样本。奖励模型所用数据还需按如下方式作进一步标注。

    Step II: Candidates output generation. We generate candidates output using 5 distinct editing models random selected from our model pool.

    步骤二：候选输出生成。 从模型池中随机选取 5 个不同的编辑模型生成候选输出。

    Step III: Annotate and filtering. For each candidate output, we use GPT-4.1 to annotate scores. Following VIEScore (Ku et al., 2023a), we score each output on SC and PQ and provide reasons for the assigned scores. Next, we apply filtering to the reward samples. It is conducted from two perspectives: (i) filtering based on group-wise maximum scores to remove unachievable editing tasks, and (ii) filtering by group standard deviation to remove cases with low discriminability.

    步骤三：标注与筛选。 对每个候选输出，我们用 GPT-4.1 标注分数。依照 VIEScore（Ku et al., 2023a），为每个输出的 SC 与 PQ 打分，并给出打分理由。随后对奖励样本施加筛选，从两个角度展开：（i）依据组内最高分筛选，剔除无法完成的编辑任务；（ii）依据组内标准差筛选，剔除区分度低的样本。

    5.2 主要结果

    Results summarized in Table 2 reveal a significant performance gap between proprietary models and open-source counterparts. Leading proprietary models such as GPT-4.1, GPT-5 and Gemini-2.5-Pro form a distinct upper tier, achieving pairwise accuracies in the 0.7-0.75 range across all dimensions.     表 2 的结果显示，专有模型与开源模型之间存在显著的性能差距。GPT-4.1、GPT-5 与 Gemini-2.5-Pro 等头部专有模型构成明显的上位梯队，各维度成对准确率处于 0.7–0.75 区间，印证了它们在这一细粒度任务上虽不完美却相当强大的零样本能力。我们特别发现，VLM 评估指令遵循（PF）通常强于一致性（C），因为后者需要在输入与输出图像之间作细粒度比对。

    In stark contrast, even the largest and most capable open-source models exhibit notable limitations.     与之形成鲜明反差的是，即便规模最大、能力最强的开源模型也暴露出明显局限。Qwen2.5-VL 系列呈现清晰的规模效应，但 72B 变体的总体准确率仍不足 0.612，在一致性判断上甚至劣于随机猜测；7B 与 32B 模型表现更差，说明现成的开源 VLM 不足以充当细粒度编辑任务的可靠奖励信号。相比之下，EditScore 取得了大幅提升：7B 变体超越了规模十倍于它的 Qwen2.5-VL-72B，72B 变体则与 GPT-4.1 打平。此外，用自集成（Avg@4）扩展推理时计算还能进一步提升各规模模型的性能，EditScore-72B 由此在 EditReward-Bench 上确立了最先进水平。

    7 Conclusion

    7 结论

    In this work, we addressed the critical bottleneck for RL in image editing: the lack of a reliable reward signal. We established a comprehensive benchmark for reward model evaluation. Guided by our benchmark, we developed EditScore, a family of specialized generative reward models that deliver a robust, high-fidelity signal. Crucially, EditScore is not only efficient for Best-of-NN selection but is the key to unlocking stable online RL where previous open-source models fail. By releasing both EditReward-Bench and EditScore, we provide a foundational toolkit for future research into RL-based image editing and more nuanced reward modeling.

    本文着力解决图像编辑中强化学习的关键瓶颈：缺少可靠的奖励信号。我们建立了一个用于评测奖励模型的综合基准；在该基准的指导下，开发了 EditScore——一系列专门化的生成式奖励模型，能够提供稳健、高保真的奖励信号。关键在于，EditScore 不仅在 Best-of-NN 选择中高效，更是解锁稳定在线强化学习的关键，而此前的开源模型在此均告失败。通过开源 EditReward-Bench 与 EditScore，我们为后续基于强化学习的图像编辑研究以及更精细的奖励建模提供了基础工具箱。

    Appendix B Disagreement Analysis of Annotation Protocol

    附录 B 标注协议的分歧分析

    To ensure the high fidelity of the EditReward-Bench dataset, we prioritized minimizing annotation noise and subjectivity. While standard crowdsourcing often relies on aggregating labels from independent workers, evaluating image editing involves subtle visual inspections that are prone to individual oversight.

    为确保 EditReward-Bench 数据集的高保真度，我们把降低标注噪声与主观性放在首位。标准众包通常依赖聚合独立标注者的标签，而图像编辑的评测涉及细微的视觉审视，容易因个体差异产生疏漏。

    To validate our choice of the Two-Annotator Discussion Protocol, we conducted a controlled study comparing it against a standard Single-Annotator baseline.

    为验证双标注员讨论协议这一选择，我们开展了一项对照研究，将其与标准的单标注员基线作比较。

    B.1 Controlled Study Setup

    B.1 对照研究设置

    We recruited experts from our annotation pool and divided the validation process into two distinct rounds to annotate the same subset of samples across three evaluation dimensions: Prompt Following (PF), Consistency (C), and Overall Quality (O).

    我们从标注员库中招募专家，把验证过程分为两轮，对同一批样本子集按三个评测维度——指令遵循（PF）、一致性（C）与总体质量（O）——进行标注。

  • •

    Baseline: Single Annotator. Four experts annotated the samples independently. We analyzed the consistency among these independent raters.

    • • 基线：单标注员。四名专家独立标注样本，我们分析这些独立评分者之间的一致性。

  • •

    Ours: Annotator Pair (Two-Annotator Group). Eight experts were randomly formed into four pairs. Each pair followed our proposed protocol: they examined images together, discussed visual artifacts in real-time to reach a consensus. This process typically requires more time per sample than single-annotator approaches.

    • • 本方法：标注员结对（双标注员组）。八名专家随机组成四对，每对遵循我们提出的协议：共同审视图像、实时讨论视觉瑕疵以达成共识。该过程通常比单标注员方式更耗时。

    B.2 Metrics

    B.2 指标

    We evaluate the reliability of our annotation process using two key metrics:

    我们用两个关键指标评估标注流程的可靠性：

  • 1.

    Convergence Rate: This metric quantifies inter-annotator consistency. It calculates the probability that distinct annotation units—whether individual experts (Single Annotators) or discussion groups (Annotator Pairs)—assign identical rankings to the same input.

    • 1. 收敛率：该指标量化标注员间的一致性，计算不同标注单元——无论是独立专家（单标注员）还是讨论组（标注员结对）——对同一输入给出相同排序的概率。

  • 2.

    Agreement with Majority Vote: Serving as a proxy for ground truth, the majority vote is derived by aggregating all available annotations for a sample. This metric measures the alignment between an individual unit’s ranking and the collective consensus. High agreement indicates that the judgment of a single unit reliably reflects the expert consensus, thereby justifying our efficient single-pass annotation strategy.

    • 2. 与多数投票的一致率：多数投票由聚合某样本的全部可得标注得到，作为真值的代理。该指标衡量单个标注单元的排序与集体共识的吻合程度。高一致率说明单个单元的判断能可靠反映专家共识，从而为我们高效的单轮标注策略提供了依据。

    B.3 Results and Analysis

    B.3 结果与分析

    Impact of Discussion on Visual Consistency.
    讨论对视觉一致性的影响。

    As shown in Table 7, the Two-Annotator protocol consistently outperforms the Single-Annotator baseline across all dimensions. Notably, the most significant improvement is observed in the Consistency dimension, where the strict agreement rate (100% threshold) increased by 12.12% (from 82.88% to 95.00%). We attribute this to the inherent nature of image editing evaluation: inconsistency often manifests as subtle background distortions or minor structural artifacts that are easily overlooked by a single individual. The real-time discussion protocol acts as a verification mechanism, forcing annotators to cross-check visual details and effectively eliminating such oversights.

    如表 7 所示，双标注员协议在各维度上均持续优于单标注员基线。最显著的提升出现在一致性维度：严格一致率（100% 阈值）提高了 12.12%（由 82.88% 升至 95.00%）。我们将其归因于图像编辑评测的固有特性——不一致往往表现为细微的背景畸变或轻微的结构瑕疵，单个人极易忽略。实时讨论协议充当了校验机制，迫使标注员交叉核对视觉细节，有效消除了此类疏漏。

    Validation of Multi-Dimensional Evaluation.
    多维度评测的有效性。

    The results also highlight the importance of our decomposed evaluation strategy. Across both annotation settings, the agreement rates for Prompt Following and Consistency are consistently higher than for Overall Quality (e.g., 93-95% vs. 90% in strict agreement). This disparity underscores the fact that ”Overall Quality” is inherently more subjective and prone to rater bias. By explicitly separating the evaluation into specific, objective dimensions (PF and C), we ensure that the majority of EditReward-Bench relies on rigorous, reproducible criteria, rather than vague holistic impressions.

    结果也凸显了我们分解式评测策略的重要性。在两种标注设置下，指令遵循与一致性的一致率都持续高于总体质量（严格一致率分别为 93–95% 与 90%）。这一差异说明“总体质量”天然更为主观、更易受评分者偏见影响。通过把评测显式拆分为具体、客观的维度（PF 与 C），我们确保 EditReward-Bench 的绝大部分内容依赖严谨、可复现的判据，而非模糊的整体印象。

    Efficiency and Reliability of Single-Pass Annotation.
    单轮标注的效率与可靠性。

    Finally, Table 8 demonstrates that the rankings produced by a single Annotator Pair achieve near-perfect alignment with the collective majority vote, averaging over 97% across all dimensions (with Consistency reaching 99.55%). This finding is crucial for the scalability of our benchmark. It indicates that the consensus reached by one expert pair is statistically equivalent to the “ground truth” derived from a larger pool of annotators.     Appendix F Derivation of the SDE Formulation for Flow Matching

    附录 F 流匹配的随机微分方程形式推导

    This appendix derives the stochastic differential equation (SDE) underlying policy sampling in our Flow Matching model. Unlike prior work such as Flow-GRPO (Liu et al., 2025a), our base model OmniGen2 defines the probability path from noise at t=0t=0 (𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0}) to data at t=1t=1 (𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1}), reversing the convention in some other studies. This leads to a different drift term in the resulting SDE. The derivation proceeds in three steps: (i) connecting the deterministic ODE and a general SDE via the Fokker–Planck equation, (ii) relating the score term to the learned vector field, and (iii) constructing the discretized transition kernel.

    本附录推导 Flow Matching 模型中策略采样所依据的随机微分方程（SDE）。与 Flow-GRPO（Liu et al., 2025a）等既有工作不同，我们的基座模型 OmniGen2 把概率路径定义为从 t=0t=0（𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0}）处的噪声到 t=1t=1（𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1}）处的数据，与另一些研究的约定相反，这导致所得 SDE 的漂移项不同。推导分三步进行：(i) 通过 Fokker–Planck 方程把确定性 ODE 与一般 SDE 联系起来；(ii) 把得分项与学习到的向量场关联起来；(iii) 构造离散化的转移核。

    F.1 Connecting the Probability Flow ODE to an SDE

    F.1 把概率流 ODE 连接到 SDE

    Our Flow Matching model  (Lipman et al., 2023) is trained to approximate the vector field vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) of a probability flow ODE, which deterministically transports samples from a noise distribution p0p_{0} to a data distribution p1p_{1}:

    我们的 Flow Matching 模型（Lipman et al., 2023）经训练用于近似概率流 ODE 的向量场 vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})，该 ODE 确定性地把样本从噪声分布 p0p_{0} 传输到数据分布 p1p_{1}：

    d​𝐱t=vt​(𝐱t)​d​t.d\mathbf{x}_{t}=v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})dt. (2)

    We wish to find an equivalent SDE of the general form

    我们希望找到如下一般形式的一个等价 SDE

    d​𝐱t=f⁡(𝐱t,t)​d​t+G⁡(t)​d​Wt,d\mathbf{x}_{t}=f(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)dt+G(t)dW_{t}, (3)

    that generates the same marginal probability density path pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x}) for all t∈[0,1]t\in[0,1]. Here, ff is the drift term, G⁡(t)G(t) is the diffusion coefficient (we assume it is state-independent), and WtW_{t} is a standard Wiener process.

    使其对所有 t∈[0,1]t\in[0,1] 生成相同的边缘概率密度路径 pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x})。其中 ff 为漂移项，G⁡(t)G(t) 为扩散系数（我们假定其与状态无关），WtW_{t} 为标准 Wiener 过程。

    The evolution of the probability density pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x}) is described by the Fokker-Planck equation (Song et al., 2021). For the deterministic ODE in equation 2, it is:

    概率密度 pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x}) 的演化由 Fokker-Planck 方程（Song et al., 2021）描述。对于式 2 中的确定性 ODE，其形式为：

    ∂pt​(𝐱)∂t=−∇⋅[vt(𝐱)pt(𝐱)].\frac{\partial p_{t}(\mathbf{x})}{\partial t}=-\nabla\cdot\left[v_{t}(\mathbf{x})p_{t}(\mathbf{x})\right]. (4)

    For the SDE in equation 3, the Fokker-Planck equation is:

    对于式 3 中的 SDE，Fokker-Planck 方程为：

    ∂pt​(𝐱)∂t=−∇⋅[f(𝐱,t)pt(𝐱)]+12∇2[G(t)2pt(𝐱)].\frac{\partial p_{t}(\mathbf{x})}{\partial t}=-\nabla\cdot\left[f(\mathbf{x},t)p_{t}(\mathbf{x})\right]+\frac{1}{2}\nabla^{2}\left[G(t)^{2}p_{t}(\mathbf{x})\right]. (5)

    For the two processes to be equivalent (i.e., to share the same pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x})), the right-hand sides of equation 4 and equation 5 must be equal. This allows us to solve for the SDE drift term f⁡(𝐱,t)f(\mathbf{x},t):

    为使两个过程等价（即共享相同的 pt​(𝐱)p_{t}(\mathbf{x})），式 4 与式 5 的右端必须相等。由此可解出 SDE 的漂移项 f⁡(𝐱,t)f(\mathbf{x},t)：

    −∇⋅[vtpt]\displaystyle-\nabla\cdot\left[v_{t}p_{t}\right] =−∇⋅[fpt]+12∇⋅[∇(G2pt)]\displaystyle=-\nabla\cdot\left[fp_{t}\right]+\frac{1}{2}\nabla\cdot\left[\nabla(G^{2}p_{t})\right] (6)
    ∇⋅[vt​pt]\displaystyle\nabla\cdot\left[v_{t}p_{t}\right] =∇⋅[fpt−12G2∇pt]\displaystyle=\nabla\cdot\left[fp_{t}-\frac{1}{2}G^{2}\nabla p_{t}\right] (7)
    vt​pt\displaystyle v_{t}p_{t} =fpt−12G2∇pt.\displaystyle=fp_{t}-\frac{1}{2}G^{2}\nabla p_{t}. (8)

    Dividing by ptp_{t} and rearranging yields the expression for the drift:

    两边除以 ptp_{t} 并整理，得到漂移项的表达式：

    f(𝐱t,t)=vt(𝐱t)+G​(t)22∇logpt(𝐱t).f(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)=v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})+\frac{G(t)^{2}}{2}\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}). (9)

    Substituting this back into equation 3, we obtain the equivalent SDE:

    把它代回式 3，即得等价的 SDE：

    d𝐱t=[vt(𝐱t)+G​(t)22∇logpt(𝐱t)]dt+G(t)dWt.d\mathbf{x}_{t}=\left[v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})+\frac{G(t)^{2}}{2}\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})\right]dt+G(t)dW_{t}. (10)

    We define that noise at t=0t=0 (𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0}) and ending with the data sample at t=1t=1 (𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1}), so equation 10 don’t need a reverse SDE. This SDE’s drift consists of the original ODE vector field plus a term proportional to the score function, ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}).

    我们定义噪声位于 t=0t=0（𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0}）、数据样本位于 t=1t=1（𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1}）作为终点，因此式 10 无需反向 SDE。该 SDE 的漂移由原始 ODE 向量场加上一个正比于得分函数 ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) 的项构成。

    F.2 Expressing the Score via the Learned Vector Field

    F.2 用学习到的向量场表示得分

    The SDE in equation 10 is not yet practical for sampling, as the score function ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) is unknown. However, for the specific probability path used in Flow Matching, this score can be expressed entirely in terms of the known vector field vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}), which is approximated by our trained model vθv_{\theta}.The derivation follows a similar approach to that for stochastic interpolants (Albergo et al., 2023).

    式 10 中的 SDE 尚不能直接用于采样，因为得分函数 ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) 未知。不过，对于 Flow Matching 所用的特定概率路径，该得分可以完全用已知的向量场 vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})（由训练模型 vθv_{\theta} 近似）来表示。推导思路与随机插值（stochastic interpolants）（Albergo et al., 2023）相近。

    Our derivation begins with the linear interpolation path: 𝐱t=(1−t)​𝐱0+t​𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{t}=(1-t)\mathbf{x}_{0}+t\mathbf{x}_{1}, where 𝐱0∼𝒩⁡(𝟎,𝐈)\mathbf{x}_{0}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I}) and 𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1} is a data sample. The velocity of this path is defined as the conditional expectation of the instantaneous change:

    推导从线性插值路径 𝐱t=(1−t)​𝐱0+t​𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{t}=(1-t)\mathbf{x}_{0}+t\mathbf{x}_{1} 出发，其中 𝐱0∼𝒩⁡(𝟎,𝐈)\mathbf{x}_{0}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I})，𝐱1\mathbf{x}_{1} 为一个数据样本。该路径的速度定义为瞬时变化的条件期望：

    vt​(𝐱t)=𝔼⁡[d​𝐱td​t|𝐱t].v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})=\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{d\mathbf{x}_{t}}{dt}\Big|\mathbf{x}_{t}\right]. (11)

    Since the time derivative is d​𝐱td​t=𝐱1−𝐱0\frac{d\mathbf{x}_{t}}{dt}=\mathbf{x}_{1}-\mathbf{x}_{0}, the vector field becomes:

    由于时间导数为 d​𝐱td​t=𝐱1−𝐱0\frac{d\mathbf{x}_{t}}{dt}=\mathbf{x}_{1}-\mathbf{x}_{0}，向量场变为：

    vt​(𝐱t)=𝔼⁡[𝐱1|𝐱t]−𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t].v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})=\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]-\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]. (12)

    A key identity, derived from Tweedie’s formula in score-based modeling, connects the marginal score ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) to the posterior mean of the initial noise sample 𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0}:

    由基于得分建模中的 Tweedie 公式可导出一个关键恒等式，把边缘得分 ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) 与初始噪声样本的后验均值 𝐱0\mathbf{x}_{0} 联系起来：

    ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)=−𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]1−t.\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})=-\frac{\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]}{1-t}. (13)

    Our goal is thus to express 𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}] using vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}). To do this, we take the conditional expectation of the path definition itself:

    因此我们的目标是用 vt​(𝐱t)v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) 表示 𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]。为此，对路径定义本身取条件期望：

    𝔼⁡[𝐱t|𝐱t]=𝐱t=(1−t)​𝔼​[𝐱0|𝐱t]+t​𝔼​[𝐱1|𝐱t].\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{t}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]=\mathbf{x}_{t}=(1-t)\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]+t\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]. (14)

    We now have a system of two linear equations ( equation 12 and equation 14) with two unknowns (𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}] and 𝔼⁡[𝐱1|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]). We can solve this system for 𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]. Rearranging equation 12 gives 𝔼⁡[𝐱1|𝐱t]=vt​(𝐱t)+𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]=v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})+\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]. Substituting this into equation 14:

    于是得到一个由两个线性方程（式 12 与式 14）构成、含两个未知量（𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}] 与 𝔼⁡[𝐱1|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]）的方程组，可解出 𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]。整理式 12 得 𝔼⁡[𝐱1|𝐱t]=vt​(𝐱t)+𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{1}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]=v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})+\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]，代入式 14：

    𝐱t\displaystyle\mathbf{x}_{t} =(1−t)​𝔼​[𝐱0|𝐱t]+t⁡(vt​(𝐱t)+𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t])\displaystyle=(1-t)\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]+t(v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})+\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}])
    𝐱t\displaystyle\mathbf{x}_{t} =(1−t+t)​𝔼​[𝐱0|𝐱t]+t​vt​(𝐱t)\displaystyle=(1-t+t)\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]+tv_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})
    𝐱t\displaystyle\mathbf{x}_{t} =𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]+t​vt​(𝐱t).\displaystyle=\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]+tv_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}). (15)

    This gives us the desired expression:

    由此得到所需表达式：

    𝔼⁡[𝐱0|𝐱t]=𝐱t−t​vt​(𝐱t).\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}_{0}|\mathbf{x}_{t}]=\mathbf{x}_{t}-tv_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t}). (16)

    Finally, substituting equation 16 back into the score identity ( equation 13), we arrive at the final, practical expression for the score function:

    最后，把式 16 代回得分恒等式（式 13），得到得分函数的最终实用表达式：

    ∇log⁡pt​(𝐱t)=−𝐱t−t​vt​(𝐱t)1−t.\nabla\log p_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})=-\frac{\mathbf{x}_{t}-tv_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})}{1-t}. (17)

    This result allows us to compute the score at any point (𝐱t,t)(\mathbf{x}_{t},t) using only the output of our trained vector field model vθ​(𝐱t,t)v_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t).

    该结果使我们只需用训练好的向量场模型 vθ​(𝐱t,t)v_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t) 的输出，就能计算任意点 (𝐱t,t)(\mathbf{x}_{t},t) 处的得分。

    F.3 Final SDE and Discretized Transition Probability

    F.3 最终 SDE 与离散化转移概率

    We now substitute the practical expression for the score equation 17 into the SDE from equation 10. For simplicity, let’s assume a constant diffusion G⁡(t)=σG(t)=\sigma:

    现在把式 17 的得分实用表达式代入式 10 的 SDE。为简化，假设扩散系数为常数 G⁡(t)=σG(t)=\sigma：

    d​𝐱t=[vt​(𝐱t)−σ22​𝐱t−t​vt​(𝐱t)1−t]​d​t+σ​d​Wt.d\mathbf{x}_{t}=\left[v_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})-\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2}\frac{\mathbf{x}_{t}-tv_{t}(\mathbf{x}_{t})}{1-t}\right]dt+\sigma dW_{t}. (18)

    This is our final SDE, where the drift is expressed entirely in terms of the learned vector field vtv_{t} (approximated by vθv_{\theta}). Unlike some prior work that requires deriving a reverse-time SDE, we can directly use this forward-time SDE for model sampling.

    这就是最终的 SDE，其漂移完全用学习到的向量场 vtv_{t}（由 vθv_{\theta} 近似）表示。与某些需要推导反向时间 SDE 的前人工作不同，我们可以直接把这个前向时间 SDE 用于模型采样。

    To implement this for our RL policy, we discretize equation 18 using the Euler-Maruyama scheme with a step size Δ​t\Delta t:

    为在强化学习策略中实现该式，我们用步长 Δ​t\Delta t 的 Euler-Maruyama 格式对式 18 作离散化：

    𝐱t+Δ​t=𝐱t+[vθ​(𝐱t,t)−σ22​𝐱t−t​vθ​(𝐱t,t)1−t]​Δ​t+σ​Δ​t⋅ϵ,\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}=\mathbf{x}_{t}+\left[v_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)-\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2}\frac{\mathbf{x}_{t}-tv_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)}{1-t}\right]\Delta t+\sigma\sqrt{\Delta t}\cdot\bm{\epsilon}, (19)

    where ϵ∼𝒩⁡(𝟎,𝐈)\bm{\epsilon}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I}).

    其中 ϵ∼𝒩⁡(𝟎,𝐈)\bm{\epsilon}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I})。

    This defines the stochastic transition probability of our policy, πθ​(𝐱t+Δ​t|𝐱t,𝐜)\pi_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}|\mathbf{x}_{t},\mathbf{c}). It is a Gaussian distribution:

    这定义了我们策略的随机转移概率 πθ​(𝐱t+Δ​t|𝐱t,𝐜)\pi_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}|\mathbf{x}_{t},\mathbf{c})，它是一个高斯分布：

    πθ​(𝐱t+Δ​t|𝐱t,𝐜)=𝒩⁡(𝐱t+Δ​t,𝝁⁡(𝐱t,t),Σ),\pi_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}|\mathbf{x}_{t},\mathbf{c})=\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t};\bm{\mu}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t),\Sigma), (20)

    with mean and covariance given by:

    其均值与协方差为：

    𝝁⁡(𝐱t,t)\displaystyle\bm{\mu}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t) =𝐱t+[vθ​(𝐱t,t)−σ22​𝐱t−t​vθ​(𝐱t,t)1−t]​Δ​t\displaystyle=\mathbf{x}_{t}+\left[v_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)-\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2}\frac{\mathbf{x}_{t}-tv_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t},t)}{1-t}\right]\Delta t (21)
    Σ\displaystyle\Sigma =σ2​Δ​t⋅𝐈.\displaystyle=\sigma^{2}\Delta t\cdot\mathbf{I}. (22)

    This completes the derivation from the deterministic ODE to a concrete, sampleable stochastic policy for reinforcement learning.

    至此，从确定性 ODE 到可供采样的具体随机策略的推导完成。

    Appendix G GRPO on SDE Flow Matching

    附录 G SDE 流匹配上的 GRPO

    With the stochastic policy πθ\pi_{\theta} defined by the SDE in Appendix F, we optimize our generative model using GRPO (Shao et al., 2024). GRPO is efficient online algorithm and lightweight than PPO (Schulman et al., 2017), well-suited for large generative models.

    有了附录 F 中由 SDE 定义的随机策略 πθ\pi_{\theta}，我们用 GRPO（Shao et al., 2024）优化生成模型。GRPO 是一种高效的在线算法，比 PPO（Schulman et al., 2017）更轻量，很适合大型生成模型。

    The optimization process unfolds over trajectories generated by our SDE-based policy. For each input condition 𝐜\mathbf{c}, we generate a group of GG trajectories. Each trajectory consists of TT discrete steps, obtained by iteratively applying the Euler-Maruyama update from equation 19 to produce a final image 𝐱1i\mathbf{x}_{1}^{i}. Our EditScore model then assigns a terminal reward rir_{i} to each resulting image.

    优化过程在我们基于 SDE 的策略所生成的轨迹上展开。对每个输入条件 𝐜\mathbf{c}，生成一组 GG 条轨迹；每条轨迹由 TT 个离散步构成，通过迭代应用式 19 的 Euler-Maruyama 更新得到最终图像 𝐱1i\mathbf{x}_{1}^{i}。随后由 EditScore 模型为每张结果图像赋予终止奖励 rir_{i}。

    The GRPO objective maximizes a clipped surrogate function over each step tt of the trajectories:

    GRPO 的目标是在轨迹的每一步 tt 上最大化一个截断的替代函数：

    𝒥GRPO​(θ)=𝔼^t​[min⁡(ρt​(θ)​A^t,clip​(ρt​(θ),1−ϵ,1+ϵ)​A^t)],\mathcal{J}_{\text{GRPO}}(\theta)=\hat{\mathbb{E}}_{t}\left[\min\left(\rho_{t}(\theta)\hat{A}_{t},\text{clip}(\rho_{t}(\theta),1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat{A}_{t}\right)\right], (23)

    where ϵ\epsilon is a clipping hyperparameter, and the advantage A^t\hat{A}_{t} is computed based on the terminal rewards within the group. For the final step of the trajectory, the advantage is calculated as:

    其中 ϵ\epsilon 为截断超参数，优势 A^t\hat{A}_{t} 依据组内的终止奖励计算。对轨迹的最后一步，优势计算为：

    A^Ti=ri−mean​({r1,…,rG})std​({r1,…,rG}),\hat{A}_{T}^{i}=\frac{r_{i}-\text{mean}(\{r_{1},\dots,r_{G}\})}{\text{std}(\{r_{1},\dots,r_{G}\})}, (24)

    For intermediate timesteps (t<Tt<T), the advantages are typically computed using Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE).

    对于中间时间步（t<Tt<T），优势通常用广义优势估计（Generalized Advantage Estimation, GAE）计算。

    Critically, in our SDE framework, the importance ratio ρt​(θ)\rho_{t}(\theta) is the ratio of the single-step state transition probabilities defined in equation 20, under the current policy πθ\pi_{\theta} and the old policy πθold\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}} that generated the data:

    关键的是，在我们的 SDE 框架中，重要性比 ρt​(θ)\rho_{t}(\theta) 是式 20 所定义的单步状态转移概率之比，分别在当前策略 πθ\pi_{\theta} 与生成数据的旧策略 πθold\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}} 下计算：

    ρt​(θ)=πθ​(𝐱t+Δ​t|𝐱t,𝐜)πθold​(𝐱t+Δ​t|𝐱t,𝐜).\rho_{t}(\theta)=\frac{\pi_{\theta}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}|\mathbf{x}_{t},\mathbf{c})}{\pi_{\theta_{\text{old}}}(\mathbf{x}_{t+\Delta t}|\mathbf{x}_{t},\mathbf{c})}. (25)
