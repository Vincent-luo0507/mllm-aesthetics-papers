中文摘要

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的近期进展把图像美学评估（IAA）从标量分数拓展到了可解释的评论与指导。然而现有基准主要评估内在视觉品质或固定的领域准则，留下一个悬而未决的问题：一张好看的图像是否适合特定的目的、受众、文化语境或领域惯例。我们提出 AesCanvas，一个包含两个互补组件的统一套件：CritiqueCanvas 含由 54,300 张图像构建的 519,136 组指令-回答对，支持跨摄影、绘画与虚拟图像的长篇多维度评论；ContextCanvas 含 301 个经专家评审的使用场景，在真实使用情境中评估上下文美学适配性。在统一协议下，我们评测了闭源前沿模型、开源权重通用模型与美学专用 MLLM。结果揭示出评论生成与语境敏感判断之间的清晰分离：基于参考的词汇与语义指标只能部分刻画评论质量，而美学专用模型虽在部分评论指标上仍有竞争力，却在 ContextCanvas 上大幅落后于强劲的通用 MLLM。进一步分析表明，美学专精并不能可靠迁移到上下文适配性，且模型的决策可能未能追踪或定位于决定性的上下文视觉线索。这些发现确立了「文化情境化、有证据支撑的适配性」作为美学建模的一个独立目标。

Abstract (原文)

Recent advances in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have extended Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA) beyond scalar scores toward interpretable critique and guidance. Yet existing benchmarks mainly assess intrinsic visual quality or fixed domain criteria, leaving open whether an appealing image is appropriate for a specific purpose, audience, cultural setting, or domain convention. We introduce AesCanvas, a unified suite with two complementary components: CritiqueCanvas with 519,136 instruction-response pairs from 54,300 images supports long-form, multi-dimensional critique across photography, painting, and virtual imagery, whereas ContextCanvas with 301 expert-reviewed use scenarios evaluates contextual aesthetic suitability in realistic use scenarios. Under a unified protocol, we evaluate closed-source frontier, open-weight general, and aesthetic-specific MLLMs. Results reveal a clear separation between critique generation and context-sensitive judgment: reference-based lexical and semantic metrics only partially capture critique quality, while aesthetic specialists remain competitive on selected critique metrics yet substantially lag strong general-purpose MLLMs on ContextCanvas. Further analyses show that aesthetic specialization does not reliably transfer to contextual suitability and that model decisions may fail to track or ground themselves in decisive contextual visual cues. These findings establish culturally situated, evidence-grounded suitability as a distinct objective for aesthetic modeling.

I Introduction

I 引言

Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA) has progressed from scalar prediction and preference modeling (Murray et al. 2012; Talebi and Milanfar 2018; Yi et al. 2023) toward language-based aesthetic perception, critique, diagnosis, and guidance (Huang et al. 2024b; Huang et al. 2024a; Zhou et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025; Qi et al. 2025b; Cao et al. 2025b). In practical use, however, visual appeal alone is insufficient: an image must also suit a particular purpose, audience, and convention. Cultural knowledge is therefore not auxiliary to aesthetic judgment, since symbols, styles, dress, and genre conventions can directly change whether an image is an appropriate visual choice. Related multimodal studies likewise show that models remain fragile when judgments require criterion-sensitive reasoning, visual grounding, or culturally situated evidence (Xiong et al. 2026; Li et al. 2026; Wu et al. 2026b; Satar et al. 2025; Singh et al. 2026).

A Image Aesthetic Assessment

A 图像美学评估

Image Aesthetic Assessment (IAA) predicts human judgments of visual aesthetic quality. Early work formulated IAA as classification, ranking, or score-distribution prediction from crowd preferences, while incorporating photographic attributes, composition-preserving representations, and individual taste variation (Murray et al. 2012; Kong et al. 2016; Mai et al. 2016; Yang et al. 2022). Recent studies extend IAA to artistic images, fine-grained comparison within image series, and open-world criteria that vary across themes (Yi et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2026b; Liao et al. 2026). Nevertheless, these methods primarily assess aesthetic strength under intrinsic or task-fixed criteria, rather than whether an image is appropriate for a particular purpose, audience, cultural setting, or domain convention. Our work addresses this complementary problem of contextual aesthetic suitability.

图像美学评估（IAA）预测人类对视觉美学质量的判断。早期工作把 IAA 形式化为分类、排序或分数分布预测，数据来源是众包偏好，并引入了摄影属性、保持构图的表示以及个体品味差异（Murray et al. 2012; Kong et al. 2016; Mai et al. 2016; Yang et al. 2022）。近期研究把 IAA 扩展到艺术图像、图像序列内的细粒度比较，以及随主题变化的开放世界准则（Yi et al. 2023; Yang et al. 2026b; Liao et al. 2026）。然而，这些方法主要在内在准则或任务固定准则下评估美学强度，而非判断一张图像是否适合特定的目的、受众、文化语境或领域惯例。本文关注的正是上下文美学适配性这一互补问题。

B Aesthetic Multimodal Models

B 美学多模态模型

Aesthetic multimodal models extend conventional IAA from scalar prediction to language-based recognition, description, interpretation, scoring, and critique. Early studies benchmarked the aesthetic perception of general-purpose MLLMs and explored aesthetic instruction tuning (Huang et al. 2024b; Huang et al. 2024a; Zhou et al. 2024). Subsequent work connected textual supervision with visual scoring through language-defined rating levels or joint scoring-and-interpretation objectives (Wu et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025), while more recent models support professional critique, multi-attribute analysis, and unified perceptual assessment (Qi et al. 2025b; Cao et al. 2025b; Cao et al. 2025a). Existing evaluation, however, remains centered on aesthetic perception, scoring, reference-aligned critique, or domain-specific analysis. AesCanvas instead examines whether aesthetic articulation transfers to contextual decisions in which an attractive or stylistically plausible image may still conflict with its intended use. Beyond conventional visual inputs, recent multimodal foundation models have also explored semantic understanding and language generation over specialized or corrupted visual representations (Wu et al. 2026a; Liang et al. 2026).

美学多模态模型把传统 IAA 从标量预测扩展到基于语言的识别、描述、解读、评分与评论。早期工作对通用 MLLM 的美学感知做基准测试，并探索美学指令微调（Huang et al. 2024b; Huang et al. 2024a; Zhou et al. 2024）。后续工作通过语言定义的评分等级或评分与解读的联合目标，把文本监督与视觉评分连接起来（Wu et al. 2024; Zhang et al. 2025）；更近期的模型支持专业评论、多属性分析与统一的感知评估（Qi et al. 2025b; Cao et al. 2025b; Cao et al. 2025a）。然而现有评测仍以美学感知、评分、与参考对齐的评论或领域特定分析为中心。AesCanvas 转而考察美学表述能否迁移到上下文决策中——在这些决策里，一张好看的或风格上合理的图像仍可能与其预期用途相冲突。除常规视觉输入外，近期的多模态基础模型还探索了对专用或受损视觉表示的语义理解与语言生成（Wu et al. 2026a; Liang et al. 2026）。

C Contextual Multimodal Reasoning

C 上下文多模态推理

Contextual multimodal reasoning requires models to combine visual evidence with task conditions, external knowledge, cultural conventions, and criterion-specific constraints. Recent benchmarks study real-world visual reasoning, context-dependent functional inference, and flexible judgment under varying criteria (Yin et al. 2026; Zhang et al. 2026; Xiong et al. 2026; Gao et al. 2024). Grounding-focused work further evaluates whether intermediate reasoning and final answers are supported by localized image evidence (Li et al. 2026; Wu et al. 2026b; Cai et al. 2024), while other studies expose failures under visual illusions, counterfactual or adversarial evidence, and conflicts between knowledge and images (Hou et al. 2026; Zhou et al. 2026; Moratelli et al. 2026). Culturally situated benchmarks likewise reveal fragility on region-specific artifacts and multilingual cultural evidence (Satar et al. 2025; Singh et al. 2026). However, these works primarily assess factual correctness, grounding, functional inference, or adherence to specified criteria, rather than whether contextual evidence should outweigh surface aesthetic appeal in a concrete visual-use decision. ContextCanvas targets this intersection through use-oriented aesthetic suitability judgments grounded in both visual and contextual evidence.

上下文多模态推理要求模型把视觉证据与任务条件、外部知识、文化惯例以及准则特定约束结合起来。近期基准研究真实世界的视觉推理、依赖上下文的功能推断，以及在变化准则下的灵活判断（Yin et al. 2026; Zhang et al. 2026; Xiong et al. 2026; Gao et al. 2024）。以定位为核心的工作进一步评估中间推理与最终答案是否得到局部化图像证据的支持（Li et al. 2026; Wu et al. 2026b; Cai et al. 2024）；另一些研究则暴露了模型在视觉错觉、反事实或对抗性证据，以及知识与图像冲突下的失败（Hou et al. 2026; Zhou et al. 2026; Moratelli et al. 2026）。文化情境化基准同样揭示了模型在区域特定器物与多语言文化证据上的脆弱性（Satar et al. 2025; Singh et al. 2026）。然而，这些工作主要评估事实正确性、定位、功能推断或对给定准则的遵循，而非在具体视觉使用决策中，上下文证据是否应当压过表面美学吸引力。ContextCanvas 正是通过同时基于视觉证据与上下文证据的、面向使用的美学适配性判断来瞄准这一交汇点。

III Dataset

III 数据集

A Dataset Overview

A 数据集概览

We introduce AesCanvas, a unified suite built from 54,300 images to study two complementary aspects of aesthetic intelligence: articulating visual qualities and judging their suitability in context. CritiqueCanvas contains 519,136 instruction–response pairs across photography, painting, and virtual imagery for long-form, multi-dimensional critique generation. ContextCanvas comprises 301 expert-designed and reviewed cases that evaluate contextual aesthetic suitability—whether an image is an appropriate visual choice for a specified purpose, audience, cultural setting, or domain convention, beyond its intrinsic visual appeal. Together, the two components test whether models that can identify and articulate aesthetic evidence can also translate it into contextually valid decisions.

我们提出 AesCanvas，一个由 54,300 张图像构建的统一套件，用于研究美学智能的两个互补侧面：表述视觉品质，以及在上下文中判断其适配性。CritiqueCanvas 包含跨摄影、绘画与虚拟图像的 519,136 组指令-回答对，用于长篇、多维度的评论生成。ContextCanvas 包含 301 个由专家设计并评审的案例，评估上下文美学适配性——即一张图像在超越其内在视觉吸引力之外，是否是针对特定目的、受众、文化语境或领域惯例的恰当视觉选择。两个组件共同检验：能够识别并表述美学证据的模型，是否也能把这些证据转化为上下文有效的决策。

如图 3 所示，我们的流程从一个共享的多领域图像池分出两支：一支进入 CritiqueCanvas 做结构化美学评论，另一支进入 ContextCanvas 做封闭形式的上下文适配性判断。

Image Collection and Filtering.
图像采集与过滤。

We collect candidate images from three visual domains: photography (e.g., natural scenes, architecture, portraiture, and commercial imagery), painting (primarily Western classical and related works), and virtual imagery (e.g., game scenes, animation-style images, and AI-generated content). Images are drawn from publicly accessible web sources and online museum and cultural-heritage collections, with available source URLs and provenance metadata retained. After removing low-quality, semantically uninformative, non-compliant, and duplicate samples, the pool contains 21,294 photographs, 17,939 paintings, and 15,067 virtual images, for a total of 54,300.

我们从三个视觉域采集候选图像：摄影（如自然场景、建筑、肖像与商业影像）、绘画（主要为西方古典及相关作品）与虚拟图像（如游戏场景、动画风格图像与 AI 生成内容）。图像取自可公开访问的网络来源以及在线博物馆与文化遗产收藏，并保留可获得的来源 URL 与出处元数据。在剔除低质量、语义无信息量、不合规与重复的样本后，图像池包含 21,294 张照片、17,939 幅绘画与 15,067 张虚拟图像，合计 54,300。

CritiqueCanvas Annotation and Construction.
CritiqueCanvas 的标注与构建。

As shown in Fig. 3(b), we use a prompt-based pipeline that combines shared aesthetic dimensions with domain-specific criteria. The shared dimensions include Content and Narrative, Composition, Color, Lighting, Lines and Brushstrokes, Style, Emotion, Technique, Symbolism, and Visual Appeal. Annotating models select only the dimensions most relevant to each image rather than addressing all dimensions uniformly.

如图 3(b) 所示，我们采用一条基于提示的流程，把共享的美学维度与领域特定准则结合起来。共享维度包括内容与叙事、构图、色彩、光影、线条与笔触、风格、情绪、技法、象征与视觉吸引力。标注模型只挑选与每张图像最相关的维度，而不是对所有维度做统一处理。

Prompts are further conditioned on the visual domain. Photography emphasizes exposure, perspective, depth of field, focus, motion, and compositional control; painting emphasizes line, brushwork, genre, style, symbolism, and emotional expression; and virtual imagery emphasizes character design, scene construction, rendering consistency, narrative setting, and stylization. Multiple MLLMs generate structured annotations, which are converted into multi-turn instruction–response conversations covering description, evaluation, interpretation, technical analysis, and constructive improvement.

提示进一步以视觉域为条件。摄影强调曝光、透视、景深、对焦、动态与构图控制；绘画强调线条、笔法、体裁、风格、象征与情绪表达；虚拟图像强调角色设计、场景构建、渲染一致性、叙事设定与风格化。多个 MLLM 生成结构化标注，这些标注被转换为多轮指令-回答对话，覆盖描述、评价、解读、技术分析与建设性改进。

An independent Claude Opus 5 verifier screens generated pairs for instruction consistency, visual grounding, and response quality. A stratified audit of 1,000 retained pairs yields 95.3% acceptance and 97% raw agreement; full criteria and statistics appear in the supplement.

一个独立的 Claude Opus 5 校验器对生成的配对筛查指令一致性、视觉定位与回答质量。对 1,000 组保留配对的分层审计得到 95.3% 的接受率与 97% 的原始一致率；完整准则与统计见补充材料。

ContextCanvas Construction and Review.
ContextCanvas 的构建与评审。

As shown in Fig. 3(c), ContextCanvas is constructed from a selected subset of the shared image pool. Four PhD-level researchers with interdisciplinary backgrounds developed and reviewed 1,060 candidate cases. Each places an image in a plausible use scenario—such as exhibition, publication, advertising, education, commemoration, or public communication—and asks whether it is an appropriate visual choice for the stated purpose, audience, and context. Some cases include a curator- or designer-proposed assessment that the model must independently evaluate.

如图 3(c) 所示，ContextCanvas 从共享图像池的一个精选子集构建。四位具有跨学科背景的博士级研究者开发并评审了 1,060 个候选案例。每个案例把一张图像置于一个可信的使用场景——如展览、出版、广告、教育、纪念或公共传播——并询问它对于所述目的、受众与语境是否是恰当的视觉选择。部分案例还包含一段由策展人或设计师提出的评估，模型须独立加以评判。

A case is retained only when it satisfies seven prespecified criteria: (1) aesthetic centrality, requiring a judgment about the image as a visual choice; (2) context dependence, such that intrinsic quality alone is insufficient; (3) visual necessity, such that the task cannot be reduced to text-only factual or cultural knowledge; (4) decisive evidence, such that relevant cultural, historical, or narrative evidence materially affects the answer; (5) scenario naturalness, requiring a plausible design or communication use; (6) answerability, requiring a unique closed-form answer from perceptible evidence; and (7) anti-shortcut validity, preventing wording, option length, or label priors from revealing the answer.

Qwen3.7-Plus was used only as a development-time probe for trivial, ambiguous, or shortcut-prone cases; model failure alone never determined retention. Culture-Grounded Counterfactual Sensitivity

We further examine whether contextual aesthetic decisions are grounded in culture-bearing visual evidence. We construct 36 paired counterfactual variants from ContextCanvas, keeping the evaluation scenario and overall visual presentation fixed while changing the decisive cue such that the intended judgment changes from No to Yes. These synthetic variants neither revise the original annotations nor constitute an additional accuracy split; they are used only as a matched diagnostic of directional decision updating. As shown in Table 5, all three general-purpose MLLMs show positive net updating, with NCU values concentrated between 25.00 and 30.56 despite their substantially different benchmark accuracies. This suggests that absolute contextual competence and responsiveness to decisive visual evidence are related but separable. More importantly, both evaluated aesthetic specialists show weaker net updating than every general-purpose model, with ArtQuant exhibiting almost no measurable response. The results indicate that aesthetic specialization alone does not establish the visual–cultural binding required for reliable contextual aesthetic judgment.

如表 5 所示，三个通用 MLLM 都表现出正的净更新，NCU 值集中在 25.00 到 30.56 之间，尽管它们的基准准确率相差很大。这说明绝对的上下文能力与对决定性视觉证据的响应能力相关但可分离。更重要的是，两个被评测的美学专用模型的净更新都弱于每一个通用模型，其中 ArtQuant 几乎没有可测量的响应。结果表明，仅有美学专精并不能建立可靠上下文美学判断所需的视觉-文化绑定。

Binary accuracy does not show whether a model is correct for the right visual reason. We therefore evaluate 100 balanced ContextCanvas cases using human judgments of answer correctness, decisive-cue identification, and contextual explanation. Evidence-Grounded Accuracy (EGA) requires all three.

V Conclusion

V 结论

We introduced AesCanvas, comprising CritiqueCanvas and ContextCanvas, to evaluate aesthetic critique and contextual suitability. Across closed-source, open-weight, and aesthetic-specific models, strong critique performance does not reliably translate into context-sensitive judgment: aesthetic specialists may remain competitive on critique metrics yet achieve low accuracy and exhibit over-acceptance on ContextCanvas. Paired and diagnostic analyses further reveal inconsistent benefits from aesthetic tuning and limited use of decisive contextual evidence. These findings support treating culturally situated suitability as a distinct training and evaluation objective that requires models to integrate visual evidence with cultural knowledge, intended use, and domain-specific constraints.

我们提出 AesCanvas（包含 CritiqueCanvas 与 ContextCanvas），用于评测美学评论与上下文适配性。在闭源、开源权重与美学专用模型上，强劲的评论性能并不能可靠转化为上下文敏感的判断：美学专用模型可能在评论指标上仍有竞争力，却在 ContextCanvas 上准确率偏低并表现出过度接受。配对分析与诊断分析进一步揭示了美学微调收益的不一致，以及对决定性上下文证据的有限使用。这些发现支持把文化情境化的适配性作为一个独立的训练与评测目标，它要求模型把视觉证据与文化知识、预期用途及领域特定约束结合起来。

    S2.1.2  CritiqueCanvas

    S2.1.2 CritiqueCanvas

    CritiqueCanvas uses ten shared dimensions: Content/Narrative, Composition, Color, Lighting, Lines/Brushstrokes, Style, Emotion, Technique, Symbolism, and Visual Appeal. Only image-relevant dimensions are selected for a given record. The shared dimensions are complemented by domain-conditioned criteria: photography emphasizes exposure, perspective, depth of field, focus, motion, and compositional control; painting emphasizes line, brushwork, genre, style, symbolism, and emotion; and virtual imagery emphasizes character design, scene construction, rendering consistency, narrative setting, and stylization.

    CritiqueCanvas 使用十个共享维度：内容/叙事、构图、色彩、光影、线条/笔触、风格、情绪、技法、象征与视觉吸引力。对给定记录只挑选与该图像相关的维度。共享维度由领域条件化的准则加以补充：摄影强调曝光、透视、景深、对焦、动态与构图控制；绘画强调线条、笔法、体裁、风格、象征与情绪；虚拟图像强调角色设计、场景构建、渲染一致性、叙事设定与风格化。

    The CritiqueCanvas record schema identifies the image and domain, selected dimensions, instruction, target response, conversation group, split, and associated provenance/governance metadata. The evaluation loader consumes a compact conversation form with id, image, and conversations; it takes the first user/human turn as the instruction and the first assistant/GPT turn as the reference. The image and instruction form the model input, while the reference is withheld until scoring. Each row records sample_id, relative and resolved image paths, the exact rendered prompt, reference, prediction, model, task, and timestamp. Each six-pair construction dialogue was converted into six pair-level records sharing a conversation-group identifier; the evaluation export therefore contains one human–assistant pair per record. Source, license, selected-dimension, split, audit, and governance fields are retained as metadata but are never supplied as model input. Representative examples spanning photography, illustration, and painting, as well as both open-ended and dimension-focused instruction functions, are shown in Fig. S1.

    CritiqueCanvas 的记录模式标识图像与领域、所选维度、指令、目标回答、对话组、划分以及相关的出处/治理元数据。评测加载器消费一种紧凑的对话形式，含 id、图像与对话；它取第一个 user/human 轮次作为指令，取第一个 assistant/GPT 轮次作为参考答案。图像与指令构成模型输入，参考答案在打分前不予提供。每行记录 sample_id、相对与解析后的图像路径、实际渲染的提示、参考答案、预测、模型、任务与时间戳。每条六配对的构建对话被转换为共享同一对话组标识符的六条配对级记录；因此评测导出中每条记录包含一组 human–assistant 配对。来源、许可、所选维度、划分、审计与治理字段作为元数据保留，但从不作为模型输入提供。图 S1 给出了涵盖摄影、插画与绘画，以及开放式与维度聚焦两类指令功能的代表性示例。

    S2.1.3  ContextCanvas

    S2.1.3 ContextCanvas

    ContextCanvas was sampled from the same 54,300-image pool. The 1,060 initial candidate cases were collected primarily through a call circulated within the university. Graduate research assistants, doctoral researchers, and faculty spanning computer science and electrical engineering, visual arts and art history, design and visual communication, and cultural and media studies contributed source ideas and image–use proposals. A separate multidisciplinary panel of four PhD-level researchers developed these contributions into formal candidate cases and reviewed them through iterative refinement, standardization, cross-disciplinary checking, and final adjudication. A candidate was retained only if it satisfied aesthetic centrality, context dependence, visual necessity, decisive evidence, scenario naturalness, answerability, and anti-shortcut validity. Qwen3.7-Plus was used only as a development-time probe for trivial, ambiguous, or shortcut-prone candidates; model failure was never an inclusion condition. The four-person review panel finalized all questions, options, gold labels, source-grounded rationales, and source records.

    ContextCanvas 从同一个 54,300 张图像的池中采样。最初的 1,060 个候选案例主要通过在校内流传的征集获得。来自计算机科学与电气工程、视觉艺术与艺术史、设计与视觉传播、文化与媒体研究等领域的研究生助理、博士研究人员与教师贡献了原始创意与图像-使用提案。一个由四位博士级研究者组成的独立跨学科小组把这些投稿发展为正式候选案例，并通过迭代精修、标准化、跨学科核对与最终裁决加以评审。候选只有在满足美学中心性、上下文依赖性、视觉必要性、决定性证据、场景自然性、可回答性与反捷径有效性时才被保留。Qwen3.7-Plus 仅作为开发期探针，用于筛查过于简单、含糊或易走捷径的候选；模型失败从不作为纳入条件。四人评审小组最终确定了所有问题、选项、金标签、基于来源的推理依据与来源记录。

    The 301 retained cases comprise 300 single-image cases and one paired-image case, covering 302 unique images. Each case record stores the stable evaluation ID, original case ID, image descriptor(s), question, options, gold option ID and text, source-grounded rationale, source basis, and provenance/rights fields. Only the image(s), question, and fixed options are model input; labels, rationales, source titles, URLs, development metadata, and model-probe results are excluded.

    保留的 301 个案例包含 300 个单图案例与 1 个成对图像案例，覆盖 302 张唯一图像。每个案例记录存储稳定的评测 ID、原始案例 ID、图像描述符、问题、选项、金选项 ID 与文本、基于来源的推理依据、来源依据以及出处/权利字段。只有图像、问题与固定选项作为模型输入；标签、推理依据、来源标题、URL、开发元数据与模型探针结果均被排除。

The examples are organized by the mechanism that makes the aesthetic-use decision non-trivial: cultural or historical alignment; narrative meaning that reverses attractive, serene, or balanced surface qualities; apparent visual mismatch that strengthens a communicative purpose; and medium- or story-specific framing. Every example distinguishes the visible cue, the contextual knowledge it activates, and the resulting visual-use decision. This presentation makes clear that the task is neither artwork-title recall nor context-free cultural question answering.

    图 S2 给出了保留案例的标签均衡展示。示例按「使美学使用决策变得非平凡」的机制组织：文化或历史对齐；能够反转吸引力、宁静感或平衡感等表面品质的叙事含义；强化传播目的的表观视觉错配；以及媒介或故事特定的框定方式。每个示例都区分可见线索、它所激活的上下文知识，以及由此得出的视觉使用决策。这一呈现清楚表明，该任务既不是艺术作品标题回忆，也不是脱离上下文的文化问答。

    S2.1.4  Provenance and Governance

    S2.1.4 出处与治理

    For each ContextCanvas image, provenance metadata include the known title, creator, provider, source URL, license note, and redistribution status. Missing creator names are preserved as unknown rather than inferred. Synthetic counterfactual edits are clearly labeled as research diagnostics and are not treated as natural benchmark images or training annotations.

    对每张 ContextCanvas 图像，出处元数据包括已知标题、创作者、提供方、来源 URL、许可说明与再分发状态。缺失的创作者姓名保留为未知，而非加以推断。合成的反事实编辑被明确标注为研究诊断用途，不被视为自然的基准图像或训练标注。

    The datasets necessarily reflect the source pool and the expertise of the case contributors and review panel. ContextCanvas is an expert diagnostic benchmark rather than exhaustive coverage of cultures, audiences, or design practice, and a source-grounded rationale does not eliminate every historically or culturally contestable interpretation. CritiqueCanvas similarly permits multiple valid analyses of the same image; its reference response is supervision, not the only legitimate critique.

    这些数据集必然反映来源池以及案例贡献者与评审小组的专业背景。ContextCanvas 是一个专家诊断基准，而非对文化、受众或设计实践的穷尽覆盖；一段基于来源的推理依据也不排除每一种在历史或文化上可争议的解读。CritiqueCanvas 同样允许多种对同一图像的有效分析；其参考回答是监督信号，而非唯一合法的评论。

    S2.2.3 评测与裁判提示

    Human critique-rating instructions. Two human evaluators independently assessed five anonymous critiques for each image without access to model identities or to each other’s ratings. For each critique, they assigned a binary judgment on four criteria: visual grounding, aesthetic specificity, criterion appropriateness, and analytical usefulness. A criterion received one point when the critique was comparatively strong on that dimension within the same image–prompt batch, and zero points when it was comparatively weak. The final human score was one plus the number of satisfied criteria, yielding a score from 1 to 5. Thus, a zero on an individual criterion denotes relative weakness among responses to the same prompt rather than the complete absence of that quality.

    人类评论评分说明。两位人类评估者在不知道模型身份、也看不到对方评分的情况下，独立评估每张图像的五份匿名评论。对每份评论，他们就四项准则给出二元判断：视觉定位、美学具体性、准则恰当性与分析有用性。某项准则在「同一图像-提示批次内相对更强」时得一分，「相对更弱」时得零分。最终人类分数为 1 加上满足的准则数，取值从 1 到 5。因此，单项准则上的零分表示在同一提示的若干回答中相对较弱，而非该品质完全缺失。

    EGA=1N​∑i𝟏​[correcti∧groundedi∧contextLinki].\mathrm{EGA}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i}\mathbf{1}[\mathrm{correct}_{i}\land\mathrm{grounded}_{i}\land\mathrm{contextLink}_{i}]. (S20)

    Among correct predictions, the unsupported rate is

    在正确预测中，无支撑率（unsupported rate）为

    Unsupported=100×#​{correct​but​not​grounded}#​{correct}.\mathrm{Unsupported}=100\times\frac{\#\{\mathrm{correct\ but\ not\ grounded}\}}{\#\{\mathrm{correct}\}}. (S21)

    For the 36 matched counterfactual pairs, Net Correct Update is

    对于 36 组配对的反事实，净正确更新（Net Correct Update）为

    NCU=100×Ncorrect​change−Nreverse​change36.\mathrm{NCU}=100\times\frac{N_{\mathrm{correct\ change}}-N_{\mathrm{reverse\ change}}}{36}. (S22)

    NCU is reported together with correct, reverse, unchanged-correct, and unchanged-wrong counts and original/edit accuracies; it is not interpreted as a standalone causal score. Bootstrap 95% confidence intervals (Efron and Tibshirani, 1993) are used for accuracy and diagnostic rates. Base–specialist comparisons use exact two-sided McNemar tests (McNemar, 1947) on paired predictions with Bonferroni correction (Dunn, 1961) across the four reported pairs. Descriptive case studies receive no significance claim.

    NCU 与正确、反向、未变-正确、未变-错误的计数以及原始/编辑准确率一并报告；它不被解释为独立的因果分数。准确率与诊断率使用 bootstrap 95% 置信区间（Efron and Tibshirani, 1993）。基座-专用模型的比较在配对预测上使用精确双侧 McNemar 检验（McNemar, 1947），并在四组报告配对间做 Bonferroni 校正（Dunn, 1961）。描述性案例研究不做显著性断言。

First, response length is not a proxy for useful aesthetic analysis: Qwen3-VL produces the longest critiques (199.07 words under the evaluation tokenizer), whereas UniPercept is substantially shorter (102.12) but has the highest dimension density (13.26). Second, breadth and controllability vary independently. Gemini obtains the broadest dimension coverage (6.53 dimensions), while Qwen3-VL has the strongest focused-prompt alignment (95.2%); by contrast, ArtQuant and AesExpert follow such prompts much less reliably (66.7% and 34.4%). Third, generic critiques concentrate heavily on composition, content, and emotion across model families. Lighting, color, and brushstrokes appear among the top dimensions for only a few models, while technique, symbolism, and style never enter the generic top three. Thus, models differ markedly in verbosity and response concentration, but their open-ended critiques still rely on a relatively narrow set of salient aesthetic concepts. These patterns complement—rather than replace—the direct quality audit below, since greater length or dimension coverage does not establish that the corresponding claims are relevant, correct, or visually grounded.

    这些统计揭示了三个互补的模式。其一，回答长度并不是有用美学分析的代理：Qwen3-VL 生成的评论最长（在评测分词器下为 199.07 词），而 UniPercept 短得多（102.12），却有着最高的维度密度（13.26）。其二，广度与可控性各自独立变化。Gemini 取得最宽的维度覆盖（6.53 个维度），而 Qwen3-VL 的聚焦提示对齐最强（95.2%）；相比之下，ArtQuant 与 AesExpert 对这类提示的遵循可靠度低得多（66.7% 与 34.4%）。其三，泛泛的评论在各模型系列中都高度集中于构图、内容与情绪。光影、色彩与笔触只在少数模型中进入前列维度，而技法、象征与风格从未进入泛泛评论的前三。因此，各模型在啰嗦程度与回答集中度上差异明显，但其开放式评论仍依赖一组相对狭窄的显著美学概念。这些模式是对下文直接质量审计的补充而非替代，因为更长的篇幅或更广的维度覆盖并不意味着相应论断相关、正确或有视觉依据。
