Abstract (原文)

We present Direct Reward Fine-Tuning (DRaFT), a simple and effective method for fine-tuning diffusion models to maximize differentiable reward functions, such as scores from human preference models. We first show that it is possible to backpropagate the reward function gradient through the full sampling procedure, and that doing so achieves strong performance on a variety of rewards, outperforming reinforcement learning-based approaches. We then propose more efficient variants of DRaFT: DRaFT-K, which truncates backpropagation to only the last K steps of sampling, and DRaFT-LV, which obtains lower-variance gradient estimates for the case when K=1. We show that our methods work well for a variety of reward functions and can be used to substantially improve the aesthetic quality of images generated by Stable Diffusion 1.4. Finally, we draw connections between our approach and prior work, providing a unifying perspective on the design space of gradient-based fine-tuning algorithms.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Diffusion models (Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Song & Ermon, 2019; Ho et al., 2020; Song et al., 2021b; Kingma et al., 2021) have revolutionized generative modeling for continuous data, achieving impressive results across modalities including images, videos, and audio. However, for many use cases, modeling the training data distribution exactly (e.g. diverse images from the web) does not align with the model’s desired behavior (e.g. generating aesthetically pleasing images). To overcome this mismatch, text-to-image diffusion models commonly employ methods like classifier-free guidance (Ho & Salimans, 2021) to improve image-text alignment, and are fine-tuned on curated datasets such as LAION Aesthetics (Schuhmann & Beaumont, 2022) to improve image quality.

扩散模型（Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Song & Ermon, 2019; Ho et al., 2020; Song et al., 2021b; Kingma et al., 2021）革新了连续数据的生成式建模，在图像、视频、音频等多种模态上取得了令人瞩目的成果。然而在许多应用场景中，精确拟合训练数据分布（例如网络上的多样图像）并不等同于模型期望的行为（例如生成具有美感的图像）。为弥合这一错位，文生图扩散模型通常采用无分类器引导（Ho & Salimans, 2021）来改善图文对齐，并在 LAION Aesthetics（Schuhmann & Beaumont, 2022）等精选数据集上微调以提升图像质量。

Within the language modeling community, aligning model behavior with human preferences is widely accomplished through fine-tuning with reinforcement learning (RL), such as with RL from human feedback (RLHF; Ziegler et al. 2019; Stiennon et al. 2020; Ouyang et al. 2022; Bai et al. 2022a). Inspired by RLHF, supervised and reinforcement learning methods have been developed to train diffusion models on human preference models or other rewards (Lee et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023b; Dong et al., 2023; Black et al., 2023; Fan et al., 2023). While showing some success at improving text alignment or image aesthetics on narrow domains, these approaches are sample inefficient and have not been scaled up to the level of generally improving generation style across diverse prompts.

在语言建模领域，使模型行为契合人类偏好普遍通过强化学习（RL）微调实现，例如基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF；Ziegler et al. 2019; Stiennon et al. 2020; Ouyang et al. 2022; Bai et al. 2022a）。受 RLHF 启发，研究者提出了用有监督与强化学习方法在人类偏好模型或其他奖励上训练扩散模型（Lee et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023b; Dong et al., 2023; Black et al., 2023; Fan et al., 2023）。这些方法在狭窄领域内改善文本对齐或图像美感方面有一定成效，但样本效率低下，也未能扩展到在多样化提示词上普遍改善生成风格的规模。

We propose a simple and efficient approach for gradient-based reward fine-tuning based on differentiating through the diffusion sampling process. We first introduce Direct Reward Fine-Tuning (DRaFT), a conceptually straightforward method that backpropagates the reward through the full sampling chain. To keep memory and compute costs low, we use gradient checkpointing (Chen et al., 2016) and optimize LoRA weights (Hu et al., 2022) rather than the full set of model parameters. Then, we introduce modifications to DRaFT that further improve its efficiency and performance. First, we propose DRaFT-KK, a variant that backpropagates through only the last KK steps of sampling to compute the gradient. We show empirically that full backpropagation can result in exploding gradients, and that using the truncated gradient performs substantially better given the same number of training steps. Then, we further improve efficiency by introducing DRaFT-LV, a variant of DRaFT-1 that computes lower-variance gradient estimates by averaging over multiple noise samples.

我们提出一种简洁高效的基于梯度的奖励微调方法，其思路是对扩散采样过程求导。首先介绍直接奖励微调（Direct Reward Fine-Tuning, DRaFT）——一种概念上直截了当的方法，它把奖励沿整条采样链反向传播。为控制显存与计算开销，我们采用梯度检查点（Chen et al., 2016），并优化 LoRA 权重（Hu et al., 2022）而非全部模型参数。随后我们对 DRaFT 加以改造，进一步提升其效率与效果：首先提出 DRaFT-KK，该变体只对采样的最后 KK 步反向传播以求梯度。我们实证发现，完整反向传播会导致梯度爆炸，而在相同训练步数下使用截断梯度效果明显更好。接着我们引入 DRaFT-LV 进一步提升效率，它是 DRaFT-1 的变体，通过对多个噪声样本取平均来获得方差更低的梯度估计。

We apply DRaFT to Stable Diffusion 1.4 (Rombach et al., 2022) and evaluate it on a variety of reward functions and prompt sets. As our methods leverage gradients, they are substantially more efficient than RL-based fine-tuning baselines, for example maximizing scores from the LAION Aesthetics Classifier (Schuhmann & Beaumont, 2022) >200×>\!200\times faster than the RL algorithms from Black et al. (2023). DRaFT-LV is particularly efficient, learning roughly 2×2\times faster than ReFL (Xu et al., 2023), a previous gradient-based fine-tuning method. We scale up DRaFT to train on hundreds of thousands of prompts and show that it improves Stable Diffusion on human preference rewards such as PickScore (Kirstain et al., 2023) and Human Preference Score v2 (Wu et al., 2023a). We further show that we can interpolate between the pre-trained and fine-tuned models by scaling the LoRA weights, and we can combine the effects of multiple rewards by linearly combining fine-tuned LoRA weights. Finally, we showcase how DRaFT can be applied to diverse reward functions including image compressibility and incompressibility, object detection and removal, and generating adversarial examples.

我们把 DRaFT 应用于 Stable Diffusion 1.4（Rombach et al., 2022），并在多种奖励函数与提示词集合上评测。由于利用了梯度，这些方法比基于 RL 的微调基线高效得多，例如在最大化 LAION Aesthetics Classifier（Schuhmann & Beaumont, 2022）得分时比 Black et al.（2023）的 RL 算法快 >200×>\!200\times；DRaFT-LV 尤其高效，比此前的梯度微调方法 ReFL（Xu et al., 2023）快约 2×2\times。我们把 DRaFT 扩展到数十万条提示词的训练规模，证明它能提升 Stable Diffusion 在 PickScore（Kirstain et al., 2023）与 Human Preference Score v2（Wu et al., 2023a）等人类偏好奖励上的表现。我们还展示了通过对 LoRA 权重做缩放可在预训练模型与微调模型之间插值，并通过对微调后的 LoRA 权重做线性组合来叠加多种奖励的效果。最后，我们展示了 DRaFT 可应用于多种奖励函数，包括图像可压缩性与不可压缩性、目标检测与移除，以及生成对抗样本。

2 Related Work

2 相关工作

We provide an extended discussion of related work in Appendix D.

我们在附录 D 给出相关工作的扩展讨论。

Learning human preferences.

学习人类偏好。

Human preference learning trains models on judgements of which behaviors people prefer, rather than on human demonstrations directly (Knox & Stone, 2009; Akrour et al., 2011). This training is commonly done by learning a reward model reflecting human preferences and then learning a policy that maximizes the reward (Christiano et al., 2017; Ibarz et al., 2018). We apply DRaFT to optimize scores from existing preference models, such as PickScore (Kirstain et al., 2023) and Human Preference Score v2 (Wu et al., 2023a), which are trained on human judgements between pairs of images generated by diffusion models for the same prompt.

人类偏好学习以“人们更偏好哪种行为”的判断来训练模型，而不是直接在人类示范数据上训练（Knox & Stone, 2009; Akrour et al., 2011）。这类训练通常先学出一个反映人类偏好的奖励模型，再学一个最大化该奖励的策略（Christiano et al., 2017; Ibarz et al., 2018）。我们用 DRaFT 优化既有偏好模型的得分，例如 PickScore（Kirstain et al., 2023）与 Human Preference Score v2（Wu et al., 2023a），二者均基于人类对同一提示词下扩散模型生成的两张图像所做的判断训练而成。

Guidance (Song et al., 2021b; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021) steers sampling towards images that satisfy a desired objective by adding an auxiliary term to the score function. Various pre-trained models can be used to provide guidance signals, including classifiers, facial recognition models, and object detectors (Bansal et al., 2023). Noisy examples obtained during sampling may be out-of-distribution for the guidance model. Two approaches to mitigate this are: (1) training the guidance model on noisy data (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021) and (2) applying a guidance model to predicted clean images from one-step denoising (Li et al., 2022). However, (1) precludes the use of off-the-shelf pre-trained guidance functions and (2) applies guidance to out-of-distribution blurry images for high noise levels. DRaFT avoids these issues by only applying the reward function to the final generated image.

引导（Guidance）（Song et al., 2021b; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021）通过在得分函数上附加一项辅助项，把采样导向满足期望目标的图像。各类预训练模型都可提供引导信号，包括分类器、人脸识别模型与目标检测器（Bansal et al., 2023）。采样过程中得到的含噪样本对引导模型而言可能属于分布外。缓解该问题有两种途径：（1）在含噪数据上训练引导模型（Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021）；（2）把引导模型作用于单步去噪预测的干净图像（Li et al., 2022）。但（1）排除了直接复用现成预训练引导函数的可能，（2）在高噪声水平下把引导施加于分布外的模糊图像。DRaFT 只把奖励函数作用于最终生成的图像，从而规避了这些问题。

Backpropagation through diffusion sampling.

贯穿扩散采样的反向传播。

Watson et al. (2022) differentiate through sampling to learn sampler hyperparameters (rather than model parameters) in order to reduce inference cost while maintaining perceptual image quality. Fan & Lee (2023) also propose an approach to speed up sampling: they consider doing this by backpropagating through diffusion sampling, but focus on an RL-based approach due to memory and exploding/vanishing gradient concerns. Both Fan & Lee (2023) and Watson et al. (2022) aim to improve sampling speed, rather than optimize arbitrary differentiable rewards, which is our focus. Like DRaFT, Direct Optimization of Diffusion Latents (DOODL; Wallace et al. 2023) backpropagates through sampling to optimize differentiable rewards; however DOODL optimizes the initial noise sample 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T} rather than the model parameters. While this does not require a training phase, it is much slower at inference time because the optimization must be redone for each prompt and metric. Outside of image generation, Wang et al. (2023a) train diffusion model policies for reinforcement learning by backpropagating through sampling.

Watson et al.（2022）通过对采样求导来学习采样器超参数（而非模型参数），以在保持感知图像质量的同时降低推理开销。Fan & Lee（2023）也提出了一种加速采样的方法：他们考虑过通过扩散采样反向传播来实现，但受制于显存与梯度爆炸／消失的顾虑，最终聚焦于基于 RL 的方案。Fan & Lee（2023）与 Watson et al.（2022）的目标都是提升采样速度，而非优化任意可微奖励——后者才是我们的关注点。与 DRaFT 类似，Direct Optimization of Diffusion Latents（DOODL; Wallace et al. 2023）也通过采样反向传播来优化可微奖励；不过 DOODL 优化的是初始噪声样本 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T} 而非模型参数。该方法虽无需训练阶段，但推理时慢得多，因为每条提示词与每个指标都要重新做一遍优化。在图像生成之外，Wang et al.（2023a）通过对采样反向传播来训练用于强化学习的扩散模型策略。

Reward Fine-Tuning with Supervised Learning.

用有监督学习做奖励微调。

Lee et al. (2023) and Wu et al. (2023b) use supervised approaches to fine-tune diffusion models on rewards. These methods generate images with the pre-trained model and then fine-tune on the images while weighting examples according to the reward function or discarding low-reward examples. Unlike DRaFT or RL methods, the model is not trained online on examples generated by the current policy. However, Dong et al. (2023) use an online version of this approach where examples are re-generated over multiple rounds of training, which can be viewed as a simple kind of reinforcement learning.

Lee et al.（2023）与 Wu et al.（2023b）用有监督方式在奖励上微调扩散模型。这些方法先用预训练模型生成图像，再在这些图像上微调，并依奖励函数对样本加权或丢弃低奖励样本。与 DRaFT 或 RL 方法不同，模型并非在由当前策略生成的样本上在线训练。不过 Dong et al.（2023）使用了该方法的在线版本，在多轮训练中反复重新生成样本，可视为一种简单的强化学习。

Reward Fine-Tuning with Reinforcement Learning.

用强化学习做奖励微调。

Fan & Lee (2023) interpret the denoising process as a multi-step decision-making task and use policy gradient algorithms to fine-tune diffusion samplers. Building on it, Black et al. (2023) and Fan et al. (2023) use policy gradient algorithms to fine-tune diffusion models for arbitrary black-box objectives. Rather than optimizing model parameters, Hao et al. (2022) apply RL to improve the input prompts. RL approaches are flexible because they do not require differentiable rewards. However, in practice many reward functions are differentiable, or can be implemented or approximated in a differentiable way, and thus analytic gradients are often available. In such cases, using reinforcement learning discards useful information.

Fan & Lee（2023）把去噪过程解释为多步决策任务，并用策略梯度算法微调扩散采样器。在此基础上，Black et al.（2023）与 Fan et al.（2023）用策略梯度算法针对任意黑盒目标微调扩散模型。Hao et al.（2022）则把 RL 用于改进输入提示词，而非优化模型参数。RL 方法的优势在于无需奖励可微。但实际上许多奖励函数本身可微，或以可微方式实现、近似，解析梯度往往唾手可得。在这种情况下使用强化学习，等于丢弃了有用的信息。

Reward Feedback Learning (ReFL).

奖励反馈学习（ReFL）。

ReFL (Xu et al., 2023) uses reward function gradients to fine-tune a diffusion model. It evaluates the reward on the one-step predicted clean image, r⁡(𝐱^0,𝐜)r(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{0},\mathbf{c}) from a randomly-chosen step tt along the denoising trajectory rather than on the final image, as DRaFT does. While ReFL performs similarly to DRaFT-1, DRaFT-LV is substantially more efficient, training approximately 2×2\times faster. We further discuss how ReFL relates to DRaFT in Section 4.

ReFL（Xu et al., 2023）利用奖励函数梯度微调扩散模型。它在去噪轨迹上随机选取某一步 tt 所对应的一步预测干净图像 r⁡(𝐱^0,𝐜)r(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{0},\mathbf{c}) 上评估奖励，而不是像 DRaFT 那样在最终图像上评估。ReFL 的表现与 DRaFT-1 相近，但 DRaFT-LV 效率明显更高，训练速度约快 2×2\times。我们在第 4 节进一步讨论 ReFL 与 DRaFT 的关系。

First, we consider solving Eq. 2 by computing ∇𝜽r​(sample​(𝜽,𝐜,𝐱T),𝐜)\nabla_{{\bm{\theta}}}r(\text{sample}({\bm{\theta}},\mathbf{c},\mathbf{x}_{T}),\mathbf{c}) and using gradient ascent. LoRA.

LoRA。

Rather than fine-tuning the full set of model parameters, Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) (Hu et al., 2022) freezes the weights of the pre-trained model, and injects new low-rank weight matrices alongside the original model weights, whose contributions are summed to produce the adapted model outputs. Mathematically, for a layer with base parameters 𝐖0\mathbf{W}_{0} whose forward pass yields 𝐡=𝐖0​𝐱\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{W}_{0}\mathbf{x}, the LoRA adapted layer is 𝐡=𝐖0​𝐱+𝐁𝐀𝐱\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{W}_{0}\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}, where 𝐁𝐀\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A} is a low-rank matrix. LoRA dramatically reduces the number of parameters that need to be optimized, which reduces the memory requirements of fine-tuning. Another benefit is that, because the information learned by the fine-tuned model is contained within the LoRA parameters, we can conveniently combine fine-tuned models by taking linear combinations of the LoRA parameters (Figure 5, right) and interpolate between the original and fine-tuned model by re-scaling the LoRA parameters (Figure 7).

低秩适配（LoRA）（Hu et al., 2022）不微调全部模型参数，而是冻结预训练模型的权重，并在原始模型权重旁注入新的低秩权重矩阵，两者贡献相加得到适配后的模型输出。形式化地说，对基础参数为 𝐖0\mathbf{W}_{0}、前向传播为 𝐡=𝐖0​𝐱\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{W}_{0}\mathbf{x} 的某一层，加入 LoRA 后的层为 𝐡=𝐖0​𝐱+𝐁𝐀𝐱\mathbf{h}=\mathbf{W}_{0}\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{x}，其中 𝐁𝐀\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A} 是低秩矩阵。LoRA 极大减少了需要优化的参数量，从而降低微调的显存需求。另一个好处是：由于微调模型学到的信息都包含在 LoRA 参数之内，我们可以方便地通过对 LoRA 参数做线性组合来合并多个微调模型（图 5 右），并通过对 LoRA 参数重新缩放来在原始模型与微调模型之间插值（图 7）。

Gradient Checkpointing.

梯度检查点。

Gradient checkpointing (Gruslys et al., 2016; Chen et al., 2016) reduces the memory cost of storing activations for use during backprop at the cost of increased compute, by storing only a subset of activations in memory and re-computing the others on the fly. Specifically, we only store the input latent for each denoising step, and re-materialize the UNet activations during backprop. Implementing checkpointing in JAX (Bradbury et al., 2018) is as simple as adding @jax.checkpoint to the body of the sampling loop.

梯度检查点（Gruslys et al., 2016; Chen et al., 2016）以计算量换取显存：只在内存中保存部分激活值，其余在需要时重新计算，从而降低为反向传播保存激活值的显存开销。具体地，我们只保存每个去噪步的输入潜变量，并在反向传播时重新生成 UNet 激活值。在 JAX（Bradbury et al., 2018）中实现检查点很简单，只需在采样循环体上加 @jax.checkpoint。

DRaFT-KK.

DRaFT-KK。

While gradient checkpointing makes it possible to backpropagate through the full sampling chain, we found that optimization speed and overall performance can be substantially improved by truncating backprop through only the last KK sampling steps. We call the resulting approach DRaFT-KK, illustrated in Figure 2. Truncating backprop reduces the compute needed per step by decreasing the number of backward passes through the UNet. Surprisingly, it also improves training efficiency per-step, which we investigate further in Section 5.2. For small KK (e.g., K=1K=1), the memory cost of unrolling is small, and we do not need gradient checkpointing.

梯度检查点使贯穿完整采样链的反向传播成为可能，但我们发现，只把反向传播截断到最后 KK 个采样步，可大幅提升优化速度与整体效果。我们把由此得到的方法称为 DRaFT-KK，如图 2 所示。截断反向传播减少了通过 UNet 的反向次数，因而降低了每步所需的计算量。出人意料的是，它还提升了每步的训练效率，我们在 5.2 节对此做进一步分析。当 KK 较小时（例如 K=1K=1），展开的显存开销很小，无需使用梯度检查点。

DRaFT-LV.

DRaFT-LV。

Empirically, we found that simply setting K=1K=1 (i.e., only differentiating through the last sampling step) results in the best reward vs. compute tradeoff. Here, we propose a method for improving the efficiency of DRaFT-1 further by reducing the variance of the gradient estimate. We call this low-variance estimator DRaFT-LV. The key idea is to use the forward diffusion process to produce additional examples to train on without re-generating new images. Specifically, we noise the generated image nn times, and use the summed reward gradient over these examples. Although DRaFT-LV adds nn additional forward and backward passes through the UNet and reward model, in practice this is fairly little overhead compared to the TT UNet calls already needed for sampling. We found that using n=2n=2 is around 2×2\times more efficient than DRaFT-1 while adding around 10% compute overhead for our reward functions.

实验表明，直接取 K=1K=1（即只对最后一个采样步求导）在奖励与计算的权衡上最优。这里我们提出一种通过降低梯度估计方差来进一步提升 DRaFT-1 效率的方法，称之为低方差估计器 DRaFT-LV。核心思路是借助前向扩散过程产生额外的训练样本，而无需重新生成新图像。具体地，我们对生成的图像加噪 nn 次，并把这些样本上的奖励梯度求和。尽管 DRaFT-LV 增加了 nn 次通过 UNet 与奖励模型的额外前向与反向传播，但与采样本身所需的 TT 次 UNet 调用相比，这部分开销在实践中相当小。我们发现，使用 n=2n=2 比 DRaFT-1 高效约 2×2\times，而对我们的奖励函数只增加约 10% 的计算开销。

General Reward Fine-Tuning Algorithm.

通用奖励微调算法。

Algorithm 1 presents a unified framework encompassing several gradient-based approaches for diffusion fine-tuning. In particular, it includes as special cases DRaFT, DRaFT-KK, DRaFT-LV, and ReFL (Xu et al., 2023), which differ in how the gradient is computed. DRaFT-KK inserts a stop-gradient operation 𝐱t=stop_grad​(𝐱t)\mathbf{x}_{t}=\texttt{stop\_grad}(\mathbf{x}_{t}) at sampling iteration KK, to ensure that the gradient is only computed through the last KK steps (as the gradient does not flow through stop_grad); vanilla DRaFT can be obtained by setting K=TK=T. ReFL inserts a stop gradient and breaks out of the sampling loop early, returning a one-step predicted clean image. Additionally, it does so at a random timestep towards the end of sampling (we use m=20m=20 for 50-step sampling). In this unifying framework, ReFL with m=1m=1 is equivalent to DRaFT-11. Lastly, DRaFT-LV averages the last-step gradient over multiple examples with different noises to reduce variance. Note that in practice we use Adam and LoRA, not shown in the algorithm for simplicity.

算法 1 给出了一个统一框架，涵盖了多种基于梯度的扩散微调方法。特别地，DRaFT、DRaFT-KK、DRaFT-LV 与 ReFL（Xu et al., 2023）都是它的特例，区别在于梯度的计算方式。DRaFT-KK 在采样迭代 KK 处插入停止梯度操作 𝐱t=stop_grad​(𝐱t)\mathbf{x}_{t}=\texttt{stop\_grad}(\mathbf{x}_{t})，以保证梯度只经最后 KK 步计算（因为梯度无法穿过 stop_grad）；令 K=TK=T 即可得到原版 DRaFT。ReFL 则插入停止梯度并提前跳出采样循环，返回一步预测的干净图像，而且它是在采样末端附近随机选取的时间步上这样做的（50 步采样时我们用 m=20m=20）。在这一统一框架下，取 m=1m=1 的 ReFL 等价于 DRaFT-11。最后，DRaFT-LV 对多个施加不同噪声的样本的最后一步梯度取平均以降低方差。注意实践中我们使用 Adam 与 LoRA，为简洁起见未在算法中列出。

5 Experiments

5 实验

In this section, we show that DRaFT can be applied to a wide range of reward functions, and that it substantially outperforms prior reward fine-tuning methods. We used Stable Diffusion 1.4 as the base diffusion model. As it is a latent diffusion model (Rombach et al., 2022), applying DRaFT involves backpropagating through both the sampling process that produces the final latent representation and the decoder used to obtain the image. DRaFT is applicable to any differentiable sampler; in our experiments, we used DDIM (Song et al., 2021a), but we found that ancestral sampling performs similarly. We used 50 sampling steps, so DRaFT-50 corresponds to backpropagating through the full sampling chain (i.e., vanilla DRaFT). We used classifier-free guidance weight 7.5. Experimental details, hyperparameters, and additional results are provided in Appendices A and B.

本节表明 DRaFT 可适用于广泛的奖励函数，并显著优于此前的奖励微调方法。我们使用 Stable Diffusion 1.4 作为基座扩散模型。由于它是潜变量扩散模型（Rombach et al., 2022），应用 DRaFT 需要同时贯穿生成最终潜变量表示的采样过程和用于得到图像的解码器做反向传播。DRaFT 适用于任何可微采样器；实验中我们使用 DDIM（Song et al., 2021a），但发现祖先采样表现相近。我们采用 50 步采样，因此 DRaFT-50 对应于贯穿完整采样链的反向传播（即原版 DRaFT），无分类器引导权重取 7.5。实验细节、超参数与补充结果见附录 A 与 B。

Scaling and Mixing LoRA Weights.

LoRA 权重的缩放与混合。

It is possible to control the strength of the fine-tuning simply by scaling the LoRA parameters: multiplying them by a scalar α<1\alpha<1 moves the adapted parameters closer to the original, pre-trained model. Figure  7 (Left) shows that this method smoothly interpolates between the pre-trained and DRaFT models. The LoRA scale could become a useful hyperparameter for controlling generations from DRaFT models similarly to how guidance strength is currently used. While we experimented with KL regularization and early stopping as alternative ways of reducing reward overfitting (see Figure 29 in Appendix B.8), we found LoRA scaling to work best.

只需缩放 LoRA 参数就能控制微调强度：把它们乘以标量 α<1\alpha<1，即可让适配后的参数向原始预训练模型靠近。图 7（左）显示该方法能在预训练模型与 DRaFT 模型之间平滑插值。LoRA 缩放有望成为控制 DRaFT 模型生成结果的有用超参数，其地位类似当前的引导强度。我们也尝试了 KL 正则化与早停作为缓解奖励过拟合的替代手段（见附录 B.8 图 29），但发现 LoRA 缩放效果最好。

We also probed the compositional properties of LoRA weights adapted for different rewards. One can combine the effects of multiple reward functions by interpolating the LoRA weights of the respective DRaFT models (Figure 5, Right). In Figure 7 (Right), we show images generated using linear combinations of LoRA parameters trained separately for the PickScore and HPSv2 rewards, using scaling coefficients α\alpha and β\beta. The resulting images mix the semantics of HPSv2 and PickScore, and are simultaneously more saturated and detailed than the pre-trained model outputs. While it would also be possible to instead directly sum the score functions of the models (similar to Liu et al. 2022), this would increase generation time proportionally to the number of combined models.

我们还考察了针对不同奖励适配的 LoRA 权重的组合性质：通过对各 DRaFT 模型的 LoRA 权重插值，可以叠加多个奖励函数的效果（图 5 右）。图 7（右）展示了用缩放系数 α\alpha 与 β\beta 对分别为 PickScore 和 HPSv2 奖励单独训练的 LoRA 参数做线性组合后生成的图像。所得图像融合了 HPSv2 与 PickScore 的语义，同时比预训练模型的输出更饱和、更精细。当然也可以直接把各模型的得分函数相加（类似 Liu et al. 2022），但那样生成时间会随合并模型数量成比例增加。

Understanding the Impact of KK.

理解 KK 的影响。

图 8：梯度范数

Interestingly, we found that truncated backpropagation improves sample efficiency as well as compute efficiency. Our analysis suggests that gradients explode as KK increases, which may lead to optimization difficulties for large KK (see Figure 8). We found that gradient clipping somewhat alleviates this issue (see Figure 28 in Appendix B.7), but does not fully close the gap. In Figure 10, we show an ablation over KK for aesthetic fine-tuning: performance degrades as KK increases for K>10K>10. In Figure 10, we show that although DRaFT-1 only computes gradients through the final generation step, it generalizes across time and affects the whole sampling chain. We compare samples generated with different LoRA start iterations MM, where we applied the LoRA-adapted diffusion parameters for the last MM steps of the sampling chain, while in the first T−MT-M steps we used the pre-trained Stable Diffusion parameters without LoRA adaptation. We observe that adaptation does not happen only at the end of sampling (also see Figure 26 in App. B.7). We also investigated the opposite scenario, where the LoRA-adapted parameters are used in the first MM steps, after which LoRA is “turned off” and we use only the original pre-trained parameters for the remaining T−MT-M steps (see Figure 27 App. B.7), which shows that it is also important to apply the LoRA parameters early in sampling.

有意思的是，我们发现截断反向传播同时提升了样本效率与计算效率。分析表明，梯度会随 KK 增大而爆炸，这可能给大的 KK 带来优化困难（见图 8）。我们发现梯度裁剪在一定程度上缓解了该问题（见附录 B.7 图 28），但未能完全填补差距。图 10 给出了美学微调下对 KK 的消融：当 KK 在 K>10K>10 范围内增大时，性能会下降。图 10 还表明，尽管 DRaFT-1 只对最后一步求导，它的影响会跨时间泛化并作用于整条采样链。我们比较了采用不同 LoRA 起始迭代 MM 生成的样本：在采样链最后 MM 步使用 LoRA 适配后的扩散参数，而前 T−MT-M 步使用未经 LoRA 适配的预训练 Stable Diffusion 参数。我们观察到适配并非只发生在采样末端（另见附录 B.7 图 26）。我们也考察了相反的情形：在前 MM 步使用 LoRA 适配参数，随后“关闭”LoRA、在剩余 T−MT-M 步只用原始预训练参数（见附录 B.7 图 27），结果表明在采样早期施加 LoRA 参数同样重要。

Inspired by Black et al. (2023), we also fine-tuned a diffusion model to generate easily-compressible images. While the JPEG compression and decompression algorithms available in common libraries are not differentiable by default, they can be implemented in a differentiable manner using deep learning libraries like PyTorch (Paszke et al., 2019) or JAX (Bradbury et al., 2018). Given a prompt 𝐜\mathbf{c}, we pass the output of the diffusion model, 𝐱0=sample​(𝜽,𝐜,𝐱T)\mathbf{x}_{0}=\text{sample}({\bm{\theta}},\mathbf{c},\mathbf{x}_{T}) through differentiable compression and decompression algorithms to obtain d⁡(c⁡(𝐱0))d(c(\mathbf{x}_{0})), and minimize the Euclidean distance between the original and reconstructed images. This encourages the diffusion model to produce simple images. The results using DRaFT to fine-tune for JPEG compressibility are shown in Figure 11 (Top): these images have simple, uniform-color backgrounds and low-frequency foregrounds. In addition, negating the compressibility loss yields incompressible images, shown in Figure 11 (Bottom), that have complex, high-frequency foregrounds and backgrounds. Additional details are provided in Appendix B.3.

受 Black et al.（2023）启发，我们也微调扩散模型使其生成易于压缩的图像。常见库中的 JPEG 压缩与解压算法默认不可微，但可以用 PyTorch（Paszke et al., 2019）或 JAX（Bradbury et al., 2018）等深度学习库以可微方式实现。给定提示词 𝐜\mathbf{c}，我们把扩散模型的输出 𝐱0=sample​(𝜽,𝐜,𝐱T)\mathbf{x}_{0}=\text{sample}({\bm{\theta}},\mathbf{c},\mathbf{x}_{T}) 送入可微的压缩与解压算法得到 d⁡(c⁡(𝐱0))d(c(\mathbf{x}_{0}))，并最小化原始图像与重建图像之间的欧氏距离。这会促使扩散模型生成简单图像。图 11（上）给出了用 DRaFT 面向 JPEG 可压缩性微调的结果：这些图像背景简单、色彩均匀，前景为低频内容。此外，对可压缩性损失取负可得到不可压缩的图像，如图 11（下）所示，其前景与背景都复杂且高频。更多细节见附录 B.3。

Adversarial Examples.

对抗样本。

图 13：针对提示词 bear、dog、mouse、目标类别为 cat 的扩散对抗样本。

Our framework also provides an avenue to study the inductive biases of both diffusion models and pre-trained classifiers: we can fine-tune a diffusion model such that images generated based on a prompt for a class yy (e.g., “mouse”) are classified as a different class y′y^{\prime} (e.g., “cat”) by a ResNet-50 pretrained on ImageNet. As the fine-tuning reward, we use the negative cross-entropy to the target class. From the qualitative results shown in Figure 13, we observe that this classifier is texture-biased (Geirhos et al., 2019), as the images generated by the fine-tuned model incorporate cat-like textures while keeping the animal shapes mostly unchanged.

我们的框架也为研究扩散模型与预训练分类器的归纳偏置提供了途径：我们可以微调扩散模型，使基于某类别 yy（例如“mouse”）提示词生成的图像被在 ImageNet 上预训练的 ResNet-50 判为另一类别 y′y^{\prime}（例如“cat”）。微调奖励取对目标类别的负交叉熵。从图 13 的定性结果可以看出，该分类器具有纹理偏置（Geirhos et al., 2019）：微调后模型生成的图像融入了猫一般的纹理，而动物形状基本保持不变。

6 Conclusion

6 结论

We introduced an efficient framework for fine-tuning diffusion models on differentiable rewards by leveraging reward gradients. Our first method, Direct Reward Fine-Tuning (DRaFT), backpropagates through the full sampling procedure, and achieves strong performance on a variety of reward functions. We then propose DRaFT-KK and DRaFT-LV, variants which improve efficiency via truncated backpropagation through time. In addition, we draw connections between our approach and prior work such as ReFL (Xu et al., 2023), to provide a unifying perspective on the design space of gradient-based reward fine-tuning algorithms. We show that various methods can be obtained as special cases of a more general algorithm, depending on the whether—and where—stop_gradient operations are included. We hope that our work will inspire the development of improved techniques for reward fine-tuning; just as RLHF has become crucial for deploying large language models, we believe that reward fine-tuning may become a key step for improving image generation models.

我们提出了一个高效的框架，利用奖励梯度在可微奖励上微调扩散模型。我们的第一个方法——直接奖励微调（DRaFT）——贯穿完整采样过程反向传播，在多种奖励函数上取得强劲表现。随后提出 DRaFT-KK 与 DRaFT-LV 两个变体，通过随时间截断反向传播提升效率。此外，我们把本方法与 ReFL（Xu et al., 2023）等既有工作联系起来，为基于梯度的奖励微调算法的设计空间提供了统一视角。我们表明，根据是否引入 stop_gradient 操作以及引入的位置，各类方法都可作为某个更通用算法的特例得到。我们希望本工作能启发更优奖励微调技术的发展；正如 RLHF 已成为部署大语言模型的关键环节，我们相信奖励微调也将成为改进图像生成模型的关键一步。

    Optimization.

    优化。

    We use the AdamW optimizer (Loshchilov & Hutter, 2019) with β1=0.9\beta_{1}=0.9, β2=0.999\beta_{2}=0.999, and a weight decay of 0.1. During training, we convert the pre-trained Stable Diffusion parameters to bfloat16 to reduce the memory footprint, but use float32 for the LoRA parameters being trained.

    A.2 Reward Function Details

    A.2 奖励函数细节

    The LAION aesthetic predictor is trained to rate images on a scale of 1 through 10. The predictor consists of a feedforward neural network on top of a CLIP (Radford et al., 2021) image encoder pre-trained on a variety of human-labeled sources.

    LAION 美学预测器按 1 到 10 的标度为图像打分。该预测器由一个前馈神经网络构成，构建在 CLIP（Radford et al., 2021）图像编码器之上，后者在多种人工标注来源上预训练。

    Human Preference Score v2 (HPSv2; Wu et al. 2023a) is trained on prompts from DiffusionDB (Wang et al., 2023b) and COCO Captions (Chen et al., 2015). Images are generated from each using a wide range of generative models. Human annotators then rate which of two images is preferred for a prompt. The reward model is OpenCLIP-H (Beaumont et al., 2022) fine-tuned on the preference judgements. Unlike the aesthetic reward, HPSv2 takes into account the prompt as well as the image.

    Human Preference Score v2（HPSv2; Wu et al. 2023a）在 DiffusionDB（Wang et al., 2023b）与 COCO Captions（Chen et al., 2015）的提示词上训练。每个提示词都用大量生成模型生成图像，随后由人类标注者评判同一提示词下两张图像中更偏好哪一张。奖励模型是在这些偏好判断上微调的 OpenCLIP-H（Beaumont et al., 2022）。与美学奖励不同，HPSv2 同时考虑提示词与图像。

    PickScore (Kirstain et al., 2023) also uses OpenCLIP-H fine-tuned on preference data. However, the data is collected in a more organic way through a web app, where users can generate and then rate pairs of images generated from different diffusion models with different sampler hyperparameters.

    PickScore（Kirstain et al., 2023）同样使用在偏好数据上微调的 OpenCLIP-H。但其数据是通过一个网页应用以更自然的方式采集的：用户可以生成图像，并对来自不同扩散模型、不同采样器超参数的两张图像做出评价。

    A.3 Baselines

    A.3 基线

    Denoising Diffusion Policy Optimization (DDPO; Black et al. 2023) essentially applies the REINFORCE policy gradient algorithm (Williams, 1992) to diffusion model generation. The method trains the model to increase the likelihoods of actions (i.e. sampling steps) that produce high-reward samples. DDPO employs additional tricks such as importance weight clipping, reward normalization, and classifier-free guidance-aware training to improve results. We show results from DDPO-IS, which extends DDPO to use an importance sampling estimator for learning on examples generated from a stale policy, enabling multiple optimization updates per reward query. Empirically, DDPO was found to outperform previous supervised approaches like reward weighted regression (Lee et al., 2023). Rather than using the results in Figure 4 of the original paper, we obtained results from direct correspondence with the authors, as their original aesthetic reward implementation had a bug.33 3 See https://github.com/kvablack/ddpo-pytorch/issues/3#issuecomment-1634723127

    去噪扩散策略优化（DDPO; Black et al. 2023）本质上是把 REINFORCE 策略梯度算法（Williams, 1992）应用到扩散模型生成上。该方法训练模型以提高产生高奖励样本的“动作”（即采样步）的概率。DDPO 还采用了重要性权重裁剪、奖励归一化、以及感知无分类器引导的训练等技巧来改善结果。我们给出的是 DDPO-IS 的结果，它把 DDPO 扩展为使用重要性采样估计器，从而可在来自陈旧策略的样本上学习，实现每次奖励查询执行多轮优化更新。实验上 DDPO 被发现优于奖励加权回归（Lee et al., 2023）等此前的有监督方法。我们没有采用原论文图 4 的结果，而是通过与作者直接通信获得，因为他们原始的美学奖励实现存在缺陷。33 3 见 https://github.com/kvablack/ddpo-pytorch/issues/3#issuecomment-1634723127

    Direct Optimization of Diffusion Latents (DOODL).

    扩散潜变量直接优化（DOODL）。

    DOODL (Wallace et al., 2023) backpropagates through diffusion sampling to update the initial noise 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T} so that the resulting generation produces a high reward. Rather than using an invertible diffusion algorithm to reduce memory costs as in Wallace et al. (2023), we simply use gradient checkpointing as with DRaFT. We optimize 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T} with Adam (which worked slightly better for us than momentum only) over 20 steps, which makes generation approximately 60×60\times slower than standard sampling. Following Wallace et al. (2023), we renormalize the latent at every step after the Adam update.

    DOODL（Wallace et al., 2023）通过扩散采样反向传播来更新初始噪声 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T}，使生成结果获得高奖励。我们没有像 Wallace et al.（2023）那样用可逆扩散算法来降低显存开销，而是像 DRaFT 一样简单地使用梯度检查点。我们用 Adam 对 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T} 优化 20 步（对我们而言比仅用动量略好），这使得生成比标准采样慢约 60×60\times。依照 Wallace et al.（2023），我们在每次 Adam 更新后对潜变量重新归一化。

    B.8 “Over-Optimization” and Reward Hacking

    B.8 “过度优化”与奖励破解

    Improving Cross-Reward Generalization.

    改善跨奖励泛化。

    DRaFT can cause models to overfit to the reward function, leading to high-reward but subjectively lower-quality images. Here, we explore regularization methods to reduce reward overfitting.

    DRaFT 可能导致模型对奖励函数过拟合，产生高奖励但主观质量更差的图像。这里我们探索缓解奖励过拟合的正则化方法。

    As an approximate quantitative measure for over-optimization, we trained models on one reward function (HPSv2) and evaluated their generalization to another reward (PickScore). We considered three strategies to mitigate reward overfitting:

    作为过度优化的近似定量度量，我们在一个奖励函数（HPSv2）上训练模型，并评估其向另一个奖励（PickScore）的泛化。我们考虑了三种缓解奖励过拟合的策略：

  • •

    LoRA Scaling: multiplying the LoRA weights by a constant <1<1 (see Section 5.2), which interpolates between the pre-trained model and fine-tuned model, similarly to Wortsman et al. (2022).

    • • LoRA 缩放：把 LoRA 权重乘以常数 <1<1（见 5.2 节），在预训练模型与微调模型之间插值，类似于 Wortsman et al.（2022）。

  • •

    Early Stopping: evaluating various checkpoints across the training run instead of only the last one; early ones will achieve lower train reward but hopefully generalize better.

    • • 早停：评估训练过程中不同检查点而不仅是最后一个；较早的检查点训练奖励较低，但有望泛化得更好。

  • •

    KL Regularization: similarly to Fan et al. (2023), we add a loss term to the model that penalizes the KL divergence between the pre-trained and fine-tuned model distributions. This amounts to a squared error term between the fine-tuned and pre-trained predicted ϵ\bm{\epsilon}, that is, βKL​‖ϵfinetuned−ϵpretrained‖22\beta_{\text{KL}}\|\bm{\epsilon}_{\text{finetuned}}-\bm{\epsilon}_{\text{pretrained}}\|_{2}^{2}, where βKL\beta_{\text{KL}} is the KL regularization coefficient. We computed the KL term using the predicted ϵ\bm{\epsilon} in the last denoising step. We trained several separate models, each with a different weight on the KL loss, which is a disadvantage over the previous methods that can be applied to a single training run.

    • • KL 正则化：类似 Fan et al.（2023），我们给模型加入一项损失，惩罚预训练模型与微调模型分布之间的 KL 散度。这相当于对微调与预训练预测的 ϵ\bm{\epsilon} 取平方误差项，即 βKL​‖ϵfinetuned−ϵpretrained‖22\beta_{\text{KL}}\|\bm{\epsilon}_{\text{finetuned}}-\bm{\epsilon}_{\text{pretrained}}\|_{2}^{2}，其中 βKL\beta_{\text{KL}} 是 KL 正则化系数。我们用最后一个去噪步预测的 ϵ\bm{\epsilon} 来计算 KL 项。我们训练了若干独立模型，每个对 KL 损失取不同权重，这是相较前两种方法的一个劣势——后两者可在单次训练中应用。

    All models used DRaFT-LV with the large-scale hyperparameters (see Table 1). The results are shown in Figure 29. Both early stopping and KL regularization are commonly used to reduce reward overfitting in large language model preference tuning, but we found LoRA scaling to be more effective than the other approaches.

    所有模型都使用 DRaFT-LV 与大规模超参数（见表 1），结果见图 29。早停与 KL 正则化都是大语言模型偏好调优中缓解奖励过拟合的常用手段，但我们发现 LoRA 缩放比其他方法更有效。

    Attempts to Increase Diversity.

    提升多样性的尝试。

    A common result of reward hacking is a collapse to a single, high-reward image. Here, we describe two of our attempts to address this lack of diversity. First, we tried adding dropout to the reward function (in this case, the aesthetic classifier); however, we found that even large dropout rates such as 0.95 led to diversity collapse, as shown in Figure 30 (Left). Next, we tried adding a term to the reward that measures the dissimilarity between images generated in the same minibatch—this encourages the model to generate different images for different prompts and noise samples. Ideally, one would take the mean dissimilarity over all pairs of examples in a minibatch, yielding (B2)B\choose 2 combinations where BB is the minibatch size. However, for computational tractability, we used a simpler approach, where we formed pairs by reversing the elements of a minibatch and computing elementwise dissimilarities, giving us B/2B/2 pairs. We tried two measures of dissimilarity: 1) the Euclidean distance, and 2) the Learned Perceptual Image Patch Similarity (LPIPS) (Zhang et al., 2018). Neither was satisfactory to increase diversity: in Figure 30 (Right), we show images generated using a large weight on the LPIPS diversity term, which increased inner-batch diversity at the expense of the aesthetic score.

    奖励破解的一个常见后果是坍缩为单张高奖励图像。这里介绍我们针对多样性缺失所做的两次尝试。首先，我们尝试给奖励函数（此处为美学分类器）加入 dropout；然而我们发现，即便 dropout 率高达 0.95，仍会出现多样性坍缩，如图 30（左）所示。其次，我们尝试在奖励中加入一项度量同一小批量内生成图像之间差异性的项——这鼓励模型对不同的提示词与噪声样本生成不同的图像。理想情况下应对小批量内所有样本对的平均差异性取均值，得到 (B2)B\choose 2 种组合，其中 BB 是小批量大小。但为便于计算，我们采用了更简单的方法：把小批量元素逆序后配对，逐元素计算差异性，得到 B/2B/2 对。我们试了两种差异性度量：1）欧氏距离；2）学习感知图像块相似度（LPIPS）（Zhang et al., 2018）。两者都未能令人满意地提升多样性：图 30（右）给出了对 LPIPS 多样性项取较大权重时生成的图像，它提升了批内多样性，代价是美学得分下降。

    图 30：即便在美学分类器中使用很大的 dropout 率，奖励破解仍然发生（左）。引入鼓励多样性的奖励项则可能导致图像过度风格化（右）。

    B.11 Fine-Tuning for Safety

    B.11 面向安全性的微调

    The datasets on which large diffusion models are trained often contain NSFW content, which poses challenges for the deployment of safe models. Safety measures can be implemented at various stages of the text-to-image pipeline: 1) at the text prompt level (e.g., refusing NSFW words); 2) during the generative process (e.g., encouraging the model to output safe images); and 3) post-hoc elimination of NSFW images that have been generated. These approaches are complementary, and in practice we imagine that all three would be used in concert. Here, we focus on stage (2), by fine-tuning diffusion models using feedback from a pre-trained NSFW classifier. This approach is particularly useful for ambiguous prompts that may lead to either SFW or NSFW generations; for example, the prompt “swimsuit” can refer to images of people wearing swimsuits, or to just the article of clothing itself (the latter being more safe-for-work). We found that, before fine-tuning, the model mostly generated images of people wearing swimsuits, while after fine-tuning, the model instead generated images of lay-flat swimsuits.

    大型扩散模型的训练数据集常含 NSFW 内容，这给安全模型的部署带来挑战。安全措施可以在文生图流水线的多个阶段实施：1）在文本提示词层面（例如拒绝 NSFW 词汇）；2）在生成过程中（例如引导模型输出安全图像）；3）事后剔除已生成的 NSFW 图像。这些方法互为补充，实践中我们设想三者会协同使用。这里我们聚焦第（2）阶段，用预训练 NSFW 分类器的反馈来微调扩散模型。该方法对那些可能生成 SFW 或 NSFW 结果的歧义提示词尤为有用；例如提示词“swimsuit”既可指穿着泳装的人，也可仅指这件衣物本身（后者更符合工作场所安全要求）。我们发现，微调前模型主要生成穿着泳装的人像，而微调后模型转而生成平铺摆放的泳装。

    Appendix D Extended Related Work

    附录 D 扩展相关工作

    Diffusion Models.

    扩散模型。

    Denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs; Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015; Song & Ermon 2019; Song et al. 2021b; Ho et al. 2020) are a class of generative models that have become the de-facto standard for most continuous data modalities, including images (Ramesh et al., 2021), videos (Ho et al., 2022; Singer et al., 2023), audio (Liu et al., 2023), and 3D models (Zeng et al., 2022; Poole et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2023). Text-to-image diffusion models, which generate images conditioned on text prompts, have become prominent tools with the advent of models such as GLIDE (Nichol et al., 2021), DALLE-2 (Ramesh et al., 2022), Imagen (Saharia et al., 2022; Ho et al., 2022), and Latent Diffusion (Rombach et al., 2022). In this paper we focus on fine-tuning text-to-image models, but DRaFT is general and could be applied to other diffusion models or fine-tuning tasks, and to different domains such as audio or video.

    去噪扩散概率模型（DDPM; Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015; Song & Ermon 2019; Song et al. 2021b; Ho et al. 2020）是一类生成模型，已成为图像（Ramesh et al., 2021）、视频（Ho et al., 2022; Singer et al., 2023）、音频（Liu et al., 2023）与三维模型（Zeng et al., 2022; Poole et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2023）等多数连续数据模态的事实标准。以文本提示词为条件生成图像的文生图扩散模型，随着 GLIDE（Nichol et al., 2021）、DALLE-2（Ramesh et al., 2022）、Imagen（Saharia et al., 2022; Ho et al., 2022）与 Latent Diffusion（Rombach et al., 2022）等模型的问世，已成为主流工具。本文聚焦文生图模型的微调，但 DRaFT 具有通用性，可应用于其他扩散模型或其他微调任务，也可拓展到音频、视频等不同领域。

    Learning human preferences.

    学习人类偏好。

    Human preference learning trains models on judgements of which behaviors people prefer, rather than on human demonstrations directly (Knox & Stone, 2009; Akrour et al., 2011). This training is commonly done by learning a reward model reflecting human preferences and then learning a policy that maximizes the reward (Christiano et al., 2017; Ibarz et al., 2018). Preference learning methods such as reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) have become widely used for fine-tuning large language models so they improve at summarization (Stiennon et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2021), instruction following (Ouyang et al., 2022), or general dialogue (Askell et al., 2021; Bai et al., 2022a; Glaese et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022b). We apply DRaFT to optimize scores from existing preference models, such as PickScore (Kirstain et al., 2023) and Human Preference Score v2 (Wu et al., 2023a), which are trained on human judgements between pairs of images generated by diffusion models for the same prompt.

    人类偏好学习以“人们更偏好哪种行为”的判断来训练模型，而不是直接在人类示范数据上训练（Knox & Stone, 2009; Akrour et al., 2011）。这类训练通常先学出一个反映人类偏好的奖励模型，再学一个最大化该奖励的策略（Christiano et al., 2017; Ibarz et al., 2018）。基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）等偏好学习方法已被广泛用于微调大语言模型，使其在摘要（Stiennon et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2021）、指令遵循（Ouyang et al., 2022）或通用对话（Askell et al., 2021; Bai et al., 2022a; Glaese et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022b）上表现更好。我们用 DRaFT 优化既有偏好模型的得分，例如 PickScore（Kirstain et al., 2023）与 Human Preference Score v2（Wu et al., 2023a），二者均基于人类对同一提示词下扩散模型生成的两张图像所做的判断训练而成。

    Guidance.

    引导。

    Guidance steers the sampling process towards images that satisfy a desired objective by adding an auxiliary term to the score function. The canonical example is classifier guidance (Song et al., 2021b; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021), which can turn an unconditional diffusion model into a class-conditional model by biasing the denoising process using gradients from a pre-trained classifier. Many other pre-trained models can also be used to provide guidance signals, including segmentation models, facial recognition models, and object detectors (Bansal et al., 2023). However, care must be taken when computing the guidance signal; naïvely, noisy examples obtained during the iterative refinement process may be out-of-distribution for the guidance model, leading to inaccurate gradients. Two approaches for mitigating this are (1) training a guidance model from scratch on noisy data (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021) and (2) applying a pre-trained guidance model (which has seen only non-noisy data) to the one-step-denoised images obtained by removing the predicted noise at time tt (Li et al., 2022). However, both approaches have downsides: (1) precludes the use of off-the-shelf pre-trained guidance functions and (2) means the guidance is applied to out-of-distribution blurry images for high noise levels. DRaFT avoids these issues by backpropagating through sampling so the reward function is only applied to the fully denoised image.

    引导通过在得分函数上附加一项辅助项，把采样过程导向满足期望目标的图像。典范例子是分类器引导（Song et al., 2021b; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021），它利用预训练分类器的梯度偏置去噪过程，可把无条件扩散模型转化为类别条件模型。许多其他预训练模型也可提供引导信号，包括分割模型、人脸识别模型与目标检测器（Bansal et al., 2023）。但计算引导信号时须谨慎：朴素做法下，迭代精修过程中得到的含噪样本对引导模型而言可能属于分布外，导致梯度不准。缓解该问题有两种途径：（1）在含噪数据上从零训练引导模型（Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021）；（2）把预训练引导模型（只见过无噪数据）作用于去除时刻 tt 预测噪声后得到的一步去噪图像（Li et al., 2022）。但两种做法都有缺陷：（1）排除了直接复用现成预训练引导函数的可能；（2）意味着在高噪声水平下把引导施加于分布外的模糊图像。DRaFT 通过贯穿采样反向传播，使奖励函数只作用于完全去噪后的图像，从而规避了这些问题。

    Backpropagation through diffusion sampling.

    贯穿扩散采样的反向传播。

    Similarly to our method, Watson et al. (2022) backpropagate through sampling using the reparameterization trick and gradient checkpointing. They learn parametric few-step samplers that aim to reduce the inference cost while maintaining perceptual image quality, using a differentiable image quality score. Fan & Lee (2023) also propose an approach to speed up sampling: they consider doing this using gradients by backpropagating through diffusion sampling, but due to memory and exploding/vanishing gradient concerns, they focus on an RL-based approach. Both Fan & Lee (2023) and Watson et al. (2022) aim to improve sampling speed, rather than optimize arbitrary differentiable rewards, which is our focus. Like DRaFT, Direct Optimization of Diffusion Latents (DOODL; Wallace et al. 2023) uses backpropagation through sampling to improve image generation with respect to differentiable objectives. Rather than optimizing model parameters, DOODL optimizes the initial noise sample 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T}. Although DOODL does not require a training phase, it is much slower at inference time because the optimization must be redone for each prompt and metric. In contrast, after finetuning with DRaFT, sampling has the same cost as a standard diffusion model. Furthermore, the latent optimization methods do not support the compositionality and interpolation properties that we can achieve with different sets of LoRA parameters tuned for different objectives.

    与我们的方法类似，Watson et al.（2022）利用重参数化技巧与梯度检查点贯穿采样做反向传播。他们用可微的图像质量得分学习参数化的少步采样器，旨在降低推理开销的同时保持感知图像质量。Fan & Lee（2023）也提出了一种加速采样的方法：他们考虑过用梯度通过扩散采样反向传播来实现，但受制于显存与梯度爆炸／消失的顾虑，最终聚焦于基于 RL 的方案。Fan & Lee（2023）与 Watson et al.（2022）的目标都是提升采样速度，而非优化任意可微奖励——后者才是我们的关注点。与 DRaFT 类似，Direct Optimization of Diffusion Latents（DOODL; Wallace et al. 2023）利用贯穿采样的反向传播，针对可微目标改进图像生成；但 DOODL 优化的是初始噪声样本 𝐱T\mathbf{x}_{T}，而非模型参数。尽管 DOODL 不需要训练阶段，它在推理时慢得多，因为每条提示词与每个指标都要重新做一遍优化。相比之下，用 DRaFT 微调之后，采样开销与标准扩散模型相同。此外，潜变量优化方法不支持我们用针对不同目标调优的不同 LoRA 参数集所实现的组合性与插值性质。

    Outside of image generation, Wang et al. (2023a) train diffusion model policies for reinforcement learning by backpropagating the Q-value function through sampling.

    在图像生成之外，Wang et al.（2023a）通过把 Q 值函数贯穿采样反向传播，训练用于强化学习的扩散模型策略。

    Reward fine-tuning with supervised learning.

    用有监督学习做奖励微调。

    Lee et al. (2023) and Wu et al. (2023b) use supervised approaches to fine-tune diffusion models on rewards. These methods generate images with the pre-trained model and then fine-tune on the images while weighting examples according to the reward function or discarding low-reward examples. Unlike DRaFT or RL methods, the model is not trained online on examples generated by the current policy. However, Dong et al. (2023) use an online version of this approach where examples are re-generated over multiple rounds of training, which can be viewed as a simple kind of reinforcement learning.

    Lee et al.（2023）与 Wu et al.（2023b）用有监督方式在奖励上微调扩散模型。这些方法先用预训练模型生成图像，再在这些图像上微调，并依奖励函数对样本加权或丢弃低奖励样本。与 DRaFT 或 RL 方法不同，模型并非在由当前策略生成的样本上在线训练。不过 Dong et al.（2023）使用了该方法的在线版本，在多轮训练中反复重新生成样本，可视为一种简单的强化学习。

    Reward fine-tuning with reinforcement learning.

    用强化学习做奖励微调。

    Fan & Lee (2023) interpret the denoising process as a multi-step decision-making task and use policy gradient algorithms to fine-tune diffusion samplers. Building on it, Black et al. (2023) and Fan et al. (2023) use policy gradient algorithms to fine-tune diffusion models for arbitrary black-box objectives. Rather than optimizing model parameters, Hao et al. (2022) apply RL to improve the input prompts. RL approaches are flexible because they do not require differentiable rewards. However, in practice many reward functions are differentiable, or can be re-implemented in a differentiable way, and thus analytic gradients are often available. In such cases, using reinforcement learning discards useful information, leading to inefficient optimization.

    Fan & Lee（2023）把去噪过程解释为多步决策任务，并用策略梯度算法微调扩散采样器。在此基础上，Black et al.（2023）与 Fan et al.（2023）用策略梯度算法针对任意黑盒目标微调扩散模型。Hao et al.（2022）则把 RL 用于改进输入提示词，而非优化模型参数。RL 方法的优势在于无需奖励可微。但实际上许多奖励函数本身可微，或以可微方式重新实现，解析梯度往往唾手可得。在这种情况下使用强化学习会丢弃有用信息，导致优化低效。

    ReFL.

    ReFL。

    Reward Feedback Learning (ReFL; Xu et al. 2023) evaluates the reward on the one-step predicted clean image, r⁡(𝐱^0,𝐜)r(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{0},\mathbf{c}) from a randomly-chosen step tt along the denoising trajectory, and backpropagates through the one-step prediction w.r.t. the diffusion model parameters. DRaFT-KK is conceptually simpler than ReFL, as it only differentiates through the last few steps (e.g., K=1K=1) of sampling, where KK is deterministic; in (Xu et al., 2023), the authors randomly choose an iteration between a min and max step of the sampling chain (which incurs more hyperparameters) from which to predict the clean image. Also, because DRaFT runs the full sampling chain, our reward functions are always evaluated on final generations. In contrast, ReFL applies the rewards to one-step denoised latents in the sampling trajectory, similar to the use of one-step denoising in guidance. Furthermore, DRaFT-LV is substantially more efficient than ReFL, speeding up training by approximately 2×2\times by reducing the variance of the single-denoising-step gradient estimates.

    奖励反馈学习（ReFL; Xu et al. 2023）在去噪轨迹上随机选取某一步 tt 所对应的一步预测干净图像 r⁡(𝐱^0,𝐜)r(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{0},\mathbf{c}) 上评估奖励，并把梯度经该一步预测反向传播到扩散模型参数。DRaFT-KK 在概念上比 ReFL 更简单，因为它只对采样的最后几步（例如 K=1K=1）求导，此处 KK 是确定性的；而 Xu et al.（2023）在采样链的最小步与最大步之间随机选取迭代（因而引入更多超参数）来预测干净图像。此外，由于 DRaFT 跑完整个采样链，我们的奖励函数始终在最终生成结果上评估；相比之下，ReFL 把奖励施加于采样轨迹中一步去噪的潜变量，类似于引导中单步去噪的用法。再者，DRaFT-LV 通过降低单去噪步梯度估计的方差，训练速度比 ReFL 快约 2×2\times，效率明显更高。
