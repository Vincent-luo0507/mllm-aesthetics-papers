中文摘要

我们提出 PosterIQ，一个以设计驱动的基准，面向海报的理解与生成，在构图结构、字体排印层次与语义意图三个层面完成标注。该基准包含 7,765 个图像—标注实例与 822 条生成提示词，覆盖真实案例、专业设计案例与合成案例。为打通视觉设计认知与生成式建模，我们定义了版式解析、图文对应、字体排印可读性与字体感知、设计质量评估，以及可控的、具备构图意识并含隐喻的生成等任务。我们对当前最先进的多模态大语言模型与基于扩散的生成器进行了评测，发现二者在视觉层次、字体排印语义、显著性控制与意图传达上存在持续差距：商用模型在高层推理上领先，但作为自动评分器却缺乏敏感度；生成器虽能较好地渲染文字，却在具备构图意识的合成上表现吃力。大量分析表明，PosterIQ 既是一个量化基准，也是设计推理的诊断工具，并提供可复现的、面向具体任务的指标。我们希望借此激发模型的创造力，把以人为本的设计原则融入生成式视觉语言系统。

Abstract (原文)

We present PosterIQ, a design-driven benchmark for poster understanding and generation, annotated across composition structure, typographic hierarchy, and semantic intent. It includes 7,765 image-annotation instances and 822 generation prompts spanning real, professional, and synthetic cases. To bridge visual design cognition and generative modeling, we define tasks for layout parsing, text-image correspondence, typography/readability and font perception, design quality assessment, and controllable, composition-aware generation with metaphor. We evaluate state-of-the-art MLLMs and diffusion-based generators, finding persistent gaps in visual hierarchy, typographic semantics, saliency control, and intention communication; commercial models lead on high-level reasoning but act as insensitive automatic raters, while generators render text well yet struggle with composition-aware synthesis. Extensive analyses show PosterIQ is both a quantitative benchmark and a diagnostic tool for design reasoning, offering reproducible, task-specific metrics. We aim to catalyze models' creativity and integrate human-centred design principles into generative vision-language systems.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) [5, 28, 2] have recently advanced in visual understanding—from object and scene recognition to cross-modal alignment, fine-grained parsing, and open-vocabulary detection—enabling high-level semantics and robust reasoning in complex contexts. In parallel, generative models have progressed across text-to-image, image-to-image, and interactive co-creation, with emerging strengths in style control, layout composition, and semantic consistency. These gains are especially evident in creative applications, where models show tangible innovation and practical utility in advertising, branding, and visual metaphor, while LLMs increasingly aid story ideation, tone transfer and narrative structuring.

多模态大语言模型（MLLM）[5, 28, 2] 近期在视觉理解上持续进步——从物体与场景识别，到跨模态对齐、细粒度解析与开放词汇检测——使模型得以在复杂语境中把握高层语义并稳健推理。与此同时，生成模型在文生图、图生图与人机协同创作方面不断推进，在风格控制、版面构成与语义一致性上展现出新的优势。这些进展在创意应用中尤为明显：模型在广告、品牌塑造与视觉隐喻方面体现出切实的创新能力与实用价值，而大语言模型也越来越多地辅助故事构思、语调迁移与叙事结构设计。

Despite benchmarks like  [7], evaluations remain largely text-centric. Image-generation assessments often prioritize aesthetics and overlook the compositional, constraint-driven nature of design. This omission is most acute in posters—a tightly integrated medium where visual understanding and content generation must align under strict constraints. Here, theme interpretation, information hierarchy, typographic rules, text–image coupling, theme consistency, and audience preference interact in ways single-dimensional metrics fail to capture as “genuine creativity.” Posters are not merely about visual appeal; they are visual communication media designed to ensure key messages are perceived, understood, and remembered with minimal cognitive load. Design without communicative purpose yields only superficial elegance. Effective poster design integrates robust text recognition, semantic understanding, faithful rendering, and hierarchical layout to keep critical elements legible in dense compositions; typographic choices must balance readability and beauty; overall style must align with audience and theme; and visual devices such as metaphor, symbolism, and whitespace should reinforce messages and aid memory through visual rhetoric. Accordingly, key assessment dimensions include accurate text understanding, goal-directed typography and layout, coherent text–image coordination, style control under audience and thematic constraints, and creative expression through metaphor—all grounded in understanding of design rules and theory, plus good taste and creative thinking, whose integration separates strong design from the merely adequate.

尽管已有 [7] 等基准，现有评测仍以文本为中心。图像生成评测往往偏重美学，忽略设计的构成性与约束驱动本质。这一缺失在海报上最为突出——海报是一种高度整合的媒介，其视觉理解与内容生成必须在严格约束下达成一致。在其中，主题解读、信息层次、字体排印规则、图文耦合、主题一致性与受众偏好相互作用，单维指标无法将其刻画为“真正的创造力”。海报不止关乎视觉吸引力，它还是一种视觉传达媒介，旨在以最小的认知负荷确保关键信息被看见、被理解并被记住。缺乏传达目的的设计只能得到表面的精致。有效的海报设计需要融合稳健的文字识别、语义理解、忠实渲染与层次化版面，使关键元素在密集构成中保持清晰可读；字体排印的选择须兼顾可读性与美感；整体风格须与受众和主题相称；隐喻、象征与留白等视觉手法应通过视觉修辞强化信息、辅助记忆。据此，核心评测维度包括：准确的文本理解、目标导向的字体排印与版面、连贯的图文协同、受众与主题约束下的风格控制，以及通过隐喻实现的创造性表达——这些维度均以对设计规则与理论的理解为基础，再加上良好的品味与创造性思维，其融合程度正是优秀设计与平庸设计的分野。

From a modeling perspective, poster-oriented understanding and generation systems must jointly satisfy: (1) text understanding and readability via robust OCR and font recognition, readability prediction (glyph shape, weight, size, contrast) with constraint-aware optimization, and automatic text hierarchy and structured layout; (2) layout reasoning and hierarchical organization through explicit modeling of grids, whitespace, alignment, layering, and figure–ground relationships for global optimization across elements, sizes, and densities; (3) semantic–style consistency with task- and audience-aware style retrieval or transfer under target constraints, suppressing style noise that could obscure key information; (4) text–image coordination and saliency control that transforms key messages into semantically aligned visuals and directs attention so core information is perceived first; and (5) rhetorical modeling and metaphor generation that produce decodable visual metaphors via semantic association and analogy while balancing novelty against misinterpretation risks.

从建模角度看，面向海报的理解与生成系统必须同时满足以下要求：（1）文本理解与可读性——依靠稳健的 OCR 与字体识别、可读性预测（字形、字重、字号、对比度）并结合约束感知的优化，以及自动的文本层级与结构化版面；（2）版面推理与层级组织——显式建模网格、留白、对齐、分层及图形—背景关系，在元素、尺寸与密度之间实现全局优化；（3）语义—风格一致性——在目标约束下进行任务与受众感知的风格检索或迁移，抑制可能遮蔽关键信息的风格噪声；（4）图文协同与显著性控制——把关键信息转化为语义对齐的视觉元素，并引导注意力，使核心信息被优先感知；（5）修辞建模与隐喻生成——通过语义联想与类比产生可解码的视觉隐喻，同时在新颖性与误读风险之间取得平衡。

To address this, we introduce PosterIQ: a systematic benchmark for highly constrained poster creation that spans the full pipeline—from understanding and ideation to composition and generation—and more comprehensively characterizes MLLMs’ creative capabilities in poster understanding and synthesis. As shown in Fig. 1, it comprises two tightly coupled, design–oriented components: an understanding module with a global quality assessment (overall rating) and four decoupled task families—(i) OCR and text readability, (ii) font shape perception and attribute understanding, (iii) multidimensional layout and hierarchy perception, and (iv) high-level style recognition, visual deconstruction, and semantic communication—and a generation module that exceeds existing benchmarks by (i) generating and organizing high-density visual content, (ii) accurately generating dense text with diverse fonts, (iii) enabling controllable poster style and thematic tone, (iv) supporting challenging structural decomposition and recomposition of visual elements, and (v) facilitating intention communication and creative evaluation via metaphor and visual rhetoric; overall, PosterIQ advances verifiable creativity in real-world design scenarios through actionable tasks, reproducible metrics, and decoupled evaluation dimensions. From a broad empirical analysis, it reveals systematic gaps between open-source and proprietary models. Frontier commercial systems generally perform better on high-level tasks such as layout reasoning, composition understanding, and intention interpretation. However, when used as automatic raters for poster quality, their scores often lack sensitivity and fail to clearly separate strong and weak designs. In the generation setting, current models already demonstrate strong visual synthesis and text rendering capabilities, but still struggle with composition-aware generation and clear expression of design intentions.     2 Related Work

    2 相关工作

    2.1 Multimodal Benchmarks

    2.1 多模态基准

    A growing range of benchmarks evaluates MLLMs across diverse tasks [12, 31, 36, 3, 17]. Concurrently, some studies explicitly investigate cross-modal diversity and complex semantic alignment [37, 38, 13]. As visual modality emerged, vision–language benchmarks such as MMBench [23], Creation-MMBench [7], and Seed-Bench-2-Plus [21] began testing multimodal understanding and instruction following from everyday recognition to complex reasoning. OCR-focused suites [24, 9] target scene text recognition and structured extraction. Image generation is now systematically assessed: ELLA’s DPG measures fine-grained prompt adherence under dense descriptions [15], while GenEval evaluates text–image alignment for multi-object, multi-attribute, and relational prompts [11]. For text rendering within images, TextCrafter’s CVTG-2K tests readability and fidelity in complex scenes [6], and X-Omni’s LongText-Bench (English/Chinese) probes paragraph-level rendering and layout consistency [10]. Specialized evaluations cover narrower domains—VGBench [44], ChartQA [25], and InfographicVQA [26] for vector graphics and chart reasoning; LiveIdeaBench [32] and DesignProbe [22] for ideation-centric evaluation; and UI-Vision [27] for desktop GUIs.

    越来越多的基准在多样任务上评测 MLLM [12, 31, 36, 3, 17]。同时，一些研究专门考察跨模态多样性与复杂语义对齐 [37, 38, 13]。随着视觉模态的兴起，MMBench [23]、Creation-MMBench [7]、Seed-Bench-2-Plus [21] 等视觉—语言基准开始检验多模态理解与指令遵循，范围从日常识别延伸至复杂推理。面向 OCR 的测试集 [24, 9] 针对场景文字识别与结构化抽取。图像生成也已被系统评测：ELLA 的 DPG 在密集描述下度量细粒度的提示词遵循度 [15]，GenEval 则针对多物体、多属性与关系型提示词评测图文对齐 [11]。针对图像内文字渲染，TextCrafter 的 CVTG-2K 检验复杂场景下的可读性与保真度 [6]，X-Omni 的 LongText-Bench（英文/中文）考察段落级渲染与版面一致性 [10]。面向更窄领域的专门评测包括：面向矢量图形与图表推理的 VGBench [44]、ChartQA [25] 与 InfographicVQA [26]；面向创意构思评测的 LiveIdeaBench [32] 与 DesignProbe [22]；以及面向桌面图形界面的 UI-Vision [27]。

    In contrast, PosterIQ adopts a visual design perspective, emphasizing creative poster design, making it more challenging and closer to real-world design practice.

    与之不同，PosterIQ 采用视觉设计视角，强调创意海报设计，因而更具挑战性，也更贴近真实的设计实践。

    2.2 Poster Design and Generative Models

    2.2 海报设计与生成模型

    Diffusion models are emerging as the leading approach for high-quality image synthesis [43, 1, 20], lowering the barrier for non-experts to engage in creative design. Beyond static images, diffusion-based methods support controllable pipelines for structured manipulation of layout, typography, and style. Recent multimodal image generators—Qwen-Image [39], Seedream 4.0 [35], GPT-Image-1 [29], and Gemini 2.5 Flash Image [8]—improve efficiency in T2I and image-editing. As these models advance, interest grows in poster design. Hierarchical frameworks like instruction-tuned models like PosterLLaVA [40] and COLE [18] enable automatic poster generation, while VASCAR [42] targets content-aware layout. FontCLIP [34], ControlText [19], and FontTS [33] advance semantic typography and font control, building on early crowdsourced studies of font attributes [30]. Pipeline-based automation is realized in systems such as OpenCOLE [16]. PosterCraft [4] uses Gemini to generate training captions, and DreamPoster [14] uses a specialized captioner for glyph- and layout-level descriptions. Our benchmark shifts the focus to the evaluation of understanding and generation in poster design.

    扩散模型正成为高质量图像合成的主流方案 [43, 1, 20]，降低了非专业人士参与创意设计的门槛。除静态图像外，基于扩散的方法还支持对版式、字体排印与风格进行结构化操控的可控流程。近期的多模态图像生成器——Qwen-Image [39]、Seedream 4.0 [35]、GPT-Image-1 [29] 与 Gemini 2.5 Flash Image [8]——提升了文生图与图像编辑的效率。随着这些模型进步，海报设计受到越来越多的关注。PosterLLaVA [40] 与 COLE [18] 等经指令微调的层次化框架实现了海报自动生成，VASCAR [42] 则面向内容感知的版式。FontCLIP [34]、ControlText [19] 与 FontTS [33] 在早期字体属性众包研究 [30] 的基础上推进了语义字体排印与字体控制。OpenCOLE [16] 等系统实现了基于流程的自动化。PosterCraft [4] 使用 Gemini 生成训练字幕，DreamPoster [14] 则采用专用字幕器生成字形级与版面级的描述。我们的基准把重心转向海报设计中理解与生成的评测。

    3 Benchmark

    3 基准

    3.1 Understanding Tasks

    3.1 理解任务

    OCR Tasks. To support poster understanding and design-oriented reasoning, MLLMs must first show reliable visual text recognition. Our benchmark targets poster-specific traits and evaluates OCR across five sub-tasks: 1) Logo OCR—recognizing highly stylized, distorted, or abstracted logo typography with irregular character shapes; 2) Real-World Poster OCR—handling complex scenes with diverse fonts, scales, dense layouts, and textured backgrounds where text–graphic interactions raise difficulty; 3) Simple OCR—estimating upper-bound performance by rendering Oxford 3000 words with varied fonts and casing as length-balanced sequences on white backgrounds; 4) Hard OCR—testing robustness via unordered letter sequences (excluding ambiguous “l”/“I”) rendered in varied fonts, slight rotations, random colors, and placed on highly textured, colorful backgrounds from real images; 5) Multi-Size OCR—assessing stability under scale variation by generating unordered letter sequences in 14 font sizes with a base font on white, repeated across multiple fonts.

    OCR 任务。为支持海报理解与面向设计的推理，MLLM 首先须具备可靠的视觉文字识别能力。我们的基准针对海报的独有特征，在五个子任务上评测 OCR：1）Logo OCR——识别高度风格化、变形或抽象的标识字体，其字符形状不规则；2）真实海报 OCR——处理字体多样、尺度多变、版面密集且背景带纹理的复杂场景，其中文字与图形的相互作用抬高了难度；3）简单 OCR——把 Oxford 3000 词以不同字体与大小写渲染为长度均衡的序列置于白底之上，用以估计性能上界；4）困难 OCR——以乱序字母序列（排除易混淆的“l”/“I”）考察鲁棒性，字母以不同字体渲染、带轻微旋转与随机颜色，并置于取自真实图像的强纹理彩色背景上；5）多字号 OCR——以基础字体在白底上生成 14 种字号的乱序字母序列，并在多种字体上重复，以评估尺度变化下的稳定性。

    Font Understanding Tasks.

    字体理解任务。

    Typography is central to poster design: font styles convey both theme and aesthetics, and require accurate perception. We evaluate these capabilities with four sub-tasks: 1) Font Matching—fine-grained style identification without font-name priors; given a target text in one font, the model selects the matching font from nine candidates showing different text, forcing reliance on visual style rather than character identity; 2) Font Attribute Perception—using 37 human-derived attributes from  [30]. (31 relative, 6 binary such as serif/italic), the model sees a pair of font-rendered texts and chooses the one best matching a target attribute, with accuracy reflecting agreement with human judgments; 3) Traditional Font Effect Recognition—recognition of nine common effects (bold, italic, underline, etc.) by selecting the correct effect from four options; 4) Advanced Font Effect Recognition—on highly stylized, model-generated and curated effects, the model first identifies the text’s dominant color, then selects the correct effect from 48 candidates spanning styles such as rock.

    字体排印是海报设计的核心：字体风格同时传达主题与美感，因而需要被准确感知。我们用四个子任务评测这些能力：1）字体匹配——在没有字体名称先验的条件下做细粒度风格辨识；给定以一种字体渲染的目标文本，模型须从展示不同文本的九个候选字体中选出匹配者，迫使模型依赖视觉风格而非字符本身；2）字体属性感知——使用源自 [30] 的 37 个人工归纳属性（31 个相对属性、6 个二值属性，如衬线、斜体），模型观看一对字体渲染文本，并选出与目标属性最匹配的一个，准确率反映其与人类判断的一致程度；3）传统字体效果识别——识别九种常见效果（粗体、斜体、下划线等），从四个选项中选出正确效果；4）高级字体效果识别——针对高度风格化、由模型生成或人工筛选的效果，模型先识别文本的主色，再从涵盖摇滚等风格的 48 个候选中选出正确效果。

    Spatial Reasoning Tasks. Effective perception and generation of layout structures are vital for poster design, reflecting an MLLM’s grasp of aesthetics, spatial balance, and composition. We evaluate layout understanding, aesthetic judgment, and spatial planning with five sub-tasks: 1) Text Localization—given a list of target phrases, the model returns a bounding box for each; coordinates are normalized to [0,1] to handle varying resolutions and aspect ratios, emphasizing precise detection of small or dense text; 2) Text Alignment and Rotation—the model infers alignment and rotation for each text sample, capturing typographic structure beyond position; 3) Empty-Space Perception—using partially completed posters divided into a 7×77\times 7 grid, annotators label suitable regions for new elements; their intersection defines the consensus empty space, and the model outputs region IDs for a requested count, evaluated by IoU with the consensus set; 4) Layout Comparison—given a professional layout and a version deliberately violating design principles, the model selects the more coherent and aesthetically sound design, testing high-level layout judgment; 5) Layout Generation—given a textual specification of relative sizes and placements (≤5\leq 5 elements), the model produces bounding boxes forming a coherent layout, evaluated on positional and area accuracy, thereby assessing instruction following and multimodal reasoning.

    空间推理任务。对版式结构的有效感知与生成对海报设计至关重要，体现了 MLLM 对美学、空间平衡与构图的把握。我们用五个子任务评测版面理解、美学判断与空间规划：1）文字定位——给定目标短语列表，模型为每个短语返回一个边界框；坐标归一化到 [0,1] 以适应不同的分辨率与宽高比，重点考察对细小或密集文字的精确检测；2）文字对齐与旋转——模型推断每个文字样本的对齐方式与旋转角度，捕捉位置之外的字体排印结构；3）留白感知——使用被划分为 7×77\times 7 网格的部分完成海报，标注员标出适合放置新元素的区域，其交集定义为共识留白，模型按请求数量输出区域编号，以与共识集合的 IoU 评价；4）版面比较——给定专业版面与刻意违反设计原则的版本，模型须选出更连贯、更美观的设计，考察高层版面判断力；5）版面生成——给定描述相对尺寸与位置的文字说明（≤5\leq 5 个元素），模型生成构成连贯版面的边界框，按位置与面积准确率评价，从而考察指令遵循与多模态推理能力。

    Advanced Visual Design Understanding. High-quality poster design goes beyond basic layout and elements, often employing sophisticated styles, visual deconstruction, and conceptual metaphors. We evaluate advanced design understanding with three sub-tasks: 1) Poster Style Classification—identify one of 17 curated styles (e.g., Minimalist, Diffuse Glow, Memphis) based on holistic cues spanning color, effects, typography, and imagery; 2) Composition Structure Understanding—describe each poster’s visual construction using operations such as misalignment, segmentation, nesting, cutouts, repetition, extension, focus shifting, and mirroring, with coverage judged by an LLM against human-annotated key concepts; 3) Intention and Metaphor Interpretation—explain metaphor-rich posters (e.g., stacked smartphones evoking a hamburger, toy soldiers forming a dove, circuit-board textures fused with leaf veins), with success measured by capturing manually annotated explanatory elements and intended messages.

    高级视觉设计理解。高质量海报设计不止于基础版式与元素，往往运用精妙的风格、视觉解构与概念隐喻。我们用三个子任务评测高级设计理解：1）海报风格分类——依据覆盖色彩、效果、字体排印与图像的整体线索，从 17 种精选风格（如极简、弥散发光、孟菲斯）中识别其一；2）构图结构理解——用错位、分割、嵌套、抠图、重复、延展、焦点转移、镜像等操作描述每张海报的视觉构造，覆盖率由大语言模型对照人工标注的关键概念判定；3）意图与隐喻解读——解释富含隐喻的海报（例如堆叠的智能手机唤起汉堡意象、玩具士兵组成鸽子、电路板纹理与叶脉融合），成功与否取决于是否捕捉到人工标注的解释性元素与预期信息。

    Rating Task. Finally, we further assess aesthetic judgment by asking models to assign each poster a quality score from 0 to 10. After normalizing scores, we measure alignment with human preferences by computing the correlation between model predictions and human rating distributions.

    评分任务。最后，我们进一步考察美学判断，要求模型为每张海报打出 0 到 10 的质量分。分数归一化后，通过计算模型预测与人类评分分布之间的相关性来衡量其与人类偏好的一致程度。

    3.2 Generation Tasks

    3.2 生成任务

    Dense Generation. Real-world themes (e.g., film IP) demand many characters, objects, and fine details in one image. We curate themes with  10 entities and specify actions, attributes, or orientations for each; given a prompt, the model must render all required elements. An MLLM verifies each item and reports overall matching accuracy.

    密集生成。真实世界的主题（如影视 IP）要求单张图像容纳众多人物、物体与精细细节。我们筛选含 10 个实体的主题，并为每个实体指定动作、属性或朝向；给定提示词，模型须渲染出全部必需元素。由 MLLM 逐项核验并给出整体匹配准确率。

    Font Generation. To assess control over typography, we ask for text-centric posters across varied scenarios while explicitly encouraging font diversity. An MLLM infers latent font attributes (e.g., bold, elegant, friendly), and the count of distinct attributes measures font-style diversity.

    字体生成。为考察对字体排印的掌控力，我们要求模型在多种场景下生成以文字为主体的海报，并明确鼓励字体多样化。由 MLLM 推断潜藏的字体属性（如粗壮、优雅、亲和），以不同属性的数量度量字体风格的丰富度。

    Style Generation. The model generates posters in 17 mainstream styles (matching our style-understanding benchmark, e.g., Memphis). An MLLM predicts each poster’s style, and accuracy is computed against the target.

    风格生成。模型需生成 17 种主流风格的海报（与我们的风格理解基准一致，如孟菲斯）。由 MLLM 预测每张海报的风格，并与目标风格比对计算准确率。

    Composition Generation. Advanced design often employs structured compositional techniques—such as misalignment, segmentation, nesting, cutouts, repetition, extension, focus shifting, and mirroring—to create visual tension and sophistication. We provide prompts describing these composition strategies and require the model to generate images accordingly. An MLLM evaluates whether each compositional element appears in the output, using the same criteria as in the composition-understanding task, and computes matching rate across all required points.

    构图生成。高级设计常采用结构化的构成手法——如错位、分割、嵌套、抠图、重复、延展、焦点转移与镜像——以制造视觉张力与精致感。我们提供描述这些构图策略的提示词，要求模型据此生成图像。由 MLLM 依据与构图理解任务相同的标准评判每个构成元素是否在输出中出现，并对全部要求要点计算匹配率。

    Intention Generation. We prompt metaphor- and concept-rich posters (e.g., dual-meaning imagery or abstract symbols). An MLLM evaluates whether key intention elements—essential visual cues and intended semantics, as defined by human annotations—are present, reusing the labels from the understanding task.

    意图生成。我们以富含隐喻与概念的海报作为提示（如双关意象或抽象符号）。由 MLLM 评判关键意图元素——即人工标注所定义的核心视觉线索与预期语义——是否呈现，标签沿用理解任务中的标注。

    Metric. We use task-specific metrics to capture both accuracy and robustness. For Logo OCR and Poster OCR, we report item-level accuracy. For Simple, Hard, and Font-Size OCR, we use a word-level recall rate (WR) over fixed-length segments, together with the standard deviation (Std) across font sizes and the gap Δ\Delta between simple and hard settings. For the font tasks, we adopt a normalized multiple-choice score (Score) in [0,1][0,1]: values near 0 indicate near-random predictions, and 1 indicates all answers are correct. For layout tasks, we use IoU-based metrics and discrete-choice accuracy. Top-1 IoU denotes the maximum IoU over predicted regions, while Alignment and Rotation are evaluated by multiple-choice accuracy. In the Empty Space task, Matching Acc. measures whether the returned region IDs match the requested count and target regions. For Layout Generation, we measure the center offset and area ratio between predicted and ground-truth boxes. For Advanced Visual Design Understanding, Score is again a multiple-choice accuracy, and Points Score measures the coverage of annotated key points in the model’s description. In the overall rating task, we compute the cosine similarity between the vector of model scores and the vector of human ratings. Generation experiments are evaluated with analogous metrics (e.g., option correctness, style agreement, and key-point coverage); find full metric definitions in the supplementary material.

    指标。我们采用面向具体任务的指标，同时刻画准确率与鲁棒性。对 Logo OCR 与海报 OCR，报告实例级准确率。对简单、困难与字号 OCR，采用固定长度片段上的词级召回率（WR），以及跨字号的标准差（Std）与简单、困难设置之间的差距 Δ\Delta。对字体类任务，采用归一化到 [0,1][0,1] 的多选得分（Score）：取值接近 0 表示接近随机猜测，1 表示全部答对。对版式任务，采用基于 IoU 的指标与离散选择准确率。Top-1 IoU 表示预测区域上的最大 IoU，对齐与旋转则以多选准确率评价。在留白任务中，匹配准确率（Matching Acc.）度量返回的区域编号是否与请求数量及目标区域相符。对版面生成，我们度量预测框与真值框之间的中心偏移与面积比。对高级视觉设计理解，Score 同样为多选准确率，要点得分（Points Score）度量模型描述对标注关键点的覆盖程度。在整体评分任务中，我们计算模型分数向量与人类评分向量之间的余弦相似度。生成实验采用类似的指标（如选项正确性、风格一致性与关键点覆盖）；完整指标定义见补充材料。

    Quantitative Results. Averaged across five tasks, Gemini-2.5-Flash-Image leads overall. Per-task results show differentiated strengths: Gemini excels in Composition and Intention, indicating strong global layout planning and instruction–semantics alignment, while GPT-Image-1 leads in Style and Intention but trails on Dense and Font, revealing weaknesses in micro-structure fidelity and text readability. Structural patterns emerge: Font and Dense are partially decoupled (e.g., Seedream-4.0 is strong on Dense but middling on Font), implying distinct capability axes and a need for targeted supervision on stroke closure, spacing, ligatures, and variant-shape modeling. Font remains the bottleneck, with uniformly low scores (best 0.391) versus high Composition (up to 0.866), posing risks for multilingual scripts, small sizes, complex fonts, and low-contrast backgrounds.

    定量结果。在五项任务的平均表现上，Gemini-2.5-Flash-Image 整体领先。分任务结果呈现差异化的优势：Gemini 在构图与意图上表现出色，说明其具备较强的全局版面规划与指令—语义对齐能力；GPT-Image-1 在风格与意图上领先，但在密集与字体上落后，暴露出微观结构保真度与文本可读性方面的不足。结构性规律随之显现：字体与密集在一定程度上相互解耦（例如 Seedream-4.0 在密集上强势而在字体上居中），意味着二者属于不同的能力轴，需要针对笔画闭合、字距、连字与变体字形建模施加专门的监督。全局—局部的权衡同样明显——主导构图与意图的模型通常不在风格与字体上领先，说明训练更偏重全局规划而非局部精度。字体仍是瓶颈，得分普遍偏低（最佳 0.391），而构图得分可高达 0.866，这给多语言字符、小字号、复杂字体与低对比度背景带来了风险。

    Understanding and Generation. We investigate how improved understanding benefits generation and validate the soundness of our benchmark. To this end, we design an iterative setup where a VLM supervises a T2I generator. We simulate a realistic use case: the input is a vague, brief requirement as the prompt. A T2I model first produces a poster. We then feed the generated poster and the original prompt into a VLM. Based on its interpretation of the poster, the VLM diagnoses issues and returns a revised prompt, which is fed back to the generator for re-generation. Notably, the VLM only receives the image and the original prompt; the enhanced prompt without any human or domain-specific design hints. By iterating this loop, we directly observe how understanding affects poster quality. As shown in Fig. 4, the two T2I models are GPT-Image-1 and Qwen-Image, and the two VLMs are GPT-5 and Qwen3-VL. From the first T2I outputs under ambiguous requirements, both models capture the main content, but GPT-Image exhibits stronger intent understanding than Qwen-Image. It conveys a cautionary message to young people about excessive play, whereas Qwen-Image attends to surface keywords such as “Halloween” and “children,” without deeper inference. After the first round of VLM-guided analysis, the VLM flags issues in visual style, mood, and typography. The regenerated results from the revised prompt address these issues and improve overall poster quality. A second iteration yields further gains, confirming that stronger understanding improves generation. At round 0 (no understanding intervention), the models tend to produce visually appealing images that neglect efficient communication. After VLM intervention, the system begins to prioritize effective information transmission. The clearest evidence is the adjustment of emotional tone to match communication goals. Without intervention, round-0 results often include elements unsuitable for the audience, such as frightening skulls or uncanny faces. With deeper understanding, these issues are corrected. These findings suggest a practical paradigm for poster generation: iteratively coupling understanding and generation—without human design hints.

    5 Conclusion

    5 结论

    PosterIQ delivers a rigorous benchmark for poster design. By decoupling core capabilities, our tasks expose where current MLLMs and generators succeed and where they fail, from saliency control to intention communication. Empirical studies reveal that frontier models excel at high-level reasoning yet remain insensitive as automatic raters and struggle with composition-aware synthesis and typographic diversity. We hope this work catalyzes multimodal intelligence and guides future models toward fidelity to design constraints, communicative intent, and creativity in real-world poster scenes.

    PosterIQ 为海报设计提供了一个严谨的基准。通过解耦核心能力，我们的任务揭示了当前 MLLM 与生成器在何处成功、何处失败，范围从显著性控制到意图传达。实证研究表明，前沿模型擅长高层推理，但作为自动评分器仍缺乏敏感度，且在构图感知的合成与字体排印多样性上表现不佳。我们希望这项工作能推动多模态智能的发展，并引导未来模型在真实海报场景中忠实于设计约束、传达意图与创造力。

    OCR Accuracy (Text Instance Level): For logo OCR and poster OCR, we evaluate accuracy at the text-instance level. Invisible characters (e.g., spaces and line breaks) are stripped from both prediction and ground truth, and an instance is counted as correct only under exact match. The overall accuracy (AC) is then given by the average proportion of correctly recognized text instances:

    OCR 准确率（文本实例级）：对 Logo OCR 与海报 OCR，我们在文本实例级别评测准确率。预测与真值均先剥离不可见字符（如空格与换行），只有在完全匹配时才计为正确。整体准确率（AC）由正确识别的文本实例平均占比给出：

    AC=1N​∑i=1N𝟙​(T^i=Ti),\textit{AC}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbbm{1}\left(\hat{T}_{i}=T_{i}\right), (1)

    where NN is the total number of text instances, T^i\hat{T}_{i} is the predicted text for instance ii, and TiT_{i} is the corresponding ground-truth text. The function 𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) is an indicator that returns 1 if the condition holds and 0 otherwise. Each logo is counted as a single text instance, while a poster can contain multiple text instances.

    其中 NN 为文本实例总数，T^i\hat{T}_{i} 为第 ii 个实例的预测文本，TiT_{i} 为对应的真值文本。函数 𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) 为指示函数，条件成立时返回 1，否则返回 0。每个标识计为一个文本实例，而一张海报可包含多个文本实例。

    Word-level Recall for Robust OCR: This metric is used for the synthetic OCR tasks: Simple OCR, Hard OCR, and Font-Size OCR. Given a ground-truth text string, we split it into consecutive units gg, each consisting of five characters. For each unit gg, we check whether it appears as a substring in the model-predicted long text output. This normalization makes scores comparable across texts of different lengths. The word-level recall (W​RWR) is then defined as the proportion of ground-truth units that are recovered in the prediction:

    鲁棒 OCR 的词级召回率：该指标用于合成类 OCR 任务，即简单 OCR、困难 OCR 与字号 OCR。给定真值文本串，我们把它切分为连续的单元 gg，每个单元由五个字符组成。对每个单元 gg，检查其是否作为子串出现在模型预测的长文本输出中。这一归一化使不同长度文本之间的分数可比。词级召回率（W​RWR）定义为在预测中被恢复的真值单元所占比例：

    W​R=∑g∈G𝟙​(g⊆o​u​t​p​u​t)|G|,\textit{$WR$}=\frac{\sum_{g\in G}\mathbbm{1}\left(g\subseteq output\right)}{|G|}, (2)

    where GG denotes the set of all five-character units extracted from the ground-truth text, and 𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) is an indicator function that returns 1 when the condition is satisfied and 0 otherwise. Before segmentation, spaces and escape characters are stripped, and each word is guaranteed to appear at most once in a given image.

    其中 GG 表示从真值文本中抽取的全部五字符单元的集合，𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) 为指示函数，条件满足时返回 1，否则返回 0。切分之前会剥离空格与转义字符，且每个词保证在给定图像中最多出现一次。

    We define Δ\Delta as the difference between the WR scores on Simple OCR and Hard OCR. This gap reflects the robustness of the model to noise: a larger Δ\Delta indicates higher sensitivity to background clutter, missing context, rotation, and other perturbations.

    我们把 Δ\Delta 定义为简单 OCR 与困难 OCR 上 WR 分数之差。该差距反映模型对噪声的鲁棒性：Δ\Delta 越大，说明模型对背景杂乱、上下文缺失、旋转等扰动越敏感。

    For the Font-Size OCR task, we additionally report the standard deviation Std of the WR scores across 14 different font sizes. This metric captures the robustness of the model’s OCR performance with respect to changes in font size: lower Std indicates more stable recognition across scales.

    对字号 OCR 任务，我们额外报告 14 种不同字号下 WR 分数的标准差 Std。该指标刻画模型 OCR 性能随字号变化的鲁棒性：Std 越低，说明跨尺度的识别越稳定。

    K-Option Scoring for Multiple-Choice Tasks: For tasks where the model predicts a label from a finite set of options, we use multiple-choice style metrics. This applies to the font-related tasks (Font Matching, Font Attributes, and Font Effects), where we report Score, Effect Score, and Color Score; to the Style Understanding task, where we report a style classification Score; to the Text Position task, where Alignment and Rotation are cast as discrete choices; and to Layout Comparison, where we also use a multiple-choice Score. For multiple-choice tasks with kk answer options, let the model’s accuracy be aa, where a∈[0,1]a\in[0,1]. The scoring formula normalizes the score such that random guessing results in a score of zero, and perfect accuracy results in a score of one:

    多选任务的 K 选项计分：对模型需从有限选项集中预测标签的任务，我们采用多选式指标。这适用于字体相关任务（字体匹配、字体属性与字体效果），其中报告 Score、Effect Score 与 Color Score；风格理解任务，其中报告风格分类 Score；文字位置任务，其中对齐与旋转被转化为离散选择；以及版面比较，同样采用多选 Score。对于含 kk 个答案选项的多选任务，设模型准确率为 aa，其中 a∈[0,1]a\in[0,1]。计分公式把分数归一化，使得随机猜测得分为零，完全答对得分为一：

    Score=max⁡(0,k⋅a−1k−1).\text{Score}=\max\left(0,\frac{k\cdot a-1}{k-1}\right). (3)

    Under random guessing, the expected accuracy is 1k\frac{1}{k}, which maps to a score of zero in our formulation. When the model attains perfect accuracy a=1a=1, the score reaches one.

    在随机猜测下，期望准确率为 1k\frac{1}{k}，在我们的公式中对应分数零。当模型达到完全准确率 a=1a=1 时，分数为一。

    该计分方案用于字体相关任务、文字定位任务与版面比较任务。

    Bbox-Related Metrics: For the Text Localization task, ground-truth bounding boxes are first sorted in descending order by area. We then evaluate the average Intersection over Union (IoU) over the top-nn predicted boxes:

    边界框相关指标：对文字定位任务，真值边界框先按面积降序排列。随后我们在前 nn 个预测框上计算平均交并比（IoU）：

    IoU=1n​∑j=1n|Bjpred∩Bjgt||Bjpred∪Bjgt|,\text{IoU}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{\lvert B_{j}^{\text{pred}}\cap B_{j}^{\text{gt}}\rvert}{\lvert B_{j}^{\text{pred}}\cup B_{j}^{\text{gt}}\rvert}, (4)

    where BjpredB_{j}^{\text{pred}} and BjgtB_{j}^{\text{gt}} denote the predicted and ground-truth boxes for the jj-th instance.

    其中 BjpredB_{j}^{\text{pred}} 与 BjgtB_{j}^{\text{gt}} 分别表示第 jj 个实例的预测框与真值框。

    To further assess prompt-following behavior, we examine how well the number of predicted boxes matches the number of queried text instances. For each sample, if the model predicts fewer boxes than requested, we compute the recall as the ratio between the number of predicted boxes and the number of queried objects. If it predicts more boxes than requested, we assign a recall of 1. The final recall score is the average over all samples:

    为进一步评估提示词遵循情况，我们考察预测框数量与被查询文本实例数量的匹配程度。对每个样本，若模型预测的框数少于请求数量，则把召回率计算为预测框数与查询物体数之比；若预测的框数多于请求数量，则赋予召回率 1。最终的召回分数为所有样本上的平均值：

    Recall Rate=1N​∑i=1N{nipredniquery,if ​nipred≤niquery,1,otherwise,\text{Recall Rate}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\begin{cases}\frac{n_{i}^{\text{pred}}}{n_{i}^{\text{query}}},&\text{if }n_{i}^{\text{pred}}\leq n_{i}^{\text{query}},\\[4.0pt] 1,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases} (5)

    where NN is the total number of evaluation samples, nipredn_{i}^{\text{pred}} is the number of predicted boxes for sample ii, and niqueryn_{i}^{\text{query}} is the number of query objects specified in the prompt.

    其中 NN 为评测样本总数，nipredn_{i}^{\text{pred}} 为样本 ii 的预测框数，niqueryn_{i}^{\text{query}} 为提示词中指定的查询物体数。

    In the Layout Generation task, there is no uniquely correct placement of layout boxes, so standard IoU-based matching is not directly applicable. Instead, we evaluate the predicted layout by comparing the relative positions and areas of predicted boxes with those of the ground truth. Higher-quality layouts exhibit smaller Center Bias and an Area Ratio closer to 1.

    在版面生成任务中，版面框不存在唯一正确的摆放方式，因此基于 IoU 的标准匹配无法直接适用。我们改为比较预测框与真值框的相对位置与面积来评价预测版面。质量更高的版面表现为更小的中心偏移（Center Bias）和更接近 1 的面积比（Area Ratio）。

    Center Bias quantifies the normalized Euclidean distance between the centers of the predicted and ground-truth boxes:

    中心偏移量化预测框与真值框中心之间的归一化欧氏距离：

    Center Bias=1N​∑i=1N‖Cipred−Cigt‖2,\text{Center Bias}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\bigl\|C_{i}^{\text{pred}}-C_{i}^{\text{gt}}\bigr\|_{2}, (6)

    where CipredC_{i}^{\text{pred}} and CigtC_{i}^{\text{gt}} are the normalized center coordinates of the predicted and ground-truth box for the ii-th element.

    其中 CipredC_{i}^{\text{pred}} 与 CigtC_{i}^{\text{gt}} 分别为第 ii 个元素的预测框与真值框的归一化中心坐标。

    Area Ratio measures how similar the box areas are by taking the ratio between the smaller and larger area:

    面积比通过取较小面积与较大面积之比来度量两个框面积的相近程度：

    Area Ratio=1N​∑i=1Nmin⁡(Aipred,Aigt)max⁡(Aipred,Aigt),\text{Area Ratio}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{\min\!\bigl(A_{i}^{\text{pred}},A_{i}^{\text{gt}}\bigr)}{\max\!\bigl(A_{i}^{\text{pred}},A_{i}^{\text{gt}}\bigr)}, (7)

    where AipredA_{i}^{\text{pred}} and AigtA_{i}^{\text{gt}} denote the areas of the predicted and ground-truth boxes, respectively.

    其中 AipredA_{i}^{\text{pred}} 与 AigtA_{i}^{\text{gt}} 分别表示预测框与真值框的面积。

We first measure the agreement between these sets using Intersection over Union (IoU):

    where 𝒫pred\mathcal{P}_{\text{pred}} and 𝒫gt\mathcal{P}_{\text{gt}} denote the predicted and ground-truth patch ID sets, respectively.

    其中 𝒫pred\mathcal{P}_{\text{pred}} 与 𝒫gt\mathcal{P}_{\text{gt}} 分别表示预测与真值的图块编号集合。

    Match Accuracy. The prompt also specifies how many patch IDs should be returned. We therefore evaluate prompt-following behavior by checking whether the predicted set size matches the requested size. Match Accuracy is defined as the proportion of samples that satisfy this constraint:

    匹配准确率。提示词中还指定了应返回的图块编号个数。因此我们通过检查预测集合大小是否与请求大小相符来评估提示词遵循情况。匹配准确率定义为满足该约束的样本占比：

    Match Accuracy=1N​∑i=1N𝟙​(|𝒫pred|=|𝒫gt|),\text{Match Accuracy}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbbm{1}\bigl(\lvert\mathcal{P}_{\text{pred}}\rvert=\lvert\mathcal{P}_{\text{gt}}\rvert\bigr), (9)

    where NN is the number of evaluation samples, 𝒫pred\mathcal{P}_{\text{pred}} is the predicted patch set for sample ii, |𝒫gt|\lvert\mathcal{P}_{\text{gt}}\rvert is the number of patch IDs requested in the prompt, and 𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) is an indicator function that returns 1 if the condition holds and 0 otherwise.

    其中 NN 为评测样本数，𝒫pred\mathcal{P}_{\text{pred}} 为样本 ii 的预测图块集合，|𝒫gt|\lvert\mathcal{P}_{\text{gt}}\rvert 为提示词请求的图块编号个数，𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) 为指示函数，条件成立时返回 1，否则返回 0。

To evaluate whether an MLLM can correctly capture and verbalize these elements, we use GPT-5 as an automatic judge. Given a model-generated caption, the judge checks for each key element whether it is correctly identified and explicitly mentioned.     高级理解指标的要点得分：对意图理解，每张创意广告海报都配有一组人工标注的关键元素，用以概括设计所要传达的语义或概念信息。为评估 MLLM 能否正确捕捉并用语言表述这些元素，我们使用 GPT-5 作为自动评审模型。给定模型生成的描述，评审模型逐一检查每个关键元素是否被正确识别并明确提及。对某张海报，若全部标注要点都被覆盖，则预测标记为“是”，否则为“否”。由此得到的要点得分定义为被判为“是”的海报占比：

    风格生成的得分。在风格生成任务中，生成模型被指示（通过文字提示词）产出指定目标风格的海报。随后要求 MLLM 把生成的海报分类到预定义的风格标签之一。我们把预测的风格标签与真值目标标签比较，并计算风格生成得分为全部被评测样本上的准确率：

    Style Score=1N​∑i=1N𝟙​(s^i=sigt),\text{Style Score}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbbm{1}\bigl(\hat{s}_{i}=s_{i}^{\text{gt}}\bigr), (13)

    where NN is the number of generated posters, s^i\hat{s}_{i} is the style label predicted by the MLLM (GPT-5) for the ii-th poster, sigts_{i}^{\text{gt}} is the corresponding ground-truth style label, and 𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) is the indicator function.

    其中 NN 为生成的海报数，s^i\hat{s}_{i} 为 MLLM（GPT-5）对第 ii 张海报预测的风格标签，sigts_{i}^{\text{gt}} 为对应的真值风格标签，𝟙​(⋅)\mathbbm{1}(\cdot) 为指示函数。

    Font Richness for Font Generation. In the Font Generation task, the generative model is prompted to produce posters with diverse typography, where the prompt explicitly specifies the target text and encourages the use of varied font styles. After generation, we ask an MLLM with strong font understanding ability (GPT-5) to describe the typography of each poster using a fixed vocabulary of font attributes (e.g., modern, playful, serif, italic, etc.).

    字体生成的字体丰富度。在字体生成任务中，生成模型被提示生成字体排印多样的海报，提示词明确指定目标文字并鼓励使用多样的字体风格。生成之后，我们请具备较强字体理解能力的 MLLM（GPT-5）用固定的字体属性词表（如现代、活泼、衬线、斜体等）描述每张海报的字体排印。

    Let 𝒜\mathcal{A} be the set of all font attributes (e.g., the 37 attributes in our implementation), and |𝒜|=M|\mathcal{A}|=M. For a batch of NN generated posters, we define a binary indicator xi,a∈{0,1}x_{i,a}\in\{0,1\} that equals 1 if GPT-5 assigns attribute a∈𝒜a\in\mathcal{A} to the ii-th poster, and 0 otherwise. For each attribute aa, we first compute its coverage ratio over the batch:

    设 𝒜\mathcal{A} 为全部字体属性的集合（在我们的实现中为 37 个属性），且 |𝒜|=M|\mathcal{A}|=M。对于一批 NN 张生成的海报，我们定义二值指示变量 xi,a∈{0,1}x_{i,a}\in\{0,1\}，当 GPT-5 把属性 a∈𝒜a\in\mathcal{A} 赋予第 ii 张海报时取 1，否则取 0。对每个属性 aa，先计算其在该批次上的覆盖率：

    Ra=1N​∑i=1Nxi,a,R_{a}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}x_{i,a}, (14)

    which measures how frequently attribute aa appears across generated posters.

    该值度量属性 aa 在生成的海报中出现的频繁程度。

    The overall Font Richness Score is then defined as the average coverage ratio over all attributes:

    整体的字体丰富度得分则定义为所有属性上覆盖率的平均值：

    Richness=1M​∑a∈𝒜Ra=1N​M​∑a∈𝒜∑i=1Nxi,a.\text{Richness}=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{a\in\mathcal{A}}R_{a}=\frac{1}{NM}\sum_{a\in\mathcal{A}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}x_{i,a}. (15)

    Intuitively, this metric reflects how widely the generator explores the font attribute space: higher values indicate that a broader range of font attributes is realized across the generated posters.

    直观地说，该指标反映生成器对字体属性空间的探索广度：取值越高，说明生成的海报覆盖的字体属性范围越广。

    Appendix C Human Evaluation

    附录 C 人工评测

    To verify the reliability of our automatic evaluation, we conduct a series of human studies on both understanding and generation tasks. For several understanding tasks that rely on LLM-based textual judgments (e.g., Composition Understanding and Intention Understanding), we compare the decisions of the automatic judge with those of human annotators. On a subset, the agreement between the LLM judge and human evaluation reaches approximately 92%, indicating that our LLM-as-judge protocol is largely consistent with human judgments.

    为验证自动评测的可靠性，我们针对理解与生成任务开展了一系列人工研究。对若干依赖大语言模型文本判断的理解任务（如构图理解与意图理解），我们把自动评审模型的判定与人工标注员的判定进行比对。在子集上，大语言模型评审与人工评测的一致率达到约 92%，说明我们的“以大语言模型为评审”的方案与人类判断大体一致。

    For the Generation tasks, we employ MLLMs to repeatedly assess the quality and faithfulness of generated images. Specifically, in Dense Generation, Composition Generation, and Intention Generation, the judge verifies whether multiple key pieces of information are correctly rendered in the image, while in Font Generation and Style Generation, the judge directly assigns font or style labels to each poster. To validate these automatic scores, we randomly sample 30 generated images per task and obtain human ratings under the same criteria. We observe that the relative ranking of generative models remains largely consistent across different MLLM judges and human annotators, suggesting that our automatic evaluation provides a stable and trustworthy proxy for human assessment.

    对生成任务，我们采用 MLLM 反复评估生成图像的质量与忠实度。具体地，在密集生成、构图生成与意图生成中，评审模型核验多个关键信息是否被正确渲染到图像中；在字体生成与风格生成中，评审模型直接为每张海报赋予字体或风格标签。为验证这些自动分数，我们在每个任务上随机抽取 30 张生成的图像，并在相同标准下获取人工评分。我们观察到，生成模型的相对排序在不同的 MLLM 评审模型与人工标注员之间大体保持一致，说明我们的自动评测为人类评估提供了稳定可信的替代。

    Appendix D Annotator Guideline

    附录 D 标注员指南

    We adopt a multi–stage pipeline for data collection and annotation. First, we gather poster images from free sources that explicitly permit research use. All raw images are manually cleaned to remove samples with blurry content, severe artifacts, or copyright concerns. For OCR-related understanding tasks, we rely on reliable digital sources as ground-truth text. Human annotation is mainly required for layout-related tasks, advanced understanding tasks, and the overall rating task.

    我们采用多阶段流程进行数据采集与标注。首先，从明确允许研究使用的免费来源收集海报图像。全部原始图像都经过人工清洗，去除内容模糊、存在严重瑕疵或有版权顾虑的样本。对与 OCR 相关的理解任务，我们依赖可靠的数字来源作为真值文本。人工标注主要用于版面相关任务、高级理解任务与整体评分任务。

    For each such task, at least three expert annotators independently label every sample. A senior annotator (the leader) then cross-checks all submissions, resolves disagreements, and filters out ambiguous cases, retaining only samples with high inter-annotator agreement. Below we summarize the concrete annotation guidelines for representative tasks.

    对每一项此类任务，至少由三名专家标注员独立标注每个样本。随后由一名资深标注员（组长）交叉核对全部提交结果，裁定分歧并剔除含糊的样本，只保留标注员间一致度高的样本。下面概述代表性任务的具体标注指南。

    Empty Space Task. We begin from partially edited poster designs, where some design elements have been intentionally removed from the original PSD files. The resulting posters are rendered with an overlaid grid, and the grid patch indices are visible to annotators. Each poster is sent to three annotators with the following instruction: “This is an unfinished poster. New design elements need to be added. Please identify all patch IDs that you consider suitable empty regions for placing new content.” The leader aggregates the proposed patch sets and retains only those samples whose recommended regions achieve more than 90% agreement across annotators, making a final decision when minor discrepancies occur.

    留白任务。我们从经过部分编辑的海报设计出发，其中一些设计元素被有意从原始 PSD 文件中移除。生成的海报渲染时叠加网格，网格图块的编号对标注员可见。每张海报分发给三名标注员，指令如下：“这是一张未完成的海报，需要加入新的设计元素。请找出你认为适合放置新内容的全部留白区域的图块编号。”组长汇总各方提出的图块集合，只保留推荐区域在标注员间一致度超过 90% 的样本，出现细微分歧时由组长做出最终裁定。

    Composition Understanding Task. We collect posters that exhibit strong visual reconstruction or structural composition (e.g., displacement, nesting, segmentation). Each poster is distributed to multiple annotators with the instruction: “Using concise natural language, list the visual design techniques used in this poster (such as displacement, nesting, segmentation, extension, focus, mirroring, cut-out, arrangement, etc.). Describe only the necessary composition cues in bullet points.” The leader reviews and consolidates all descriptions, and keeps only those posters for which different annotators provide highly consistent composition cues.

    构图理解任务。我们收集具有强烈视觉重构或结构构成特征的海报（如位移、嵌套、分割）。每张海报分发给多名标注员，指令如下：“用简洁的自然语言，列出这张海报所使用的视觉设计手法（如位移、嵌套、分割、延展、聚焦、镜像、抠图、排布等）。以要点形式只描述必要的构图线索。”组长审阅并整合所有描述，只保留不同标注员给出高度一致构图线索的海报。

    Intention Understanding Task. We curate posters that contain clear visual metaphors or conceptual designs. Each poster is assigned to several annotators with the instruction: “First, carefully read the content in the poster. Then, search for the original source or explanation of this poster online. If the external explanation aligns with your own understanding, keep this sample and decompose its core metaphor or concept into several key pieces of information. If the external explanation conflicts with your interpretation, discard this sample.” The leader then collects and refines the key-intention annotations, merging overlapping items and removing noisy or inconsistent samples.

    意图理解任务。我们筛选包含明确视觉隐喻或概念设计的海报。每张海报分配给若干标注员，指令如下：“先仔细阅读海报中的内容。然后在网上检索该海报的原始出处或阐释。若外部阐释与你的理解一致，则保留该样本，并把其核心隐喻或概念拆解为若干条关键信息。若外部阐释与你的解读相冲突，则丢弃该样本。”组长随后收集并精炼关键意图标注，合并重叠项，剔除噪声与不一致的样本。

    Overall Rating Task. For the overall quality assessment, we distribute each poster to multiple annotators with the instruction: “Please rate the overall design quality of this poster on a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 is the worst and 10 is the best. Consider font properties, layout, textual communication, and creative concept in your score.” Because different annotators may use different scoring ranges, we first standardize their score distributions (zero-mean and variance normalization), and then discard posters whose inter-annotator score range exceeds a predefined threshold. The remaining posters, which exhibit high rating consistency, are averaged to obtain a stable ground-truth score used in our benchmark.

    整体评分任务。对整体质量评估，我们把每张海报分发给多名标注员，指令如下：“请按 0 到 10 分评价这张海报的整体设计质量，0 分为最差，10 分为最好。评分时请考虑字体属性、版式、文字传达与创意概念。”由于不同标注员可能使用不同的评分区间，我们先对各自的分数分布做标准化（零均值与方差归一化），再剔除标注员间分数极差超过预设阈值的海报。剩余评分一致性高的海报取平均，得到本基准使用的稳定真值分数。
