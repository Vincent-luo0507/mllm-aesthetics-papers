中文摘要

评测文本到图像生成模型需要与人类感知保持一致，然而现有的以人为中心的指标受限于数据覆盖不足、特征提取欠佳与损失函数效率不高。为应对这些挑战，我们提出人类偏好分数 v3（HPSv3）。其一，我们发布 HPDv3——首个宽谱人类偏好数据集，整合了来自当前最优生成模型与低质量到高质量真实世界图像的 108 万文本-图像对与 117 万条标注成对比较。其二，我们提出一个基于视觉语言模型的偏好模型，采用不确定性感知排序损失进行训练，以实现细粒度排序。此外，我们提出人类偏好链（CoHP），一种迭代式图像精化方法，无需额外数据即可提升图像质量，并在每一步用 HPSv3 选出最佳图像。大量实验表明，HPSv3 可作为宽谱图像评测的稳健指标，而 CoHP 提供了一种高效且贴合人类偏好的图像生成质量提升途径。代码与数据集已在 HPSv3 主页发布。

Abstract (原文)

Evaluating text-to-image generation models requires alignment with human perception, yet existing human-centric metrics are constrained by limited data coverage, suboptimal feature extraction, and inefficient loss functions. To address these challenges, we introduce Human Preference Score v3 (HPSv3). (1) We release HPDv3, the first wide-spectrum human preference dataset integrating 1.08M text-image pairs and 1.17M annotated pairwise comparisons from state-of-the-art generative models and low to high-quality real-world images. (2) We introduce a VLM-based preference model trained using an uncertainty-aware ranking loss for fine-grained ranking. Besides, we propose Chain-of-Human-Preference (CoHP), an iterative image refinement method that enhances quality without extra data, using HPSv3 to select the best image at each step. Extensive experiments demonstrate that HPSv3 serves as a robust metric for wide-spectrum image evaluation, and CoHP offers an efficient and human-aligned approach to improve image generation quality. The code and dataset are available at the HPSv3 Homepage.

1 Introduction

1 引言

With the rapid advancement of text-to-image generation models, human-centric evaluation methods, such as HPS [43, 41], ImageReward [46], PickScore [11], and MPS [49], have emerged to incorporate subjective human feedback. These methods construct human-annotated datasets and train models to capture the distribution of human preferences, enabling more perceptually aligned evaluations.

随着文本到图像生成模型的迅速进步，以人为中心的评测方法相继出现，例如 HPS [43, 41]、ImageReward [46]、PickScore [11] 与 MPS [49]，它们将主观的人类反馈纳入评测。这类方法构建人工标注数据集，并训练模型去捕捉人类偏好的分布，从而实现更贴合感知的评测。

However, these human-centric evaluation models remain limited by constrained data distributions, suboptimal model designs, and simplified training schemes.

然而，这些以人为中心的评测模型仍受制于数据分布狭窄、模型设计欠佳与训练方案过于简化。

On the one hand, prior human-centric evaluation metrics [43, 11, 46] are limited by the narrow quality spectrum of their training data, which primarily consists of images generated by diffusion-based models [28]. This makes them insufficient for evaluating more advanced approaches, such as diffusion transformers [3, 1] and autoregressive models [39, 10]. In addition, the lack of standardized and reliable annotation practices introduces potential biases in the data labeling process.

一方面，已有的人类中心评测指标 [43, 11, 46] 受训练数据质量谱系过窄的限制，其数据主要来自基于扩散的模型 [28]，因此不足以评测更先进的方法，如扩散 Transformer [3, 1] 与自回归模型 [39, 10]。此外，缺乏标准化、可靠的标注流程，也在数据标注环节引入了潜在偏差。

On the other hand, from a model architecture perspective, commonly used feature extractors such as CLIP [25] and BLIP [14], employed in works like [43, 41, 11, 47], have been shown to be less effective at multimodal feature extraction [15, 40], resulting in less comprehensive representations for final ranking. Moreover, directly optimizing models with KL divergence [41, 49] overlooks potential inconsistencies in annotation ground truth, a common issue in human preference annotation. These challenges highlight the need for a more robust model architecture and training paradigm that better captures human preferences.

另一方面，从模型架构看，[43, 41, 11, 47] 等工作常用的 CLIP [25] 与 BLIP [14] 特征提取器在多模态特征提取上表现较弱 [15, 40]，导致用于最终排序的表示不够全面。此外，直接用 KL 散度 [41, 49] 优化模型，忽视了标注真值中潜在的不一致性，而这在人类偏好标注中十分常见。这些挑战说明，需要更稳健的模型架构与训练范式来更好地捕捉人类偏好。

This paper introduces two key advancements to solve these challenges: (a) a meticulously annotated dataset spanning the wide quality spectrum, enriched with diverse text-image pairs and curated through a carefully designed pipeline to align with human preferences, and (b) a preference model with a robust backbone for comprehensive feature encoding, paired with an uncertainty-aware ranking loss to alleviate the uncertainty or errors in the training samples caused by annotators.

本文针对上述挑战提出两项关键改进：（a）一个覆盖宽质量谱系、经过精细标注的数据集，包含丰富多样的文本-图像对，并通过精心设计的流程构建，以契合人类偏好；（b）一个具备强健骨干网络以完成充分特征编码的偏好模型，配合不确定性感知排序损失，缓解标注者导致的训练样本不确定性或错误。

As illustrated in Figure 1, inspired by the concept of the spectrum [20], we introduce Human Preference Dataset v3 (HPDv3) — a more comprehensive and systematic dataset comprising 1.08 million text-image pairs and 1.17 million annotated pairwise comparisons. HPDv3 expands upon previous efforts by integrating outputs from state-of-the-art autoregressive and diffusion models alongside high-quality real images sourced from the Internet, representing the upper bound of human-collected image quality.

如图 1 所示，受光谱概念 [20] 启发，我们提出人类偏好数据集 v3（HPDv3）——一个更全面、更系统的数据集，包含 108 万文本-图像对与 117 万条人工标注的成对比较。HPDv3 在既有工作之上加以扩展，整合了当前最优的自回归与扩散模型输出，以及从互联网采集的高质量真实图像，后者代表了人工采集图像质量的上界。

Specifically, HPDv3 is built from three key sources: (1) expanding HPDv2 by generating images with 1010 additional state-of-the-art models, (2) curating high-quality Internet photographs, captioned by Visual Language Models (VLMs) to generate additional images, and (3) incorporating Midjourney outputs, including four generated images per prompt and a user-selected preference. To enhance reliability, we conduct pairwise comparisons with 9∼199\sim 19 human annotators per sample, ensuring diverse subjective evaluations. HPDv3 mitigates biases in prior datasets, serving as both a foundation for training preference-aligned models and a benchmark for evaluating generative models.

具体而言，HPDv3 由三个主要来源构建：（1）用 1010 个当前最优模型生成图像，以扩展 HPDv2；（2）整理高质量互联网照片，由视觉语言模型（VLM）为其生成描述文本，据此再生成图像；（3）纳入 Midjourney 的输出，包括每个提示词生成的四张图像以及一个用户选择的偏好结果。为提升可靠性，每个样本由 9∼199\sim 19 名标注者进行成对比较，以保证主观评价的多样性。HPDv3 缓解了既有数据集中的偏差，既可作为训练偏好对齐模型的基础，也可作为评测生成模型的基准。

Based on HPDv3, we introduce Human Preference Score v3 (HPSv3), a comprehensive model trained on the HPDv3 dataset, to better align image generation models with human expectations. HPSv3 features two key advancements: (1) it leverages powerful visual encoders from Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to capture rich, high-dimensional features, and (2) it employs an uncertainty-aware ranking loss to more accurately distinguish subtle differences in image quality. Trained on a diverse dataset of both generated and high-quality real images, HPSv3 offers a robust and reliable evaluation framework that closely mirrors human preferences.

在 HPDv3 的基础上，我们提出人类偏好分数 v3（HPSv3）——一个在该数据集上训练的综合性模型，用于使图像生成模型更好地契合人类期望。HPSv3 有两项关键改进：（1）借助视觉语言模型（VLM）强大的视觉编码器捕捉丰富的高维特征；（2）采用不确定性感知排序损失，更准确地区分图像质量上的细微差异。由于在生成图像与高质量真实图像构成的多样化数据上训练，HPSv3 提供了稳健可靠的评测框架，能够高度贴合人类偏好。

Finally, we introduce Chain-of-Human-Preference (CoHP), a novel, reasoning-driven process for image generation. CoHP’s first innovation is the integration of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) inspired reasoning, which systematically improves image quality without the need for a training dataset. Second, it utilizes HPSv3 as a reward model to evaluate images based on semantic alignment, realism, and aesthetic appeal. By guiding the reasoning process, HPSv3 ensures the selection of the highest-quality image at each step, leading to a progressively refined and enhanced final output.

最后，我们提出人类偏好链（CoHP）——一种由推理驱动的图像生成新流程。CoHP 的第一项创新是引入受思维链（CoT）启发的推理机制，无需任何训练数据即可系统性地提升图像质量。第二项创新是把 HPSv3 用作奖励模型，从语义对齐、真实感与美学吸引力三个方面评估图像。HPSv3 通过引导推理过程，保证每一步都选出质量最高的图像，使最终输出得到逐步精化与提升。

    2.2 Human Preference Models

    2.2 人类偏好模型

    Recently, several studies such as HPS [43], HPSv2 [41], MPS [49] and PickScore [11] have suggested fine-tuning CLIP based on human preferences for images generated from the same textual prompt. ImageReward [46] utilizes BLIP encoder as the feature extractor to train a reward model to evaluate and improve the generative models. However, these models have primarily been adjusted using images from Stable Diffusion and its variants, indicating that their ability to generalize across different datasets has yet to be validated. Also, utilizing CLIP/BLIP encoders as feature extractors is constrained due to either broad semantic information or limited feature dimensions, which may not effectively encapsulate user behaviors and expectations.

    近来，HPS [43]、HPSv2 [41]、MPS [49] 与 PickScore [11] 等研究提出基于人类偏好对 CLIP 进行微调，用于评估由同一文本提示词生成的图像。ImageReward [46] 则以 BLIP 编码器为特征提取器训练奖励模型，以评测并改进生成模型。然而，这些模型主要使用 Stable Diffusion 及其变体的图像进行调整，其跨数据集的泛化能力尚未得到验证。此外，用 CLIP/BLIP 编码器作特征提取器存在局限：语义信息过于宽泛或特征维度有限，难以有效刻画用户行为与期望。

    2.3 Image quality dataset

    2.3 图像质量数据集

    Text-to-image generative models often rely on high-quality real images sourced from the Internet to ensure the fidelity of their generated outputs. HPDv1 [42], ImageReward [46] and Pick-a-Pic [11] are human-annotated datasets of comparison between paired images, focusing on evaluating generated images and improving the image quality by aligning text-to-image models with human preference. Similar to these dataset, HPDv2 [41] includes more images from a wider range of generative models, enabling a more comprehensive evaluation of these preference prediction models. However, these datasets mainly compare images generated from existing models, neglecting comparisons with high-quality real images.

    文本到图像生成模型通常依赖从互联网获取的高质量真实图像，以保证生成结果的保真度。HPDv1 [42]、ImageReward [46] 与 Pick-a-Pic [11] 是对成对图像进行比较的人工标注数据集，侧重评测生成图像，并通过使文本到图像模型对齐人类偏好来提升图像质量。与此类似，HPDv2 [41] 纳入了更多生成模型、范围更广的图像，从而对这些偏好预测模型进行更全面的评测。然而，这些数据集主要比较已有模型生成的图像，忽略了与高质量真实图像的比较。

    3 Human Preference Dataset v3

    3 人类偏好数据集 v3

    Existing human preference benchmarks fail to provide a robust evaluation framework for generative models due to two key limitations: the absence of high-quality real photographs to define an upper quality bound, and the use of outdated generative models.

    已有的人类偏好基准存在两处关键局限，因而无法为生成模型提供稳健的评测框架：缺少用于定义质量上界的高质量真实照片，以及所采用的生成模型已经过时。

    We present Human Preference Dataset v3 (HPDv3) to overcome these issues. This comprehensive dataset contains 1.171.17 million binary preference choices across 1.081.08 million images, grouped in pairs by prompt. The annotations are highly reliable, with each decision validated by 99 to 1919 specialist annotators to meet a 90%90\% agreement threshold. Source model details are provided in the supplementary materials.

    为此，我们提出人类偏好数据集 v3（HPDv3）。该综合性数据集按提示词将图像两两分组，包含 1.171.17 百万条二值偏好选择，覆盖 1.081.08 百万张图像。标注具有很高的可靠性：每项判断都由 99 至 1919 名专业标注者核验，并需达到 90%90\% 的一致率阈值。来源模型的细节见补充材料。

    3.1 Text-image Pairs Collection

    3.1 文本-图像对采集

    HPDv2 represents the most extensive annotated human preference dataset to date, comprising 798,000798,000 binary preference choices over 434,000434,000 images.     HPDv2 是目前规模最大的人工标注人类偏好数据集，包含 798,000798,000 条二值偏好选择，覆盖 434,000434,000 张图像。然而，其可用性受两处主要缺陷制约。其一，图像语料由生成模型输出与低质量真实图像构成，缺少建立稳健质量上界所必需的高质量真实照片。其二，数据集的模型覆盖已经过时，仅延伸到 Stable Diffusion 2.0，因而缺少与近期最优模型的比较。

    3.2 Annotation Pipeline

    3.2 标注流程

    We conduct the annotation by inviting users to select a preferred image from an image pair with the identical text prompt. Our annotation emphasis the annotator expertise and annotation quality.

    我们通过邀请用户从具有相同文本提示词的图像对中选出更偏好的一张来完成标注。标注工作强调标注者的专业能力与标注质量。

    To ensure annotator proficiency, we created a validation set comprising 600600 image pairs annotated by 2020 professional artists, achieving an 80%80\% convergence rate.     4 Human Preference Score v3

    4 人类偏好分数 v3

    While human evaluation remains the gold standard for assessing preferences toward synthetic images, it suffers from high costs and limited scalability. To address this, we introduce Human Preference Score v3 (HPSv3), a model that learns human preferences from annotations to serve as a scalable and automated evaluator.

    人工评测仍是评估合成图像偏好的金标准，但成本高昂且难以规模化。为此，我们提出人类偏好分数 v3（HPSv3）——一个从标注中学习人类偏好的模型，可作为可扩展的自动化评测器。

    6 Conclusion

    6 结论

    We introduce Human Preference Score v3 (HPSv3), involving: (1) HPDv3, the first wide-spectrum human preference dataset with 1.081.08 millon text-image pairs and 1.171.17 millon annotated comparisons from state-of-the-art generative models and high-quality real images, and (2) a preference model leveraging VLM-based feature extraction with uncertainty-aware ranking loss for accurate image scoring. Additionally, we propose Chain-of-Human-Preference (CoHP), a novel reasoning-based approach for iterative image refinement without requiring extra training data. Extensive experiments validate HPSv3 as a robust benchmark and CoHP as an efficient, human-aligned method for enhancing image generation quality.

    我们提出人类偏好分数 v3（HPSv3），包括：（1）HPDv3，首个宽谱人类偏好数据集，包含来自当前最优生成模型与高质量真实图像的 1.081.08 百万文本-图像对与 1.171.17 百万条标注比较；（2）一个基于视觉语言模型进行特征提取、并采用不确定性感知排序损失实现准确打分的偏好模型。此外，我们提出人类偏好链（CoHP）——一种基于推理的迭代图像精化新方法，无需额外的训练数据。大量实验验证了 HPSv3 作为稳健基准的有效性，以及 CoHP 作为高效且贴合人类偏好的图像生成质量提升方法的作用。

    5.1 Training Dataset

    5.1 训练数据集

    For training our final model, we use data from four sources: HPDv3, subsets of Pick-A-Pic and ImageReward, and real user preference data collected from Midjourney. In total, the training dataset comprises 1.51.5 million pairwise samples.

    训练最终模型时，我们使用了四个来源的数据：HPDv3、Pick-A-Pic 与 ImageReward 的子集，以及从 Midjourney 收集的真实用户偏好数据。训练集共包含 1.51.5 百万条成对样本。

    Specifically, the HPDv3 dataset is composed of two parts. The first part includes pairwise comparisons of real images and images generated based on prompts from JourneyDB and HPDv2. These comparisons are annotated using the annotation pipeline, and only those with a confidence score of 0.950.95 or higher are selected for training.

    具体而言，HPDv3 数据集由两部分组成。第一部分包含真实图像之间、以及基于 JourneyDB 与 HPDv2 提示词生成图像之间的成对比较。这些比较通过前述标注流程完成，仅置信度达到 0.950.95 及以上的样本被选入训练。

    The second part is a manually curated golden training set. This contains high-quality sample data. We use this golden trainset to train a filtering model, which shares the same architecture and training methodology as HPSv3. This filtering model is applied to choose samples from HPDv2.     5.3 Clarification on Loss Function

    5.3 关于损失函数的说明

    In this section, we clarify that various loss functions mentioned in the literature [46, 11, 41, 49] (including this paper) and the bradley-terry loss [2] share the same underlying optimization objective.

    本节说明文献 [46, 11, 41, 49]（含本文）中提到的各种损失函数与 Bradley-Terry 损失 [2] 具有相同的底层优化目标。

    Form 1: Optimizing KL-divergence.
    形式 1：优化 KL 散度。

    In [41, 49, 11], the predicted preference yi^\hat{y_{i}} is calculated as::

    在 [41, 49, 11] 中，预测偏好 yi^\hat{y_{i}} 计算为：

    y^i=exp⁡(ri)OPEN∑j=12exp⁡(rj)),\hat{y}_{i}=\frac{\exp\left(r_{i}\right)}{\sum_{j=1}^{2}\exp\left(r_{j})\right)}, (7)

    where rir_{i} denotes the preference score of sample xix_{i}. And the model is optimized by minimizing the KL-divergence between the ground truth yy and the predicted distribution. Specifically, y=[1,0]y=[1,0] if sample x1x_{1} is preferred over x2x_{2}, and y=[0,1]y=[0,1] otherwise. The loss function is formalized as below to minimize KL-divergence:

    其中 rir_{i} 表示样本 xix_{i} 的偏好分数。模型通过最小化真值 yy 与预测分布之间的 KL 散度进行优化。具体地，若样本 x1x_{1} 优于 x2x_{2}，则 y=[1,0]y=[1,0]，否则取 y=[0,1]y=[0,1]。最小化 KL 散度的损失函数形式化如下：

    Lpref=∑j=12yi​(log⁡yi−log⁡yj^).L_{\mathrm{pref}}=\sum_{j=1}^{2}y_{i}\left(\log y_{i}-\log\hat{y_{j}}\right). (8)

    To simplify the problem, we assume that the sample xhx_{h} is the preferred sample, while xlx_{l} is the dispreferred one. According to the order, yiy_{i} will always be 11. Substituting them into the loss function, we obtain:

    为简化问题，我们假设样本 xhx_{h} 为偏好样本，而 xlx_{l} 为非偏好样本。按此顺序，yiy_{i} 恒为 11。将其代入损失函数，可得：

    Lpref=−log⁡(exp⁡(rh)exp⁡(rh)+exp⁡(rl))=−log⁡(11+exp⁡(rl−rh))=log⁡(1+exp⁡(rl−rh)).\begin{split}L_{\mathrm{pref}}&=-\log\left(\frac{\exp(r_{h})}{\exp(r_{h})+\exp(r_{l})}\right)\\ &=-\log\left(\frac{1}{1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})}\right)\\ &=\log\left(1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})\right).\end{split} (9)

    This shows that the KL-divergence formulation reduces to a logistic loss comparing the preference scores of the two samples.

    这表明 KL 散度形式最终归结为比较两个样本偏好分数的 logistic 损失。

    Form 2: Bradley-Terry Loss.
    形式 2：Bradley-Terry 损失。

    [46, 2] adopt the Bradley-Terry loss to maximize the probability of the winning (higher-ranked) samples over the losing (lower-ranked) samples. The probability of the winning samples in Bradley-Terry model can be defined as:

    [46, 2] 采用 Bradley-Terry 损失，以最大化获胜（排名较高）样本相对落败（排名较低）样本的概率。Bradley-Terry 模型中获胜样本的概率可定义为：

    P⁡(xh≻xl)=exp⁡(rh)exp⁡(rh)+exp⁡(rl)=11+exp⁡(rl−rh)=sigmoid​(rl−rh).\begin{split}P(x_{h}\succ x_{l})&=\frac{\exp(r_{h})}{\exp(r_{h})+\exp(r_{l})}\\ &=\frac{1}{1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})}\\ &=\text{sigmoid}(r_{l}-r_{h}).\end{split} (10)

    The goal is to maximize this probability. Therefore, the model is optimized by minimizing the negative log-likelihood:

    目标是最大化该概率，因此模型通过最小化负对数似然进行优化：

    LBT=−log⁡P⁡(xh≻xl)=−log⁡(sigmoid​(rl−rh))\begin{split}L_{\mathrm{BT}}&=-\log P(x_{h}\succ x_{l})\\ &=-\log(\text{sigmoid}(r_{l}-r_{h}))\\ \end{split} (11)

    However, we can continue to simplify it.

    不过，我们还可以继续对其化简。

    LBT=−log⁡(sigmoid​(rl−rh))=−log⁡(11+exp⁡(rl−rh))=log⁡(1+exp⁡(rl−rh)).\begin{split}L_{\mathrm{BT}}&=-\log(\text{sigmoid}(r_{l}-r_{h}))\\ &=-\log(\frac{1}{1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})})=\log\left(1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})\right).\end{split} (12)
    Equivalence Conclusion.
    等价性结论。

    We can observe that both Form 1 (KL-divergence) and Form 2 (Bradley-Terry) ultimately converge to the same pair-wise logistic ranking loss:

    可以看到，形式 1（KL 散度）与形式 2（Bradley-Terry）最终都收敛到同一个成对 logistic 排序损失：

    L=log⁡(1+exp⁡(rl−rh)),L=\log\left(1+\exp(r_{l}-r_{h})\right), (13)

    demonstrating their fundamental equivalence in optimization objectives despite different theoretical origins.

    这说明二者尽管理论来源不同，在优化目标上具有根本的等价性。

    8 HPSv3 as Reward Model

    8 HPSv3 作为奖励模型

    When using reinforcement learning (RL) to improve the quality of generated images, the design of the reward model is critically important. A well-designed reward model can significantly improve outputs by boosting realism, aesthetic quality, and text-image alignment, or by aligning outputs more closely with human preferences. Leveraging a carefully built wide-spectrum image quality dataset and a backbone based on a Visual Language Model, HPSv3 excels at capturing human preferences more accurately. This reduces reward hacking behaviors in RL, guiding the model to produce content that better matches human expectations.

    在使用强化学习（RL）提升生成图像质量时，奖励模型的设计至关重要。设计良好的奖励模型可以通过提升真实感、美学质量与图文对齐度，或使输出更贴合人类偏好，显著改善生成结果。依托精心构建的宽谱图像质量数据集与基于视觉语言模型的骨干网络，HPSv3 能更准确地捕捉人类偏好，从而减少 RL 中的奖励破解行为，引导模型生成更符合人类期望的内容。

    DanceGRPO We employ DanceGRPO [48] as the reinforcement learning algorithm for image generation and compare the results when using ImageReward, PiscScore, HPSv2 and HPSv3 as reward models, respectively. DanceGRPO performs multiple sampling of diffusion trajectories, scores the final generated image of each trajectory using the reward model, and conducts policy gradient optimization by calculating the advantage value of each trajectory relative to the average reward, thereby improving the model’s performance. For all reward models, we use the same default experimental settings in DanceGRPO. We use Stable-Diffusion v1.4[27] as our base model, and performs 300 training iteration.

    DanceGRPO。我们采用 DanceGRPO [48] 作为图像生成的强化学习算法，并分别比较以 ImageReward、PickScore、HPSv2 与 HPSv3 作为奖励模型时的结果。DanceGRPO 对扩散轨迹进行多次采样，用奖励模型为每条轨迹的最终生成图像打分，并通过计算各轨迹相对平均奖励的优势值执行策略梯度优化，从而提升模型性能。所有奖励模型均沿用 DanceGRPO 的默认实验设置，基础模型为 Stable-Diffusion v1.4 [27]，共训练 300 次迭代。

    HPDv3 包含 1.08M 个文本-图像对与 1.17M 条成对数据，旨在反映真实世界的用户偏好，但也存在固有的局限，可能影响其在某些情境下的泛化能力与适用性。

    Prompt Distribution Bias The dataset construction is primarily based on the prompt categories frequently observed in the JourneyDB database, which reflects general user input patterns. While this approach captures a broad range of typical generative use cases, it may inadvertently overlook specialized domains such as medicine, biology, physics, and other specialized fields requiring unique data. For example, generative models tailored for medical imaging or scientific diagram generation might not perform accurately when benchmarked with our dataset. This potential bias could limit the dataset’s usefulness for evaluating models designed for these specialized applications.

    提示词分布偏差。数据集构建主要依据 JourneyDB 数据库中高频出现的提示词类别，反映了一般用户的输入模式。这种做法虽覆盖了广泛的典型生成用例，却可能无意中遗漏医学、生物、物理等需要专用数据的专门领域。例如，面向医学影像或科学图表生成的生成模型，用本数据集评测时可能表现不准。这种潜在偏差会限制数据集在评估面向这些专门应用设计的模型时的作用。

    Unified Scoring Metric Our annotation pipeline employs a unified scoring metric to evaluate text-image pairs holistically across all dimensions. While this approach simplifies the evaluation process, it does not provide insights into more fine-grained dimensions such as color fidelity, artistic style, sharpness, or image clarity. This lack of granularity might hinder more detailed analysis and benchmarking of generative models, especially for applications where specific attributes are critical.

    统一评分指标。我们的标注流程采用统一评分指标，从整体上评估文本-图像对。这种做法简化了评测过程，却无法给出色彩保真度、艺术风格、锐度或图像清晰度等更细粒度维度的信息。粒度不足会妨碍对生成模型进行更细致的分析与基准测试，尤其是在特定属性至关重要的应用中。

    Annotator Demographics The dataset annotation process did not enforce strict demographic controls or categorizations. Information about annotators’ ethnicity, age, professional expertise, and cultural background was not collected or utilized during data annotation. As a result, the annotations may reflect unintended bias based on the subjective perspectives of the annotators. This lack of demographic diversity could reduce the robustness of the dataset in evaluating generative models designed for global or culturally sensitive contexts.

    标注者人口统计。数据集标注过程未实施严格的人口统计控制或分类，标注中也未采集或使用标注者的族裔、年龄、专业背景与文化背景等信息。因此，标注结果可能反映出基于标注者主观视角的无意偏差。人口统计多样性的缺乏，会降低数据集在评测面向全球或文化敏感场景的生成模型时的稳健性。

    Challenges in Difficult Cases To ensure robust annotations, we adopted a multi-annotator approach for labeling text-image pairs, allowing feedback from multiple individuals to improve the reliability of scores. However, this approach encountered challenges when dealing with difficult or ambiguous cases. For prompts or images that were subjective or had conflicting interpretations, annotators often struggled to converge on a consistent score. These unresolved discrepancies can affect the accuracy of the dataset and limit its ability to serve as a definitive benchmark in such cases. Despite these challenges, the multi-annotator mechanism remains a valuable method for improving dataset reliability overall.

    困难样本的挑战。为保证标注稳健，我们采用多标注者方式为文本-图像对打标，借助多人反馈提升分数的可靠性。然而，这一方式在处理困难或含糊的样本时遇到了挑战：对于主观性强或存在理解冲突的提示词或图像，标注者往往难以收敛到一致的分数。这些未消解的分歧会影响数据集的准确性，限制其在上述情形下作为确定性基准的能力。尽管存在这些挑战，多标注者机制整体上仍是提升数据集可靠性的有效方法。
