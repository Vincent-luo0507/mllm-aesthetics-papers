**EN** 1 Introduction

**ZH** 1 引言

**EN** The rapid advancement of Large Language Models (LLMs) (Brown et al., 2020; Chowdhery et al., 2022) has underscored the importance of evaluating their alignment with human intent in generated responses, making it an active field of research. Traditional n-gram metrics like BLEU Papineni et al. (2002) and ROUGE Lin (2004), as well as more sophisticated model-based evaluations such as BERTScore Zhang et al. (2020) and BARTScore Yuan, Neubig, and Liu (2021), are insufficient for thoroughly assessing this alignment He et al. (2023). While human evaluation provides the most accurate measure of model performance and valuable insights, it can often be costly and time-consuming. As a result, there is a growing demand for automated assessment methods that can consistently align with human judgments while being more efficient and cost-effective.

**ZH** 大语言模型（LLM）的快速发展（Brown 等，2020；Chowdhery 等，2022）凸显了评估其生成回复与人类意图对齐程度的重要性，使这一方向成为活跃的研究领域。BLEU（Papineni 等，2002）与 ROUGE（Lin，2004）等传统的 n 元组指标，以及 BERTScore（Zhang 等，2020）与 BARTScore（Yuan、Neubig 与 Liu，2021）等更复杂的基于模型的评估方法，都不足以充分衡量这种对齐（He 等，2023）。人工评估能提供对模型性能最准确的度量与有价值的洞见，却往往成本高、耗时长。因此，人们越来越需要既能与人类判断保持一致、又更高效且更经济的自动评估方法。

**EN** Figure 1: Simply changing the order of candidate responses leads to overturned comparison results, even though we add the command “ensuring that the order in which the responses were presented does not affect your judgment” into the prompt.

**ZH** 图 1：仅仅改变候选回复的顺序就会导致比较结果被推翻，即便我们在提示中加入了「确保回复呈现的顺序不影响你的判断」这样的指令。

**EN** ChatGPT (OpenAI, 2022) and GPT-4 OpenAI (2023) have recently demonstrated remarkable performance across various tasks, leading to their widespread use as both the annotators Peng et al. (2023); Xu et al. (2023) and evaluators Zheng et al. (2023); Peng et al. (2023); Sun et al. (2023); Zhou et al. (2023); Gao et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2023b); Dubois et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2023a). For example, The evaluation pipeline of Vicuna Zheng et al. (2023) has gained significant interest and wide usage due to its simplicity and interpretability. It prompts GPT-4 to score and compare candidate responses and provide explanations, making it a valuable tool for evaluation. However, it is unclear how reliable LLMs are as evaluators, as they are known to be sensitive to textual instructions and inputs Dong et al. (2022); Turpin et al. (2023); Bowman (2023). This raises questions about the resilience of this paradigm against perturbations, such as the ordering of candidates during scoring, potentially becoming the Achilles’ Heel that can be easily hacked for unreliable evaluations.

**ZH** ChatGPT（OpenAI，2022）与 GPT-4（OpenAI，2023）近期在各类任务上展现出卓越性能，因而被广泛用作标注者（Peng 等，2023；Xu 等，2023）与评估者（Zheng 等，2023；Peng 等，2023；Sun 等，2023；Zhou 等，2023；Gao 等，2023；Wang 等，2023b；Dubois 等，2023；Wang 等，2023a）。例如，Vicuna 的评估流程（Zheng 等，2023）因其简洁与可解释性而受到广泛关注并被大量使用：它提示 GPT-4 对候选回复打分与比较，并给出解释，使其成为有价值的评估工具。然而，LLM 作为评估者究竟有多可靠仍不清楚，因为它们对文本指令与输入十分敏感（Dong 等，2022；Turpin 等，2023；Bowman，2023）。这就引出一个问题：这一范式在面对扰动——例如打分时候选回复的排列顺序——时稳健性如何；这种扰动可能成为其阿喀琉斯之踵，轻易被利用来得到不可靠的评估结果。

**EN** In this paper, we take a sober look at the LLMs-as-evaluator paradigm and uncover a significant positional bias. Specifically, we demonstrate that GPT-4 exhibits a preference for the first displayed candidate response by consistently assigning it higher scores, even when the order of candidates is subtly altered. As illustrated in Figure 1, merely swapping the presentation order can reverse evaluation outcomes. This bias is also present in ChatGPT, which typically favors the second response. These findings highlight previously overlooked limitations in the current evaluation paradigm.

**ZH** 本文冷静审视「以 LLM 为评估者」这一范式，揭示出一种显著的位置偏差。具体地，我们证明 GPT-4 对第一个展示的候选回复表现出偏好，持续给它打更高的分数，即便候选顺序被细微改变。如图 1 所示，仅仅交换呈现顺序就能逆转评估结果。这种偏差在 ChatGPT 中同样存在，它通常偏爱第二个回复。这些发现凸显了当前评估范式此前被忽视的局限。

**EN** To address this issue, we propose three simple yet effective strategies to calibrate positional bias: 1) Multiple Evidence Calibration (MEC): We prompt the model to generate evaluation evidence before assigning scores, leveraging the inherent properties of causal language models for calibration. We also employ ensemble techniques to incorporate multiple evidence calibration results to further stabilize the evaluation. 2) Balanced Position Calibration (BPC): To further reduce positional bias, we evaluate each candidate in both positions across two runs and compute the final score as the average of the two runs. 3) Human In The Loop Calibration (HITLC): We also explore human-in-the-loop evaluation and consider a diversity-based method to get a cue to indicate biased candidates based on the evaluation results of MEC and BPC.

**ZH** 为解决这一问题，我们提出三种简单而有效的策略来校准位置偏差：1) 多证据校准（MEC）：我们提示模型在打分之前先生成评估证据，利用因果语言模型的固有性质进行校准；我们还采用集成技术把多个证据校准结果结合起来，进一步稳定评估。2) 均衡位置校准（BPC）：为进一步降低位置偏差，我们在两轮评估中让每个候选分别处于两个位置，并以两轮得分的平均值作为最终分数。3) 人在回路校准（HITLC）：我们还探索人在回路的评估方式，并考虑一种基于多样性的方法，依据 MEC 与 BPC 的评估结果得到一条线索，用以指示哪些候选可能存在偏差。

**EN** To assess the efficacy of our methods, we manually annotate the “win/tie/lose” outcomes of responses from ChatGPT and Vicuna-13B in the Vicuna benchmark Zheng et al. (2023), encompassing ⟦SLOT_0⟧ questions spanning ⟦SLOT_1⟧ distinct question categories. Our MEC and BPC enhance the evaluation alignment of GPT-4 and ChatGPT by ⟦SLOT_2⟧% and ⟦SLOT_3⟧% accuracy, respectively. Moreover, based on MEC and BPC, our HITLC can further effectively integrate human assistance into the evaluation process. Specifically, with only a ⟦SLOT_4⟧% human annotation cost, GPT-4 and ChatGPT can achieve comparable or even better annotation alignment with the average human performance, reducing the annotation cost by up to ⟦SLOT_5⟧%.

**ZH** 为检验这些方法的有效性，我们人工标注了 Vicuna 基准（Zheng 等，2023）中 ChatGPT 与 Vicuna-13B 回复的「胜/平/负」结果，涵盖 ⟦SLOT_0⟧ 个问题、横跨 ⟦SLOT_1⟧ 个不同的问题类别。我们的 MEC 与 BPC 分别把 GPT-4 与 ChatGPT 的评估一致性提升了 ⟦SLOT_2⟧% 与 ⟦SLOT_3⟧% 的准确率。此外，在 MEC 与 BPC 的基础上，我们的 HITLC 能进一步有效地把人工协助融入评估流程。具体地，仅需 ⟦SLOT_4⟧% 的人工标注成本，GPT-4 与 ChatGPT 就能达到与人类平均水平相当甚至更好的标注一致性，同时把标注成本最多降低 ⟦SLOT_5⟧%。

**EN** In summary, our key contributions are: 1) We reveal that LLMs exhibit severe positional bias, compromising their fairness as evaluators; 2) We develop a calibration framework with three simple yet effective strategies to calibrate the positional bias of LLMs; 3) We manually annotate the “win/tie/lose” outcomes of responses from ChatGPT and Vicuna-13B in the Vicuna benchmark and demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed approach through experimental results, which show closer alignment with human judgments.

**ZH** 总而言之，我们的主要贡献是：1) 我们揭示 LLM 存在严重的位置偏差，损害了其作为评估者的公平性；2) 我们开发一个包含三种简单而有效策略的校准框架，用于校准 LLM 的位置偏差；3) 我们人工标注了 Vicuna 基准中 ChatGPT 与 Vicuna-13B 回复的「胜/平/负」结果，并通过实验结果证明了所提方法的有效性——这些结果显示出与人类判断更接近的一致性。

**EN** 2.1 LLMs as Evaluators

**ZH** 2.1 以 LLM 为评估者

**EN** Recently, researchers have been utilizing LLMs such as GPT-4 as evaluators to compare the performance of two AI assistants. As shown in Table 1, an evaluation template with three placeholders ⟦SLOT_6⟧, is used to query the LLM for evaluation. For each testing question ⟦SLOT_7⟧, given two responses ⟦SLOT_8⟧ and ⟦SLOT_9⟧ from Assistant 1 and Assistant 2, respectively, the researchers populate these responses into the corresponding slots of the evaluation template to form a prompt: ⟦SLOT_10⟧ The prompt is then used to query the LLM in order to obtain the comparison result. **ZH** 近来，研究者开始利用 GPT-4 等 LLM 作为评估者来比较两个 AI 助手的性能。如表 1 所示，人们使用一个含三个占位符 ⟦SLOT_6⟧ 的评估模板向 LLM 查询评估。对每个测试问题 ⟦SLOT_7⟧，给定分别来自 Assistant 1 与 Assistant 2 的两个回复 ⟦SLOT_8⟧ 与 ⟦SLOT_9⟧，研究者把这些回复填入评估模板的相应插槽构成提示：⟦SLOT_10⟧ 随后用该提示查询 LLM 以获得比较结果。本文发现，LLM 存在严重的位置偏差，即：交换两个回复的插槽并向 LLM 查询两次，评估者极有可能给出相互矛盾的评估结果，并且评估者偏好处于某一特定位置的回复。

**EN** 3 Calibrating the Positional Bias

**ZH** 3 校准位置偏差

**EN** We have identified that positional bias can significantly impact the evaluation results of LLMs, making them unfair evaluators. In this section, we propose a calibration framework with three simple yet effective strategies to alleviate this bias to achieve a more reliable and fair evaluation result.

**ZH** 我们已经确认，位置偏差会显著影响 LLM 的评估结果，使它们成为不公平的评估者。本节提出一个包含三种简单而有效策略的校准框架，以缓解这一偏差，从而获得更可靠、更公平的评估结果。

**EN** 3.1 Multiple Evidence Calibration

**ZH** 3.1 多证据校准

**EN** Previous studies Zheng et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2023b) utilize the evaluation template that draws the conclusion first and then makes an explanation, e.g., the template used in Table 1. However, due to the nature of the auto-regressive model, the conclusions generated by the model are not supported by the explanation generated afterward. To this end, as shown in Table 3, we design an evidence calibration (EC) evaluation template ⟦SLOT_30⟧ that requires the model to generate the explanation (evaluation evidence) first and then give the score. In this way, the score can be calibrated with the evaluation evidence. To further improve the reliability of the evaluation, rather than generating only a single EC score for each response, we perform a multiple evidence calibration (MEC, Figure 3(a)) that samples ⟦SLOT_31⟧ EC scores ⟦SLOT_32⟧ and ⟦SLOT_33⟧ for responses ⟦SLOT_34⟧ and ⟦SLOT_35⟧, where ⟦SLOT_36⟧ and ⟦SLOT_37⟧ denotes scores of the response ⟦SLOT_38⟧ at the first and second positions, respectively.

**ZH** 先前研究（Zheng 等，2023；Wang 等，2023b）使用的评估模板先给出结论、再作解释，例如表 1 中的模板。然而，由于自回归模型的性质，模型生成的结论并不由随后生成的解释所支撑。为此，如表 3 所示，我们设计了一个证据校准（EC）评估模板 ⟦SLOT_30⟧，要求模型先生成解释（评估证据）再给出分数。这样一来，分数就能由评估证据加以校准。为进一步提升评估的可靠性，我们不为每个回复只生成单个 EC 分数，而是执行多证据校准（MEC，图 3(a)）：采样 ⟦SLOT_31⟧ 个 EC 分数 ⟦SLOT_32⟧ 与 ⟦SLOT_33⟧，分别对应回复 ⟦SLOT_34⟧ 与 ⟦SLOT_35⟧，其中 ⟦SLOT_36⟧ 与 ⟦SLOT_37⟧ 表示回复 ⟦SLOT_38⟧ 在第一个与第二个位置上的得分。

**EN** LLMs have demonstrated powerful general generation capabilities, becoming universal assistants OpenAI (2022); OpenAI (2023); Song et al. (2023b). With the rapid advancement of LLMs, it becomes crucial to evaluate their ability to follow human instructions. Traditional evaluation methods assess the ability by calculating a metric, like BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, or BARTScore, to compare the generated response with a reference response. However, these metrics do not adequately measure the alignment of the generated response with human intent He et al. (2023). While human evaluation is treated as the most accurate measurement of model performance, it is costly and time-consuming to operate at scales. Considering the potent capabilities of LLMs, researchers have started utilizing LLMs to evaluate the proficiency of generative models in adhering to human instructions Zheng et al. (2023); Lu et al. (2023); Li et al. (2023). In these works, Vicuna’s evaluation paradigm Zheng et al. (2023) is widely adopted, where it provides a question and two responses from two models, and uses GPT-4 to determine which response has better quality.

**ZH** LLM 已展现出强大的通用生成能力，成为通用助手（OpenAI，2022；OpenAI，2023；Song 等，2023b）。随着 LLM 的快速发展，评估其遵循人类指令的能力变得至关重要。传统评估方法通过计算某个指标——如 BLEU、ROUGE、BERTScore 或 BARTScore——把生成的回复与参考回复作比较来衡量这一能力。然而这些指标并不能充分度量生成回复与人类意图的对齐程度（He 等，2023）。尽管人工评估被视为对模型性能最准确的度量，但在规模化运作时成本高、耗时长。鉴于 LLM 的强大能力，研究者开始利用 LLM 来评估生成模型遵循人类指令的熟练程度（Zheng 等，2023；Lu 等，2023；Li 等，2023）。在这些工作中，Vicuna 的评估范式（Zheng 等，2023）被广泛采用：它提供一个问题与来自两个模型的两个回复，并用 GPT-4 判断哪个回复质量更好。

**EN** 6.2 Bias of Deep Neural Networks

**ZH** 6.2 深度神经网络的偏差

**EN** Deep Neural Networks have been proven to easily learn biases from the data, which significantly impacts their reliability. Specifically, bias has also been investigated in natural language inference (Gururangan et al., 2018; McCoy, Pavlick, and Linzen, 2019; Belinkov et al., 2019; Liu et al., 2020a; Liu et al., 2020b), question answering (Min et al., 2019), ROC story cloze (Cai, Tu, and Gimpel, 2017; Schwartz et al., 2017), lexical inference Levy et al. (2015), visual question answering Goyal et al. (2017), information extraction Wang et al. (2021); Wang et al. (2022); Song et al. (2023a); Xia et al. (2023) and so on. LLMs are pre-trained using a vast amount of data from the internet, making it highly likely for them to learn biases present in those materials. Although the LLMs are already widely adopted as a proxy of human evaluators, the reliability of this paradigm is not well explored. In this paper, we critically examine the LLMs-as-evaluator paradigm and uncover a significant positional bias. Furthermore, we propose three simple yet effective methods to calibrate the positional bias to achieve reliable and fair evaluation results.

**ZH** 深度神经网络已被证明很容易从数据中学到偏差，这显著影响其可靠性。具体地，偏差也在如下方向被研究过：自然语言推理（Gururangan 等，2018；McCoy、Pavlick 与 Linzen，2019；Belinkov 等，2019；Liu 等，2020a；Liu 等，2020b）、问答（Min 等，2019）、ROC 故事完形填空（Cai、Tu 与 Gimpel，2017；Schwartz 等，2017）、词汇推理（Levy 等，2015）、视觉问答（Goyal 等，2017）、信息抽取（Wang 等，2021；Wang 等，2022；Song 等，2023a；Xia 等，2023）等等。LLM 使用海量互联网数据预训练，因而极有可能学到其中存在的偏差。尽管 LLM 已被广泛用作人类评估者的代理，这一范式的可靠性却未得到充分考察。本文批判性地审视「以 LLM 为评估者」的范式，揭示出显著的位置偏差；此外，我们提出三种简单而有效的方法来校准位置偏差，以获得可靠而公平的评估结果。

**EN** 7 Conclusion

**ZH** 7 结论

**EN** In this paper, we reveal a systematic positional bias in evaluation with advanced ChatGPT/GPT-4 models: by manipulating the order of candidate responses during evaluation, the quality ranking results can be significantly influenced. To this end, we introduce three effective strategies, namely Multiple Evidence Calibration (MEC), Balanced Position Calibration (BPC), and Human-in-the-Loop Calibration (HITLC). MEC requires the LLM evaluator to first provide multiple evaluation evidence to support their subsequent ratings and BPC aggregates the results from various orders to determine the final score. Based on the results of MEC and BPC, HITLC further calculates a balanced position diversity entropy to select examples for human annotations. These strategies successfully reduce the evaluation bias and improve alignment with human judgments. We provide our code and human annotations to support future studies and enhance the evaluation of generative models.

**ZH** 本文揭示了在使用先进的 ChatGPT/GPT-4 模型进行评估时存在的系统性位置偏差：通过在评估过程中操纵候选回复的顺序，质量排序结果会被显著影响。为此，我们引入三种有效的策略，即多证据校准（MEC）、均衡位置校准（BPC）与人在回路校准（HITLC）。MEC 要求 LLM 评估者先提供多条评估证据来支撑其随后的评分，BPC 聚合来自不同顺序的结果以确定最终分数；在 MEC 与 BPC 结果的基础上，HITLC 进一步计算均衡位置多样性熵，以挑选需要人工标注的样本。这些策略成功降低了评估偏差，并提升了与人类判断的一致性。我们公开代码与人工标注，以支持后续研究并促进生成模型的评估。
