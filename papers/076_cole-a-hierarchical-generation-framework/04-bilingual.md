**EN** Our objective in this work is to establish an efficient and dependable autonomous text-to-design system capable of generating high-quality graphic design images from user intent prompts. We suggest breaking down the complex process of graphic design image generation into a hierarchical generation process. This process involves multiple specialized generation models, each designed to handle different sub-tasks: First, we train a Design-LLM to comprehend vague intentions and undertake design-oriented planning tasks. We formulate this as a text-to-JSON prediction task, which requires transforming naive and sparse user intentions into structured, layer-wise captions or attributes essential for the following task decomposition. Second, to perform design-oriented visual reasoning and generation, we train additional three models including (i) a text-to-background diffusion model for background layer generation: it is crucial to guarantee that the generated background images reserve adequate space for the incorporation of various elements, which is different from conventional methods such as DALL⟦SLOT_11⟧E3, which often produce objects filling the entire image space. (ii) a text-to-object diffusion model for object layer generation, it’s essential to ensure the model to understand both the content and style of the background image. Subsequently, the model should generate an object layer that not only aligns stylistically but is also placed in logical positions. (iii) a Typography-LMM for typography prediction, it needs to predict ⟦SLOT_12⟧ distinct typography attributes for each text box. This component requires a sophisticated level of reasoning about the visual contents of the merged background and object image layers, then predicting the typography attributes accordingly. We also demonstrate the generalization capability of our Typography-LLM by applying it to processed design images originally generated using DALL⟦SLOT_13⟧E3. Refer to Figure 1 for the visual results. Besides, we also introduce a feedback and quality assurance process to enhance the quality.

**EN** 2 Related Work

**ZH** 2 相关工作

**EN** Autonomous Agent based on LLMs Recent studies [44, 19, 38, 43, 28, 11, 27, 39, 7, 39, 21] have proposed the development of advanced (multi-)agent systems leveraging powerful LLMs to tackle various tasks. Some of them [44, 19, 38, 43, 28] concentrate more on image generation tasks. Our model primarily distinguishes itself from these works in the mechanism of task decomposition and the usage of tools. While most existing studies incorporate tool usage knowledge by designing tailored prompt instructions or in-context examples for GPT-⟦SLOT_18⟧/⟦SLOT_19⟧, or GPT-4V(ision) to iteratively refine the text prompts, our Cole decomposes the complicated graphic design image generation task into a hierarchical set of generation tasks, and performs dedicated fine-tuning of each generation model on our constructed professional graphic design dataset.

**ZH** 基于 LLM 的自主智能体 近期研究 [44, 19, 38, 43, 28, 11, 27, 39, 7, 39, 21] 提出借助强大的 LLM 构建先进的（多）智能体系统以应对各类任务，其中一部分 [44, 19, 38, 43, 28] 更侧重于图像生成任务。我们的模型与这些工作的主要区别在于任务分解机制与工具使用方式。现有研究大多通过为 GPT-⟦SLOT_18⟧/⟦SLOT_19⟧ 或 GPT-4V(ision) 设计定制的提示指令或上下文示例来注入工具使用知识，从而迭代优化文本提示；而 Cole 把复杂的平面设计图像生成任务分解为一组层次化的生成任务，并在我们构建的专业平面设计数据集上对每个生成模型进行针对性微调。

**EN** Design Image Generation The majority of studies [12, 22, 5, 45, 29, 34, 15, 42] primarily concentrate on individual aspects within the graphic design generation task, assuming that all other information is at hand. Unlike the these methods, this study aims to develop an autonomous, comprehensive system capable of executing all design generation tasks from clear user intent descriptions. This task formulation presents a significantly greater challenge compared to most existing approaches. To the best of our knowledge, our Cole is among the pioneering works that seek to tackle the complex task of autonomous graphic design generation based solely on a basic user intent prompt.

**ZH** 设计图像生成 多数研究 [12, 22, 5, 45, 29, 34, 15, 42] 主要关注平面设计生成任务中的单一环节，并假定其余信息均已具备。与这些方法不同，本文旨在构建一个自主、完备的系统，能够依据清晰的用户意图描述完成全部设计生成任务。相较于多数现有方法，这一任务设定难度显著更高。据我们所知，Cole 是最早尝试仅凭简单用户意图提示来解决自主平面设计生成这一复杂任务的工作之一。

**EN** 3 Our Approach

**ZH** 3 本文方法

**EN** 3.1 Cole Framework Overview

**ZH** 3.1 Cole 框架概览

**EN** Motivated by the necessity to generate high-quality graphic designs that accurately reflect user intentions, we recognize the importance of a seamless integration of multiple roles. Each of these roles is designed to address specific aspects of the graphic design process, as indicated in the references [35, 33]. Consequently, we train an LLM for multi-layered task planning, breaking down the text-to-design generation into a hierarchical structure of tasks. These include the intention-to-JSON generation, text-to-background image generation, text-to-object image and alpha mask generation, typography attribute generation, and the multi-layered graphic editing and rendering tasks. The tasks of intention-to-JSON and typography attribute generation both adhere to a hierarchical structure, requiring the prediction of missing values based on a predefined structured request.

**ZH** 为了生成准确反映用户意图的高质量平面设计，我们认识到多个角色无缝协作的重要性，每个角色负责平面设计流程中的特定环节，如文献 [35, 33] 所述。为此，我们训练一个 LLM 负责多图层任务规划，把文本到设计生成拆解为层次化的任务结构，涵盖意图到 JSON 生成、文本到背景图像生成、文本到对象图像与 alpha 掩码生成、排版属性生成，以及多图层编辑与渲染等任务。其中，意图到 JSON 生成与排版属性生成都遵循层次化结构，需要依据预定义的结构化请求预测缺失字段。

**EN** We introduce the comprehensive hierarchical framework of our Cole system, as depicted in Figure 4. To effectively train the Cole system, we initially amass approximately 100,000 high-quality raw graphic design images from the internet, covering multiple graphic design sources. Following this, we organize the graphic design data into a hierarchical structure that includes components such as (intention, JSON) pairs, (caption, background-image) pairs, (caption, object-image) pairs, and (text-image, typography JSON) pairs. We will further explore the process of constructing each component in the ensuing discussion.

**ZH** 我们在图 4 中给出 Cole 系统的完整层次化框架。为有效训练 Cole 系统，我们首先从互联网收集约 100,000 张高质量平面设计原始图像，覆盖多个平面设计来源。随后，我们把这些平面设计数据组织为层次化结构，包含（意图, JSON）对、（描述, 背景图像）对、（描述, 对象图像）对以及（文本图像, 排版 JSON）对等组成部分。下文将逐一介绍各部分的构建流程。

**EN** 3.2 Design LLM: Intention Recaption and Layout Planning

**ZH** 3.2 Design LLM：意图重述与版面规划

**EN** We first detail the process of generating (intention, JSON) pair data for graphic design and then demonstrate the implementation of the Design LLM, aimed at intention recapitulation and planning multi-layered layouts.

**ZH** 我们首先介绍平面设计（意图, JSON）对数据的生成流程，随后说明 Design LLM 的实现，它负责意图重述与多图层版面规划。

**EN** Creating Intention for Design Image Given that both intentions and detailed captions are absent in the raw graphic design data, we first employ GPT-⟦SLOT_20⟧ to generate the intentions from the raw image information. This includes the image title, format, keywords, and all visual text present in the image. This is accomplished with the aid of the system prompt displayed in the “Intention Generation Prompt” (shown in Supplementary). The left of Figure 5 presents a representative example, showcasing the ground-truth pairs of graphic design images and the generated user intentions. We generate the intention for nearly ⟦SLOT_21⟧ graphic design images.

**ZH** 为设计图像构造意图 由于原始平面设计数据中既无意图也无详细描述，我们先用 GPT-⟦SLOT_20⟧ 从原始图像信息生成意图，所用信息包括图像标题、版式、关键词以及图像中出现的全部视觉文本。这一过程借助补充材料中「意图生成提示」所示的系统提示完成。图 5 左侧给出一个代表性示例，展示了平面设计图像的真值对与生成的用户意图。我们为近 ⟦SLOT_21⟧ 张平面设计图像生成了意图。

**EN** Figure 4: Illustrating the detailed hierarchical pipelines of the proposed Cole system. Upon receiving a user’s intention, our initial step involves using a Design-LLM to translate the intention into a detailed JSON file. This process necessitates multi-layered layout planning by predicting a wide range of attributes for the required visual elements. Next, we engage a pair of cascaded diffusion models for the text-to-background generation and text-to-object (and alpha mask) generation processes. These models play a crucial role in creating visual assets, guided not only by the text instructions specified in the JSON file but also by the need to reason about their visual spatial relationships to ensure a coherent design. Additionally, we have developed a typography-LMM that predicts the typography JSON file by analyzing and reasoning about the previously predicted text contents, background image, and object image. Last, we apply a multi-layered SVG editor and rendering system to enable flexible user modifications on individual layers, allowing for the composition and output of the final image.

**EN** 3.4 Text-to-Object Diffusion Model: Visual Reasoning based on the Generated Background Image

**ZH** 3.4 文本到对象扩散模型：基于已生成背景图像的视觉推理

**EN** This section outlines the method for creating (caption, object image) pair data and showcases the deployment of the text-to-object diffusion model. This model is designed to produce an object layer that is not only stylistically coherent but also logically positioned. A key technical insight in this endeavor is the model’s need to predict an additional composite image, a step that is essential for ensuring the precision of the object placements.

**ZH** 本节介绍（描述, 对象图像）对数据的构造方法，并展示文本到对象扩散模型的部署。该模型生成的对象图层既要风格连贯，也要位置合理。其中的关键技术要点在于，模型需要额外预测一张合成图像，这对保证对象放置的精度至关重要。

**EN** Preparing Text-Object-Background-AlphaMask Quadruples To train a text-to-object diffusion model capable of perceiving both the object captions present in the JSON file and the visual pixels predicted by the text-to-background diffusion models, we prepare the (object-caption, background-image, object-image, alpha-mask) quadruples. We extract the object-image and the alpha-mask from the raw data, while the object-caption and the background-image are prepared following the above-mentioned scheme.

**ZH** 准备文本—对象—背景—alpha 掩码四元组 为训练一个既能感知 JSON 文件中对象描述、又能感知文本到背景扩散模型所预测视觉像素的文本到对象扩散模型，我们准备了（对象描述, 背景图像, 对象图像, alpha 掩码）四元组。其中对象图像与 alpha 掩码从原始数据中提取，对象描述与背景图像则按前述方案准备。

**EN** Implementation and Training Settings Following the instruct-P2P approach [3], we concatenate the noise latent vectors with the latent vectors of the conditioning background image with the alpha mask for the object layer, and we add additional input channels to the first convolutional layer. All available weights of the diffusion model are initialized from pre-trained checkpoints, while weights operating on the newly added input channels are initialized to zero. As a result, we can predict both the object image and its alpha mask layer, which enables us to control the transparency of the object pixels. Consequently, we can seamlessly blend the object image layer with the background image layer in accordance with the alpha mask. To ensure a more reasonable placement of object pixels and coherence in style with the background image, we propose predicting an additional composed image. This is achieved by adding three extra channels to both the input noise vectors and the output prediction, a step that proves critical for accurate object layer prediction. Therefore, our text-to-object diffusion model needs to predict ⟦SLOT_41⟧ channels, where the object image consists of ⟦SLOT_42⟧ channels, the object mask consists of ⟦SLOT_43⟧ channel, and the composed image consists of ⟦SLOT_44⟧ channels. Besides, we also increased the maximum number of text tokens from ⟦SLOT_45⟧ to ⟦SLOT_46⟧ following the text-to-background diffusion model.

**ZH** 实现与训练设置 我们沿用 instruct-P2P 方法 [3]，把噪声潜变量与作为条件的背景图像潜变量以及对象图层 alpha 掩码拼接起来，并为第一个卷积层增加额外的输入通道。扩散模型的原有权重全部由预训练检查点初始化，而作用于新增输入通道的权重初始化为零。这样，模型可同时预测对象图像及其 alpha 掩码图层，从而控制对象像素的透明度，进而依据 alpha 掩码把对象图层与背景图层无缝融合。为使对象像素的位置更合理、风格与背景图像更协调，我们提出额外预测一张合成图像：在输入噪声向量与输出预测两端各增加三个通道，这一步对准确预测对象图层极为关键。因此，我们的文本到对象扩散模型需要预测 ⟦SLOT_41⟧ 个通道，其中对象图像占 ⟦SLOT_42⟧ 个通道，对象掩码占 ⟦SLOT_43⟧ 个通道，合成图像占 ⟦SLOT_44⟧ 个通道。此外，与文本到背景扩散模型一样，我们也将文本词元的最大数量从 ⟦SLOT_45⟧ 提高到 ⟦SLOT_46⟧。

**EN** Similar to the text-to-background generation model, we optimize our assembled dataset, comprising approximately ⟦SLOT_47⟧ (object-caption, background-image, object-image, alpha-mask) quadruples. The relatively smaller number of quadruples is due to the frequent absence of the object image layer in many graphic design images. We employ the instruct-P2P scheme for optimization, which is based on the DeepFloyd [18] IF diffusion models, and this process is carried out over an estimated span of ⟦SLOT_48⟧ epochs.

**ZH** 与文本到背景生成模型类似，我们在约 ⟦SLOT_47⟧ 组（对象描述, 背景图像, 对象图像, alpha 掩码）四元组组成的数据集上进行优化。四元组数量相对较少，是因为许多平面设计图像并不包含对象图层。我们采用基于 DeepFloyd [18] IF 扩散模型的 instruct-P2P 方案进行优化，训练约 ⟦SLOT_48⟧ 个轮次。

**EN** 3.5 Typography LMM: Layout Planning and Attribute Reasoning for Visual Text

**ZH** 3.5 Typography LMM：视觉文本的版面规划与属性推理

**EN** Typography [37] is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, clear, and visually engaging for the reader. It therefore requires complex reasoning and planning capabilities. It encompasses the design of font style, appearance, and structure, with the goal of eliciting specific emotions and conveying certain messages. In essence, typography brings text to life. Contrary to recent efforts, such as [31], [13], and [10], which primarily employ diffusion models to generate novel letter-level or word-level semantic or artistic typographies, our focus is more directed towards the utilization of an LMM (Large Multimodal Model). This approach allows us to address the highly challenging task of image-level typography. This task encompasses selecting font types, establishing hierarchical arrangements, and adjusting leading (line spacing), tracking (character spacing), and kerning (space between specific characters) based on the composed images. Consequently, it demands a robust capability for layout planning and attribute reasoning from the Typography LMM.

**ZH** 排版 [37] 是一门安排文字的技艺，使书面语言清晰易读、赏心悦目，因而需要复杂的推理与规划能力。它涵盖字体风格、外观与结构的设计，旨在唤起特定情绪、传达特定信息，本质上赋予文字以生命。与 [31]、[13]、[10] 等近期工作主要使用扩散模型生成新颖的字母级或单词级语义/艺术字体不同，我们更侧重于使用大型多模态模型（LMM）来应对图像级排版这一极具挑战性的任务。该任务包括选择字体类型、确立层次关系，并基于合成图像调整行距（leading）、字距（tracking）与字偶间距（kerning）。这对 Typography LMM 的版面规划与属性推理能力提出了很高要求。

**EN** Preparing Composed Images and Typography Data We construct nearly ⟦SLOT_49⟧ triplets of data, each consists of multiple texts, composed images of backgrounds and objects, and typography information. **EN** 5 Conclusion

**ZH** 5 结论

**EN** This paper introduces an exceptionally efficient hierarchical generation framework, Cole, that simplifies the intricate process of graphic design generation by breaking it down into manageable, coordinated sub-tasks, each yielding distinct outputs. By leveraging design-oriented visual planning, reasoning, and layer-wise generation capabilities during this hierarchical task decomposition, our system can generate multi-layered and editable graphic design images. We demonstrate the advantages of our approach by comparing it to the most powerful systems like DALL⟦SLOT_90⟧E3 and CanvaGPT. Additionally, we have pinpointed several key limitations of our Cole system in the context of generating graphic design images, and we plan to address these in future research.

**ZH** 本文提出了一个极为高效的层次化生成框架 Cole，它把平面设计生成这一复杂过程拆解为若干可管理、相互协调的子任务，每个子任务各自产出明确的结果。通过在层次化任务分解中运用面向设计的视觉规划、推理与逐图层生成能力，本系统能够生成多图层、可编辑的平面设计图像。我们通过与 DALL⟦SLOT_90⟧E3、CanvaGPT 等最强系统的比较展示了本方法的优势。此外，我们也指出了 Cole 系统在生成平面设计图像方面的若干关键局限，并计划在未来研究中加以解决。

**EN** D More Details of Bin Partition Scheme within Typography-LMM

**ZH** D Typography-LMM 中分箱方案的更多细节

**EN** To streamline the Typography-LMM’s prediction process, we adopt a discretization strategy, i.e., bin partition scheme, for continuous floating-point values based on the approach described in [6]. This involves categorizing continuous variables into discrete bins and representing the values within each bin with a unique token. The specifics of our bin partitioning scheme are detailed below:

**ZH** 为简化 Typography-LMM 的预测过程，我们依照 [6] 的方法对连续浮点数值采用离散化策略，即分箱方案：把连续变量归入离散区间，并用唯一词元表示每个区间内的取值。分箱方案的具体细节如下：

**EN** • Font Size: The range [2, 200] is divided into 100 sub-intervals.

**ZH** • 字号：区间 [2, 200] 划分为 100 个子区间。

**EN** • Angle: The range [0, 2⟦SLOT_91⟧] is segmented into 64 sub-intervals.

**ZH** • 角度：区间 [0, 2⟦SLOT_91⟧] 划分为 64 个子区间。

**EN** • Color (RGB values): The range [0, 255] is split into 32 sub-intervals.

**ZH** • 颜色（RGB 值）：区间 [0, 255] 划分为 32 个子区间。

**EN** • Text Bounding Box Coordinates: The normalized coordinates within the range [-1, 1] are partitioned into 256 subspaces.

**ZH** • 文本边界框坐标：[-1, 1] 范围内的归一化坐标划分为 256 个子空间。

**EN** • Opacity: The range [0, 255] is divided into 8 subspaces.

**ZH** • 不透明度：区间 [0, 255] 划分为 8 个子空间。

**EN** • Letter/Line Spacing: The normalized values within the range [0, 1] are divided into 40 sub-intervals, adjusted according to the image width.

**ZH** • 字距/行距：[0, 1] 范围内的归一化值划分为 40 个子区间，并按图像宽度进行调整。

**EN** We estimate the start and end points of all the above partitions based on the statistics derived from the training set, accommodating a broad spectrum of typography characteristics, including font size, angle, letter and line spacing, opacity, dimensions of the font bounding box (left, top, width, height), and color.

**ZH** 上述所有划分的起止点均依据训练集统计结果估计，以覆盖广泛的排版特征，包括字号、角度、字距与行距、不透明度、字体边界框尺寸（左、上、宽、高）以及颜色。
