中文摘要

文本到图像模型如今已在生产规模上生成平面设计作品，但其监督信号仍主要来自照片风格的偏好数据集，每次比较只给出一个总体判断。而设计师是沿若干彼此独立的轴（如字体排版、版面、色彩和谐）来评判设计的，单一偏好标签会把这些轴压扁。我们发布 TASTE（Typography, Aesthetics, Spatial, Tone, Etc.），一个多维偏好数据集：由两个各含五位专业设计师、互不重叠的队列，对四个当前文本到图像模型的输出按九项准则排序，并逐图标注幻觉标记。数据集另配两项贡献。其一，一个与准则无关的信号验证框架，基于 Kendall's τ、多数投票概率与 Condorcet 环，对照精确的 iid 均匀零假设；分析显示设计师一致度显著但中等，TASTE 的每一项准则都拒绝了随机评分者零假设。其二，我们在 TASTE 上基准测试了偏好模型，发现现成的 VLM 裁判与专用 T2I 打分器都达不到与设计师小组的多数一致，而一个直接在 TASTE 上训练的小型 MLP 头则大幅缩小了与单一评分者上限的差距，为未来基于 TASTE 训练的偏好模型设定了基线。

Abstract (原文)

Text-to-image models now generate graphic design at production scale, yet their supervision still comes primarily from photo-style preference datasets with a single overall verdict per comparison. Designers evaluate designs along several distinct axes (e.g., typography, layout, color harmony) that a single preference label collapses. We release \emph{TASTE} \textit{(Typography, Aesthetics, Spatial, Tone, Etc.)}, a multi-dimensional preference dataset in which two disjoint cohorts of five professional designers each ranked outputs from four current text-to-image models across nine criteria along with per-image hallucination flags. We pair the dataset with two contributions. First, a criterion-agnostic signal-validation framework based on Kendall's $τ$, majority-vote probability, and Condorcet cycles against exact iid-uniform nulls; the analysis reveals significant but moderate designer agreement, with every TASTE criterion rejecting the random-rater null. Second, we benchmark preference models on TASTE and find that off-the-shelf VLM judges and dedicated T2I scorers fail to reach majority agreement with the designer panel, while a small MLP head trained directly on TASTE substantially narrows the gap to the single-rater ceiling, setting a baseline for future TASTE-trained preference models.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Text-to-image (T2I) models have crossed from research artifact to deployed design tool. Current systems generate UI mockups, posters, ad creatives, and typographic compositions end-to-end and ship them to end users in production design pipelines; a recent survey of AI-driven graphic design [Survey2025DesignAI] catalogues the scope of this shift. Yet these systems are still largely evaluated and optimized using preference signals derived from photographic generation. This creates a misalignment between what models are rewarded for and what downstream design users actually value, potentially favoring photorealistic plausibility over typography, hierarchy, or structural fidelity.

文本到图像（T2I）模型已经从研究原型转变为投入使用的设计工具。当前的系统能端到端地生成 UI 线框、海报、广告创意与字体排版作品，并在生产设计流程中直接交付给终端用户；一项关于 AI 驱动平面设计的近期综述 [Survey2025DesignAI] 记录了这一转变的规模。然而，这些系统的评测与优化在很大程度上仍依赖源自照片式生成的偏好信号。这就造成了模型被奖励的方向与下游设计用户真正看重的东西之间的错位，可能使模型偏向照片级真实感，而忽视字体排版、视觉层级或结构保真度。

The public preference datasets that drive evaluation, reward modeling and preference optimization are collected on photo-style generation rather than on design. HPSv2 and HPSv3 [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3], Pick-a-Pic [Kirstain2023PickaPic], ImageReward [Xu2023ImageReward], RichHF-18K [Liang2024RichHF], MHP/MPS [Zhang2024MPS], and VisionReward [Xu2026VisionReward] all collect preferences over photo-style outputs. Survey work [Survey2025DesignAI] confirms the gap, and DesignPref [Peng2025DesignPref] describes a UI-design pairwise dataset whose data has not yet been released. A complementary line of recent work targets the structural and task-level side of design: LICA [Hirsch2026LICA] provides 1.55M layered design compositions with structural metadata, and Graphic-Design-Bench [Deganutti2026GDB] evaluates current models on 50 design tasks across layout, typography, infographics, template semantics, and animation. These resources test what models can do; neither records what designers prefer.

驱动评测、奖励建模与偏好优化的公开偏好数据集，都是在照片式生成（而非设计）上收集的。HPSv2 与 HPSv3 [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3]、Pick-a-Pic [Kirstain2023PickaPic]、ImageReward [Xu2023ImageReward]、RichHF-18K [Liang2024RichHF]、MHP/MPS [Zhang2024MPS] 以及 VisionReward [Xu2026VisionReward] 收集的偏好都针对照片式输出。综述工作 [Survey2025DesignAI] 证实了这一缺口，DesignPref [Peng2025DesignPref] 描述了一个 UI 设计成对比较数据集，但其数据尚未发布。近期还有一条互补的路线关注设计的结构与任务层面：LICA [Hirsch2026LICA] 提供了 1.55M 个带结构元数据的分层设计作品，Graphic-Design-Bench [Deganutti2026GDB] 在版面、字体排版、信息图、模板语义与动画等 50 项设计任务上评估了当前模型。这些资源检验的是模型能做什么，却都没有记录设计师偏好什么。

Sub-dimensional preference remains a missing piece. Photo-style preference is dominated by a small set of objective cues such as anatomical plausibility and prompt alignment, so a single “which is better” label captures most of the signal. Graphic design splits along several partly independent axes including typography rules, visual hierarchy, color harmony, mood and tone, spatial layout fidelity, and description-fidelity criteria of the form does the color requested in the prompt actually appear in the output. A single overall label averages over these axes and loses the per-axis signal. A layout may preserve spatial structure while violating color intent; another may satisfy prompt semantics but break typographic hierarchy. Both could receive the same overall preference label despite failing for different reasons. Work on visual importance and saliency in graphic design supports treating these axes as distinct cognitive processes [Bylinskii2017VisualImportance, Fosco2020Predimportance], and multi-axis benchmarking is standard practice in adjacent modalities, for example VBench and VBench-2.0 in video [Huang2024VBench, Huang2025VBench2].

子维度偏好仍是缺失的一环。照片式偏好由少量客观线索主导，例如解剖结构合理性与提示对齐度，因此单个「哪个更好」的标签就能捕获大部分信号。平面设计则沿着若干彼此部分独立的轴线展开，包括字体排版规则、视觉层级、色彩和谐、情绪与基调、空间版面保真度，以及「提示中要求的颜色是否真的出现在输出中」这类描述保真度准则。单一的整体标签在这些轴线上做平均，从而丢失了逐轴信号：一个版面可能保持了空间结构却违背了色彩意图，另一个可能满足了提示语义却破坏了字体层级。两者可能因完全不同的原因失败，却得到相同的整体偏好标签。关于平面设计中视觉重要性与显著性的研究支持把这些轴视为彼此独立的认知过程 [Bylinskii2017VisualImportance, Fosco2020Predimportance]；在相邻模态中，多轴基准也已是标准做法，例如视频领域的 VBench 与 VBench-2.0 [Huang2024VBench, Huang2025VBench2]。

Building a design-preference dataset is not sufficient on its own: the ratings must encode stable, learnable signal rather than arbitrary ratings. Similar aggregate disagreement can reflect random ratings, a weak shared preference with rater-specific noise, or designer groups with internally consistent but incompatible tastes. We propose a three-part diagnostic for separating these cases: graded agreement (per-prompt Kendall’s τ\tau), majority decisiveness (majority-vote probability pmaxp_{\max}), and triadic intransitivity (Condorcet cycle existence). Tested against exact null distributions at (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5), every TASTE criterion rejects the random-rater null on at least two of the three statistics. Three subsampled reference datasets at the same shape (Sushi [Kamishima2003Sushi], MovieLens [Harper2015MovieLens], and the HPSv2 test data [Wu2023HPSv2]) place designer agreement on graphic design between food and movie preferences and below photo-style image-quality preference. Signal level varies across criteria, with typography and spatial accuracy carrying roughly twice the signal of color harmony. Beyond TASTE, we argue that this kind of analysis should be standard practice on any new preference dataset before model training begins, since it answers the prior question of whether learnable signal is present.

构建设计偏好数据集本身并不充分：评分必须编码稳定、可学习的信号，而不是任意的评分。相似的总体分歧可能反映三种情况：随机评分、带有评分者个体噪声的弱共同偏好，或内部一致但彼此口味不相容的设计师群体。我们提出一个三部分诊断来区分这些情形：分级一致性（逐提示的 Kendall τ\tau）、多数决断性（多数投票概率 pmaxp_{\max}）与三元非传递性（Condorcet 环的存在性）。在 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 下与精确零分布做检验，TASTE 的每一项准则都在三个统计量中的至少两个上拒绝了随机评分者零假设。三个同形状的子采样参考数据集（Sushi [Kamishima2003Sushi]、MovieLens [Harper2015MovieLens] 与 HPSv2 测试数据 [Wu2023HPSv2]）表明，设计师在平面设计上的一致度介于食物偏好与电影偏好之间，低于照片式图像质量偏好。信号强度随准则而异，字体排版与空间准确性的信号约为色彩和谐的两倍。除 TASTE 之外，我们认为这类分析应当成为任何新偏好数据集在模型训练开始前的标准流程，因为它回答了「是否存在可学习信号」这一前置问题。

Off-the-shelf systems do not yet read this signal. Across six open-weight VLM judges spanning four families and three dedicated T2I-preference scorers (HPSv2.1, PickScore-v1, LAION-Aesthetic-V2), no pre-trained system exceeds 0.550.55 macro agreement with the 5-designer majority on TASTE under a position-bias-corrected protocol [Zheng2023MTBench]. Within the VLM slate, position-bias rate and content sensitivity trade off (Spearman ρ=+0.94\rho{=}{+}0.94), so scaling moves the operating point along this frontier rather than to a better one.

现成系统还读不出这种信号。在涵盖四个系列的六个开源权重 VLM 裁判，以及三个专用的 T2I 偏好打分器（HPSv2.1、PickScore-v1、LAION-Aesthetic-V2）中，在位置偏差校正协议 [Zheng2023MTBench] 下，没有任何预训练系统在 TASTE 上与 5 人设计师多数的宏一致率超过 0.550.55。在 VLM 阵容内部，位置偏差率与内容敏感度相互牵制（Spearman ρ=+0.94\rho{=}{+}0.94），因此规模化只是沿这条前沿移动工作点，而不是移向更好的工作点。

TASTE is designed not only as a benchmark, but as a decision layer for design generation systems. Its criterion-level structure enables fine-grained evaluation of model strengths and weaknesses, allowing practitioners to select or route among generators based on task-specific priorities (e.g., typography fidelity, spatial accuracy, or color control) rather than relying on a single aggregate preference score. This same structure provides supervision for training preference judges, reward models, and alignment objectives that optimize for specific design dimensions, enabling more controllable and designer-aligned generation.

TASTE 的定位不止于基准，它还可以作为设计生成系统的决策层。其准则级结构支持对模型长短处做细粒度评估，让实践者依据任务优先级（例如字体排版保真度、空间准确性或色彩控制）在生成器之间做选择与路由，而不必依赖单一的总分。同一结构也为训练偏好裁判、奖励模型与对齐目标提供了监督，使优化可以针对特定设计维度进行，从而实现更可控、更贴合设计师的生成。

    2 Related work

    2 相关工作

    Graphic-design ecosystem.

    平面设计生态。

    LICA [Hirsch2026LICA] releases 1.55M layered design compositions with structural metadata covering text, image, vector, and group elements. Graphic-Design-Bench [Deganutti2026GDB] builds on LICA to evaluate current models on 50 understanding-and-generation tasks across layout, typography, infographics, template semantics, and animation. These two resources cover the structural and task sides of graphic-design AI but record no human preferences. A recent survey [Survey2025DesignAI] maps the broader landscape and flags multi-rater design preference data as missing. Other works [Bylinskii2017VisualImportance, Fosco2020Predimportance] provide foundational work on visual-importance prediction across graphic design types and motivate sub-dimensional analysis of design quality. TASTE positions on top of this stack as the human-preference layer.

    LICA [Hirsch2026LICA] 发布了 1.55M 个分层设计作品，带有覆盖文本、图像、矢量与组元素的结构元数据。Graphic-Design-Bench [Deganutti2026GDB] 在 LICA 之上构建，在版面、字体排版、信息图、模板语义与动画等 50 项理解与生成任务上评估当前模型。这两项资源覆盖了平面设计 AI 的结构与任务侧面，但没有记录人类偏好。一项近期综述 [Survey2025DesignAI] 描绘了更广阔的图景，并指出多评分者设计偏好数据是缺失的。其他工作 [Bylinskii2017VisualImportance, Fosco2020Predimportance] 为跨平面设计类型的视觉重要性预测奠定了基础，并启发了对设计质量做子维度分析。TASTE 位于这一栈的顶层，充当人类偏好层。

    Design-domain preference.

    设计域偏好。

    DesignPref [Peng2025DesignPref] is the closest in spirit to TASTE. It collects 12k pairwise UI design comparisons from 20 designers with identity-linked annotations and reports Krippendorff α=0.25\alpha=0.25 on a single overall preference label. TASTE differs along three concrete axes: it covers nine sub-dimensional criteria rather than a single overall label, it is collected over four current text-to-image generators rather than a UI-only context, and the data is publicly released (DesignPref’s data has not been released publicly at submission time). TASTE is therefore complementary to DesignPref rather than substitutive: a researcher interested in the broad design domain, with sub-dimensional generation preference, would use TASTE, and one interested in single-label personalised UI preference would use DesignPref once it releases.

    DesignPref [Peng2025DesignPref] 在精神上与 TASTE 最接近。它从 20 位设计师处收集了 12k 条 UI 设计成对比较，带有与身份关联的标注，并在单一整体偏好标签上报告 Krippendorff α=0.25\alpha=0.25。TASTE 在三个具体方面与之不同：它覆盖九项子维度准则而非单一整体标签；数据采集自四个当前的文本到图像生成器，而非仅限 UI 场景；数据公开发布（DesignPref 的数据在投稿时尚未公开）。因此 TASTE 与 DesignPref 互补而非相互替代：关注广义设计域、需要子维度生成偏好的研究者会用 TASTE；而关注单一标签的个性化 UI 偏好的研究者则会在 DesignPref 发布后使用它。

    HPSv2 and HPSv3 [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3] use pairwise preference on photo-realistic generation, with one annotator per pairwise comparison in the training split and a small multi-rater test split. Pick-a-Pic [Kirstain2023PickaPic] collects pairwise preferences from real users in the wild.     HPSv2 与 HPSv3 [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3] 在照片真实感生成上使用成对偏好，训练划分中每个成对比较配一名标注者，另有一个小规模多评分者测试划分。Pick-a-Pic [Kirstain2023PickaPic] 在真实场景用户中收集成对偏好。ImageReward [Xu2023ImageReward] 扩展到逐提示的 kk 路排序，kk 介于 44 与 99 之间，但公开版本未保留评分者身份。GenAI-Bench [Lin2024GenAIBench] 提供每条目三名标注者、跨六个 T2I 模型的 Likert 评分。ImagenWorld [Sun2026ImagenWorld] 以面向真实任务的可解释人工评测对图像生成做压力测试。

    Multi-dimensional T2I preference.

    多维 T2I 偏好。

    RichHF-18K [Liang2024RichHF] adds per-image scores for plausibility, alignment, and aesthetics on Pick-a-Pic outputs. E-commerce poster evaluation is closer to design but evaluates AI judges rather than collecting designer preferences [Ecomiq2026].

    RichHF-18K [Liang2024RichHF] 在 Pick-a-Pic 的输出上增加了合理性、对齐度与美学的逐图评分。MHP/MPS [Zhang2024MPS] 在四个正交维度上收集了约 918k 条成对判断。VisionReward [Xu2026VisionReward] 进一步扩展为约 63 个子维度问题的二元清单。在相邻模态中，VBench [Huang2024VBench] 与 VBench-2.0 [Huang2025VBench2] 把多轴分解确立为视频生成评测的标准范式，并启发了在静态图像上采用同样范式。这五者都针对照片真实感或通用内容，而非设计。电商海报评测更接近设计，但它评估的是 AI 裁判，而非收集设计师偏好 [Ecomiq2026]。

    AIGC quality assessment.

    AIGC 质量评估。

    AGIQA-3K [Li2023AGIQA3K], AIGIQA 20K [Li2024AIGIQA20K], and AGIN [Chen2024AGIN] collect Mean Opinion Scores on AI-generated images. These target perceptual quality and naturalness rather than design preference, and their public releases either aggregate per-image scores or use a different statistical shape, preventing direct overlay on our histograms.

    AGIQA-3K [Li2023AGIQA3K]、AIGIQA 20K [Li2024AIGIQA20K] 与 AGIN [Chen2024AGIN] 收集了 AI 生成图像的平均意见分。这些工作针对的是感知质量与自然度，而非设计偏好；其公开版本要么只给出逐图聚合分数，要么统计形状不同，无法直接叠加到我们的直方图上。

    Classical preference data and statistical machinery.

    经典偏好数据与统计工具。

    Sushi [Kamishima2003Sushi] and MovieLens [Harper2015MovieLens] are canonical multi-rater preference datasets in the food and movie domains. Both can be subsampled to a (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) shape and bracket subjective taste-based agreement. Our statistical pipeline builds on Mallows permutation models [Mallows1957], the Bradley–Terry [BradleyTerry1952] and Plackett–Luce [Plackett1975, Luce1959] parametric formulations, Kendall’s τ\tau [Kendall1938], and Krippendorff’s α\alpha [Krippendorff2004alpha, Krippendorff2018].

    Sushi [Kamishima2003Sushi] 与 MovieLens [Harper2015MovieLens] 是食物与电影域中经典的多评分者偏好数据集，二者都可子采样到 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 形状，并为基于主观口味的一致度提供了上下界。我们的统计流程建立在 Mallows 排列模型 [Mallows1957]、Bradley–Terry [BradleyTerry1952] 与 Plackett–Luce [Plackett1975, Luce1959] 的参数化形式、Kendall τ\tau [Kendall1938] 以及 Krippendorff α\alpha [Krippendorff2004alpha, Krippendorff2018] 之上。

    3 TASTE 数据集

    3.1 Generators, designers, prompts

    3.1 生成器、设计师与提示

    We evaluate four current text-to-image models: FLUX.2 max [BlackForest2024Flux], GPT Image 1.5 [OpenAI2024DALLE], Nano Banana 2 [Google2024Imagen], and Seedream 5.0 Lite [Bytedance2024Seedream]. Generators are presented to evaluators behind blind code-names. The mapping from code-name to model is recorded but not shown to the rater, which prevents brand-anchoring effects.

    我们评估四个当前的文本到图像模型：FLUX.2 max [BlackForest2024Flux]、GPT Image 1.5 [OpenAI2024DALLE]、Nano Banana 2 [Google2024Imagen] 与 Seedream 5.0 Lite [Bytedance2024Seedream]。生成器以盲测代号呈现给评测者，代号到模型的映射被记录下来但不展示给评分者，以避免品牌锚定效应。

    We recruited two disjoint cohorts of professional designers, an Aesthetics cohort of 5 designers and a Descriptions cohort of 5 different designers. We refer to evaluators within each cohort as Designer A through Designer E to preserve anonymity in the analyses that follow. Each evaluator within a cohort sees every prompt batch in that cohort, so per-rater identity carries across the cohort’s sub-dimensions. We paid designers hourly market rates and did not disclose the statistical analyses applied to their judgments.

    我们招募了两个互不重叠的专业设计师队列：由 5 位设计师组成的 Aesthetics（美学）队列，与由另 5 位设计师组成的 Descriptions（描述）队列。为在后续分析中保持匿名，我们把每个队列内的评测者称为设计师 A 至设计师 E。队列内的每位评测者都会看到该队列的每一批提示，因此评分者身份在该队列的各子维度间保持一致。我们按市场时薪向设计师支付报酬，且未向他们披露将对其判断施加的统计分析。

    3.2 Sub-dimensional decomposition

    3.2 子维度分解

    Nine ranking sub-dimensions are collected, organised into the two cohorts (Table 1). The criteria were not chosen by the authors but surfaced from pilot interviews with the same pool of professional designers who later annotated the data; the exact interview format is documented in §3.4. The Aesthetics cohort covers an overall preference (intent-grounded) and four sub-criteria the designers consistently flagged as separate cognitive processes (mood and tone, visual hierarchy, color harmony, typography). The Descriptions cohort covers overall preference and three description-fidelity sub-criteria that designers identified as verifiable against the prompt (color accuracy, spatial accuracy, typography). The two typography criteria are distinct: Aesthetics Typography rates the typographic craft of the rendered design (font selection, spacing, sizing hierarchy, alignment), while Descriptions Typography rates whether the text demanded by the prompt is rendered correctly (presence, spelling, fit to the brief’s directions). Each sub-dimension was rated on a separate, disjoint set of 80 prompts. This design isolates per-dimension signal at the cost of preventing within-rater within-prompt cross-dimensional comparison.

    我们收集了九个排序子维度，组织为两个队列（表 1）。这些准则并非由作者选定，而是从对同一批专业设计师（他们后来也参与了数据标注）的先导访谈中浮现出来的；访谈的确切形式记录在 §3.4。Aesthetics 队列涵盖一个整体偏好（以意图为依据）与四个被设计师一致认定为独立认知过程的子准则（情绪与基调、视觉层级、色彩和谐、字体排版）。Descriptions 队列涵盖整体偏好与三个设计师认为可对照提示核验的描述保真度子准则（色彩准确性、空间准确性、字体排版）。两个字体排版准则彼此不同：Aesthetics Typography 评价渲染成品的字体工艺（字体选择、间距、字号层级、对齐），而 Descriptions Typography 评价提示所要求的文字是否被正确渲染（是否存在、拼写是否正确、是否符合简报的方向）。每个子维度都在一组彼此独立、互不重叠的 80 条提示上评分。这一设计以牺牲「同一评分者、同一提示下跨维度比较」为代价，隔离出了逐维度信号。

    For each prompt the rater performs the (42)=6\binom{4}{2}=6 pairwise comparisons of the four generator outputs, presented blind side by side. The 6 pairwise judgments per (prompt, evaluator) tuple are then aggregated into a strict 4-way ranking by the collection pipeline; the exact aggregation procedure is documented in §3.4. Each (prompt, evaluator) cell of the released data is therefore both a set of 6 pairwise comparisons and a derived 4-way rank, and the analyses in §6 use the derived rank.

    对每条提示，评分者对四个生成器输出执行 (42)=6\binom{4}{2}=6 次成对比较，以盲测方式并排呈现。每个（提示，评测者）元组的 6 次成对判断随后由采集流程聚合为一个严格的四路排序，聚合的确切步骤记录在 §3.4。因此，所发布数据中每个（提示，评测者）单元既是 6 次成对比较的集合，也是一个推导出的四路排序；§6 的分析使用的是推导出的排序。

    3.3 Hallucination flags

    3.3 幻觉标记

    For the two overall-preference cohorts (Aesthetic Preference and Descriptions Preference, 160160 prompts in total), each evaluator additionally flags every generator’s image as No, Minor, or Major hallucination, encoded as {0,1,2}\{0,1,2\}. This yields 1,6001{,}600 ordinal flags per cohort, distributed across three classes rather than a low-prevalence binary: roughly 55%55\% No, 35%35\% Minor, and 10%10\% Major in both cohorts. Inter-rater agreement is modest. Krippendorff’s α\alpha with an ordinal distance metric reaches 0.1410.141 on Aesthetics and 0.1650.165 on Descriptions, slightly below the α≈0.19\alpha\approx 0.19 measured on the same cohorts’ rankings. The disagreement is concentrated rather than diffuse: on 57%57\% of Aesthetics items and 52%52\% of Descriptions items, raters split only between No and Minor, while only 5%5\% split solely between Minor and Major. Per-evaluator Major rates span 3%3\% to 26%26\%, reflecting per-rater calibration drift rather than disagreement on which items count as severe failures. We use these flags only as a documented dataset feature in this paper and do not filter the ranking data with them. Tightening the No–Minor rubric, possibly through automated VQA-style probes in the spirit of recent T2I hallucination evaluation work [Lim2025IHallA, Liu2025HallucinationSurvey], is a natural follow-on for the next collection round.

    对于两个整体偏好队列（Aesthetic Preference 与 Descriptions Preference，共 160160 条提示），每位评测者还要额外把每个生成器的图像标记为无幻觉（No）、轻微幻觉（Minor）或严重幻觉（Major），编码为 {0,1,2}\{0,1,2\}。这为每个队列产生 1,6001{,}600 个序数标记，分布在三个类别上而非低发生率的二元标记：两个队列中大致为 55%55\% 个 No、35%35\% 个 Minor 与 10%10\% 个 Major。评分者间一致度不高。采用序数距离度量的 Krippendorff α\alpha 在 Aesthetics 上达到 0.1410.141，在 Descriptions 上达到 0.1650.165，略低于在同一队列排序上测得的 α≈0.19\alpha\approx 0.19。分歧是集中的而非弥散的：在 Aesthetics 的 57%57\% 条目与 Descriptions 的 52%52\% 条目上，评分者只在 No 与 Minor 之间分歧，而仅有 5%5\% 的条目只在 Minor 与 Major 之间分歧。各评测者的 Major 率介于 3%3\% 到 26%26\% 之间，反映的是逐评分者的校准漂移，而非对「哪些条目算严重失败」的分歧。本文仅把这些标记作为数据集的一项已记录特征使用，并未用它们过滤排序数据。收紧 No–Minor 的判定细则（可能借助受近期 T2I 幻觉评测工作 [Lim2025IHallA, Liu2025HallucinationSurvey] 启发的自动化 VQA 式探针）是下一轮采集的自然延伸。

    标注界面。

    4 Testing for preference signal

    4 偏好信号检验

    Before treating TASTE as training data for a preference model, we test whether the ratings carry meaningful preference signal. A dataset of random rankings has the same shape as TASTE and would survive any conventional descriptive report; the relevant test is whether the observed agreement exceeds what independent uniform raters would produce. We adopt three complementary signal tests, each addressing a different mode of disagreement. Formal definitions, support sets, and null PMFs are tabulated in Appendix A; the body uses each statistic intuitively.

    在把 TASTE 当作偏好模型的训练数据之前，我们先检验这些评分是否携带有效的偏好信号。一份随机排序数据集与 TASTE 形状相同，也能通过任何常规的描述性报告；真正相关的检验是所观察到的一致度是否超过独立均匀评分者所能产生的水平。我们采用三项互补的信号检验，每一项针对不同的分歧模式。形式化定义、支撑集与零假设 PMF 列于附录 A；正文只直观地使用各个统计量。

    Kendall’s τ\tau summarises rank correlation between rater pairs. For a single prompt with R=5R=5 raters we compute one Kendall τ\tau per rater pair and report the per-prompt mean T=1(R2)​∑r<sτ⁡(πr,πs)T=\tfrac{1}{\binom{R}{2}}\sum_{r<s}\tau(\pi_{r},\pi_{s}). T=+1T=+1 if all raters agree exactly, T=0T=0 under random ranking, and T<0T<0 if raters systematically disagree. At (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) the support of TT is finite and its iid-uniform null PMF has a closed form, which lets us test the full histogram of per-prompt TT values against the null rather than only its mean.

    Kendall τ\tau 概括评分者对之间的排序相关性。对于有 R=5R=5 位评分者的单条提示，我们对每对评分者计算一个 Kendall τ\tau，并报告逐提示均值 T=1(R2)​∑r<sτ⁡(πr,πs)T=\tfrac{1}{\binom{R}{2}}\sum_{r<s}\tau(\pi_{r},\pi_{s})。若所有评分者完全一致则为 T=+1T=+1，随机排序下为 T=0T=0，若评分者系统性相左则为 T<0T<0。在 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 下，TT 的支撑集有限，其 iid 均匀零假设 PMF 有闭式表达，这使我们可以拿逐提示 TT 值的完整直方图（而不只是其均值）对零假设做检验。

    Majority-vote probability pmaxp_{\max} asks how often a clear winner emerges between two items. For each pair of generator outputs (a,b)(a,b) we count how many of the R=5R=5 raters place aa above bb, call this count kk, and report pmax=max⁡(k/R,1−k/R)p_{\max}=\max(k/R,1-k/R). At R=5R=5 this takes values in {3/5,4/5,5/5}\{3/5,4/5,5/5\}. Larger pmaxp_{\max} means a sharper majority on a given pair. Under iid-uniform raters, pmaxp_{\max} follows the symmetric-folded Bin⁡(5,1/2)\mathrm{Bin}(5,1/2) distribution.

    多数投票概率 pmaxp_{\max} 考察两个条目之间多频繁地出现明确赢家。对于每对生成器输出 (a,b)(a,b)，我们统计 R=5R=5 位评分者中有多少把 aa 排在 bb 之上，记该计数为 kk，并报告 pmax=max⁡(k/R,1−k/R)p_{\max}=\max(k/R,1-k/R)。在 R=5R=5 下其取值落在 {3/5,4/5,5/5}\{3/5,4/5,5/5\}。pmaxp_{\max} 越大，表示某一对上的多数越分明。在 iid 均匀评分者下，pmaxp_{\max} 服从对称折叠的 Bin⁡(5,1/2)\mathrm{Bin}(5,1/2) 分布。

    The Condorcet cycle indicator distinguishes shared-ordering disagreement from factional disagreement. For each prompt we ask whether the majority pairwise preferences across the four generator outputs contain an intransitive triple (A≻BA\succ B, B≻CB\succ C, C≻AC\succ A). A single weak shared preference order with rater-specific noise on top suppresses cycles below the iid rate; a mixture of factions with internally consistent but mutually orthogonal preferences elevates cycles above the iid rate. At (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) the iid-uniform cycle rate is 0.2110.211 by Monte Carlo, and we test observed cycle counts against this rate with a binomial test.

    Condorcet 环指示量用于区分「共同排序下的分歧」与「派系式分歧」。对每条提示，我们考察四个生成器输出上的多数成对偏好是否包含非传递三元组（A≻BA\succ B, B≻CB\succ C, C≻AC\succ A）。单一的弱共同偏好排序叠加评分者个体噪声，会把环率压到 iid 水平之下；而由内部一致但彼此正交的偏好混合而成的派系，会把环率抬到 iid 水平之上。在 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 下，iid 均匀的环率经蒙特卡洛为 0.2110.211，我们用二项检验把观察到的环计数与该比率做比较。

    Why the three statistics together. Kendall’s τ\tau alone does not separate shared-ordering noise from factional disagreement, because both can produce similar mean τ\tau. pmaxp_{\max} alone cannot diagnose triadic intransitivity. The cycle indicator alone gives no information about graded agreement. Together the three test the same null (iid-uniform raters) along three independent axes; rejection on any one is evidence of preference signal, and the pattern of rejection across the three discriminates between the three competing stories about what causes the observed disagreement (§1). We argue this signal-check procedure should be standard practice for any new preference dataset before training begins.

    为什么需要三个统计量并用。Kendall τ\tau 单独无法区分共同排序噪声与派系式分歧，因为二者可能产生相近的 τ\tau 均值。pmaxp_{\max} 单独无法诊断三元非传递性。环指示量单独又不提供关于分级一致性的信息。三者合起来沿三条独立的轴检验同一个零假设（iid 均匀评分者）；在任一轴上被拒绝即为存在偏好信号的证据，而三个轴上的拒绝模式则能区分造成所观察分歧的三种竞争性解释（§1）。我们认为这一信号检验流程应当成为任何新偏好数据集在训练开始前的标准做法。

    6.3 Cycle rates rule out factional disagreement

    6.3 环率排除了派系式分歧

    The third signal-test statistic, the Condorcet cycle indicator, discriminates between two structured-agreement stories that τ\tau and pmaxp_{\max} alone cannot tell apart. A factional model predicts cycle rates above the iid-uniform rate; a weak shared ordering predicts cycle rates below it. For each criterion we test the observed cycle count against the binomial null Bin⁡(80,0.211)\mathrm{Bin}(80,0.211) with a two-sided test. All nine observed rates lie at or below the null rate of 0.2110.211 (Figure 2). Four criteria reject the null at α=0.05\alpha=0.05, in each case with the rate significantly below it (Aesthetics Visual Hierarchy at p=0.006p=0.006, Aesthetics Typography at p=0.001p=0.001, Descriptions Color Accuracy at p=0.028p=0.028, Descriptions Typography at p=4.9×10−4p=4.9\times 10^{-4}). The remaining five criteria do not reject the null individually at n=80n=80, but the joint pattern across all nine is strongly one-sided; no criterion shows the elevated-cycle signature that a factional model would generate. Combined with the left-shifted TT histogram from §6.2, this rules out the random-rater and factional hypotheses for every criterion. A weak shared preference order with rater-specific noise is the residual explanation. An aggregate model with agreement-weighted soft labels (such as Bradley–Terry or an HPS-style cosine model) should therefore work better than a mixture-of-experts setup on TASTE, since the mixture has no factional structure to recover.

    6.6 Pre-trained preference models and VLM judges

    We benchmark two classes of pre-trained models on TASTE. The first class is dedicated preference and aesthetic scorers trained on AI-image preferences: HPSv2.1 [Wu2023HPSv2], PickScore-v1 [Kirstain2023PickaPic], and LAION-Aesthetic-V2.     7 Preference-model analysis

    7 偏好模型分析

    Given the preference data, we turn to two model-side questions: what is the right ceiling for a model trained to imitate the panel, and how close can a small, modular preference head reach when trained on that data alone. Both depart from standard classification practice for non-trivial reasons. Labels are aggregated from a small panel of raters with measurable disagreement, and roughly half of all pairwise comparisons sit in a 3-2 split where even a perfect predictor cannot distinguish a true preference from a coin flip. Reporting a single val-accuracy number against such labels is uninterpretable without a calibrated upper bound, and is misleading without a per-bucket breakdown.

    有了偏好数据之后，我们转向两个模型侧的问题：一个被训练来模仿评审小组的模型，其合理的上限是什么；以及一个小型、模块化的偏好头在只使用该数据训练时能达到多接近。二者都出于非平凡的原因偏离了标准分类实践。标签是从一个存在可测量分歧的小型评分者小组聚合而来的，且大约一半的成对比较处于 3-2 分裂状态，在这种情况下即便是完美预测器也无法区分真实偏好与抛硬币。针对这类标签报告单一验证准确率，若没有经过校准的上界就无法解释，若没有逐桶拆解则会产生误导。

    We separate these two concerns. §7.1 gives the calibrated ceilings against which any model trained on this data should be reported; §7.2 describes the modular head we trained, the architectural choice that lifted accuracy beyond a flat regularization sweep, and the four downstream uses the model unlocks.

    我们把这两个问题分开处理。§7.1 给出经过校准的上限，任何在该数据上训练的模型都应当对照它来报告；§7.2 描述我们所训练的模块化头部、把准确率提升到平坦正则化搜索之上的架构选择，以及该模型解锁的四类下游用途。

    7.2 Model training, results, and use

    7.2 模型训练、结果与用途

    Architecture.

    架构。

    We train a modular preference head on top of a frozen vision-language encoder (Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B [qwen3vlembedding]), keeping the encoder out of the optimization budget. The architecture is one small MLP per ranking dimension, dispatched at training time by the row’s criterion slug, plus one binary head for the hallucination labels. All heads consume the same fused [t,i,t⊙i,|t−i|][t,\,i,\,t\odot i,\,|t-i|] representation of the ℓ2\ell_{2}-normalized text (tt) and image (ii) embeddings, where ⊙\odot is the element-wise product; concatenating the unimodal vectors with explicit alignment (t⊙it\odot i) and disagreement (|t−i||t-i|) terms avoids forcing the MLP to learn these cross-modal interactions from scratch.

    我们在一个冻结的视觉语言编码器（Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B [qwen3vlembedding]）之上训练模块化偏好头，把编码器排除在优化预算之外。架构是每个排序维度各配一个小型 MLP，训练时由该行的准则 slug 分发，外加一个用于幻觉标签的二元头。所有头都消费同一份融合后的 [t,i,t⊙i,|t−i|][t,\,i,\,t\odot i,\,|t-i|] 表示，它由 ℓ2\ell_{2} 归一化后的文本（tt）与图像（ii）嵌入构成，其中 ⊙\odot 为逐元素乘积；把单模态向量与显式的对齐项（t⊙it\odot i）和分歧项（|t−i||t-i|）拼接起来，避免了让 MLP 从零学习这些跨模态交互。

    Training objectives.

    训练目标。

    The per-dimension scoring heads are trained with an agreement-weighted Bradley-Terry (BT) loss [bradley1952rank]. The BT model treats each pairwise comparison as a Bernoulli trial whose success probability is σ⁡(τ⋅(sa−sb))\sigma(\tau\cdot(s_{a}-s_{b})), where σ\sigma is the logistic function, sas_{a} and sbs_{b} are the head’s scalar scores for the two images, and τ=exp⁡(logit_scale)\tau=\exp(\textsl{logit\_scale}) is a single learnable temperature.22 2 The learnable temperature rescales the potentially small score differences produced by the frozen-backbone scoring head, allowing the Bradley–Terry model to convert relative rankings into calibrated preference probabilities with sharper discrimination. The per-pair loss is the binary cross-entropy of this probability against the empirical win-rate of aa across raters, weighted by the panel’s per-pair agreement so unanimous and near-unanimous cases contribute more. The hallucination head is trained with binary cross-entropy against the per-asset positive-vote share. Both losses are minimized jointly with the two batch streams alternated. Training uses per-evaluator rows (one labeled pair per annotator) rather than aggregated soft labels, which gives the BT loss ≈5×\approx 5\times more independent gradient signal per pair without losing the consensus cases.

    逐维度打分头用一致性加权的 Bradley-Terry（BT）损失 [bradley1952rank] 训练。BT 模型把每个成对比较视为一次伯努利试验，其成功概率为 σ⁡(τ⋅(sa−sb))\sigma(\tau\cdot(s_{a}-s_{b}))，其中 σ\sigma 为 logistic 函数，sas_{a} 与 sbs_{b} 是该头对两张图像的标量分数，τ=exp⁡(logit_scale)\tau=\exp(\textsl{logit\_scale}) 是一个可学习的温度。22 2 可学习温度重新缩放了冻结骨干打分头可能产生的微小分数差，使 Bradley–Terry 模型能把相对排序转换为校准后的偏好概率，并获得更锐利的区分度。逐对损失是该概率相对 aa 在各评分者上的经验胜率的二元交叉熵，并按小组逐对的一致性加权，使一致与接近一致的样本贡献更大。幻觉头用相对逐资产正票比例的二元交叉熵训练。两个损失联合最小化，两个批流交替进行。训练使用逐评测者的行（每位标注者一个标注对）而非聚合的软标签，这使 BT 损失 ≈5×\approx 5\times 在每个对上获得更多独立梯度信号，同时不丢失共识样本。

    Architectural choices.

    架构选择。

    A baseline sweep varying head width (∈{64,128,256}\in\{64,128,256\}), dropout (∈{0.2,0.3}\in\{0.2,0.3\}), weight decay (∈{0,0.05,0.1}\in\{0,0.05,0.1\}), and label form (hard vs. soft) is essentially flat at val accuracy 0.583±0.0110.583\pm 0.011, so none of the standard regularization knobs produces a change above the noise floor. This points to a structural ceiling rather than a capacity one. In the architecture described above, each image is scored independently by a shared head ff before the comparison happens, so the BT logit is forced into the form

    一个基线搜索在头部宽度（∈{64,128,256}\in\{64,128,256\}）、dropout（∈{0.2,0.3}\in\{0.2,0.3\}）、权重衰减（∈{0,0.05,0.1}\in\{0,0.05,0.1\}）与标签形式（硬标签与软标签）上变化，验证准确率基本持平于 0.583±0.0110.583\pm 0.011，因此没有一个标准正则化旋钮能产生高于噪声底的变化。这指向结构性上限而非容量上限。在上述架构中，每张图像在比较发生之前都由共享头 ff 独立打分，于是 BT logit 被强制写成如下形式

    τ⋅(f⁡(t,ia)−f⁡(t,ib)).\tau\cdot\bigl(f(t,i_{a})-f(t,i_{b})\bigr).

    Any decision rule that requires joint attention to features of iai_{a} and ibi_{b}, for example, ”iai_{a} wins because it renders text legibly while ibi_{b} does not, given the prompt asks for typography”, cannot be expressed as a difference of two prompt-conditioned per-image scalars, no matter how wide ff is. We lift this restriction with a pairwise-difference scoring head that computes g⁡(t,ia,ib)g(t,i_{a},i_{b}) directly. The head consumes

    任何需要对 iai_{a} 与 ibi_{b} 的特征做联合注意的决策规则，例如「iai_{a} 胜出，因为它把文字渲染得清晰可读而 ibi_{b} 没有，鉴于提示要求的是字体排版」，都无法表达为两个以提示为条件的逐图像标量之差，无论 ff 有多宽。我们通过一个直接计算 g⁡(t,ia,ib)g(t,i_{a},i_{b}) 的成对差分打分头来解除这一限制。该头消费

    [t,ia,ib,ia−ib,|ia−ib|,t⊙(ia−ib)],[t,\,i_{a},\,i_{b},\,i_{a}-i_{b},\,|i_{a}-i_{b}|,\,t\odot(i_{a}-i_{b})],

    where the cross-term t⊙(ia−ib)t\odot(i_{a}-i_{b}) gives explicit access to the linear directions in embedding space along which the prompt rewards a difference between the two images. The hallucination head, which operates on a single image, retains the original 4-chunk fusion.

    其中交叉项 t⊙(ia−ib)t\odot(i_{a}-i_{b}) 显式地给出了嵌入空间中「提示奖励两张图像之间差异」的那些线性方向。作用于单张图像的幻觉头则保留原来的 4 段融合。

    Results.

    结果。

    We report two views of accuracy: the overall val accuracy aggregated across all ranking dimensions, and the per-bucket val accuracy that separates the easy 5-0 cases from the inherently ambiguous 3-2 splits.     Scope and ablations.

    范围与消融。

    The pairwise-head gain is obtained with the encoder frozen. A LoRA-adaptation experiment on the encoder yielded val accuracy 0.5280.528, below the frozen-MLP baseline of 0.5830.583, so we have no evidence that fine-tuning the encoder improves preference accuracy on this data. Augmenting the text input with criterion-specific prefixes did not help in a controlled comparison (<0.01<0.01 val change vs. baseline) and actively interfered with the pairwise mechanism when combined; the relevant criterion information is already accessible to the encoder, and the bottleneck is on the head side rather than the prompt side.

    成对头的增益是在编码器冻结的前提下取得的。在编码器上做 LoRA 适配实验得到 0.5280.528 的验证准确率，低于 0.5830.583 的冻结 MLP 基线，因此我们没有证据表明微调编码器能提升该数据上的偏好准确率。在受控比较中，用准则专属前缀增补文本输入没有帮助（相对基线 <0.01<0.01 的验证准确率变化），并且与成对机制组合时会主动干扰；相关准则信息对编码器本就可得，瓶颈在头一侧而非提示一侧。

    Use cases.

    用途。

    A preference model supports four downstream uses. (i) Best-of-NN selection: given NN candidate generations for a prompt, pick the highest-scoring image under the weighted sum of per-dimension scores most relevant to the deployment (e.g. uniform for general use, typography-weighted for poster generation). (ii) Reward modeling for DPO [Rafailov2023DPO] or RLHF-style fine-tuning of design-oriented T2I models: the per-dimension heads provide a multi-objective reward instead of the single scalar typical of HPSv2 / HPSv3 reward heads [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3], so the optimiser can trade off, e.g. typography accuracy against color harmony explicitly. (iii) Designer-anchored evaluation of new T2I models on a held-out data subset: per-dimension Kendall τ\tau against the modal annotator gives a quality report that complements task-level benchmarks such as GDB [Deganutti2026GDB] and generic preference scores by reporting where, axis-wise, the model diverges from designer preference. (iv) The hallucination head functions as a stand-alone filter for production pipelines, with lower precision and higher recall than human inspection but cheap enough to run on every generation as a first-pass quality gate.

    一个偏好模型支持四类下游用途。（i）Best-of-NN 选择：给定某提示的 NN 个候选生成结果，在与部署场景最相关的逐维度分数加权和（例如通用场景取均匀权重，海报生成取字体排版加权）下选出得分最高的图像。（ii）面向设计型 T2I 模型的 DPO [Rafailov2023DPO] 或 RLHF 式微调的奖励建模：逐维度头提供多目标奖励，而非 HPSv2 / HPSv3 奖励头 [Wu2023HPSv2, Ma2025HPSv3] 典型的单一标量，因此优化器可以显式地在字体排版准确性与色彩和谐之间做权衡。（iii）在留出数据子集上对新 T2I 模型做以设计师为锚的评测：相对众数标注者的逐维度 Kendall τ\tau 能给出一份质量报告，通过指出模型在哪些轴上偏离设计师偏好，补足 GDB [Deganutti2026GDB] 等任务级基准与通用偏好分数。（iv）幻觉头可作为生产流程中的独立过滤器，其精确率低于人工检查但召回率更高，且成本低到可以对每次生成运行，充当首道质量闸门。

    8 Discussion

    8 讨论

    TASTE records designer preference along nine sub-dimensions of design quality on outputs from four current text-to-image models, in a form that complements LICA’s structural data and Graphic-Design-Bench’s task suite. The three-statistic signal check confirms that the data carries learnable preference signal at every criterion, and the cross-domain anchors locate that signal between subjective taste tasks and photo-style image quality.

    TASTE 在四个当前文本到图像模型的输出上，沿设计质量的九个子维度记录了设计师偏好，其形式补足了 LICA 的结构数据与 Graphic-Design-Bench 的任务套件。三统计量信号检验证实，数据在每一项准则上都携带可学习的偏好信号，而跨域锚点把该信号定位在主观口味任务与照片式图像质量之间。

    The agreement structure also drives concrete modeling choices. Cycle rates below the iid-uniform null (§6.3) rule out a factional regime, so an aggregate reward model with agreement-weighted soft labels is the natural baseline and a mixture-of-experts approach has no factional structure to recover. Signal-level heterogeneity across criteria (§6.4) points to specialist heads or per-criterion loss terms on description-fidelity axes and broad soft preferences on aesthetic axes. Per-evaluator outliers (§6.5) admit standard outlier-aware re-weighting.

    一致性结构还直接驱动了具体的建模选择。环率低于 iid 均匀零假设（§6.3）排除了派系机制，因此带一致性加权软标签的聚合奖励模型是自然的基线，而混合专家方案并无派系结构可供还原。跨准则的信号强度异质性（§6.4）指向在描述保真度轴上采用专精头或逐准则损失项，在美学轴上采用宽泛的软偏好。逐评测者离群者（§6.5）则可以采用标准的离群感知重加权处理。

    Signal checks before training. The check we apply to TASTE (Kendall’s τ\tau, pmaxp_{\max}, cycle indicator, all against exact (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) nulls and three subsampled reference distributions) is generic. It can be applied to any new preference dataset whose ranking shape can be subsampled to a small (p,R)(p,R) panel, and answers the prior question of whether the data carries learnable signal. We recommend it as a routine prior step to model training rather than as a TASTE-specific contribution.

    训练前的信号检验。我们施加于 TASTE 的检验（Kendall τ\tau、pmaxp_{\max}、环指示量，全部针对精确 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 零假设与三个子采样参考分布）是通用的。它可应用于任何排序形状能子采样到小型 (p,R)(p,R) 评分小组的新偏好数据集，并回答「数据是否携带可学习信号」这一前置问题。我们建议把它作为模型训练的常规前置步骤，而不是把它当作 TASTE 专属的贡献。

    Limitations. Five raters per prompt is the smallest RR at which (R2)=10\binom{R}{2}=10 pair-τ\tau values produce a usable histogram; larger RR would tighten the binomial pmaxp_{\max} null and lift power on individual prompts. Each criterion is rated on a disjoint 80-prompt batch, which forecloses within-rater within-prompt cross-criterion analysis but bounds the annotation cost. Designers come from a single platform and prompts are sourced in English, so cross-cultural and cross-lingual preference variation is not captured. Several criteria that designers care about in practice are not part of the present collection, including accessibility (WCAG-style contrast and legibility), brand consistency, motion and animation (covered structurally by LICA [Hirsch2026LICA]), and audience-appropriateness; future collection rounds should extend the criterion set in those directions.

    局限性。每条提示 5 位评分者是 RR 中使 (R2)=10\binom{R}{2}=10 个成对 τ\tau 取值能构成可用直方图的最小值；更大的 RR 会收紧二项 pmaxp_{\max} 零假设并提升单条提示上的检验功效。每个准则在一批互不重叠的 80 条提示上评分，这排除了「同一评分者、同一提示下跨准则分析」的可能，但也控制了标注成本。设计师来自单一平台，提示以英语采集，因此没有覆盖跨文化、跨语言的偏好差异。设计师在实践中关心但本轮未纳入的准则还有若干，包括无障碍性（WCAG 式的对比度与可读性）、品牌一致性、动效与动画（其结构层面由 LICA [Hirsch2026LICA] 覆盖）以及受众适配性；后续采集轮次应向这些方向扩展准则集。

    Release plan. The 1,600 ratings per criterion (both the (42)=6\binom{4}{2}=6 pairwise judgments and the derived 4-way rankings), the 1,600 ordinal hallucination flags per overall-preference cohort, the analysis pipeline, the per-criterion distribution-test reports, and the appendix’s exact null PMFs will be released under a permissive licence on a public dataset host upon acceptance, alongside an evaluation script that runs the three-statistic signal check on a user-supplied ranking dataset of arbitrary (p,R)(p,R) shape.

    发布计划。每准则 1,600 次评分（含 (42)=6\binom{4}{2}=6 次成对判断与推导出的四路排序）、每个整体偏好队列 1,600 个序数幻觉标记、分析流程、逐准则分布检验报告，以及附录中的精确零假设 PMF，将在录用后以宽松许可发布在公开数据集托管平台上；同时发布一份评测脚本，可在用户提供的任意 (p,R)(p,R) 形状的排序数据集上运行三统计量信号检验。

    9 Conclusion

    9 结论

    We release TASTE, a designer-annotated multi-dimensional preference dataset for AI-generated graphic design covering nine criteria across two disjoint cohorts of professional designers on outputs from four current text-to-image models. Together with LICA and Graphic-Design-Bench, TASTE adds the missing designer-side preference layer to the graphic-design generation stack. We complement the dataset with a three-statistic signal check (Kendall’s τ\tau, majority-vote probability pmaxp_{\max}, and Condorcet cycle existence) against exact null distributions at (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5), confirming that learnable signal is present in every criterion, placing designer agreement between subjective taste tasks and photo-style image quality, and ruling out a factional cluster-of-tastes regime.

    我们发布 TASTE，一个面向 AI 生成平面设计的、由设计师标注的多维偏好数据集，在四个当前文本到图像模型的输出上、由两个互不重叠的专业设计师队列覆盖九项准则。与 LICA 和 Graphic-Design-Bench 一起，TASTE 为平面设计生成技术栈补上了缺失的设计师侧偏好层。我们还为数据集配上针对精确零分布的三统计量信号检验（Kendall τ\tau、多数投票概率 pmaxp_{\max} 与 Condorcet 环的存在性），零分布取在 (p=4,R=5)(p=4,R=5) 处，确认每一项准则都存在可学习信号，把设计师一致度定位在主观口味任务与照片式图像质量之间，并排除了「口味成簇」的派系机制。

    Beyond validating the dataset itself, TASTE establishes a benchmark for criterion-level evaluation of design generation systems. Its multidimensional structure enables more precise comparison of model capabilities, supports routing and orchestration decisions based on task-specific design requirements, and provides supervision for preference judges, reward models, and alignment objectives for graphic-design generation. The data are available at https://huggingface.co/datasets/purvanshi/TASTE and the analysis pipeline at https://github.com/purvanshi-lica/taste; we recommend applying the same signal-validation protocol to future preference datasets before model training begins.

    除验证数据集本身之外，TASTE 还为设计生成系统的准则级评测确立了一个基准。其多维结构支持对模型能力做更精细的比较，支撑基于任务特定设计需求的路由与编排决策，并为平面设计生成的偏好裁判、奖励模型与对齐目标提供监督。数据见 https://huggingface.co/datasets/purvanshi/TASTE，分析流程见 https://github.com/purvanshi-lica/taste；我们建议对未来的偏好数据集在模型训练开始前应用同样的信号验证协议。

    B.4 Protocol details

    B.4 协议细节

    Each VLM is queried with greedy (argmax) decoding to obtain a single deterministic verdict per query. Each VLM is run twice on every pair, in both image orders (8,6408{,}640 inferences per model), and a verdict is scored under MT-Bench S1 [Zheng2023MTBench]: weight 11 if it agrees with the designer majority and is consistent across both orderings, weight 00 if it disagrees and is consistent, and weight 0.50.5 if it is order-inconsistent.

    每个 VLM 用贪婪（argmax）解码查询，每次查询得到一个确定性的判定。每个 VLM 在每对上以两种图像顺序各运行一次（每模型 8,6408{,}640 次推理），判定按 MT-Bench S1 [Zheng2023MTBench] 计分：若与设计师多数一致且在两种顺序下都一致，权重 11；若不一致但顺序一致，权重 00；若顺序不一致，权重 0.50.5。

    The text prompt has four parts: (i) the brief shown to designers, verbatim; (ii) the criterion name; (iii) a one-paragraph rubric that summarises the criterion (a one-sentence rephrasing of the guidance designers received during pilot interviews); and (iv) a question. The brief portion is byte-identical to the text shown to designers in the annotation interface, so the VLM and the designers see the same description of the design task. To control for phrasing sensitivity, the question is drawn from a pool of eight per-criterion paraphrases, deterministically assigned per (prompt_id,modelA,modelB)(\text{prompt\_id},\text{model}_{A},\text{model}_{B}) tuple by hash; the per-paraphrase accuracy standard deviation is reported as “Para σ\sigma” in Table 8. A worked example for the descriptions-typography criterion is below; the bracketed ellipsis stands for the brief paragraphs.

    文本提示包含四个部分：（i）展示给设计师的简报，逐字照录；（ii）准则名称；（iii）一段概括该准则的评分细则（对先导访谈中设计师所获指引的一句话改写）；（iv）一个问题。简报部分与标注界面中展示给设计师的文本逐字节一致，因此 VLM 与设计师看到的是同一份设计任务描述。为控制措辞敏感性，问题从每准则八种改写的池中抽取，按 (prompt_id,modelA,modelB)(\text{prompt\_id},\text{model}_{A},\text{model}_{B}) 元组以哈希确定性地分配；逐改写的准确率标准差在表 8 中报告为「Para σ\sigma」。下面给出 descriptions-typography 准则的一个完整示例；方括号中的省略号代表简报段落。
