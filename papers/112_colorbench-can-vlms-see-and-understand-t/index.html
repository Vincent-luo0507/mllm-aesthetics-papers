中文摘要

颜色在人类感知中扮演重要角色，并常在视觉推理中提供关键线索。然而，视觉语言模型（VLM）能否以及如何像人类那样感知、理解并利用颜色，仍不清楚。本文提出 ColorBench，一个精心构建的全新基准，用于评估 VLM 在颜色理解上的能力，涵盖颜色感知、颜色推理与颜色鲁棒性。通过策划一组植根于真实应用的多样化测试场景，ColorBench 评估这些模型如何感知颜色、如何从基于颜色的线索中推断含义，以及如何在各种颜色变换下保持一致的表现。借助对 32 个采用不同语言模型与视觉编码器的 VLM 的大规模评测，本文揭示出若干未曾发现的结论：（i）缩放定律（模型越大越好）在 ColorBench 上依然成立，而语言模型的作用比视觉编码器更重要；（ii）然而模型之间的性能差距相对较小，说明颜色理解在现有 VLM 中长期被忽视；（iii）思维链推理能提升颜色理解的准确率与鲁棒性，尽管这些都是以视觉为中心的任务；（iv）颜色线索确实被 VLM 在 ColorBench 上加以利用，但在某些任务中也会误导模型。这些发现凸显了当前 VLM 的关键局限，并强调提升颜色理解能力的必要性。本文的 ColorBench 可作为推进多模态人工智能类人颜色理解研究的基础工具。

Abstract (原文)

Color plays an important role in human perception and usually provides critical clues in visual reasoning. However, it is unclear whether and how vision-language models (VLMs) can perceive, understand, and leverage color as humans. This paper introduces ColorBench, an innovative benchmark meticulously crafted to assess the capabilities of VLMs in color understanding, including color perception, reasoning, and robustness. By curating a suite of diverse test scenarios, with grounding in real applications, ColorBench evaluates how these models perceive colors, infer meanings from color-based cues, and maintain consistent performance under varying color transformations. Through an extensive evaluation of 32 VLMs with varying language models and vision encoders, our paper reveals some undiscovered findings: (i) The scaling law (larger models are better) still holds on ColorBench, while the language model plays a more important role than the vision encoder. (ii) However, the performance gaps across models are relatively small, indicating that color understanding has been largely neglected by existing VLMs. (iii) CoT reasoning improves color understanding accuracies and robustness, though they are vision-centric tasks. (iv) Color clues are indeed leveraged by VLMs on ColorBench but they can also mislead models in some tasks. These findings highlight the critical limitations of current VLMs and underscore the need to enhance color comprehension. Our ColorBenchcan serve as a foundational tool for advancing the study of human-level color understanding of multimodal AI.

1 Introduction

1 引言

Color is widely recognized as a fundamental component of human visual perception [11, 34], playing a critical role and providing critical clues in object detection, scene interpretation, contextual understanding, planning, etc., across critical application scenarios such as scientific discovery, medical care, remote sensing, shopping, visualization, artwork interpretation, etc. For instance, [19] leverages spectral color signatures to distinguish vegetation, health, and water bodies in satellite imagery, and [1] utilizes sediment color patterns to detect marine ecosystems. These applications underscore how color-driven features play an important role in real-world scenarios. Moreover, colors can convey affective or semantic information beyond simply recognizing and naming colors since colors are highly correlated to other attributes or concepts and thus can provide key information to various downstream tasks that do not even directly ask about colors [18, 37, 45]. As modern vision-language models (VLMs) [12, 41, 48] continue to be deployed to increasingly diverse scenarios, color—an essential visual feature—plays a growing role in the processes of understanding and reasoning. It is essential to examine whether and how these models can understand and leverage color information as in human perception and reasoning, how color influences their overall perceptual and reasoning capabilities, and whether they can interpret visual illusions, resolve ambiguous cues, and maintain reliable performance under color variations.

颜色被公认为人类视觉感知的基础组成部分 [11, 34]，在目标检测、场景解读、语境理解、规划等任务中扮演关键角色并提供关键线索，覆盖科学发现、医疗护理、遥感、购物、可视化、艺术品解读等关键应用场景。例如，[19] 利用光谱颜色特征区分卫星影像中的植被、健康状况与水体，[1] 则利用沉积物颜色模式探测海洋生态系统。这些应用凸显了颜色驱动的特征在真实场景中的重要作用。此外，颜色还能传递情感或语义信息，其作用不止于识别与命名颜色——颜色与其他属性或概念高度相关，因而能为各种下游任务提供关键信息，即便这些任务并不直接询问颜色 [18, 37, 45]。随着现代视觉语言模型（VLM）[12, 41, 48] 被部署到日益多样的场景中，颜色这一基本视觉特征在理解与推理过程中的作用也日益重要。因此有必要考察：这些模型能否像人类那样在感知与推理中理解并利用颜色信息；颜色如何影响其整体的感知与推理能力；以及它们能否解读视觉错觉、消解歧义线索，并在颜色变化下保持稳定表现。

图 1：ColorBench 的测试样例。ColorBench 在三个核心能力上评测 VLM：感知、推理与鲁棒性。该基准包含 11 个任务，用于评估细粒度的颜色理解能力，以及颜色对计数、比例计算、鲁棒性估计等其他推理技能的影响。ColorBench 含 1,400 余个实例，覆盖绘画分析、检测试剂读数、购物、卫星与野生动物影像分析等广泛的真实应用场景。

However, existing benchmarks for VLMs mainly focus on tasks that may not heavily depend on color understanding or require color-centric reasoning, thereby overlooking nuanced color-related factors [25, 29]. Hence, there is a lack of benchmarks that systematically assess how well VLMs understand color when it serves as the main or distinguishing feature of a scene and key information to a task. Moreover, robustness to variations in color, such as recoloring and shifting hues, has also been largely neglected in the LLM era [6, 8, 20]. Consequently, it remains unclear whether VLMs can perceive and reason about color with human-like proficiency and to what extent their performance deteriorates under significant color perturbations. This shortfall underscores the need for a dedicated benchmark that comprehensively probes various facets of color comprehension in VLMs. A detailed discussion of related works is provided in Appendix A.

然而，现有的 VLM 基准主要聚焦于那些不太依赖颜色理解、或不需要以颜色为中心的推理的任务，因而忽略了与颜色相关的细微因素 [25, 29]。因此，当颜色构成场景的主要或区分性特征、以及任务的关键信息时，目前仍缺乏系统评估 VLM 颜色理解能力的基准。此外，对颜色变化（如重着色与色相偏移）的鲁棒性，在大语言模型时代也大体被忽视 [6, 8, 20]。其结果是，我们仍不清楚 VLM 能否以接近人类的水平感知并推理颜色，也不清楚在显著的颜色扰动下其性能会退化到何种程度。这一缺口说明，需要一个专门的基准来全面探测 VLM 颜色理解的各个方面。相关工作的详细讨论见附录 A。

To bridge this gap, we propose a novel benchmark, ColorBench, that aims at comprehensively evaluating VLMs on three core capabilities of color understanding: Color Perception, Color Reasoning, and Color Robustness. Color Perception examines VLMs’ fundamental capability to correctly detect and interpret colors from inputs. Color Reasoning refers to the reasoning skills to draw further conclusions based on the understanding of colors from input and prior knowledge, in which colors act as a crucial clue to formulate accurate judgments. Color Robustness assesses how consistently VLMs perform when an image’s colors are altered, ensuring they maintain accurate predictions across different color variants of an image. Under these three core dimensions, 1111 fine-grained tasks assessing different aspects of color understanding capabilities are formulated as shown in Figure 1, which not only shows test examples in ColorBench but also presents potential real-world applications.

为弥合这一缺口，我们提出一个新颖的基准 ColorBench，旨在从颜色理解的三个核心能力——颜色感知、颜色推理与颜色鲁棒性——上全面评测 VLM。颜色感知考察 VLM 从输入中正确检测并解读颜色的基础能力。颜色推理指在对输入颜色与先验知识的理解之上作进一步推断的推理技能，其中颜色是形成准确判断的关键线索。颜色鲁棒性评估当图像颜色被改变时 VLM 表现的一致程度，确保其在同一图像的不同颜色变体上保持准确预测。在这三个核心维度之下，我们设计了 1111 个细粒度任务，各自评估颜色理解能力的不同侧面，如图 1 所示；该图既展示了 ColorBench 中的测试样例，也呈现了潜在的真实应用场景。

By focusing on these facets, ColorBench offers a granular view of VLMs’ capabilities in color understanding, aiming to illuminate both their strengths and shortcomings. We evaluate 3232 widely used VLMs in our benchmark, ranging from open-source to proprietary models, from relatively small models (0.50.5B) to larger models (7878B), and obtain some unrevealed observations.

通过聚焦这些侧面，ColorBench 提供了 VLM 颜色理解能力的细粒度视图，旨在同时揭示其长处与不足。我们在本基准上评测了 3232 个广泛使用的 VLM，涵盖从开源到闭源模型、从较小的模型（0.50.5B）到较大的模型（7878B），并得到一些此前未被揭示的观察。

    2 ColorBench Construction

    2 ColorBench 的构建

    图 2：ColorBench 中 3 个类别与 11 个任务的统计。

    We present ColorBench, the first benchmark explicitly designed to comprehensively evaluate the color understanding capabilities of VLMs across three key dimensions: Color Perception, Color Reasoning, and Color Robustness. This benchmark consists of 1,4481,448 instances and 5,8145,814 image-text questions spanning 1111 diverse tasks. For the Color Perception and Color Reasoning categories, each instance contains an image, a question, and multiple-choice (33 to 66) options, with only one correct answer. For Color Robustness, each instance consists of 10 multiple-choice image-text questions, including a seed image and 9 edited images with color changes. Given that color is a fundamental visual feature influencing most vision-related tasks, disentangling color understanding from other general capabilities (e.g., object recognition, counting) is challenging. To address this, we design questions with explicit color constraints for Color Perception and Reasoning dimensions, enabling a focused evaluation of VLMs’ perception and reasoning abilities in relation to color.

    我们提出 ColorBench，这是首个明确设计用于从三个关键维度——颜色感知、颜色推理与颜色鲁棒性——全面评测 VLM 颜色理解能力的基准。该基准由 1,4481,448 个实例与 5,8145,814 道图文问题组成，跨越 1111 个多样任务。对颜色感知与颜色推理两个类别，每个实例包含一张图像、一个问题与多项选择选项（33 到 66 个），且只有一个正确答案。对颜色鲁棒性，每个实例由 10 道图文选择题组成，包括一张种子图像与 9 张经过颜色改变的编辑图像。鉴于颜色是影响多数视觉相关任务的基本视觉特征，把颜色理解与其他通用能力（如物体识别、计数）解耦并非易事。为此，我们在颜色感知与推理维度上设计了带有显式颜色约束的问题，从而可以对 VLM 与颜色相关的感知与推理能力做有针对性的评测。

    2.1 Taxonomy

    2.1 分类体系

    Motivated by the existing evaluation criteria from prior benchmarks and real-world application scenarios, we categorize the color understanding capability into 33 core dimensions and 1111 detailed axes, as shown in Figure 1. The detailed question templates and sample cases are shown in Appendix 16.

    受已有基准的评测标准与真实应用场景启发，我们把颜色理解能力划分为 33 个核心维度与 1111 个细分子轴，如图 1 所示。详细的问题模板与样例见附录 16。

    2.1.1 Color Perception

    2.1.1 颜色感知

    This core dimension refers to the fundamental capability to correctly detect and interpret colors from inputs. We assess this capability through 33 key aspects: i) Color Recognition, ii) Color Extraction, and iii) Object Recognition.

    这一核心维度指从输入中正确检测并解读颜色的基础能力。我们通过 33 个关键方面评估该能力：i）颜色识别，ii）颜色提取，iii）物体识别。

    Color Recognition includes questions that either ask for the color of a given object or determine whether a specific color is present in the image. Color Extraction requires the model to extract the value of color code (e.g., RGB, HSV, or HEX) for a given single color image. This task measures the ability to perform fine-grained color retrieval from visual input. Object Recognition evaluates the model’s capability to identify objects that match a specified color described in the text input. These two tasks require VLMs to be able to detect and interpret the color in either the image or text input.

    颜色识别包含两类问题：询问给定物体的颜色，或判断图像中是否存在某种特定颜色。颜色提取要求模型为给定的纯色图像提取颜色码（如 RGB、HSV 或 HEX）的取值。该任务度量从视觉输入中做细粒度颜色检索的能力。物体识别评估模型依据文本输入中描述的指定颜色识别对应物体的能力。这三个任务要求 VLM 能够检测并解读图像或文本输入中的颜色。

    2.1.2 Color Reasoning

    2.1.2 颜色推理

    This dimension refers to the reasoning skills to draw further conclusions based on the understanding of colors from input and prior knowledge, in which colors act as a crucial clue to formulate accurate judgments. This category encapsulates 77 key aspects: i) Color Proportion, ii) Color Comparison, iii) Color Counting, iv) Object Counting, v) Color Illusion, vi) Color Mimicry and vii) Color Blindness.

    这一维度指在对输入颜色与先验知识的理解之上作进一步推断的推理技能，其中颜色是形成准确判断的关键线索。该类别包含 77 个关键方面：i）颜色占比，ii）颜色比较，iii）颜色计数，iv）物体计数，v）颜色错觉，vi）颜色拟态，vii）色盲。

    Color Proportion tests the model’s capability to estimate the relative area occupied by a specific color. Questions in this task require both color perception and proportion calculation capabilities. Color Comparison requires the model to be able to distinguish among multiple colors in the image, assessing its sensitivity to hue, saturation, and brightness differences in visual input. Color Counting focuses on identifying the number of unique colors in the image, evaluating the model’s perception and differentiation of distinct color variations, and counting ability. Object Counting extends this challenge by requiring the model to count objects that match a specific color pattern. This task requires an integration of object recognition and color perception. Color Illusion questions query VLMs to compare colors in potential illusionary environments. This task evaluates the model’s ability to account for color-induced optical illusions. Color Mimicry challenges the model to detect objects camouflaged within their surroundings, where color serves as a misleading factor, requiring advanced pattern recognition and contextual reasoning. These two tasks both assess the model’s ability to make correct predictions under the misleading of color-related information in visual input. Color Blindness, inspired by Ishihara tests, assesses the model’s ability to recognize numbers or text embedded in color patterns, testing its understanding of shape-color relationships. These 77 tasks comprehensively assess the model’s capacity for logical reasoning, spatial awareness, and adaptive interpretation of color-based visual cues.

    颜色占比检验模型估计某一特定颜色所占相对面积的能力，该类问题同时要求颜色感知与比例计算能力。颜色比较要求模型区分图像中的多种颜色，评估其对视觉输入中色相、饱和度与明度差异的敏感度。颜色计数关注识别图像中不同颜色的数量，评估模型对不同颜色变化的感知与区分能力以及计数能力。物体计数在此之上进一步加码，要求模型统计符合特定颜色模式的物体，需要把物体识别与颜色感知结合起来。颜色错觉问题要求 VLM 在潜在的错觉环境中比较颜色，评估模型考虑由颜色引起的光学错觉的能力。颜色拟态要求模型检测与周围环境融为一体的伪装物体，其中颜色充当误导因素，需要高级的模式识别与语境推理。这两个任务都评估模型在视觉输入中颜色相关信息误导下做出正确预测的能力。色盲任务受石原测试启发，评估模型识别嵌入在色块图案中的数字或文字的能力，检验其对形状—颜色关系的理解。这 77 个任务全面评估模型在逻辑推理、空间意识以及对基于颜色的视觉线索作适应性解读方面的能力。

    2.1.3 Color Robustness

    2.1.3 颜色鲁棒性

    图 3：颜色鲁棒性的生成流程。对每张种子图像，我们施加 33 种重着色策略（整幅图像、目标分割区域、最大分割区域）以生成编辑后的图像。对每种策略，我们通过在 HSV 色彩空间中把色相值偏移 9090°、180180° 或 270270° 来改变重着色区域的颜色。

    Color Robustness assesses how consistently VLMs perform and whether they can consistently deliver accurate predictions under color variants of a given image. It involves measuring the stability of a VLM’s responses when confronted with the same text input and a series of recolored images. To ensure that color does not influence the predictions, we select questions and corresponding answers that are independent of color attributes. Under these conditions, a robust model should produce unchanged predictions regardless of recoloring manipulation. Any variation in the model’s responses is then used to quantify its susceptibility to color changes, providing a direct measure of robustness.

    颜色鲁棒性评估 VLM 表现的一致程度，即在同一给定图像的颜色变体下能否持续给出准确预测。它度量的是：当面对相同的文本输入与一系列重着色图像时，VLM 回答的稳定程度。为确保颜色不影响预测，我们选取与颜色属性无关的问题及对应答案。在此条件下，一个鲁棒的模型无论施加何种重着色操作，都应给出不变的预测。模型回答中的任何变化，则被用来量化其对颜色变化的敏感程度，从而直接度量鲁棒性。

    2.2 Data Curation

    2.2 数据构建

    For most of the tasks in the category of Color Perception and Color Reasoning, we rely on human experts to manually collect images from multiple online benchmarks and websites. For the Color Proportion task, to ensure the correctness of the ground truth, an extra color extraction tool is firstly utilized to obtain the color histogram of the image. Questions and options are then manually designed based on these color statistics. For tasks including Color Extraction, Color Blindness, and Color Illusion, testing images are generated by corresponding code programs to ensure the controllability of the questions and answers. The detailed data sources are shown in Appendix B.

    对颜色感知与颜色推理类别中的多数任务，我们依靠人工专家从多个在线基准与网站手工收集图像。对颜色占比任务，为保证真值的正确性，我们首先用一个额外的颜色提取工具获取图像的颜色直方图，再依据这些颜色统计手工设计问题与选项。对颜色提取、色盲与颜色错觉等任务，测试图像由相应的代码程序生成，以确保问题与答案的可控性。详细的数据来源见附录 B。

    After the initial data is collected, additional filtering processes are conducted in a human-machine interactive process. We first conduct inference on a variety of VLMs and discard low-quality samples based on the GPT-4o prediction result and human evaluation. For synthesized data, similar processes are conducted, but with additional code (for generation) and image assessment. The above process is conducted in three rounds before the final benchmark instances are settled. This refinement process ensures ColorBench a rigorous and informative benchmark for assessing color-related understanding.

    初始数据收集完成后，我们在人机交互流程中开展额外的过滤处理。我们首先在多种 VLM 上做推理，并依据 GPT-4o 的预测结果与人工评估剔除低质量样本。对合成数据，我们执行相似流程，但额外增加（用于生成的）代码检查与图像评估。上述过程共进行三轮，才最终确定基准实例。这一精炼过程确保了 ColorBench 成为评估颜色相关理解的严谨而富有信息量的基准。

    For Color Robustness, we create evaluation instances by modifying images or specific regions through color changes. We define 33 recoloring strategies to determine the recoloring region: i) Entire Image, where the whole image is recolored; ii) Target Segment, where only the segment relevant to the question is altered; and iii) Largest Segment, where the largest region unrelated to the question is modified. Further details can be found in Appendix C. While generating color variants, we derive seed images from CV-Bench [42], a publicly available benchmark. For each seed image, as shown in Figure 3, we first employ a Grounded Segmentation Model (GAM) [38] to extract segments and their corresponding labels. We then apply the predefined recoloring strategies to determine the editing region and perform recoloring by shifting the Hue value in the HSV color space at three levels to cover entire color wheel: (9090°, 180180°, and 270270°). This process produces 99 variations per seed image, covering different strategies and degrees of color change to enable a comprehensive robustness assessment. To ensure interpretability, human experts filter out unnatural or negligible modifications, resulting in a final selection of 493493 seed images for robustness evaluation.

    对颜色鲁棒性，我们通过改变图像或特定区域的颜色来构造评测实例。我们定义 33 种重着色策略以确定重着色区域：i）整幅图像，即整张图像被重着色；ii）目标分割区域，即只改变与问题相关的分割区域；iii）最大分割区域，即修改与问题无关的最大区域。更多细节见附录 C。在生成颜色变体时，我们从公开基准 CV-Bench [42] 中取材种子图像。对每张种子图像，如图 3 所示，我们首先采用 Grounded Segmentation Model（GAM）[38] 提取分割区域及其对应标签，随后施加预定义的重着色策略确定编辑区域，并在 HSV 色彩空间中按三个层级偏移色相值以完成重着色，从而覆盖整个色环：（9090°、180180° 与 270270°）。这一过程为每张种子图像产生 99 个变体，覆盖不同策略与不同程度的颜色改变，以支持全面的鲁棒性评估。为保证可解释性，人工专家会滤除不自然或可忽略的修改，最终选出 493493 张种子图像用于鲁棒性评测。

    However, between the performances and vision encoder sizes, most of the tasks either have a correlation lower than 0.50.5 or a p-value greater than 0.050.05, which is not sufficient to conclude with the evident positive correlation. Despite these findings, we try to avoid conveying the message that there is no positive correlation between performances and vision encoder sizes. We think it is because of the negligence of the current community to focus on the scaling laws of vision encoders. The vision encoders used in the current mainstream VLMs are constrained in a very small set: (i) most of the VLMs only use one type of vision encoders for the whole family, except for the InternVL2 and InternVL2.5 series; (ii) most of the VLMs use the vision encoder with the size of 300300 - 400400M. These challenges make it hard to evaluate the scaling laws of vision encoders. Further visualizations are presented in Appendix L.2.

    Within the category of Color Reasoning, CoT benefits most of the tasks. However, in the Color Illusion task, CoT harms the model performance. After a manual investigation, we observe that more reasoning steps might cause VLMs to focus more on the misleading environments rather than directly compare the assigned colors, as shown in Figure 61. Another observation occurs in the Color Blindness task. Unlike other reasoning-related tasks, humans can read a color blindness test image with a simple glimpse without any slow thinking. This fascinating misalignment between humans and VLMs intrigues us to further investigation. We find that VLMs recognize these digits in a button-up pattern: they need to first infer that the dots in the image can form a digit before they really recognize these dots as digits.

    在颜色推理类别内部，CoT 对多数任务有益。但在颜色错觉任务上，CoT 反而损害了模型表现。经人工考察，我们观察到更多的推理步骤可能使 VLM 更关注误导性环境，而非直接比较所指定的颜色，如图 61 所示。另一个观察出现在色盲任务上。与其他推理相关任务不同，人类只需一瞥便能读出一张色盲测试图，无需任何慢思考。人与 VLM 之间这一耐人寻味的错位促使我们进一步探究。我们发现，VLM 以自底向上的方式识别这些数字：它们必须先推断出图像中的点可以构成一个数字，才会真正把这些点识别为数字。

    In addition, the consistent improvement of CoT on Color Robustness is also an unrevealed phenomenon. In our setting, only the colors of the image are altered, and the questions are strictly the same as the original. Thus, under this circumstance, color is the only variant, which is supposed to be more related to the capability of the vision encoder. However, counterintuitively, as shown in our experiments, more reasoning steps make the VLMs more robust to the color changes, which is probably caused by the higher confidence of correct answers after reasoning.

    此外，CoT 对颜色鲁棒性的持续改善也是一个未被揭示的现象。在我们的设置中，只有图像的颜色被改变，问题与原始问题严格相同。因此在这种情形下，颜色是唯一的变量，本应更关乎视觉编码器的能力。然而与直觉相反，我们的实验显示，更多的推理步骤使 VLM 对颜色变化更加鲁棒，这可能是因为推理之后正确答案的置信度更高。

    In order to examine whether VLMs really leverage color clues to handle tasks in ColorBench, experiments are conducted by converting all the original colorful images in the Color Perception and Reasoning categories into gray-scale ones, without changing the questions. Under this circumstance, the accuracies are expected to decrease dramatically as all our questions are related to colors. For quantitative analysis, we calculate the accuracy changing ratio as (A​c​co​r​i−A​c​cg​r​a​y)/A​c​co​r​i(Acc_{ori}-Acc_{gray})/Acc_{ori} for each VLM on each task. This value directly represents how the original accuracy changes with a gray-scale transformation. The positive value represents that the VLM has a higher accuracy on the original colored images, indicating that it needs color clues to solve the task. Higher positive values represent higher significance of the color clues. On the contrary, if the value is negative, it means that the VLM can reach a better accuracy after the gray-scale transformation, indicating that it does not need color clues for the task, and colors might even mislead VLM’s judgment. Lower negative values represent the severe harm the color can have on the task.

    为检验 VLM 是否真的利用颜色线索来处理 ColorBench 中的任务，我们把颜色感知与推理类别中全部原始彩色图像转为灰度图像，而不改变问题，然后开展实验。在这种情形下，由于我们所有的问题都与颜色相关，准确率本应大幅下降。为做定量分析，我们为每个 VLM 在每个任务上计算准确率变化比率 (A​c​co​r​i−A​c​cg​r​a​y)/A​c​co​r​i(Acc_{ori}-Acc_{gray})/Acc_{ori}。该值直接反映原始准确率在灰度变换下的变化情况。正值表示 VLM 在原始彩色图像上的准确率更高，说明它需要颜色线索才能解决该任务；正值越大，颜色线索越重要。相反，若该值为负，则说明 VLM 在灰度变换后能达到更好的准确率，即它并不需要该任务的颜色线索，颜色甚至可能误导 VLM 的判断；负值越低，说明颜色对该任务的危害越严重。

    The accuracy changing ratio distributions across all VLMs and tasks are presented in Figure 5 as the violin plot. As shown in the figure, for most of the tasks, the ratios of VLMs are above 00, indicating that VLMs indeed leverage color clues to correctly solve the tasks; removing the color directly harms the original accuracies dramatically. However, when it comes to Color Illusion and Color Mimicry, the majority of the changing ratios are below 00, which means that VLMs can get better accuracies when all the color information is removed. This phenomenon is reasonable as the colors on both of these two tasks are more likely serving as the misleading factors. In the meantime, for the Color Counting and Color Blindness tasks, almost half the accuracies increase and half decrease, indicating that the color clues might not be so significant in this task, thus, some of the models can find other ways to get the answer. We also investigate the correlation between accuracy changing ratios and model sizes, while no significant correlation can be concluded.

    所有 VLM 与所有任务上的准确率变化比率分布以小提琴图的形式呈现在图 5 中。如图所示，对多数任务，VLM 的比率都在 00 以上，说明 VLM 确实利用颜色线索来正确解决任务；移除颜色会直接大幅损害原始准确率。然而到了颜色错觉与颜色拟态任务上，多数变化比率低于 00，这意味着当全部颜色信息被去除时，VLM 反而能取得更好的准确率。这一现象是合理的，因为这两个任务中的颜色更可能充当误导因素。同时，对颜色计数与色盲任务，几乎一半的准确率上升、一半下降，说明颜色线索在这些任务中可能并不那么关键，因此部分模型能找到其他途径得到答案。我们还考察了准确率变化比率与模型规模之间的相关性，但未能得出显著相关。

    4 Conclusion, Limitation, and Future Works

    4 结论、局限性与未来工作

    In this paper, we introduce ColorBench, the first benchmark designed to comprehensively evaluate the color understanding capabilities of VLMs, including Perception, Reasoning, and Robustness. After evaluating 3232 widely used VLMs on our benchmark, several undiscovered observations are revealed by us. These observations emphasize the need for more sophisticated model architectures that integrate deeper color reasoning capabilities. To ensure high-quality and reliable annotations, ColorBench relies on manual data collection, annotation, and assessment across most domains. While this guarantees consistency, it inevitably limits dataset scale, style diversity, and category coverage. As future work, we aim to develop a trustworthy automated data collection pipeline and expand ColorBench to larger-scale, more diverse tasks involving complex interplays of color with texture, shape, and spatial relationships. Furthermore, investigating the impact of different visual encoders and language models could further elucidate the pathways through which VLMs process color information.

    本文提出 ColorBench——首个旨在全面评估 VLM 颜色理解能力（包括感知、推理与鲁棒性）的基准。在评测了 3232 个广泛使用的 VLM 之后，我们揭示出若干此前未被发现的观察。这些观察凸显出需要更复杂的模型架构，以整合更深层的颜色推理能力。为保证标注的高质量与可靠性，ColorBench 在多数领域依赖人工数据采集、标注与评估。这虽然确保了连贯性，却不可避免地限制了数据集的规模、风格多样性与类别覆盖。作为未来工作，我们计划开发一套可信的自动化数据采集流程，并把 ColorBench 扩展到更大规模、更具多样性的任务，涵盖颜色与纹理、形状及空间关系之间的复杂交互。此外，研究不同视觉编码器与语言模型的影响，可进一步阐明 VLM 处理颜色信息的通路。

    Appendix A Related Works

    附录 A 相关工作

    A.1 VLM Benchmarks

    A.1 VLM 基准

    With the rapid advancements in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) [9], numerous benchmarks have emerged to systematically evaluate VLM capabilities across diverse dimensions [29]. These benchmarks generally fall into two categories: text-centric and vision-centric evaluations, each designed to assess distinct multimodal competencies. Text-centric benchmarks primarily measure commonsense knowledge, reasoning, and complex problem-solving capabilities, exemplified by tasks in MMMU [47] and NaturalBench [23]. Conversely, vision-centric benchmarks focus on visual perception and reasoning (MMBench [32] and MME [10]), and robustness to visual perturbations (Grit [14] and Visual Robustness [17]). Furthermore, several benchmarks have extended their scope to evaluate specialized visual tasks, such as spatial relationship comprehension (SEED-Bench [22] and MM-Vet [46]), chart and map understanding (MMSTAR [4] and MuirBench [43]), visual grounding (Flickr30k [36] and TRIG [27]) and the detection and understanding of visual hallucinations (POPE [28] and HallusionBench [13]). However, despite the extensive scope covered by existing VLM benchmarks, none currently provide an integrated evaluation that simultaneously assesses visual perception, reasoning, and robustness within a unified framework. Moreover, although certain benchmarks [32, 10] have incorporated color-related questions, these have typically addressed basic color perception and recognition, neglecting deeper assessments of reasoning and robustness associated with color understanding.

    随着视觉语言模型（VLM）[9] 的迅速进步，涌现出大量基准，用以从多个维度系统评测 VLM 的能力 [29]。这些基准大体分为两类：以文本为中心的评测与以视觉为中心的评测，各自用于考察不同的多模态能力。以文本为中心的基准主要度量常识知识、推理与复杂问题解决能力，以 MMMU [47] 与 NaturalBench [23] 中的任务为代表。相反，以视觉为中心的基准关注视觉感知与推理（MMBench [32] 与 MME [10]），以及对视觉扰动的鲁棒性（Grit [14] 与 Visual Robustness [17]）。此外，若干基准还把范围扩展到专门的视觉任务，如空间关系理解（SEED-Bench [22] 与 MM-Vet [46]）、图表与地图理解（MMSTAR [4] 与 MuirBench [43]）、视觉定位（Flickr30k [36] 与 TRIG [27]），以及视觉幻觉的检测与理解（POPE [28] 与 HallusionBench [13]）。然而，尽管现有 VLM 基准覆盖的范围已相当广泛，尚无一个能在统一框架内同时评估视觉感知、推理与鲁棒性。此外，虽然某些基准 [32, 10] 已纳入与颜色相关的问题，它们通常只涉及基础的颜色感知与识别，忽略了与颜色理解相关的推理与鲁棒性的更深层评估。

    A.2 Color Evaluation

    A.2 颜色评测

    Color understanding is increasingly recognized as a crucial aspect of Vision-Language Models’ ability to perceive and interpret visual content. Limited studies have explored how color information influences model performance on specific tasks. Some studies [51, 50] explore the understanding of color by replacing color-related words in textual inputs to evaluate the models’ ability to handle color-specific information. More recent research [16, 21] focuses on assessing fine-grained color discrimination by asking models to distinguish subtle color differences in visual inputs. Samin et al. [39] introduced color-related foils to test VLMs’ capacity to cognize basic colors like red, white, and green, particularly in contexts requiring attention to subtle cues. Additionally, Burapacheep et al. [3] developed a benchmark dataset to evaluate and enhance compositional color comprehension in VLMs, emphasizing tasks where understanding minimal color relationships is essential. IllusionVQA [40] evaluates model perception of color illusions in photorealistic scenes. While these works have addressed isolated aspects of color understanding, none have provided a holistic assessment framework. In contrast to these previous works, our study establishes the first comprehensive and specialized benchmark for evaluating the color-related abilities of VLMs, offering a quantitative, automated approach to further this area of research.

    颜色理解正日益被视为 VLM 感知与解读视觉内容能力的关键一环。已有少量研究探讨颜色信息如何影响模型在特定任务上的表现。部分研究 [51, 50] 通过替换文本输入中与颜色相关的词，考察模型处理颜色特定信息的能力。更近期的研究 [16, 21] 关注细粒度颜色辨别，要求模型区分视觉输入中细微的颜色差异。Samin 等人 [39] 引入与颜色相关的干扰项，测试 VLM 认知红、白、绿等基础颜色的能力，尤其是在需要注意细微线索的语境下。此外，Burapacheep 等人 [3] 构建了一个基准数据集，用于评测并增强 VLM 的组合式颜色理解，强调那些必须理解最小颜色关系的任务。IllusionVQA [40] 评估模型对照片级真实场景中颜色错觉的感知。尽管这些工作触及了颜色理解的孤立侧面，却没有提供一个整体性的评估框架。与这些前人工作不同，我们的研究建立了首个全面而专门的基准，用于评估 VLM 与颜色相关的能力，并提供一种定量、自动化的方法来推进这一领域的研究。

    Appendix C Detailed Generation Process for Robustness

    附录 C 鲁棒性的详细生成过程

    For the Color Robustness, we evaluate the consistency of VLMs when faced with instances that differ only in the color of the visual input. To systematically assess this effect, we define 33 recoloring strategies that determine which part of the image is altered: i) Target Segment, ii) Largest Segment, and iii) Entire Image. As mentioned in Table 6, Target Segment strategy recolors only the segment containing the object referenced in the question. This strategy ensures that the modification directly affects the model’s perception of task-relevant content. Largest Segment strategy alters the color of the largest segment that is irrelevant to the question, testing whether models are distracted by dominant but unrelated visual changes. In contrast, Entire Image strategy applies a global color shift to evaluate the model’s sensitivity to overall color variations. As summarized in Table 6, the first two strategies introduce localized modifications, while the third assesses robustness to broader image-wide color changes. Importantly, only color attributes are altered without modifying object shapes or contextual elements, which preserves the overall realism of the image. By incorporating both task-relevant and irrelevant edits, our benchmark provides a comprehensive evaluation of VLMs’ ability to handle color perturbations across different contexts.

    对颜色鲁棒性，我们评估 VLM 在面对仅在视觉输入颜色上有所不同的实例时的连贯性。为系统评估这一效应，我们定义 33 种重着色策略，用以决定图像被改变的部位：i）目标分割区域，ii）最大分割区域，iii）整幅图像。如表 6 所述，目标分割区域策略只对包含问题所提及物体的分割区域重着色。该策略确保修改直接作用于模型对任务相关内容的感知。最大分割区域策略改变与问题无关的最大分割区域的颜色，检验模型是否会被显著但无关的视觉变化所干扰。相反，整幅图像策略施加全局颜色偏移，以评估模型对整体颜色变化的敏感度。如表 6 所总结，前两种策略引入局部化修改，第三种则评估对更广泛的整图颜色变化的鲁棒性。重要的是，只有颜色属性被改变，物体形状与语境元素均未改动，从而保持了图像的整体真实感。通过同时纳入与任务相关和无关的编辑，我们的基准全面评估了 VLM 在不同语境下应对颜色扰动的能力。

    While generating color variations, we derive seed images from CV-Bench [42], a publicly available benchmark. For each seed image, as shown in Figure 3, we first employ a Grounded Segmentation Model (GAM) [38] to extract segments and their corresponding labels. We then apply the predefined recoloring strategies to determine the editing region. Once the editing region is determined, we modify the color of the corresponding region. In HSV color space, since Saturation and Value control the purity or brightness of the color, and only Hue controls the color of the part, we only adjust the Hue value in the HSV color space. Specifically, we shift the Hue by 9090°, 180180°, and 270270°. These three values ensure that the color manipulations cover significant perceptual differences across the color spectrum. This process produces nine variations per seed image, covering different strategies and degrees of color change to enable a comprehensive robustness assessment. To ensure interpretability, human experts filter out unnatural or negligible modifications, resulting in a final selection of 493 seed images for robustness evaluation. Additionally, we select questions that are color-invariant, which means answers remain valid regardless of whether the recoloring appears fully natural. This design choice isolates color variation as the sole variable of interest and prevents confounding effects from semantic or contextual changes. Through these steps, we evaluate whether VLMs rely excessively on color information and whether they maintain consistency in their predictions despite substantial color shifts.

    在生成颜色变体时，我们从公开基准 CV-Bench [42] 中取材种子图像。对每张种子图像，如图 3 所示，我们首先采用 Grounded Segmentation Model（GAM）[38] 提取分割区域及其对应标签，随后施加预定义的重着色策略来确定编辑区域。编辑区域确定后，我们修改相应区域的颜色。在 HSV 色彩空间中，由于饱和度与明度控制颜色的纯度或亮度，只有色相控制该部分的颜色，因此我们只调整 HSV 色彩空间中的色相值。具体地，我们把色相分别偏移 9090°、180180° 与 270270°。这三个取值确保颜色操作覆盖整个色谱上显著的感知差异。这一过程为每张种子图像产生九个变体，覆盖不同策略与不同程度的颜色改变，以支持全面的鲁棒性评估。为保证可解释性，人工专家会滤除不自然或可忽略的修改，最终选出 493 张种子图像用于鲁棒性评测。此外，我们选取与颜色无关的问题，即无论重着色是否完全自然，答案都依然成立。这一设计选择把颜色变化隔离为唯一关注的变量，避免来自语义或语境变化的混淆效应。通过上述步骤，我们评估 VLM 是否过度依赖颜色信息，以及在大幅颜色偏移下能否保持预测的一致性。

    To further advance our understanding of VLMs’ capabilities in color perception, reasoning, and robustness dimensions, we conduct an extensive evaluation of 3232 vision-language models (VLMs) spanning a range of large language model (LLM) sizes and architectures.     为进一步加深我们对 VLM 在颜色感知、推理与鲁棒性三个维度上能力的理解，我们对 3232 个视觉语言模型（VLM）开展了大规模评测，覆盖不同规模与架构的大语言模型（LLM）。评测对象包括 GPT-4o[35]、Gemini-2-flash[7]、LLaVA-OV[24]、LLaVA-NEXT [31]、Cambrian[42]、InternVL2[5]、InternVL2.5[5]、Qwen2.5-VL[2] 与 Eagle[41] 等最先进模型。GPT-4o 与 Gemini-2-flash 通过 API 调用使用。我们还进一步考察通过思维链（CoT）提示 [44] 增强推理的效果，把它应用于 GPT-4o 与 Gemini-2-Flash，以评估中间推理步骤如何影响颜色理解。此外，我们还在感知与推理任务上纳入最新的 GPT-o3——它是具备长程内部思维链过程的最强模型。这一选择覆盖了多样的架构，兼含闭源与开源模型，从而能够全面评估它们在不同计算约束下的推理能力。

This task demands capabilities of precise color recognition combined with a holistic spatial perception. One plausible reason for GPT-o3’s inferior performance is its longer and more complex reasoning path, which may lead to overthinking. This might cause the model to focus too much on local details or choices of tool, at the expense of the global and intuitive perception needed for this task.

    M.3 Failure with LLM and Vision

    M.3 视觉与语言双重失效

    We present a representative failure case that highlights limitations in both the vision and language components of the model. As shown in Figure 72, the model fails to correctly interpret the visual content—it misidentifies the target colors by focusing on pink and purple flowers instead of red and yellow ones, indicating a vision encoder error. Furthermore, the language model compounds this mistake by generating an incorrect chain-of-thought reasoning and arriving at an erroneous answer based on the wrong color categories. This example underscores the necessity of evaluating both visual perception and language reasoning when diagnosing failure modes in vision-language models.

    我们给出一个代表性的失败案例，凸显模型视觉与语言两部分的局限。如图 72 所示，模型未能正确解读视觉内容——它把目标颜色误认为粉色与紫色的花，而非红色与黄色的花，说明视觉编码器出错。进一步地，语言模型因生成了错误的思维链推理、并基于错误的颜色类别得出错误答案，而放大了这一失误。该例说明，在诊断视觉语言模型的失效模式时，有必要同时评估视觉感知与语言推理。
