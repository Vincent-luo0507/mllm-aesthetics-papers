中文摘要

Abstract (原文)

Assessing the aesthetic quality of graphic design is central to visual communication, yet remains underexplored in vision language models (VLMs). We investigate whether VLMs can evaluate design aesthetics in ways comparable to humans. Prior work faces three key limitations: benchmarks restricted to narrow principles and coarse evaluation protocols, a lack of systematic VLM comparisons, and limited training data for model improvement. In this work, we introduce AesEval-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark spanning four dimensions, twelve indicators, and three fully quantifiable tasks: aesthetic judgment, region selection, and precise localization. Then, we systematically evaluate proprietary, open-source, and reasoning-augmented VLMs, revealing clear performance gaps against the nuanced demands of aesthetic assessment. Moreover, we construct a training dataset to fine-tune VLMs for this domain, leveraging human-guided VLM labeling to produce task labels at scale and indicator-grounded reasoning to tie abstract indicators to concrete design regions.Together, our work establishes the first systematic framework for aesthetic quality assessment in graphic design. 1INTRODUCTION

1 引言

The rapid development of vision-language models (VLMs) (Yang et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024a; Hurstet al., 2024) has opened new opportunities for understanding and acting upon multimodal informa-tion. While they have already achieved remarkable progress in traditional vision tasks such as imagecaptioning (Lin et al., 2024b; Li et al., 2025b) and visual question answering (An et al., 2024; Zhanget al., 2024; Lin et al., 2025), VLMs are increasingly expected to contribute to new applications.Among these, graphic design—which integrates textual and visual elements to convey informationacross advertising, branding, and digital media—represents a promising direction given its broadsocietal and practical impact. Crucially, the success of graphic design critically depends on its aes-thetic quality, shaped by principles such as balance, contrast, and hierarchy.

视觉语言模型（VLM）的快速发展（Yang 等，2025；Li 等，2024a；Hurst 等，2024）为理解与处理多模态信息带来了新的机遇。尽管它们在图像描述（Lin 等，2024b；Li 等，2025b）与视觉问答（An 等，2024；Zhang 等，2024；Lin 等，2025）等传统视觉任务上已取得显著进展，人们对 VLM 的期待正转向新的应用方向。其中，平面设计融合文字与视觉元素，在广告、品牌与数字媒体中传递信息，凭借其广泛的社会影响与实用价值，成为一个颇具前景的方向。关键在于，平面设计的成败在很大程度上取决于其美学质量，而后者由平衡、对比、层级等设计原则所塑造。

In this work, we aim to explore a central question: can VLMs understand and evaluate aestheticquality of graphic design in a manner comparable to humans? This question is of significant im-portance for at least three reasons. First, it can assist human designers by identifying where a designfalls short and explaining why, thereby enabling more effective improvement. Second, for generativeAI systems, it provides the basis for automatic feedback loops that can guide iterative refinementwithout extensive human intervention. Third, it suggests an opportunity to extend VLMs beyondfactual recognition toward aesthetic evaluation.

本文旨在探讨一个核心问题：VLM 能否以接近人类的方式理解并评价平面设计的美学质量？这一问题的重要性至少体现在三方面。其一，它能辅助人类设计师，指出设计在何处不足并解释原因，从而实现更有效的改进。其二，对于生成式 AI 系统，它提供了自动反馈闭环的基础，可在无需大量人工干预的情况下引导迭代式优化。其三，它提示了一条把 VLM 从事实性识别拓展到美学评估的路径。

As a first step toward addressing these limitations, we introduce AesEval-Bench, a new benchmarkfor evaluating the aesthetic quality of graphic designs (see Figure 1). Drawing on prior literature (Li& Chen, 2009; Wangwiwattana & Meeyen, 2024), we identify four critical dimensions—typography,layout, color, and graphics—that together comprehensively capture design aesthetics. These dimen-sions reflect the major factors that humans consistently emphasize when assessing visual appeal. Toprovide finer granularity, we further define twelve indicators that specify concrete aspects withineach dimension, such as hierarchy and legibility under typography. For each indicators, we then de-sign three challenging tasks: 1) aesthetic judgment, which asks models to decide whether a designis aesthetically pleasing (yes/no), providing a straightforward measure of overall perception; 2) re-gion selection, which requires models to choose from candidate regions where unpleasing elementsappear, testing their ability to pinpoint problematic areas beyond a global judgment; 3) precise local-ization, which challenges models to predict the exact bounding box (bbox) coordinates of unpleasingareas, offering the most detailed diagnosis and reflecting a deeper understanding of aesthetics. Un-like prior benchmarks (), AesEval-Bench not only covers a broad range of aesthetic factors throughits dimensions and indicators, but also defines evaluation tasks that are fully quantifiable via choiceor bbox prediction formats, enabling systematic and reproducible assessment of design aesthetics.

作为弥补上述不足的第一步，我们提出 AesEval-Bench，一个用于评估平面设计美学质量的新基准（见图 1）。借鉴已有文献（Li & Chen，2009；Wangwiwattana & Meeyen，2024），我们识别出四个关键维度——字体排版、版式、色彩与图形，它们合在一起全面刻画了设计美学。这些维度反映了人类在评判视觉吸引力时一贯强调的主要因素。为提供更细的粒度，我们进一步定义了十二项指标，用以规定每个维度下的具体方面，例如字体排版维度下的层级与易读性。针对每一项指标，我们设计了三项具有挑战性的任务：1）审美判断，要求模型判断一件设计是否美观（是/否），为整体感知提供直观度量；2）区域选择，要求模型从候选区域中选出出现不佳元素的位置，考察其在全局判断之外定位问题区域的能力；3）精确定位，要求模型预测不佳区域的确切边界框（bbox）坐标，提供最细致的诊断，反映对美学更深层的理解。与已有基准不同，AesEval-Bench 不仅通过其维度与指标覆盖了广泛的美学因素，还定义了可通过选择或 bbox 预测格式完全量化的评测任务，从而支持对设计美学的系统化、可复现评估。

With AesEval-Bench, we systematically evaluate VLMs on their ability to assess the aesthetic qual-ity of graphic designs (see Table 1). For each design, models perform three tasks across twelveindicators. We use human annotation as ground truth, and measure performance by accuracy (foraesthetic judgment and region selection) and bbox IoU (for precise localization). Overall, our resultsreveal clear gaps between current state-of-the-art VLMs and the nuanced demands of aesthetic qual-ity assessment. Specifically, proprietary VLMs (e.g., GPT series (Achiam et al., 2023)) outperformopen-source ones (e.g., Qwen-VL (Wang et al., 2024), Intern-VL (Zhu et al., 2025), LLaVA (Liuet al., 2023)). Among open-source models, larger variants (32B, 72B) generally achieve better per-formance than smaller ones (7B). Surprisingly, reasoning-augmented VLMs (e.g., GPT-o1 (Jaechet al., 2024), GPT-o3, Gemini-2.5-Pro (Comanici et al., 2025)) offer no clear advantage over theirnon-reasoning counterparts. Together, these findings expose the limitations of existing VLMs andunderscore the need for domain-specific training tailored to aesthetic quality assessment.

借助 AesEval-Bench，我们系统评测了 VLM 评估平面设计美学质量的能力（见表 1）。对于每件设计，模型需围绕十二项指标完成三项任务。我们以人工标注作为真值，用准确率（审美判断与区域选择）和 bbox IoU（精确定位）衡量性能。总体而言，结果揭示了当前最优 VLM 与美学质量评估的细腻要求之间的明显差距。具体而言，闭源 VLM（如 GPT 系列（Achiam 等，2023））优于开源模型（如 Qwen-VL（Wang 等，2024）、Intern-VL（Zhu 等，2025）、LLaVA（Liu 等，2023））。在开源模型中，更大的变体（32B、72B）通常优于更小的（7B）。令人意外的是，推理增强型 VLM（如 GPT-o1（Jaech 等，2024）、GPT-o3、Gemini-2.5-Pro（Comanici 等，2025））相比其非推理版本并无明显优势。这些发现共同暴露了现有 VLM 的局限，也凸显了面向美学质量评估的领域专用训练的必要性。

Building on these findings, we move beyond evaluation and turn to training VLMs for aestheticquality assessment. To this end, we construct a training dataset consisting of three components: thetask (what the model is asked to do), the task label (the expected answer), and the reasoning path(the explanation leading to the answer) (see Figure 2). We treat the reasoning path as essential,since generic reasoning has shown little benefit. However, constructing such data poses two keychallenges: producing task labels at scale is costly, and generating reasoning paths that genuinelyimprove performance requires new approaches. To address these, we introduce two solutions. First,human-guided VLM labeling, where a small set of human annotations serve as in-context exam-ples to instruct powerful VLMs in producing task labels. This approach maintains alignment withhuman understanding while reducing manual annotation costs. Second, indicator-grounded reason-ing, where abstract indicators (e.g., hierarchy or layering) are explicitly tied to concrete regions inthe design. Each reasoning path consists of bounding-box coordinates linked to the indicator andtextual explanations for their relevance, providing fine-grained and interpretable supervision. Wefine-tune VLMs with the task as input and both the reasoning path and the task label as supervision,and evaluate on AesEval-Bench. The results show consistent performance gains across all tasks andindicators (e.g., 5.97%, 2.70% and 17.17%), demonstrating that human-guided VLM labeling yieldsreliable labels and indicator-grounded reasoning supplies effective supervision.

在上述发现的基础上，我们不止于评测，转而研究如何训练 VLM 进行美学质量评估。为此，我们构建了一个训练数据集，包含三个组成部分：任务（要求模型做什么）、任务标签（期望的答案）与推理路径（通向答案的解释）（见图 2）。我们认为推理路径不可或缺，因为通用推理的收益甚微。然而，构建这类数据面临两个关键挑战：规模化产出任务标签代价高昂，而生成真正能提升性能的推理路径需要新的方法。为此，我们提出两项解决方案。其一，人类引导的 VLM 标注：以少量人工标注作为上下文示例，指导强大的 VLM 生成任务标签。该方式在保持与人类理解一致的同时降低了人工标注成本。其二，指标锚定推理：把抽象指标（如层级或层次）显式绑定到设计中的具体区域。每条推理路径由与该指标关联的边界框坐标以及说明其相关性的文本解释组成，提供了细粒度且可解释的监督信号。我们以任务为输入、以推理路径与任务标签为监督对 VLM 做微调，并在 AesEval-Bench 上评测。结果显示各项任务与指标上均有一致的性能增益（例如 5.97%、2.70% 与 17.17%），说明人类引导的 VLM 标注能产出可靠标签，而指标锚定推理提供了有效的监督。

2 相关工作

Aesthetic Quality Assessment. Aesthetic quality assessment (Deng et al., 2017) aims to automat-ically evaluate visual appeal, serving as a computational proxy for human judgment. Within thisarea, two major lines of research have emerged (see Table 1). Image aesthetics assessment (Huanget al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2024; Jiang & Chen, 2025) focuses on photographic images, where qualityis determined by factors such as color harmony, lighting, and subject placement. Design aestheticsassessment (Lin et al., 2023; 2024a; Haraguchi et al., 2024; Jung et al., 2025) targets graphic designssuch as posters, advertisements, or user interfaces, which depend on design-related factors includingtypography, hierarchy, and alignment. Our work falls within design aesthetics assessment.

美学质量评估（Deng 等，2017）旨在自动评价视觉吸引力，作为人类判断的计算代理。该领域形成了两条主要研究脉络（见表 1）。图像美学评估（Huang 等，2024；Zhou 等，2024；Jiang & Chen，2025）聚焦摄影图像，其质量由色彩和谐、光照与主体位置等因素决定。设计美学评估（Lin 等，2023；2024a；Haraguchi 等，2024；Jung 等，2025）则面向海报、广告或用户界面等平面设计，后者取决于字体排版、层级与对齐等设计相关因素。我们的工作属于设计美学评估。

Despite its importance, design aesthetics assessment remains underexplored. Existing benchmarkscapture only a narrow subset of design dimensions. For instance, (Lin et al., 2024a) omits graphics-related factors, while (Haraguchi et al., 2024) ignores both fonts and graphics. Furthermore, theirtask formulations lack rigor. Some adopt free-form question answering, which is difficult to quan-tify (Lin et al., 2023), while others provide only holistic scores without identifying problematicregions, limiting interpretability and actionability (Jung et al., 2025). Our work introduces a bench-mark that comprehensively covers design-related aesthetic factors across font, layout, graphics andcolor, defining well-structured and quantifiable tasks using choice and bbox prediction formats.

尽管意义重大，设计美学评估仍欠缺探索。现有基准只捕捉了设计维度的一个狭窄子集。例如（Lin 等，2024a）遗漏了图形相关因素，而（Haraguchi 等，2024）同时忽略了字体与图形。此外，它们的任务形式不够严谨：有的采用自由问答，难以量化（Lin 等，2023）；有的只给出整体分数而不指出问题区域，限制了可解释性与可操作性（Jung 等，2025）。我们的工作提出了一个基准，全面覆盖字体、版式、图形与色彩等设计相关的美学因素，并用选择与 bbox 预测格式定义了结构良好、可量化的任务。

3.1OVERVIEW

3.1 概览

AesEval-Bench formulates design aesthetics assessment as a question–answering task. The inputcontains a task description and a design image, optionally accompanied by metadata such as layout,font, or color information in JSON format. The output is the answer corresponding to the task.

AesEval-Bench 把设计美学评估表述为问答任务。输入包含任务描述与一幅设计图像，并可选附带 JSON 格式的元数据，如版式、字体或色彩信息；输出为任务对应的答案。

We consider twelve indicators (Figure 1(B)), grouped into four dimensions (Figure 1(A)).

For design images, we sample 1200 designs from the test split of Crello dataset (Yamaguchi, 2021),which contains professional graphic designs with both the design image and its metadata. Theexpected answers differ across tasks. For aesthetic judgment, the answer is yes or no. For regionselection, it is the index of one region among four candidates. For precise localization, it is thebounding box coordinates of the identified region or None if the design has no aesthetic issues.

设计图像方面，我们从 Crello 数据集（Yamaguchi，2021）的测试划分中采样 1200 件设计，该数据集包含专业平面设计，同时提供设计图像与元数据。各任务的预期答案不同：审美判断的答案为“是”或“否”；区域选择为四个候选区域中某一区域的索引；精确定位为所识别区域的边界框坐标，若该设计没有美学问题则为 None。

Overall, AesEval-Bench comprises 4500 base question–answer pairs (three tasks across 1500 de-signs), each further instantiated across twelve indicators to enable fine-grained evaluation.

总体而言，AesEval-Bench 包含 4500 个基础问答对（1500 件设计 × 三项任务），每个问答对又进一步在十二项指标上实例化，以支持细粒度评估。

3.3EVALUATION PROTOCOLS

3.3 评测协议

For aesthetic judgment and region selection, both formulated as choice problems, we adopt accuracyas the metric, measuring the exact match between model predictions and the ground truth. Forprecise localization, the task combines two components: a choice problem (predicting None whenno aesthetic issue exists) and a bounding box regression problem (predicting the exact bbox whenan issue is present). Accordingly, we use accuracy for cases where the ground truth is None, andintersection over union (IoU)—which quantifies the overlap between the predicted and ground-truthbboxes—for cases where a bbox is required.

对于审美判断与区域选择，两者都被表述为选择题，我们采用准确率作为指标，衡量模型预测与真值之间的精确匹配。对于精确定位，该任务包含两个部分：一个选择题（当不存在美学问题时预测 None）与一个边界框回归问题（当存在问题时预测精确的 bbox）。相应地，我们对真值为 None 的情形使用准确率，对需要 bbox 的情形使用交并比（IoU）——它量化预测 bbox 与真值 bbox 之间的重叠程度。

4TRAINING DATA CONSTRUCTION

4 训练数据构建

Evaluation on popular VLMs reveals clear gap between the capabiliteis of current state-of-the-arts VLMs and the nuanced requirements of aesthetic quality assessment. Moreover, reasoning-augmented VLMs show no clear performance gains (see Section 5.1).

在主流 VLM 上的评测显示，当前最优 VLM 的能力与美学质量评估的细腻要求之间存在明显差距。此外，推理增强型 VLM 并未表现出清晰的性能增益（见第 5.1 节）。

To this end, we construct a training dataset, named AesEval-Train, to fine-tune VLMs for this do-main. First, we adopt the same procedure as benchmark construction to construct potentially flaweddesigns (see Section 3.2). Next, since relying solely on human annotation to determine whetherperturbed designs exhibit aesthetic issues is neither scalable nor cost-effective for training at largescale, we introduce human-guided VLM labeling. Then, we follow the benchmark construction togenerate question–answer pairs (see Section 3.2). Finally, we introduce indicator-grounded rea-soning to generate domain-specific reasoning paths aimed at improving task performance. In thefollowing, we describe in detail the two steps that differ from benchmark construction.

为此，我们构建了一个名为 AesEval-Train 的训练数据集，用于在该领域微调 VLM。首先，我们采用与基准构建相同的流程来构建潜在缺陷设计（见第 3.2 节）。其次，由于仅靠人工标注来判定被扰动的设计是否存在美学问题，在大规模训练时既不具扩展性也不经济，我们引入了人类引导的 VLM 标注。然后，我们沿用基准构建的方式生成问答对（见第 3.2 节）。最后，我们引入指标锚定推理，生成面向该领域的推理路径，以提升任务性能。下面详述与基准构建不同的两个步骤。

Human-Guided VLM Labeling. We leverage a small set of human annotations as demonstrations,together with the bbox coordinates of perturbed regions, as input to strong VLMs. The model isinstructed to generate a binary label indicating whether the perturbed design exhibits an aestheticissue (see Figure 2(A)). By incorporating human annotations, we preserve alignment with humanjudgment while substantially reducing manual annotation costs. Moreover, providing the perturba-tion region as a prior, which is unavailable in real-world scenarios, simplifies the labeling processand improves reliability. With these two sources of guidance, while the generated labels may not beperfectly accurate, they yield a training set of sufficient quality to enhance fine-tuning performance.

人类引导的 VLM 标注。我们以少量人工标注作为示范，连同被扰动区域的 bbox 坐标一起输入给强大的 VLM，并指示模型生成一个二值标签，表明被扰动的设计是否存在美学问题（见图 2(A)）。通过引入人工标注，我们保持了与人类判断的一致性，同时大幅降低了人工标注成本。此外，把扰动区域作为先验（这一信息在真实场景中并不可得）提供出来，简化了标注过程并提升了可靠性。在这两种引导之下，尽管生成的标签未必完全准确，它们足以形成质量达标的训练集，从而提升微调性能。

Indicator-Grounded Reasoning. As illustrated in Figure 2(B), generic reasoning often explainsor analyzes a given indicator and task without grounding the discussion in relevant regions of thedesign. To address this limitation, we propose explicitly linking abstract indicators to concreteregions within the design. Specifically, we include both the bounding box (bbox) coordinates ofrelevant regions and textual explanations of their relevance to the indicator in the reasoning path.

指标锚定推理。如图 2(B) 所示，通用推理往往只是解释或分析给定指标与任务，而没有把讨论锚定到设计中的相关区域。为克服这一局限，我们提出把抽象指标显式关联到设计内的具体区域。具体而言，我们在推理路径中同时包含相关区域的边界框（bbox）坐标，以及说明其与指标相关性的文本解释。

To obtain such reasoning paths, we instruct powerful VLMs (e.g., GPT in our experiments) byproviding them with the bbox coordinates of the target regions and the corresponding design layers(Figure 2(A)). The model is required to output the provided coordinates alongside an explanationof how the region relates to the indicator, thereby ensuring that the reasoning path consistentlycontains the desired information. We further adopt task-specific strategies to determine the regionsof interest. For aesthetic judgment, we directly use the bbox of the perturbed regions. For regionselection, we include both the perturbed and non-perturbed regions to strengthen the model’s abilityto discriminate among candidate regions. For precise localization, we not only highlight the bbox ofperturbed regions but also emphasize their relationship to the overall design, enabling the model tobetter localize problematic regions within the global design context.

为获得这样的推理路径，我们向强大的 VLM（实验中为 GPT）提供目标区域的 bbox 坐标与相应的设计图层（图 2(A)），并要求模型输出给定的坐标，同时解释该区域与指标的关系，从而确保推理路径始终包含所需信息。我们还采用任务特定策略来确定关注区域。对于审美判断，我们直接使用被扰动区域的 bbox。对于区域选择，我们同时纳入被扰动与未被扰动的区域，以强化模型在候选区域之间的判别能力。对于精确定位，我们不仅突出被扰动区域的 bbox，还强调其与整体设计的关系，使模型能在全局设计语境下更好地定位问题区域。

6 结论

In this work, we introduce AesEval-Bench for design aesthetics assessment, which spans four di-mensions, twelve indicators and three quantifiable tasks. Based on it, we systematically evaluateproprietary, open-source and reasoning-augmented VLMs, revealing clear gaps in design aesthet-ics assessment. Furthermore, we construct a training dataset to fine-tune VLMs for this domain.Experiments show that this dataset significantly improves model performance across all tasks.

本文提出 AesEval-Bench 用于设计美学评估，它涵盖四个维度、十二项指标与三项可量化任务。基于此，我们系统评测了闭源、开源与推理增强型 VLM，揭示了设计美学评估上明显的差距。此外，我们构建了一个训练数据集用于在该领域微调 VLM。实验表明，该数据集显著提升了模型在所有任务上的性能。
