中文摘要

个性化图像美学评价（PIAA）旨在预测随个体而异的图像美学评分。美学偏好在不同视觉刺激上表现出的程度各异，并呈现群组特有的模式。受这一事实驱动，本文提出一种基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的方法，通过富偏好样本挖掘与美学共鸣群组合并（PRAC）来建模个体美学偏好。具体地，PRAC 首先通过分析图像的群体争议度与个性化偏离度来识别富偏好样本，从而最大化有限用户数据的效用；在此基础上，通过比较偏好嵌入来度量跨用户的偏好相似性；随后提出一种基于群组的模型合并策略，聚合美学共鸣用户的偏好模式，进一步增强对目标个体的个性化。在四个 PIAA 基准数据库上的大量实验与对比表明，所提出的 PRAC 模型优于当前最优方法。代码与模型将在 https://github.com/yzc-ippl/PRAC 公开。

Abstract (原文)

Personalized Image Aesthetic Assessment (PIAA) aims to predict aesthetic ratings of images that vary across individuals. The aesthetic preferences manifest to different extents across distinct visual stimuli and exhibit cohort-specific patterns. Motivated by the above fact, this paper presents a Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM)-based approach, which models individual aesthetic preferences by Preference-Rich sample mining and Aesthetically-resonant Cohort merging (PRAC). Specifically, PRAC first identifies preference-rich samples by analyzing both Collective Controversy and Personalized Deviation of images, maximizing the utility of limited user data. Based upon the preference-rich samples, cross-user preference similarities are measured by comparing preference embeddings. Then, a cohort-based model merging strategy, is proposed by aggregating preference patterns from aesthetically-resonant users, which further enhances the personalization for the target individual. Extensive experiments and comparisons on four benchmark PIAA databases demonstrate the superiority of the proposed PRAC model over the state-of-the-arts. The code and model will be public at https://github.com/yzc-ippl/PRAC.

1. Introduction

1. 引言

The widespread use of smartphones and social networks have dramatically transformed the consumption patterns of visual media. This shift has intensified user attention on image aesthetics, establishing image aesthetic assessment (IAA) as a prominent research topic (Deng et al., 2017; Yang et al., 2026b; Li et al., 2024). Early studies primarily focused on generic IAA, which predicts average aesthetic ratings that reflect general judgments (Murray et al., 2012; Yang et al., 2024b; Huang et al., 2024b; Sheng et al., 2026b). However, aesthetics is inherently subjective—different users may have substantially different aesthetic evaluations of the same image (as illustrated in Figure 1). This has driven growing interest in personalized image aesthetic assessment (PIAA), which models individual preferences by adapting to user-centric aesthetic tastes (Ren et al., 2017; Chen et al., 2025; Maerten et al., 2025). With the surging demand for customization, PIAA offers expanding applications including personalized image recommendations (Chen et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2025), user-centric photo enhancement (Zhang et al., 2022; Ni et al., 2022; Sheng et al., 2026a), and personalized image generation (Xu et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2026a; Yang et al., 2025).

智能手机与社交网络的广泛使用，极大地改变了视觉媒体的消费方式。这一转变强化了用户对图像美学的关注，使图像美学评价（IAA）成为一个重要的研究课题（Deng et al., 2017; Yang et al., 2026b; Li et al., 2024）。早期研究主要关注通用 IAA，即预测反映一般性判断的平均美学评分（Murray et al., 2012; Yang et al., 2024b; Huang et al., 2024b; Sheng et al., 2026b）。然而，美学本质上是主观的——不同用户对同一图像的美学评价可能大相径庭（如图 1 所示）。这推动了个性化图像美学评价（PIAA）的兴起，它通过适配以用户为中心的美学趣味来建模个体偏好（Ren et al., 2017; Chen et al., 2025; Maerten et al., 2025）。随着定制化需求的激增，PIAA 的应用不断拓展，包括个性化图像推荐（Chen et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2025）、以用户为中心的照片增强（Zhang et al., 2022; Ni et al., 2022; Sheng et al., 2026a）以及个性化图像生成（Xu et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2026a; Yang et al., 2025）。

In the past few years, a proliferation of novel PIAA models have been reported in the literature. People’s aesthetic tastes are typically determined by complex factors including both image attributes and user characteristics. This requires that models possess not only visual understanding capabilities but also knowledge of various demographic and personality dimensions, such as age, cultural background, and psychological traits (Kim et al., 2018). To address these challenges, previous vision-based PIAA methods have largely resorted to auxiliary tasks related to preference representation, such as personalized feedback or personality trait prediction (Lv et al., 2021; Li et al., 2020). Another challenge in PIAA is the few-shot learning (FSL) nature, as it is difficult to collect a large number of annotated images for a specific user. To address the dilemma, previous approaches are typically designed by fine-tuning generic IAA models (Ren et al., 2017; Wang et al., 2018), or resort to meta-learning for extracting common aesthetic features across users that can efficiently transfer to target individuals (Zhu et al., 2020; Li et al., 2022).

过去几年中，文献中涌现出大量新的 PIAA 模型。人们的美学趣味通常由复杂因素决定，既包括图像属性，也包括用户特征。这要求模型不仅具备视觉理解能力，还要掌握人口统计学与人格等多个维度的知识，例如年龄、文化背景与心理特质（Kim et al., 2018）。为应对这些挑战，以往基于视觉的 PIAA 方法很大程度上依赖与偏好表示相关的辅助任务，例如个性化反馈或人格特质预测（Lv et al., 2021; Li et al., 2020）。PIAA 的另一重挑战在于其小样本学习（FSL）性质——为特定用户收集大量标注图像十分困难。为摆脱这一困境，以往方法通常设计为对通用 IAA 模型做微调（Ren et al., 2017; Wang et al., 2018），或借助元学习提取跨用户的共性美学特征，以便高效迁移到目标个体（Zhu et al., 2020; Li et al., 2022）。

Despite the encouraging progress achieved, two important aspects remain underexplored in current PIAA methods. First, psychological research has demonstrated that some stimuli naturally elicit more active preference responses than others (Risko et al., 2012), indicating that not all images are equally informative for modeling aesthetic taste. Second, researches in cognitive aesthetics (Celikors and Field, 2025) revealed that aesthetic preferences often cluster among individuals with shared sensibilities—‘beauty is in the eye of your cohort’—suggesting that identifying aesthetically-resonant cohort offers valuable insights for target user modeling. Inspired by the above psychological studies, we argue that 1) quantifying the richness of aesthetic preferences across images and 2) identifying aesthetically-resonant user cohorts, provide viable pathways for modeling personalized aesthetic preferences, as illustrated in Figure 2. In this context, identified informative samples and auxiliary cohorts can navigate the few-shot learning constraints in PIAA, maximizing the utility of the limited aesthetic annotations available for target users. With comprehensive visual understanding and extensive open-world knowledge of demographic and personality factors (Jiang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a), Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) present natural advantages for sample measurement and cohort identification.

尽管已取得令人鼓舞的进展，当前 PIAA 方法仍有两方面未得到充分探索。第一，心理学研究表明，某些刺激天然会比另一些引发更活跃的偏好反应（Risko et al., 2012），这意味着并非所有图像对建模美学趣味都同等有效。第二，认知美学的研究（Celikors and Field, 2025）揭示，美学偏好常在具有共通感受力的个体之间聚集——「美存在于你的同好眼中」——说明识别美学共鸣群组能为目标用户建模提供有价值的线索。受上述心理学研究启发，我们认为：1）量化图像间美学偏好的丰富程度，2）识别美学共鸣的用户群组，为建模个性化美学偏好提供了可行路径，如图 2 所示。在此框架下，所识别的高信息量样本与辅助群组可以缓解 PIAA 中的小样本学习约束，最大化目标用户有限美学标注的效用。凭借全面的视觉理解能力，以及在人口统计学与人格因素上的丰富开放世界知识（Jiang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a），多模态大语言模型（MLLM）在样本度量与群组识别上具有天然优势。

Motivated by the above facts, this paper presents PRAC, a novel MLLM-based PIAA model that captures individual aesthetics by preference-rich sample mining and aesthetically-resonant cohort merging. PRAC is designed with two key components: PreferSelect and PreferMerge. Specifically, the MLLM is first instructed to predict aesthetic divergence across the public through aesthetic distribution training on rich generic aesthetic data. Building upon the learned consensus aesthetic understanding and MLLMs’ open-world knowledge, PreferSelect identifies preference-rich samples by analyzing both Collective Controversy and Personalized Deviation. Collective Controversy measures the intrinsic aesthetic controversy of an image among the general public, while Personalized Deviation captures the deviation of a specific user’s aesthetic taste from the public consensus. Subsequently, targeted fine-tuning is performed on the identified preference-rich samples to encode user-specific aesthetic patterns. PreferMerge searches for aesthetically-resonant user cohorts by comparing MLLM-derived preference embeddings. Cohort-based model merging is then performed to aggregate preference patterns from aesthetically-resonant users with similar tastes to further enhance personalization for the target individual.     2. Related Works

    2. 相关工作

    Personalized Image Aesthetic Assessment. PIAA aims to model individual aesthetic tastes based on few user-provided aesthetic annotations. To capture aesthetic preferences, existing vision-based PIAA methods leveraged aesthetics-related auxiliary tasks, including users’ photo favoriting behavior (Kim et al., 2018), image re-ranking (Hamzah, 2012), and personalized retouching (Lv et al., 2021), etc. Li et al. found that individual aesthetic preference strongly correlates with their personality traits and proposed a multi-task learning framework to jointly estimate both personality traits and aesthetic preferences (Li et al., 2020). Zhu et al. further introduced a personalized aesthetic assessment approach that models aesthetic attributes of images as subjective factors and users’ personality traits as objective factors (Zhu et al., 2021). To address the few-shot learning challenge, meta-learning was adopted to identify common aesthetic features across users that can be adapted to target individuals (Li et al., 2022; Zhu et al., 2020). Recently, Yang et al. applied contrastive learning to explore users’ preference differences across various aesthetic levels (Yang et al., 2024c). Yun et al. innovatively employed Task Arithmetic to model target users’ preferences by leveraging existing generic data (Yun and Choo, 2024). Different from these approaches, we model personalized aesthetics by quantifying preference richness across images and measuring preference similarity among users.

    个性化图像美学评价。PIAA 旨在基于少量用户提供的美学标注来建模个体美学趣味。为捕捉美学偏好，已有的基于视觉的 PIAA 方法利用了与美学相关的辅助任务，包括用户的照片收藏行为（Kim et al., 2018）、图像重排序（Hamzah, 2012）与个性化修图（Lv et al., 2021）等。Li 等人发现个体美学偏好与其人格特质高度相关，并提出多任务学习框架联合估计人格特质与美学偏好（Li et al., 2020）。Zhu 等人进一步提出一种个性化美学评价方法，把图像的美学属性建模为主观因素、把用户的人格特质建模为客观因素（Zhu et al., 2021）。为应对小样本学习的挑战，元学习被用来识别可适配到目标个体的跨用户共性美学特征（Li et al., 2022; Zhu et al., 2020）。近来，Yang 等人用对比学习探索用户在不同美学层级上的偏好差异（Yang et al., 2024c）。Yun 等人创新性地采用任务算术（Task Arithmetic），利用已有的通用数据来建模目标用户的偏好（Yun and Choo, 2024）。与这些方法不同，我们通过量化图像间偏好的丰富程度、并度量用户之间的偏好相似性来建模个性化美学。

    LLMs/MLLMs-based Personalization. The emergence of LLMs and MLLMs has opened new possibilities for personalization tasks due to their excellent capabilities in understanding complex patterns, reasoning about user behaviors, and generating contextually appropriate responses (Zhang et al., 2024b). Recent efforts employ techniques like personalized prompting, adaptation, and alignment to tailor these models to individual users (Liu et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2024a). Tan et al. employed personalized parameter-efficient fine-tuning to store user-specific behavior patterns and preferences, thereby democratizing LLMs (Tan et al., 2024). Zhang et al. proposed a parameterized memory-injected approach with a bayesian optimization search strategy to achieve LLM personalization (Zhang et al., 2024a). In this study, we present the first exploration of personalizing MLLMs for modeling individual aesthetic preferences.

    基于 LLM/MLLM 的个性化。LLM 与 MLLM 的出现为个性化任务带来了新的可能，因为它们在理解复杂模式、推理用户行为以及生成贴合语境的回答方面表现出色（Zhang et al., 2024b）。近期工作采用个性化提示、适配与对齐等技术，使这些模型适配到个体用户（Liu et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2024a）。Tan 等人用个性化的参数高效微调来存储用户特有的行为模式与偏好，从而推动 LLM 的普惠化（Tan et al., 2024）。Zhang 等人提出一种参数化记忆注入方法，配合贝叶斯优化搜索策略实现 LLM 个性化（Zhang et al., 2024a）。本文首次探索对 MLLM 做个性化，用于建模个体美学偏好。

    Model Merging. Model merging efficiently combines parameters from multiple specialized models to enhance overall capabilities without additional training. Representative approaches include linear interpolation, task arithmetic, and selective parameter merging(Li et al., 2023b). For LLMs and MLLMs, this paradigm enables capability integration without the prohibitive computational costs and data requirements of full-scale retraining (Yang et al., 2024a; Lu et al., 2024). Wu et al. proposed aggregating parameters from lightweight task-specific experts learned from similar tasks to benefit target downstream tasks (Wu et al., 2023). Tang et al. designed a partial linearization method for parameter-efficient fine-tuned models, improving multi-task fusion capabilities with low computational overhead (Tang et al., 2023). In this paper, we introduce cohort-based model merging for PIAA task.

    模型合并。模型合并能高效地把多个专用模型的参数组合起来，在无需额外训练的情况下提升整体能力。代表性方法包括线性插值、任务算术与选择性参数合并（Li et al., 2023b）。对 LLM 与 MLLM 而言，这一范式可以在不承担全面重训练的高昂计算成本与数据需求的前提下实现能力整合（Yang et al., 2024a; Lu et al., 2024）。Wu 等人提出聚合从相似任务中学得的轻量级任务专用专家的参数，以惠及目标下游任务（Wu et al., 2023）。Tang 等人针对参数高效微调模型设计了部分线性化方法，以较低的计算开销提升多任务融合能力（Tang et al., 2023）。本文则把基于群组的模型合并引入 PIAA 任务。

    3. Method

    3. 方法

    In this section, we detail the proposed PRAC, which models aesthetic preferences by mining preference-rich samples and merging aesthetically-resonant cohorts. The overall framework is shown in Figure 3, which consists of three stages. First, we establish a Generic Aesthetic Predictor by training an MLLM on rich generic aesthetic data, enabling consensus aesthetic understanding. Building on this foundation and leveraging its powerful open-world knowledge, we design PreferSelect to mine preference-rich samples. Finally, we propose PreferMerge, a cohort-based model merging strategy that identifies aesthetically-resonant users and integrates them to further boost personalization for the target individual.

    本节详述所提出的 PRAC，它通过挖掘富偏好样本与合并美学共鸣群组来建模美学偏好。总体框架如图 3 所示，包含三个阶段。首先，我们在丰富的通用美学数据上训练 MLLM，建立通用美学预测器，使其具备共识性的美学理解。在此基础之上，并借助其强大的开放世界知识，我们设计 PreferSelect 来挖掘富偏好样本。最后，我们提出 PreferMerge——一种基于群组的模型合并策略，它识别美学共鸣用户并加以整合，进一步增强对目标个体的个性化。

    3.1. Generic Aesthetic Predictor

    3.1. 通用美学预测器

    We first train a generic aesthetic predictor to model the distribution of public aesthetic judgments. Its ability to capture preference divergence serves as a crucial prerequisite for subsequent personalization stages. Given an image II, we obtain its aesthetic distribution from the statistics of individual annotations. The distribution label is represented as probabilities over five discrete aesthetic levels [Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad, Poor], denoted as 𝐪=[q1,q2,…,q5]\mathbf{q}=[q_{1},q_{2},\dots,q_{5}], where qiq_{i} denotes the probability of the ii-th aesthetic level. Utilizing these generic aesthetic data, we integrate a parameter-efficient LoRA module (Hu et al., 2022) into MLLMs to conduct aesthetic distribution training. Specifically, we design specialized prompt templates to guide MLLMs in generating aesthetic distributions and employ the Kullback-Leibler (KL) Divergence loss to optimize parameters.

    我们首先训练一个通用美学预测器来建模公众美学判断的分布。它捕捉偏好分歧的能力，是后续个性化阶段的重要前提。给定图像 II，我们从个体标注的统计中得到其美学分布。分布标签表示为五个离散美学层级 [Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad, Poor] 上的概率，记为 𝐪=[q1,q2,…,q5]\mathbf{q}=[q_{1},q_{2},\dots,q_{5}]，其中 qiq_{i} 表示第 ii 个美学层级的概率。利用这些通用美学数据，我们在 MLLM 中集成参数高效的 LoRA 模块（Hu et al., 2022）来执行美学分布训练。具体地，我们设计专门的提示模板来引导 MLLM 生成美学分布，并用 Kullback-Leibler（KL）散度损失优化参数。

    Guiding MLLMs for Distribution Prediction. Inspired by the training of previous MLLMs-based scoring methods (Wu et al., 2024; You et al., 2025), we prompt the MLLM with an image along with a fixed question: ‘How would you rate the aesthetic quality of this image?’. Simultaneously, we specify the output template as ‘The aesthetic quality of the image is <<Level Token>>’, where <<Level Token>> represents the predicted aesthetic level. Given that the token constitutes probabilities over all possible vocabulary in the language model, we perform a close-set softmax over the five textual aesthetic levels to obtain predicted aesthetic distribution of MLLMs, denoted as 𝐩=[p1,p2,…,p5]\mathbf{p}=[p_{1},p_{2},\dots,p_{5}].

    引导 MLLM 做分布预测。受以往基于 MLLM 的打分方法训练方式的启发（Wu et al., 2024; You et al., 2025），我们用一张图像与一个固定问题来提示 MLLM：“How would you rate the aesthetic quality of this image?”。同时，我们把输出模板指定为 “The aesthetic quality of the image is <<Level Token>>”，其中 <<Level Token>> 表示预测的美学层级。由于该词元在语言模型中构成覆盖全部词表的概率，我们对五个文本美学层级做闭集 softmax，得到 MLLM 预测的美学分布，记为 𝐩=[p1,p2,…,p5]\mathbf{p}=[p_{1},p_{2},\dots,p_{5}]。

    KL Loss for Parameter Update.     where DK​LD_{KL} denotes the KL divergence operation, NgN_{g} is the number of training images and jj indexes the five aesthetic levels of [Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad, Poor].

    3.2. Preference-rich Sample Mining

    3.2. 富偏好样本挖掘

    The varying richness of aesthetic preference across images lies in the fact that certain samples are more informative for personalization. PreferSelect aims to utilize the acquired aesthetics principles of the Generic Aesthetic Predictor along with MLLM’s extensive open-world knowledge to identify preference-rich samples. Inspired by research in cognitive aesthetics showing that aesthetic experiences arise from the interplay of demographics and personality traits (Washizu et al., 2025), we design a dual-metric selection mechanism to measure the preference richness of samples. This mechanism synergistically combines two perspectives: 1) the intrinsic aesthetic controversy of an image among the general public (Collective Controversy), and 2) the predicted deviation of a specific user’s taste from the public (Personalized Deviation).

    不同图像上美学偏好的丰富程度各异，其原因在于某些样本对个性化更具信息量。PreferSelect 旨在利用通用美学预测器习得的美学规律，以及 MLLM 丰富的开放世界知识，来识别富偏好样本。受认知美学研究发现——美学体验源于人口统计学特征与人格特质的交互（Washizu et al., 2025）——的启发，我们设计了双指标选择机制来度量样本的偏好丰富度。这一机制协同结合了两个视角：1）图像在公众中固有的美学争议性（群体争议度）；2）特定用户趣味相对公众的预测偏离（个性化偏离度）。

    Collective Controversy Metric (CCM). Images eliciting significant controversy in aesthetic judgments inherently encode richer personalized preference information. Based on this observation, we design a simple yet effective metric to quantify such controversy. Specifically, for a given image IiI^{i}, we utilize the Generic Aesthetics Predictor to predict its aesthetic distribution 𝐩i\mathbf{p}^{i} and calculate the standard deviation (STD) of this distribution as the measurement:

    群体争议度指标（CCM）。在美学判断上引发显著争议的图像，天然编码了更丰富的个性化偏好信息。基于这一观察，我们设计了一个简洁而有效的指标来量化这种争议性。具体地，对给定图像 IiI^{i}，我们用通用美学预测器预测其美学分布 𝐩i\mathbf{p}^{i}，并计算该分布的标准差（STD）作为度量：

    (2) CCM​(Ii)=σ⁡(𝐩i)=∑j=15(j−μi)2⋅pji,\text{CCM}(I^{i})=\sigma(\mathbf{p}^{i})=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^{5}(j-\mu^{i})^{2}\cdot{p_{j}^{i}}},

    where σ\sigma denotes the standard deviation, and μi=∑j=15j⋅pji\mu^{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{5}j\cdot p_{j}^{i} is the mean of the distribution for image IiI^{i}.

    其中 σ\sigma 表示标准差，μi=∑j=15j⋅pji\mu^{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{5}j\cdot p_{j}^{i} 为图像 IiI^{i} 对应分布的均值。

    Personalized Deviation Metric (PDM). People’s aesthetic preferences are significantly influenced by their intrinsic attributes, particularly personality traits and photographic expertise (Yang et al., 2022). MLLMs with extensive open-world knowledge of demographic and personality traits (Jiang et al., 2023), provide a foundation for measuring the impact of these factors on aesthetic judgments. Inspired by in-context learning (Dong et al., 2024), we convert discrete user profiles (age, traits, expertise) into natural language descriptions and integrate them into the prompt: ‘You are a <<User Profile>>, how would you rate the aesthetic quality of this image?’. This prompt is then input to the Generic Aesthetic Predictor to generate an in-context aesthetic distribution. The PDM is defined as the KL Divergence between the generic distribution 𝐩i\mathbf{p}^{i} and the in-context distribution 𝐩ui\mathbf{p}_{u}^{i}:

    个性化偏离度指标（PDM）。人们的美学偏好受其内在属性显著影响，尤其是人格特质与摄影专业经验（Yang et al., 2022）。具备人口统计学与人格特质方面丰富开放世界知识的 MLLM（Jiang et al., 2023），为度量这些因素对美学判断的影响提供了基础。受上下文学习（Dong et al., 2024）启发，我们把离散的用户画像（年龄、特质、专业经验）转换为自然语言描述，并整合进提示：“You are a <<User Profile>>, how would you rate the aesthetic quality of this image?”。该提示随后被输入通用美学预测器，生成上下文条件下的美学分布。PDM 定义为通用分布 𝐩i\mathbf{p}^{i} 与上下文分布 𝐩ui\mathbf{p}_{u}^{i} 之间的 KL 散度：

    (3) PDM(Ii)=DK​L(𝐩ui∥𝐩i).\text{PDM}(I^{i})=D_{KL}(\mathbf{p}_{u}^{i}\parallel\mathbf{p}^{i}).

    To comprehensively integrate both Collective Controversy and Personalized Deviation, we propose a unified preference richness score Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}}. For image IiI^{i}, it is formulated as a weighted combination of the two fundamental metrics:

    为综合考量群体争议度与个性化偏离度，我们提出统一的偏好丰富度分数 Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}}。对图像 IiI^{i}，它被表述为两个基础指标的加权组合：

    (4) Pscore​(Ii)=(1−α)⋅CCM​(Ii)+α⋅PDM​(Ii),\text{P}_{\text{score}}(I^{i})=(1-\alpha)\cdot\text{CCM}(I^{i})+\alpha\cdot\text{PDM}(I^{i}),

    where α\alpha is a weighting hyperparameter that balances the contributions of collective controversy and personalized deviation. For a specific user UiU_{i} with his/her personal images, we compute Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}} for each image and rank all candidates accordingly. Images with higher scores are selected to form the personalized query set 𝒟u\mathcal{D}_{u} for the user. This curated set facilitates efficient encoding of user-specific aesthetic patterns in the PreferMerge stage. Figure 4 illustrates the PreferSelect results: two users with distinct demographic and personality traits demonstrate markedly different preference-rich samples. Furthermore, within each user, top-3 samples exhibit significantly higher Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}} than bottom-3 samples, validating the quantification of preference-richness.

    其中 α\alpha 是平衡群体争议度与个性化偏离度贡献的加权超参数。对拥有其个人图像的特定用户 UiU_{i}，我们计算每张图像的 Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}} 并据此为所有候选样本排序。分数较高的图像被选出，构成该用户的个性化查询集 𝒟u\mathcal{D}_{u}。这一精选集合有助于在 PreferMerge 阶段高效编码用户特有的美学模式。图 4 展示了 PreferSelect 的结果：人口统计学特征与人格特质各异的两位用户呈现出明显不同的富偏好样本。此外，在每位用户内部，前 3 个样本的 Pscore\text{P}_{\text{score}} 明显高于后 3 个样本，验证了偏好丰富度量化是有效的。

    3.3. Aesthetically-resonant Cohort Merging

    3.3. 美学共鸣群组合并

    Based on the identified preference-rich images, we propose PreferMerge, a cohort-based model merging strategy that discovers and integrates aesthetically-resonant user cohorts to enhance target user personalization. Under limited annotation scenarios (e.g., 10-shot or 100-shot settings, commonly adopted in the PIAA task) where the target user provides aesthetic ratings on preference-rich samples, PreferMerge first measures aesthetic preference similarity across users, then identifies and merges aesthetically-resonant users to strengthen personalization for the target individual.

    在识别出的富偏好图像基础上，我们提出 PreferMerge——一种基于群组的模型合并策略，用于发现并整合美学共鸣的用户群组，以增强对目标用户的个性化。在标注有限的场景（例如 PIAA 任务中常用的 10-shot 或 100-shot 设定）下，目标用户对富偏好样本给出美学评分；PreferMerge 先度量用户之间的美学偏好相似性，再识别并合并美学共鸣用户，从而强化对目标个体的个性化。

    Measuring Cross-user Preference Similarity. Individual annotations on preference-rich samples directly reflect their aesthetic sensibilities, providing a foundation for measuring cross-user preference similarity. We fine-tune the Generic Aesthetic Predictor using these personalized annotations and extract gradient directions as preference embeddings. The correlation between preference embeddings serves as a robust metric for quantifying preference similarity among users.

    度量跨用户偏好相似性。个体在富偏好样本上的标注直接反映其美学感受力，为度量跨用户偏好相似性提供了基础。我们用这些个性化标注微调通用美学预测器，并提取梯度方向作为偏好嵌入。偏好嵌入之间的相关性可作为量化用户间偏好相似性的稳健指标。

    Given a query set 𝒟u=(Ik,yk)k=1Nu\mathcal{D}_{u}={(I^{k},y^{k})_{k=1}^{N_{u}}} of preference-rich samples for user UiU_{i}, where yky^{k} denotes the user’s personalized annotation to image IkI^{k} as a discrete aesthetic level label, we perform targeted fine-tuning. A user-specific LoRA module is injected into the Generic Aesthetic Predictor and fine-tuned exclusively on its parameters θu\theta_{u}. The cross-entropy loss between predicted and ground-truth aesthetic levels guides the parameter updates. We compute the gradient fluctuations of parameters θu\theta_{u} on the query set 𝒟u\mathcal{D}_{u} as the preference embedding for user UiU_{i}. Specifically, we employ the Fisher Information Matrix (FIM) as an indicator (Amari, 1998), which quantifies the model’s sensitivity to parameter perturbations under limited data:

    给定用户 UiU_{i} 的富偏好样本查询集 𝒟u=(Ik,yk)k=1Nu\mathcal{D}_{u}={(I^{k},y^{k})_{k=1}^{N_{u}}}，其中 yky^{k} 表示用户对图像 IkI^{k} 的个性化标注（离散的美学层级标签），我们执行针对性微调：把一个用户专用的 LoRA 模块注入通用美学预测器，并仅在其参数 θu\theta_{u} 上微调。预测美学层级与真值之间的交叉熵损失指导参数更新。我们计算参数 θu\theta_{u} 在查询集 𝒟u\mathcal{D}_{u} 上的梯度波动，作为用户 UiU_{i} 的偏好嵌入。具体地，我们采用 Fisher 信息矩阵（FIM）作为指标（Amari, 1998），它量化了在有限数据下模型对参数扰动的敏感程度：

    (5) 𝐅u=𝔼(I,y)∼𝒟u[∇θu​log​p​(y∣I)​∇θu​log⁡p​(y∣I)T],\mathbf{F}_{u}=\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{(I,y)\sim\mathcal{D}_{u}}\left[\nabla_{\theta_{u}}\log p\left(y\mid I\right)\nabla_{\theta_{u}}\log p\left(y\mid I\right)^{T}\right],

    where 𝐅u\mathbf{F}_{u} represents the preference embedding. For two users UiU_{i} and UjU_{j}, with respective embeddings 𝐅ui\mathbf{F}_{u}^{i} and 𝐅uj\mathbf{F}_{u}^{j}, we compute the cosine distance between them to measure preference similarity Sim⁡(Ui,Uj)\operatorname{Sim}\left(U_{i},U_{j}\right):

    其中 𝐅u\mathbf{F}_{u} 表示偏好嵌入。对两个用户 UiU_{i} 与 UjU_{j}，其嵌入分别为 𝐅ui\mathbf{F}_{u}^{i} 与 𝐅uj\mathbf{F}_{u}^{j}，我们计算二者之间的余弦距离来度量偏好相似性 Sim⁡(Ui,Uj)\operatorname{Sim}\left(U_{i},U_{j}\right)：

    (6) Sim⁡(Ui,Uj)=𝐅ui⋅𝐅uj‖𝐅ui‖⋅‖𝐅uj‖.\operatorname{Sim}\left(U_{i},U_{j}\right)=\frac{\mathbf{F}_{u}^{i}\cdot\mathbf{F}_{u}^{j}}{\left\|\mathbf{F}_{u}^{i}\right\|\cdot\left\|\mathbf{F}_{u}^{j}\right\|}.

    Cohort Selection and Merging. With cross-user preference similarities quantified, we identify an optimal cohort of aesthetically-resonant users and merge them to enhance target user personalization. The selection is performed on a pre-constructed User Pool, which contains personalized LoRAs from multiple training users obtained through targeted fine-tuning on their respective query sets, serving as a rich library of diverse aesthetic preferences. The selection strategy balances two key objectives: Target Relevance and Cohort Diversity. Target Relevance ensures that selected users are maximally relevant to the target individual, while Cohort Diversity prevents redundancy among the selected users. To balance these two aspects, we introduce a trade-off parameter β\beta and formulate the optimal selection problem as:

    where 𝒫\mathcal{P} denotes the User Pool, and 𝒮∗\mathcal{S}^{*} is the optimal subset of KK aesthetically-resonant users that maximizes relevance to the target user while minimizing intra-cohort redundancy.

    其中 𝒫\mathcal{P} 表示用户池，𝒮∗\mathcal{S}^{*} 是 KK 个美学共鸣用户的最优子集，它在最大化与目标用户相关性的同时最小化群组内部冗余。

    Once the optimal set 𝒮∗\mathcal{S}^{*} is identified, we merge the personalized LoRA models from these selected users through a weighted fusion operation. Each model contributes proportionally to its preference similarity with the target user:

    5. Conclusion

    5. 结论

    In this paper, we rethink personalized image aesthetic assessment by modeling preference richness across images and cross-user preference similarity. We have proposed PRAC, the first MLLM-based PIAA model that accurately predicts users’ personalized aesthetic ratings on images and provides interpretable rationales explaining divergent judgments. Through preference-rich sample mining and aesthetically-resonant cohort merging, we effectively model individual aesthetic preferences under few-shot annotations. Extensive evaluations across four PIAA benchmark datasets demonstrate the superior performance of the proposed model. While very encouraging results have been achieved in this work, investigations on the cohort-relatedness of aesthetic preferences would further facilitate preference modeling, which is worth of more explorations.

    本文通过建模图像间的偏好丰富程度与跨用户的偏好相似性，重新思考了个性化图像美学评价。我们提出 PRAC——首个基于 MLLM 的 PIAA 模型，它能准确预测用户对图像的个性化美学评分，并提供可解释的依据来说明判断为何产生分歧。借助富偏好样本挖掘与美学共鸣群组合并，我们在小样本标注条件下有效地建模了个体美学偏好。在四个 PIAA 基准数据集上的大量评测表明，所提模型性能优越。尽管本工作已取得令人鼓舞的结果，对美学偏好的群组关联性展开研究将进一步完善偏好建模，值得更多探索。
